/ What NRI taxation covers

The work, in one list.

  • Residential status each year (resident, RNOR or non-resident), including the Section 6(1A) deemed-resident rule.
  • Indian income tax returns for rent, interest, capital gains, dividends and business income, and refunds of excess TDS.
  • Treaty claims with a Tax Residency Certificate and Form 10F (Form 41 from 1 April 2026), and foreign tax credit through Form 67 (Form 44 from tax year 2026-27) for residents.
  • Selling Indian property: Section 195 TDS, Section 197 lower deduction certificate, Section 54 (section 82 of the 2025 Act)/54F/54EC exemptions.
  • Moving money out: NRO to abroad up to USD 1 million a year with Form 15CA/15CB (Forms 145/146 from 1 April 2026).
  • Gifts, inheritance, NRE/NRO/FCNR accounts and planning a return to India.

/ By country of residence

What changes depending on where you live.

RegionKey point
USAUS citizens and green-card holders are taxed by the US on worldwide income and by India on Indian-source income; double tax is relieved through foreign tax credits under the India-US DTAA.
UKThe UK replaced the remittance basis with a four-year foreign income and gains regime from 6 April 2025; Indian income of long-term UK residents is now generally taxable in the UK, with credit for Indian tax under the India-UK DTAA.
UAE & GulfGulf countries largely have no personal income tax, so Indian income is taxed only in India; filing correctly in India is the whole job.
Singapore & SE AsiaSingapore taxes residents on Singapore-source income and most foreign income remitted is exempt in practice, so Indian income is usually taxed only in India.
CanadaCanadian residents are taxed on worldwide income; Indian tax is credited under the India-Canada DTAA.
EuropeEuropean tax residents are generally taxed on worldwide income at home, with credit for Indian tax under the relevant DTAA.
JapanJapanese residents are taxed on worldwide income (non-permanent residents on a narrower base); Indian tax is credited under the India-Japan DTAA.
AfricaLarge Indian-origin communities in East and Southern Africa hold Indian property, deposits and family assets that need correct Indian filings.
Latin AmericaIndians working in Latin America keep Indian property and deposits that need Indian filings; India has tax treaties with Brazil, Mexico and Chile among others.
Australia & NZAustralian residents are taxed on worldwide income with credit for Indian tax under the India-Australia DTAA.

Do NRIs need to file an Indian return if TDS was deducted?
Often yes: TDS on NRI rent, interest and property sales is frequently higher than the actual tax, and a return is the only way to get the excess back. Capital gains must be reported regardless.
Can BQP claim treaty benefits for me?
Yes, with a Tax Residency Certificate from your country of residence and Form 10F filed electronically in India.
Can BQP help me move money from India to my country?
Yes: Form 15CA/15CB for remittances from NRO accounts within the USD 1 million annual limit.
Does BQP handle returning-NRI planning?
Yes, including RNOR status, NRE/FCNR conversion and timing of foreign asset sales.
