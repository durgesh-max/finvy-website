Partnership Firm Registration in India (2026): Online Process, Documents, Partnership Deed, Certificate, Form 1 and Why an Unregistered Firm Cannot Sue, optional in law, essential in practice.

A partnership firm is formed by a partnership deed between two or more people; registering it with the state Registrar of Firms is optional under the Indian Partnership Act, 1932, but an unregistered firm and its partners cannot sue third parties to enforce the firm’s contracts (section 69). Registration is done by filing a statement (usually Form 1) signed by all partners with the Registrar of Firms of the state where the firm does business, with the stamped deed and the prescribed fee; many states now accept it online. The Registrar then enters the firm in the register and issues a certificate of registration. Fees and stamp duty are set by each state.

/ Quick answers

What people search for.

  • Is registration compulsory? No, but section 69 bars an unregistered firm from suing to enforce its contracts, and partners from suing the firm or each other on the deed. Most firms register for that reason; banks and government buyers also often ask for it.
  • Can I register online? In many states, yes, through the state Registrar of Firms or a state services portal. Other states still take paper filings.
  • How do I download the registration certificate? Where the state registers online, the certificate is usually available from the same portal after approval; otherwise it is issued by the Registrar’s office.
  • What are the fees? Each state sets its own registration fee and stamp duty on the deed; check the state portal before filing.
  • How many partners? At least two. The Companies Act rules cap a partnership at 50 partners.
  • Can I register later? Yes. Registration can be done at any time, and a firm that needs to sue can register before filing the suit.

/ Tax and alternatives

What changes after registration.

  • Income tax: the firm is taxed as a separate entity and files ITR-5. Partner salaries and interest on capital are deductible within the limits of section 40(b), and from 1 April 2025 the firm deducts TDS on such payments to partners; see TDS on payments to partners.
  • GST: see is GST registration mandatory?.
  • NRI partners: an NRI or OCI can contribute capital on a non-repatriation basis, except in barred activities; see can an NRI start a business in India.
  • Limited liability: partners in a firm have unlimited, joint liability. If that matters, compare LLP registration and LLP vs private limited.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is partnership firm registration compulsory in India?
No. Registration with the state Registrar of Firms is optional under the Indian Partnership Act, 1932, but under section 69 an unregistered firm cannot sue third parties to enforce its contracts, and partners cannot sue the firm or each other on the partnership, so most firms register.
How do I register a partnership firm online?
Draft and stamp the partnership deed, get the firm's PAN, then file the Form 1 statement signed by all partners with the state Registrar of Firms, online where the state allows it, with the deed, partners' ID and address proof, proof of the place of business and the state fee. The Registrar then issues a certificate of registration.
What should a partnership deed contain?
At minimum: the firm's name, business and principal place of business; the partners' names and addresses; the date the partnership starts and whether it is at will or for a fixed term; each partner's capital contribution; the profit and loss sharing ratio; and rules for drawings, bank account operation, admission and retirement of partners, death of a partner, dissolution, and settling disputes. For tax, the deed matters more than people expect: interest on capital (deductible up to 12% simple a year) and salary or remuneration to working partners (deductible within the section 40(b) limits based on book profit) are allowed only if the deed authorises them and states the rate or the method of working them out. Since 1 April 2025 the firm also deducts TDS on remuneration, interest and similar payments to partners above the threshold. Have the deed stamped under the state's stamp law before signing, and amend it by a supplementary deed when terms change. Book a call to draft or review the deed.
What documents are required for partnership firm registration?
The stamped partnership deed, Form 1 signed by all partners, the firm's PAN, ID and address proof of each partner, and proof of the place of business such as a rent agreement or ownership document with an NOC.
What is the fee for partnership firm registration?
Each state sets its own registration fee and stamp duty on the partnership deed, so check the state Registrar of Firms' portal for the current amounts.
Can an unregistered partnership firm file a case?
It cannot sue a third party to enforce a contract, but courts have held the bar does not apply to statutory or common law rights. The firm can also register before filing the suit to cure the defect.
