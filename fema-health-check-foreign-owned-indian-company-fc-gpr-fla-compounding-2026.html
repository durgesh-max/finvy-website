FEMA Health Check for Foreign-Owned Indian Companies (2026): FC-GPR, FC-TRS, FLA, Form DI, ECB and Valuation Gaps, and How to Fix Them, find missed FEMA filings before a buyer, investor or bank does.

Every Indian company with foreign investment carries a FEMA filing history, and gaps surface at the worst moments: a fundraise, a sale, a dividend or a bank's KYC review. The core obligations are to allot shares within 60 days of receiving foreign money, file FC-GPR within 30 days of allotment and FC-TRS within 60 days of a transfer, file the FLA return by 15 July every year, file Form DI within 30 days of a downstream investment, register foreign loans (ECB) before drawdown, and keep a fair-value report for every issue or transfer. Reporting delays within three years of the due date are usually settled with a late submission fee (INR 7,500 plus 0.025% of the amount for each year). Older delays and substantive breaches go to compounding with the RBI. Run the check below, then book a call to plan the clean-up.

    / What each check covers

    And why it matters.

    • Allotment within 60 days: money not allotted or refunded in time is a contravention.
    • FC-GPR and FC-TRS: reporting of issues and transfers on the RBI FIRMS portal; see FC-GPR checklist and FC-TRS checklist.
    • Valuation: fair-value report for every issue or transfer with a non-resident; see FEMA valuation report.
    • FLA return: annual, by 15 July, for every company with foreign investment; see FLA return.
    • Form DI: for downstream investment by a foreign-owned company; see downstream investment.
    • ECB: LRN before drawdown and ECB 2 returns; see loans from foreign shareholders.
    • Sector caps and approvals: including Press Note 3 for land-border investors; see Press Note 2 (2026).

    FAQ

    Common questions, answered.

    What FEMA filings does a foreign-owned Indian company need?
    FC-GPR within 30 days of each allotment, FC-TRS within 60 days of transfers with residents, the FLA return by 15 July every year, Form DI for downstream investment, ECB registration and returns for foreign loans, and a fair-value report for each issue or transfer.
    What happens if FC-GPR was filed late?
    Delays within three years of the due date are usually regularised with a late submission fee of INR 7,500 plus 0.025% of the amount per year; longer delays go to compounding.
    What if shares were not allotted within 60 days?
    Holding foreign money beyond 60 days without allotment or refund within 15 days is a contravention, generally regularised through compounding with the RBI.
    Is the FLA return needed every year?
    Yes, by 15 July each year for every company that has received foreign direct investment or made overseas investment, even if nothing new happened.
    When should FEMA gaps be fixed?
    Before a fundraise, sale, dividend repatriation or bank review, because buyers and banks check the FEMA history.
