/ Entity structure decision tree

Six options.

Six legal entity options for foreign company in India:

  1. Wholly-Owned Subsidiary (Pvt Ltd) — the default. Separate legal entity, 100% foreign ownership, limited liability, standard corporate tax, easy to operate. Choose if: you'll have Indian employees, invoice Indian customers, build material India business. See WOS guide.
  2. Joint Venture (Pvt Ltd with Indian partner) — same as WOS but with Indian equity partner. Choose if: regulated sector with FDI cap (defence 74%, insurance 74%, print media 26%), or local market knowledge / government relationship is essential.
  3. Branch Office — extension of foreign parent, not a separate entity. Requires RBI prior approval. Limited to specified activities (export/import, consultancy, representation, R&D). Choose if: narrow operational scope, parent wants direct accountability.
  4. Liaison Office — representative office only; cannot do business. RBI prior approval. Cannot invoice; expenses funded by parent remittance. Choose if: exploratory presence, market research, no revenue plan.
  5. Project Office — for a specific project (government contract, infrastructure). Time-bound.
  6. LLP — foreign investment permitted only in specific sectors + under automatic route. Less common for foreign parents; chosen occasionally for small operations.

For 90%+ of foreign companies entering India for business, the answer is WOS (Pvt Ltd). See the full comparison in our Branch vs Liaison vs WOS guide.

/ FDI regulatory check

Automatic vs Approval route.

India's FDI policy:

  • Automatic route: 100% FDI allowed, no prior government approval. Applies to most sectors: IT/SaaS, trading, manufacturing, services, retail trading (single-brand under conditions).
  • Approval route: FDI capped (e.g., multi-brand retail 51%, print media 26%, broadcasting 49%, insurance 74%, defence 74%, telecom under conditions). Requires prior Government approval via FDI portal (DPIIT).
  • Prohibited sectors: lottery, gambling, chit funds, Nidhi companies, trading in transferable development rights, real estate business (not construction), atomic energy, railway operations (certain areas), tobacco manufacturing.
  • Press Note 3 (April 2020) (eased for non-controlling stakes within 10% by Press Note 2 (2026)): for countries sharing land border with India (China, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar, Afghanistan) OR beneficial ownership from such countries, prior government approval mandatory regardless of sector / FDI cap.

Pre-check your sector + beneficial ownership before incorporation.

/ GST + tax regime

The compliance stack.

  • Corporate tax: 25% / 22% (Section 115BAA) / 15% (Section 115BAB new manufacturing). See corporate tax guide.
  • Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT): 15% on book profit (does not apply if 115BAA / 115BAB opted).
  • Dividend Distribution Tax (DDT): abolished from April 2020. Dividend taxable in shareholder's hands (foreign parent → Section 195 TDS at treaty rate).
  • GST: 18% standard; 5% / 12% reduced; 28% luxury. Monthly GSTR-1 + 3B. Annual GSTR-9 if turnover > INR 2 crore.
  • IGST on imports: paid on customs value + Basic Customs Duty; credit available if used for GST-taxable supply.
  • Customs duty: Basic Customs Duty + Social Welfare Surcharge + IGST. HSN-wise rates. Free Trade Agreement countries (ASEAN, Japan, Korea, UAE post-CEPA, UK post-CETA 2026) have preferential rates.
  • Transfer pricing: Form 3CEB (Form 48 from tax year 2026-27) + benchmarking study. See TP guide.
  • Equalisation Levy: 2% on digital services payments to non-residents (though under BEPS Pillar 1 phase-out).

/ Hiring Indian talent + payroll

People side.

  • Employment contracts: Indian subsidiary hires Indian employees directly. Standard offer letter + employment agreement + leave + termination terms. Governed by Shops & Establishment Act (state-specific) + Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act.
  • PF (Provident Fund): 12% employer + 12% employee contribution. Mandatory if headcount >= 20.
  • ESI (Employee State Insurance): 3.25% employer + 0.75% employee. Mandatory if gross salary <= INR 21,000/month.
  • Professional Tax: state-specific, max INR 2,500/year per employee.
  • Gratuity: 15 days salary per completed year of service, payable on exit after 5 years. Provision required in books.
  • Section 192 TDS: employer deducts income tax on employee salary monthly.
  • Alternative - EOR: if you don't yet have Indian subsidiary, use Employer of Record. See EOR guide.

/ Data protection + IP + banking

Three must-check items.

  • DPDP Act 2023: India's data protection law (effective FY 2024-25 onwards). Covers 'personal data' of Indian residents. Consent-based processing, data principal rights, breach notification, cross-border transfer restrictions (specific countries approved by govt). Fines up to INR 250 crore for violation.
  • IP registration: trademark + copyright + patent + design — foreign company can register directly or via Madrid Protocol (trademark) / PCT national phase (patent). See India IP guide.
  • Banking: current account for Indian subsidiary with HDFC / ICICI / Kotak / Axis / foreign bank Indian branch (Citi, DBS, HSBC). AD-I category banks handle FEMA remittance.
  • Repatriation: dividend / royalty / FTS / interest to foreign parent. See repatriation guide.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's the standard entity for foreign company entering India?
Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) as 100% Wholly-Owned Subsidiary (WOS) of foreign parent. Chosen in ~90% of foreign market entries. Alternatives (Branch, Liaison, Project Office, LLP, JV) chosen in specific scenarios - sector restrictions, exploratory presence, time-bound projects.
Does my FDI need government approval?
Depends on sector + source. Automatic route for most sectors (IT, SaaS, trading, manufacturing, services) - no prior approval. Approval route for sector-capped (multi-brand retail 51%, defence 74%, print media 26%, insurance 74%). Press Note 3: any investment from countries sharing land border with India (China, Pakistan, Bangladesh, etc.) requires prior approval regardless of sector.
How long does India market entry take?
12-24 weeks typical: FDI sector verification + beneficial ownership check (2 weeks), apostille of parent docs (3-4 weeks), DSC/DIN + incorporation (3-4 weeks), bank account + capital remittance + FC-GPR (4 weeks), GST + professional tax + Shops & Establishment (2-4 weeks), DPDP + IP registrations (ongoing).
What are India's typical tax rates for a foreign subsidiary?
Corporate tax: 22% + surcharge + cess = ~25.17% effective under Section 115BAA (opted by most foreign subsidiaries). Dividend from subsidiary to foreign parent: Section 195 TDS at 20% (standard) or treaty rate (typically 10-15% under DTAA with TRC + Form 10F [Form 41 from 1 April 2026]). GST 18% on most B2B services + goods.
Can I hire Indian employees without an Indian entity?
Yes - via Employer of Record (EOR). The EOR is the legal employer in India (handles payroll, PF, ESI, taxes); you pay EOR a monthly fee + employee cost. Useful for 1-10 Indian hires + exploratory presence. For 10+ hires or long-term India plan, incorporating your own Indian subsidiary is more cost-effective.
