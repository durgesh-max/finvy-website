How to File Form 10F Electronic India for NRI Without PAN, PAN-less registration + e-filing.

NRIs without Indian PAN can file electronic Form 10F on Indian Income Tax e-filing portal via specific PAN-less registration. CBDT Notification/Circular July 2024 clarified this route. Required for every NRI claiming DTAA treaty rate on India-source income. Here's the complete step-by-step process.

/ Pre-registration preparation

What NRI needs.

  • Foreign Tax Identification Number (TIN) from home country tax authority.
  • If no formal TIN: foreign passport number acceptable.
  • Country of residence.
  • Email + mobile (international - specific country code).
  • TRC (Tax Residency Certificate) from home country tax authority.
  • Foreign address proof.

/ PAN-less registration on India e-filing portal

The access path.

  1. Visit incometax.gov.in.
  2. Click 'Register' as 'Taxpayer'.
  3. Select user type: 'Non-Resident Not Holding and Not Required to Have PAN'.
  4. Enter personal details: name + DOB + nationality + foreign TIN (or passport #) + country of residence.
  5. Enter communication: email + international mobile.
  6. OTP verification: email + mobile.
  7. Account created + login credentials.
  8. Account linked to foreign TIN / passport (NOT Indian PAN).

/ Form 10F e-filing

The declaration.

  1. Login to e-filing portal with new account.
  2. Navigate: e-File → Income Tax Forms → File Income Tax Forms → Form 10F.
  3. Complete 9 fields:
  4.   — Status (individual / company / firm / other).
  5.   — Nationality / country of incorporation.
  6.   — TIN in home country.
  7.   — Period for which residential status (per TRC) applies.
  8.   — Address in home country.
  9.   — Indian PAN if available (otherwise foreign TIN as substitute).
  10.   — Confirmation of tax residency in home country.
  11.   — Confirmation of beneficial ownership of income.
  12.   — Signature declaration.
  13. Upload TRC from home country.
  14. Submit (OTP-based for non-resident; or specific e-sign).
  15. Acknowledgement number generated - retain + share with Indian payer.

/ Post-filing + ongoing

Share with Indian payer.

  • Form 10F now visible to Indian payer when they look up non-resident's Indian tax profile.
  • Indian payer uses Form 10F + TRC + no-PE declaration to apply treaty rate (not 20% default) under Section 195.
  • Form 10F valid for period covered by TRC (typically 1 year).
  • Renew annually: new TRC + fresh Form 10F.
  • If circumstances change (country of residence, treaty eligibility): refile Form 10F.
  • Form 15CA (Form 145 from 1 April 2026) + 15CB by Indian payer references Form 10F for treaty rate claim.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can NRI without PAN file Form 10F?
Yes - CBDT Notification/Circular July 2024 specifically clarified this route. NRI registers on India e-filing portal as 'Non-Resident Not Holding and Not Required to Have PAN' using foreign TIN or passport + country of residence + email + mobile. Account enables Form 10F e-filing. Not required to obtain Indian PAN specifically for Form 10F.
What's TRC and why is it needed?
Tax Residency Certificate - issued by home country tax authority confirming NRI is tax resident there. Required under Section 90(4) Income Tax Act for DTAA treaty rate. Form 10F + TRC together form the stack for Indian payer to apply treaty rate under Section 195.
How often to refile Form 10F?
Annually - validity tied to TRC period (typically 1 year). Fresh Form 10F needed for each FY where DTAA benefit continues. If NRI's circumstances change (country of residence, treaty eligibility, beneficial ownership): refile immediately.
Can Indian payer apply treaty rate without Form 10F?
No - Section 90(4) + Section 90(5) require Form 10F + TRC for DTAA benefit. Without these: Section 195 default rate (20%) or Section 206AA rate (20%+) applies. Overpaid TDS can be refunded via Indian ITR but immediate deduction is at higher rate.
Does BQP help NRIs with Form 10F + TRC + DTAA claim?
Yes - standard NRI advisory. Form 10F e-filing (via PAN-less registration or Indian PAN) + TRC coordination from home country + no-PE declaration drafting + DTAA treaty rate analysis + Form 15CA/15CB coordination with Indian payer + refund claim via Indian ITR if applicable.
