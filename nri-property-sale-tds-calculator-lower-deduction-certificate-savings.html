NRI Property Sale TDS Calculator: How Much Extra Tax the Buyer Withholds, and What a Lower TDS Certificate Saves, TDS on the price, tax on the gain.

When an NRI sells property in India, the buyer deducts TDS on the full sale price, not on the profit: for a long-term sale that is 12.5% plus surcharge and cess, up to 14.95% of the price. Your actual tax is on the gain only, so the difference sits with the Income Tax Department until you file and get a refund. A lower or nil deduction certificate, obtained before the sale, aims to cut TDS to your real tax. Use the calculator to see the gap on your numbers.

    / How it works

    The rules behind the numbers.

    • Rate: long-term gains on property (held more than 24 months) transferred on or after 23 July 2024 are taxed at 12.5% without indexation.
    • Surcharge and cess: 10% surcharge above INR 50 lakh and 15% above INR 1 crore (capped at 15% for long-term gains), plus 4% cess.
    • Base: without a certificate, the buyer applies these rates to the whole sale consideration.
    • Short-term: TDS at 30% plus surcharge and cess on the price; your actual tax is at slab rates.
    • Certificate: apply to the Income Tax Department with the sale details and your cost; the buyer then deducts at the certified rate.

    FAQ

    Common questions, answered.

    How much TDS is deducted when an NRI sells property?
    For a long-term sale, 12.5% plus surcharge and cess on the full sale price unless the NRI has a lower deduction certificate; up to 14.95% of the price above INR 1 crore. For a short-term sale, 30% plus surcharge and cess.
    Why is TDS more than my actual tax?
    Because the buyer applies the rate to the whole price, while your tax is only on the gain. The difference is refunded after you file your return.
    What does a lower deduction certificate do?
    It authorises the buyer to deduct TDS at a lower or nil rate based on your estimated tax, so less money is locked up until the refund.
    Can NRIs use indexation on property sales?
    Long-term gains on transfers from 23 July 2024 are taxed at 12.5% without indexation. The 20% with-indexation option for property bought before that date is framed for residents; check your case.
    Is the calculator result exact?
    No. It assumes the gain is your only Indian income and ignores exemptions and marginal relief. Use it to size the gap, then get the certificate worked out on your documents.
