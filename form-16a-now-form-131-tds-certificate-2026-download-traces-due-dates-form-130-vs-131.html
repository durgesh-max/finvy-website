Form 16A Is Now Form 131 (2026): TDS Certificate for Non-Salary Payments, Quarterly Due Dates, How to Download from TRACES and Form 130 vs 131, same certificate, new number from tax year 2026-27.

From tax year 2026-27, Form 16A, the TDS certificate for payments other than salary (rent, professional fees, interest, commission and similar payments), is called Form 131 under the Income-tax Rules, 2026. The deductor downloads it from TRACES and must issue it every quarter within 15 days after the TDS return is due, for example by 15 November 2026 for July to September 2026. Salary TDS gets a separate annual certificate, Form 130 (formerly Form 16).

/ How to download and check Form 131

For deductors and recipients.

  • Deductor: file the quarterly TDS statement, log in to TRACES with the TAN, request the certificate for the quarter and download it once generated; certificates should be the TRACES-generated ones.
  • Recipient: compare each Form 131 with Form 168 (old 26AS) and the Annual Information Statement before filing your return; see fixing AIS mismatches.
  • Mismatch: if TDS is missing or wrong, the deductor must file a correction statement; until then the credit may not appear.
  • Late issue: deductors face a daily penalty for issuing certificates late, besides late fees for the return itself.
  • Rates and thresholds: see the TDS rate chart for 2026-27; salary TDS: TDS on salary and Form 130.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is Form 16A called now?
From tax year 2026-27, Form 16A is Form 131 under the Income-tax Rules, 2026. It is the TDS certificate for payments other than salary.
When is Form 131 (Form 16A) due?
Quarterly, within 15 days after the due date of the quarterly TDS statement: by 15 August, 15 November, 15 February and 15 June for the four quarters of tax year 2026-27.
What is the difference between Form 130 and Form 131?
Form 130 (formerly Form 16) is the annual salary TDS certificate from the employer; Form 131 (formerly Form 16A) is the quarterly certificate for other payments such as rent, fees, interest and commission.
How do I download Form 131?
The deductor downloads it from TRACES with its TAN after filing the quarterly TDS statement and gives it to you. As a recipient you can check the same TDS in Form 168 (old 26AS) and AIS.
What if TDS in Form 131 does not match Form 26AS / Form 168?
Ask the deductor to file a correction statement; the credit appears only once the corrected statement is processed.
