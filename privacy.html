Privacy and Cookie Notice, what this website collects, why, and your choices.

This website is run by Bharat Quantum Prospera, the chartered accountancy practice of CA Durgesh Chavda, Ahmedabad, India. The site has no advertising and we do not sell personal information. / Your choice

Accept, decline or change your mind.

  • UK, EEA and Switzerland: we ask first. Until you accept, Clarity is not loaded and nothing is stored on your device for analytics.
  • Other countries: Clarity runs when you arrive and a notice lets you decline.
  • If you decline: Clarity’s cookies are deleted, our own record of how you arrived is deleted, and Clarity is not loaded on the pages you visit afterwards.
  • Changing your mind: use the button below, or clear this site’s cookies and site data in your browser.
  • We decide which rule applies from your browser’s time zone, so no location lookup is made for this.

/ How we use what you send us

Enquiries and client information.

  • Purpose: to reply to your enquiry and, if you engage us, to provide the professional services you ask for and meet our legal and professional obligations.
  • Sharing: we do not sell your information. It is shared only where needed to do the work you ask for or where the law requires.
  • Retention: enquiries are kept for as long as needed to deal with them; client records are kept for the periods that law and professional rules require.
  • Where it is processed: we are based in India. WhatsApp, email, Microsoft, GitHub, Google and FormSubmit may process information in other countries, including the United States.
  • Please do not send passwords, full card numbers or documents you would not want kept in a WhatsApp or email thread until we have agreed how to share them.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Does this website use cookies?
Yes, only for Microsoft Clarity, which shows us how visitors use the site. Visitors in the UK, EEA and Switzerland are asked first; elsewhere Clarity runs unless you decline. There are no advertising cookies.
Can I decline cookies and still use the site?
Yes. Everything on the site works if you decline. Clarity's cookies are deleted and Clarity is not loaded on later pages.
Does the website store what I type into its forms?
No. The quote, call-request and contact forms open your own WhatsApp or email app with the message filled in. The US incorporation intake form is sent to our email through FormSubmit. Clarity masks text typed into forms by default.
How do I ask for my information to be deleted?
Email info@bharatquantumprospera.com or WhatsApp +91 78018 87130 with any question about your information, or to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion.
