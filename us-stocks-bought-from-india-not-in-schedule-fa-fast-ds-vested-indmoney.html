/ US stocks via LRS · Schedule FA · Updated 2026-11-10

Bought US Stocks From India but Never Reported Them in Schedule FA? What to Do Before 31 December 2026 (FAST-DS), the app made buying easy; the return did not.

If you are resident and ordinarily resident in India and hold US shares or ETFs bought through an investment app, every holding belongs in Schedule FA of your return, even if you never sold anything, and so does uninvested cash in the app's US wallet. For the return for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27), Schedule FA follows the calendar year ending 31 December 2025. Missed it? Shares bought with income already taxed in India usually fall in FAST-DS Category 2: a flat INR 1 lakh for assets up to INR 5 crore, if you declare by 31 December 2026. Dividends or gains that were never offered to tax are a separate Category 1 item at 60%.

/ If you missed Schedule FA

Your options before 31 December 2026.

  • FAST-DS Category 2, INR 1 lakh: for shares bought from income already taxed in India (salary, business income, savings) and not shown in Schedule FA. One fee covers foreign assets worth up to INR 5 crore in total, and it brings immunity under the Black Money Act for what you declare.
  • FAST-DS Category 1, 60%: for dividends or gains that were taxable in India but never offered to tax, within the INR 1 crore aggregate.
  • Revised return: fixes the FY 2025-26 return only (free until 31 December 2026, with a fee until 31 March 2027). It does not cover earlier years or give Black Money Act immunity for them.
  • Small holdings: if the money was taxed and your foreign assets other than property never exceeded INR 20 lakh in total, the INR 10 lakh Black Money Act penalty for non-disclosure does not apply. A revised return for FY 2025-26 and correct reporting from now on may then be enough; FAST-DS mainly buys certainty for earlier years. Check the totals year by year before paying INR 1 lakh.
  • Doing nothing: the Black Money Act allows a penalty of INR 10 lakh for not disclosing foreign assets in a return where they exceed INR 20 lakh (other than immovable property), apart from tax on undisclosed income.

/ Before you declare

Collect these.

  1. Your app's tax documents: the Schedule FA report (calendar year) and the dividend and tax statement.
  2. The value of each holding as on 31 March 2026, the valuation date for FAST-DS.
  3. Proof the money came from taxed income: LRS remittance records (Form A2, bank debit) and your returns for those years.
  4. Whether dividends and any sales were reported in each year's return.
  5. Your residential status for each year: in RNOR or NRI years Schedule FA was not required.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Do I need to report US stocks in Schedule FA if I never sold them?
Yes, if you are resident and ordinarily resident. Holdings are reported whether or not you sold, along with any uninvested cash in the US wallet.
Which period does Schedule FA cover for AY 2026-27?
The calendar year ending 31 December 2025, not the April to March financial year.
I bought US stocks through an app and missed Schedule FA. Is FAST-DS Category 1 or 2?
Usually Category 2, a flat INR 1 lakh, if the money came from income already taxed in India. Unreported dividends or gains are a separate Category 1 item at 60%.
My US portfolio is small. Is FAST-DS worth INR 1 lakh?
Not always. If the money was taxed and your foreign assets other than property stayed within INR 20 lakh in total, the INR 10 lakh Black Money Act penalty does not apply, and a revised return for FY 2025-26 with correct reporting going forward may be enough. Larger holdings or several missed years usually favour FAST-DS.
Where do I report dividends from US stocks?
In Schedule FSI, with the US tax withheld claimed as credit in Schedule TR. File Form 67 to claim the credit.
What is the deadline to fix it under FAST-DS?
Declarations close on 31 December 2026. The payment follows the Form 2 order.
