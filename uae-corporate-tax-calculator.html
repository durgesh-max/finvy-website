How UAE CT works.

Standard rate: 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000 (small-business relief for all entities on first AED 375K).

Free Zone Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP) regime: 0% on Qualifying Income (transactions with other Free Zone persons + Qualifying Activities + specific intangibles + treasury financing to related parties) + 9% on non-qualifying income. Requires substance + Qualifying Income analysis + audited financials + TP documentation.

Small Business Relief: entities with revenue below AED 3 million in current AND preceding year can elect 0% rate on all income (not just the first AED 375K). Election via FTA portal.

De minimis for QFZP: up to 5% of total revenue OR AED 5 million (whichever is lower) can come from non-qualifying sources without losing QFZP status.
