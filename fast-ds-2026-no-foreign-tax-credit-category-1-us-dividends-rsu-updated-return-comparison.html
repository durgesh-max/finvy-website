FAST-DS 2026 Gives No Credit for Tax Paid Abroad: US Dividends, RSUs and When an Updated Return Costs Less, check this before you pay 60% on income already taxed overseas.

Foreign income declared under Category 1 of FAST-DS 2026 is charged at a flat 60%, and the scheme gives no credit for tax already paid abroad on that income. Neither the Finance Act, the rules nor the official FAQ provide for it. This matters most for dividends on US shares and RSUs, where the US has usually withheld up to 25% already. An updated return taxes the same income at normal rates with interest and an additional 25% to 70%, and it does allow the foreign tax credit through Form 67. For small amounts of already-taxed income it can cost far less. The trade-off is that an updated return gives no Black Money Act immunity, is not available for every year or after certain notices, and does not by itself fix a foreign asset that was never reported. The shares themselves usually fall under Category 2 (flat INR 1 lakh). The last date for a FAST-DS declaration is 31 December 2026.

/ How to decide

Cost against protection.

  • Split the problem: shares taxed through payroll but missing from the foreign-assets schedule are usually Category 2 (INR 1 lakh in total). Unreported dividends, interest and sale gains are income, and that is where the 60% applies.
  • Where FAST-DS still wins: income never taxed anywhere, older years that an updated return cannot reach, cases where a notice has blocked the updated return, and anyone who wants the certainty of immunity.
  • Where an updated return may be cheaper: small amounts of dividend or interest already taxed abroad, in years still open, where you can show the foreign tax paid.
  • What an updated return does not do: it gives no Black Money Act immunity, cannot be used only to add an asset, and cannot reduce tax or claim a refund; see updated returns.
  • Open question: practitioners have flagged uncertainty about using an updated return for the income together with Category 2 for the asset. There is no clarification from the CBDT yet, so take advice on your facts before choosing.
  • Payment is final: amounts paid under FAST-DS are not refundable, so settle the route before filing Form 1.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Does FAST-DS 2026 allow foreign tax credit?
No. Income declared under Category 1 is charged at a flat 60%, and neither the Finance Act, the rules nor the official FAQ provide credit for tax already paid abroad on that income.
I paid 25% US tax on my dividends. Do I still pay 60% under FAST-DS?
Yes, if you declare those dividends under Category 1. The US tax is not credited, so the combined cost can exceed the income's normal Indian tax many times over.
Can I use an updated return instead for foreign dividends?
For years still open, yes: the income is taxed at normal rates with interest and an additional 25% to 70%, and foreign tax credit can be claimed with Form 67. It gives no Black Money Act immunity and is barred after certain notices.
Which FAST-DS category applies to RSU shares?
Shares taxed as a perquisite through Indian payroll but not reported in the foreign-assets schedule are usually Category 2, with a flat fee of INR 1 lakh. Unreported dividends and sale gains are Category 1.
Can I combine an updated return with FAST-DS Category 2?
Practitioners have raised this as an open question and the CBDT has not clarified it. Take advice on your facts before choosing a route.
