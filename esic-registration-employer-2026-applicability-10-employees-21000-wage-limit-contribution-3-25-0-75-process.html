/ Employers, startups and foreign-owned subsidiaries · Payroll compliance · Updated 2026-11-25

ESIC Registration for Employers 2026: Who Must Register (10+ Employees), Rs 21,000 Wage Limit, 3.25% + 0.75% Contribution and Online Process, once you hit 10 employees, registration is not optional.

Any factory or establishment with 10 or more employees in an ESIC-notified area must register with the Employees’ State Insurance Corporation and cover employees earning up to ₹21,000 a month. The employer contributes 3.25% and the employee 0.75% of wages, paid monthly by the 15th of the following month. Registration is online on the ESIC portal (or through SPICe+ when a company is incorporated) and gives the employer a 17-digit code number.

/ ESIC at a glance

Applicability, rates and dates.

PointRule
Who must registerFactories and establishments with 10 or more employees in notified areas (20 for some establishments in some states)
Which employees are coveredGross wages up to ₹21,000 a month (₹25,000 for persons with disabilities)
Employer contribution3.25% of wages
Employee contribution0.75% of wages (nil if daily wage is up to ₹176; employer still pays)
Payment due date15th of the following month
Employer code17-digit number allotted on registration
Employee numberInsurance (IP) number and e-Pehchan card

The scheme continues under the Code on Social Security, 2020; see the payroll and benefits guide for EPF, gratuity and bonus alongside ESI.

/ How to register (online)

Step by step.

  • Check applicability: count all employees, including contract and casual staff, against the 10-employee threshold in your area.
  • Sign up on the ESIC portal: employer registration with PAN, business registration (incorporation certificate, partnership deed or shop licence), address proof, bank details and employee list.
  • New companies: ESIC registration can be obtained through SPICe+ at incorporation.
  • Get the code: the 17-digit employer code and registration certificate are issued online and can be downloaded from the portal.
  • Register employees: add each covered employee to get an IP number; they and their families can then use ESIC dispensaries and hospitals.
  • Pay monthly: deduct 0.75%, add 3.25% and pay by the 15th; keep wage registers for inspection.

/ Benefits and penalties

Why it matters.

  • Benefits for employees: medical care for the employee and family, cash benefit during sickness, maternity benefit, disablement and dependants’ benefits.
  • Late payment: attracts interest and damages, and non-registration can lead to assessment of contributions for past periods.
  • Above the wage limit: employees earning more than ₹21,000 a month are outside ESIC; many employers give them group health insurance instead.
  • Other payroll items: TDS on salary and PF; see TDS on salary, PF withdrawal rules and the gratuity calculator.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Who needs ESIC registration?
Factories and establishments with 10 or more employees in ESIC-notified areas (20 for some establishments in some states) must register and cover employees earning up to Rs 21,000 a month (Rs 25,000 for persons with disabilities).
What is the ESIC contribution rate in 2026?
3.25% of wages from the employer and 0.75% from the employee, 4% in total. Employees earning up to Rs 176 a day do not pay their share, but the employer still pays 3.25%.
How do I register for ESIC as an employer?
Register online on the ESIC portal with PAN, business registration documents, address proof, bank details and the employee list; new companies can get ESIC through SPICe+ at incorporation. A 17-digit employer code is issued online.
What is the ESIC registration number?
The 17-digit employer code number allotted on registration; each covered employee gets a separate insurance (IP) number.
What is the due date for ESIC payment?
Contributions for a month are due by the 15th of the following month; late payment attracts interest and damages.
