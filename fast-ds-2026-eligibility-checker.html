    / How the checker decides

    The scheme's tests.

    • Eligible person (section 131(1)(a)): resident in the year; or a non-resident/RNOR who was resident in the year the income arose or the asset was acquired.
    • Undisclosed foreign asset (section 131(1)(j)): an asset outside India whose source of investment you cannot satisfactorily explain. Category 1.
    • Undisclosed foreign income (section 131(1)(k)): foreign-source income chargeable to tax in India but not offered to tax. Category 1.
    • Category 2 (section 133, item 2): asset bought from foreign income while non-resident, or from income taxed in India, not shown in the foreign-assets schedule.
    • Exclusions (section 140): proceeds of crime with PMLA proceedings initiated or pending; years where a Black Money Act assessment is completed.

    Source: Finance Act, 2026, Chapter IV (sections 130 to 144), the Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers Disclosure Scheme, 2026, as published by the Income Tax Department. Forms, valuation and payment mechanics are in the FAST-DS Rules. Last updated: 4 November 2026.

    FAQ

    Common questions, answered.

    How do I know if I am eligible for FAST-DS?
    You are eligible if you were resident in India in the year, or are now non-resident but were resident when the foreign income arose or the asset was acquired, and the item is an undisclosed foreign asset or income (Category 1, total up to INR 1 crore) or an asset bought from explained income but missing from the foreign-assets schedule (Category 2, up to INR 5 crore).
    Can an NRI use FAST-DS?
    Yes, if he or she was resident in India in the year the income arose or the asset was acquired (section 131(1)(a)(ii)).
    Do I need FAST-DS if I reported everything?
    No. If the asset was bought from explained funds, shown in the foreign-assets schedule and its income was taxed, nothing is undisclosed.
