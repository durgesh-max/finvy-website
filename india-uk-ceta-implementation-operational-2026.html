/ India-UK · Trade agreement · Updated 2026-11-21

India-UK CETA: In Force Since 15 July 2026 - Tariff Cuts, Whisky Duty, Social Security Relief and What Businesses Should Do, the trade agreement, in plain terms.

The India-UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) was signed on 24 July 2025 and entered into force on 15 July 2026. The UK removed tariffs on 99% of Indian goods from that day. India is cutting tariffs on 90% of its tariff lines, covering about 92% of UK goods exports, with 64% tariff-free immediately and the rest phased. Duty on Scotch whisky fell from 150% to 75% on entry into force and falls to 40% over ten years. A separate Double Contributions Convention started the same day: employees posted between the two countries can stay in their home social security system for up to 60 months. To use a lower tariff, the goods must meet the agreement’s rules of origin and the importer must claim the preference with proof of origin. This page was corrected on 21 November 2026; an earlier version gave the wrong dates and tariff figures.

/ What to do

Exporters, importers and investors.

  • Indian exporters to the UK: check that your goods meet the product-specific origin rule and keep the supplier and production records that prove it.
  • UK exporters to India: look up the staging for your tariff line; many cuts are phased over several years.
  • Customs in India: see IEC, GST and customs for foreign exporters and importers.
  • Posting employees: see social security agreements and the UK convention.
  • Tax on cross-border payments: the trade agreement does not change the income tax treaty; see the India-UK tax treaty.
  • Setting up in India: see the India entry structure chooser and Indian CA for UK clients.
