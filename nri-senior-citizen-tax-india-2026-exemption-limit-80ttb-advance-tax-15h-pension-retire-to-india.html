/ NRIs · Senior citizens and retirement · Updated 2026-11-23

NRI Retirement in India and Senior Citizen Tax (2026): Which Senior Benefits NRIs Do Not Get, and What Changes When You Retire to India, age helps only residents.

An NRI aged 60 or 80 does not get India’s senior-citizen tax benefits: almost all of them are for residents only. A non-resident’s basic exemption is INR 2.5 lakh at any age under the old regime (residents get INR 3 lakh at 60 and INR 5 lakh at 80), and INR 4 lakh under the new regime, but without the rebate that makes income up to INR 12 lakh tax-free for residents. NRIs also lose the INR 50,000 interest deduction for seniors, the advance tax exemption, Form 15H and the no-return rule for those over 75. What NRIs keep: treaty rates on NRO interest, the standard deduction on an Indian pension, and the health insurance deduction for resident parents. The picture flips when you retire to India: once resident, the senior benefits apply, and a foreign pension can be shielded for a few years as RNOR.

/ Quick answers

What senior NRIs and their children ask.

  • Does an NRI senior citizen get a higher tax exemption? No. The higher limits of INR 3 lakh (60 to 79) and INR 5 lakh (80+) under the old regime are for residents only. A non-resident gets INR 2.5 lakh at any age.
  • Can an NRI senior citizen claim the INR 50,000 interest deduction? No. It is for resident senior citizens under the old regime only. An NRI can claim up to INR 10,000 on savings account interest under the old regime.
  • Does an NRI senior citizen pay advance tax? Yes, if the tax due after TDS is INR 10,000 or more. The advance tax exemption is for resident seniors without business income.
  • Can an NRI senior submit Form 15H? No. Form 15H is for residents. NRO interest has TDS at 30% plus surcharge and cess, or a lower treaty rate with a tax residency certificate.
  • Is income up to INR 12 lakh tax-free for an NRI? No. The rebate that makes it tax-free under the new regime is for residents only.

/ What NRIs can still do

Cutting tax on Indian income without the senior benefits.

  • Claim the treaty rate on NRO interest and dividends: 10% to 15% in most treaties instead of 30% plus surcharge and cess. See the NRO TDS refund calculator.
  • Move savings to NRE or FCNR deposits if the money came from abroad: their interest is exempt for NRIs. See NRE, NRO and FCNR tax rules.
  • File a return to get excess TDS back: where total Indian income is low, TDS at 30% is often refundable in full or in part.
  • Compare regimes each year: an NRI with mostly interest and capital gains often pays less under the new regime; an NRI with home loan interest or health insurance may not.
  • Keep the tax residency certificate current, since the treaty rate depends on it.

/ Retiring to India

What changes when a senior NRI moves back.

  • Resident senior benefits start in the first year you are resident: higher exemption (old regime), the INR 50,000 interest deduction, no advance tax without business income, Form 15H.
  • RNOR years: a returning NRI is usually ‘resident but not ordinarily resident’ for up to two or three years. Foreign pension and foreign interest received abroad are not taxed in India in those years. See RNOR planning.
  • Foreign pensions after RNOR: taxable in India once you are ordinarily resident, with treaty relief. US Social Security is taxable only in the US under the India-US treaty; see US Social Security in India. UK and other pensions depend on the treaty and the type of pension.
  • Foreign tax credit needs Form 67 (Form 44 from tax year 2026-27) filed with the return; see foreign tax credit.
  • Report foreign assets in Schedule FA once ordinarily resident: pensions, accounts, property abroad.
  • Convert accounts: NRE and NRO accounts must be redesignated as resident accounts; FCNR deposits can run to maturity, and an RFC account can hold foreign currency.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Does an NRI senior citizen get tax exemption in India?
No higher exemption. The INR 3 lakh (60 to 79) and INR 5 lakh (80+) limits under the old regime are for residents only. A non-resident gets INR 2.5 lakh under the old regime and INR 4 lakh under the new regime, at any age, without the rebate.
What is the tax exemption limit for senior citizens in India in 2026?
For resident senior citizens: INR 3 lakh (60 to 79) or INR 5 lakh (80+) under the old regime; under the new regime, INR 4 lakh basic exemption for everyone, and a resident pays no tax up to INR 12 lakh because of the rebate.
Can an NRI senior citizen claim section 80TTB?
No. The INR 50,000 interest deduction is for resident senior citizens under the old regime. An NRI can claim up to INR 10,000 on savings account interest under the old regime.
Do NRI senior citizens have to pay advance tax?
Yes, if the tax due after TDS is INR 10,000 or more. The exemption from advance tax is only for resident senior citizens without business or professional income.
Can an NRI claim health insurance for parents in India?
Yes, under the old regime: up to INR 50,000 if the parents are resident senior citizens, and INR 25,000 otherwise. The NRI's own policy is limited to INR 25,000.
Is foreign pension taxable when an NRI retires to India?
Not while you are RNOR, usually for the first two or three years, if received abroad. After that it is taxable in India with treaty relief; US Social Security is taxable only in the US under the treaty.
I am planning NRI retirement in India. What should I sort out on tax and money before moving back?
First, plan the year of return: you usually become RNOR for the first two or three years, when foreign income received abroad (pension, rent, interest) is generally not taxed in India; after that you are a full resident and your worldwide income is taxable here, with treaty relief. Before or soon after arriving: inform the bank so NRE and NRO accounts are redesignated as resident accounts (an RFC account can hold foreign currency, and FCNR deposits can run to maturity); keep foreign pension, 401(k), IRA or super accounts in order - Form 40 (old 10-EE) can defer Indian tax on some foreign retirement accounts, and once you are a resident and ordinarily resident every foreign asset goes into Schedule FA. Once resident, the senior benefits NRIs do not get apply - the higher exemption and section 80TTB in the old regime, and no advance tax if you have no business income - and Form 15H can stop TDS on interest where tax is nil. Also update your will, nominations and health cover. How can a pensioner living abroad submit a life certificate online?
Through Jeevan Pramaan: with an Aadhaar number, install the AadhaarFaceRD and Jeevan Pramaan Face apps on a smartphone and complete face authentication to generate a digital life certificate, which reaches the pension disbursing bank or office directly. November is the usual submission window for central government pensioners. Pensioners abroad without Aadhaar, or whose authentication fails, should ask their pension disbursing bank or the Indian mission which paper route it accepts, and a 2026 provision allows an authorised agent to help in some cases.
Is Indian pension taxable for an NRI?
Under Indian law, yes: a pension paid by an Indian employer or the government is Indian-source income, usually with TDS by the payer. A tax treaty can change that: government pensions are usually taxable only in India, while private pensions are often taxable only in your country of residence, so check the treaty before accepting the TDS and claim a refund or foreign tax credit as needed.
