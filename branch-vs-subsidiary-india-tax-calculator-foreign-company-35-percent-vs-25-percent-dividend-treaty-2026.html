Branch vs Subsidiary in India: Tax Calculator for Foreign Companies (2026) - 35% Branch Rate vs 25.17% Plus Dividend Withholding, see the total Indian tax on each structure for your country.

A foreign company’s branch in India is taxed as a foreign company at 35%, plus a 2% surcharge above INR 1 crore of profit or 5% above INR 10 crore, plus 4% cess. That is about 36.4% to 38.2%, and profits sent back to head office carry no further Indian tax. An Indian subsidiary pays 25.168% under section 115BAA. When it pays dividends to the parent, India withholds tax at the treaty rate, for example 15% for a US parent with a 10% holding, 10% for the UK, Singapore (25% holding), UAE, Germany, the Netherlands and Japan, and 5% for Mauritius, Saudi Arabia and Qatar. Without a treaty the rate is 20% plus surcharge and cess. For a US parent paying out all its profit, the two structures come out almost equal. For most treaty countries the subsidiary costs less, and it costs much less if profits are reinvested in India. Tax is only part of the decision: a branch needs RBI approval and can carry on only permitted activities, while a subsidiary limits liability. Enter your numbers below.

    / How the numbers work

    Rates used and what is left out.

    • Branch: 35% (cut from 40% by the Finance Act 2024), surcharge 2% above INR 1 crore and 5% above INR 10 crore, 4% cess; no tax on remitting branch profits.
    • Subsidiary: 22% under section 115BAA plus 10% surcharge and 4% cess = 25.168%; no MAT under this regime.
    • Dividends: treaty rate with a tax residency certificate and Form 10F (Form 41 from 1 April 2026); otherwise 20% plus surcharge and cess. See the India-US withholding rates.
    • Reinvesting: profits kept in the subsidiary bear only the 25.168%; set the payout to see the effect.
    • Home country: your country may tax branch profits or dividends and give credit for Indian tax; check both sides.
    • Not modelled: surcharge marginal relief, transfer pricing adjustments, buyback or capital reduction routes, and state taxes.

    FAQ

    Common questions, answered.

    What is the tax rate on a foreign company's branch in India?
    35% plus surcharge (2% above INR 1 crore, 5% above INR 10 crore) and 4% cess, about 36.4% to 38.2%; remitting branch profits has no further Indian tax.
    What is the tax rate on an Indian subsidiary of a foreign company?
    25.168% under section 115BAA, plus withholding tax on dividends paid to the parent at the treaty rate or 20% plus surcharge and cess without a treaty.
    Is a branch or a subsidiary cheaper for a US company in India?
    With all profits paid out as dividends at the 15% treaty rate, the total Indian tax is almost the same; a subsidiary is cheaper if profits are reinvested in India.
    Which countries get a 5% or 10% dividend rate?
    Under the site's treaty summaries: 5% for Mauritius (10% holding), Saudi Arabia and Qatar (25% holding); 10% for the UK, Singapore (25% holding), UAE, Germany, the Netherlands and Japan; 15% for the US, Canada and Australia.
    Is tax the only factor in choosing a branch?
    No. A branch needs RBI approval and can do only permitted activities; a subsidiary limits liability and permanent establishment exposure and is the usual route for operating businesses.
