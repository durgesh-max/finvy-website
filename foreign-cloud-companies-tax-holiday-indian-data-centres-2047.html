Tax Holiday Until 2047 for Foreign Cloud Companies Using Indian Data Centres, what Budget 2026 offers and the conditions.

Budget 2026-27 (1 February 2026) proposed a tax holiday until 2047 for foreign companies that provide cloud services to customers worldwide using data centre services from India. The conditions: the data centre is operated by an Indian company, the foreign company is notified, and services to Indian customers go through an Indian reseller, which remains taxable in India. Where the Indian data centre is a related party working on cost-plus, a 15% safe harbour margin on cost was proposed.

/ The conditions

As announced.

  • The foreign company provides cloud services to customers globally using data centre services procured from India.
  • The data centre services come from an Indian company operating a data centre in India.
  • Sales to Indian customers are made through an Indian reseller entity, which is taxed in India normally.
  • The foreign company is notified under the relevant provisions.
  • Related-party Indian data centres operating on cost-plus can use a 15% margin on cost as a safe harbour.

/ Why it matters

The permanent establishment question.

Foreign cloud providers have been cautious about putting large infrastructure in India because of the risk that it creates a permanent establishment and exposes global cloud income to Indian tax. The holiday removes that cost for the specified income, while keeping the Indian data centre operator and the Indian reseller fully taxable.

/ What foreign cloud and SaaS companies should check

Practical points.

  • Whether your Indian data centre arrangement meets the conditions and notification requirements.
  • Reseller structure for Indian customers: contracts, pricing, GST on supplies to Indian customers and transfer pricing.
  • Transfer pricing for a related Indian data centre: whether to use the 15% safe harbour.
  • Permanent establishment exposure for other activities in India not covered by the holiday.
  • Separately, Budget 2026 announced a single IT services safe harbour (15.5% margin) with the eligibility threshold raised from INR 300 crore to INR 2,000 crore.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the Budget 2026 tax holiday for cloud companies?
A tax holiday until 2047 for foreign companies providing cloud services globally using data centre services from India, subject to conditions including an Indian reseller for Indian customers and notification of the foreign company.
Does the Indian data centre also get a tax holiday?
No. The Indian data centre operator and the Indian reseller remain taxable in India. A related-party data centre on cost-plus can use a 15% margin on cost as a safe harbour.
Why was this introduced?
To attract global cloud workloads to Indian data centres by removing the permanent establishment tax risk that foreign providers feared.
