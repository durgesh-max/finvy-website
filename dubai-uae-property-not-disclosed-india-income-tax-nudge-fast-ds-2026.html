/ UAE property · FAST-DS nudge · Updated 2026-11-11

Dubai or UAE Property Not in Your Indian Tax Return? The Income Tax Department Has Started Asking (FAST-DS 2026), seven weeks left to fix it cheaply.

Business Standard reported on 26 October 2026, citing Finance Ministry sources, that the Income Tax Department has begun selectively contacting taxpayers who may hold undisclosed residential or commercial property in the UAE, as FAST-DS declarations reached about 1,100. If you are resident and ordinarily resident in India and own a flat, villa or off-plan unit in Dubai or elsewhere in the UAE that is not in Schedule FA, the cheapest fix closes on 31 December 2026: a flat INR 1 lakh under FAST-DS Category 2 if it was bought from income taxed in India or while you were an NRI, and the property is worth up to INR 5 crore.

/ UAE-specific points

Where people go wrong.

  • No UAE income tax means no credit in India. Rent from a UAE property is fully taxable in India for a resident and ordinarily resident, at slab rates, with no foreign tax to set off.
  • Property gets no small-asset relief. The Black Money Act spares assets other than immovable property up to INR 20 lakh in total from the INR 10 lakh non-disclosure penalty; a UAE property gets no such relief at any value.
  • Off-plan and instalment purchases still count once you hold rights in the unit; keep the developer's payment schedule and the remittance records.
  • Value matters for eligibility: many Dubai properties are worth more than INR 5 crore. Value is taken as on 31 March 2026; check this before assuming Category 2 applies.
  • Residential status: years spent working in the UAE as an NRI, and the first RNOR years after return, need no Schedule FA. Check with the residential status calculator.

/ What to do this month

Before 31 December 2026.

  • Collect the title deed (or Oqood for off-plan), the payment trail and your returns for the years since purchase.
  • Work out the value as on 31 March 2026 and whether rent was ever shown in India.
  • File FAST-DS Form 1 on the e-Filing portal by 31 December 2026, pay after the Form 2 order, file Form 3, and receive Form 4.
  • From then on, show the property in Schedule FA and the rent in your return every year you are resident and ordinarily resident.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is the Income Tax Department contacting people with property in Dubai?
Yes. Business Standard reported on 26 October 2026, citing Finance Ministry sources, that the department has begun selectively nudging taxpayers who may hold undisclosed UAE residential or commercial property to use FAST-DS before 31 December 2026.
Is a nudge a tax demand?
No. It is a prompt to review your return. But it shows the department has information, and after 31 December 2026 the FAST-DS route closes.
How much does FAST-DS cost for a Dubai flat?
INR 1 lakh under Category 2 if it was bought from income taxed in India or while you were an NRI and is worth up to INR 5 crore as on 31 March 2026. Property bought with untaxed money, or rent never taxed, is Category 1 at 60% within INR 1 crore.
Is rent from a Dubai property taxable in India?
Yes, for a resident and ordinarily resident. The UAE has no personal income tax, so there is no foreign tax credit to claim; the rent is taxed in India at slab rates.
My Dubai property is worth more than INR 5 crore. Can I use FAST-DS?
Not under Category 2, which is limited to INR 5 crore. You need advice on the regular route, including revised or updated returns where they are still allowed.
