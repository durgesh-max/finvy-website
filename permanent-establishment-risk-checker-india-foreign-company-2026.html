Permanent Establishment Risk Checker for Foreign Companies in India (2026): Eight Questions on Offices, Staff, Agents, Services and Projects, find out whether your India activity may be taxable in India.

A foreign company becomes taxable in India on the profits linked to its India activity if it has a permanent establishment (PE) here. The usual triggers are a fixed place at its disposal, employees working from India, an agent who concludes contracts for it, services in India beyond the treaty's day threshold (commonly 90 days in 12 months), or a project beyond the treaty's time limit. Under Indian domestic law, large revenue or user numbers from India (INR 2 crore or 3 lakh users) also create a business connection, though treaty protection usually decides the final answer for treaty residents with a tax residency certificate.     / What each answer means

    The PE types behind the questions.

    • Fixed place PE: an office, branch, factory or other place through which the business is carried on, including space provided by others but at your disposal.
    • Dependent agent PE: a person in India who habitually concludes contracts, or plays the principal role leading to them, for your company.
    • Service PE: services furnished in India by your staff or contractors beyond the treaty's threshold (often 90 days in a 12-month period; some treaties differ).
    • Construction or installation PE: a site or project beyond the treaty time limit (often 6, 9 or 12 months).
    • Significant economic presence: Indian domestic-law test for remote and digital businesses; treaties usually still require a PE for treaty residents.
    • No treaty or no TRC: without treaty protection, the wider domestic business-connection rules apply.

    FAQ

    Common questions, answered.

    What creates a permanent establishment in India?
    Typically a fixed place at the company's disposal, employees working from India, an agent who concludes contracts for it, services beyond the treaty's day threshold, or a construction or installation project beyond the treaty's time limit.
    Does a remote employee in India create a PE?
    It can. An employee working from India for a foreign company, especially in sales or management roles or from a home office the company relies on, can create a fixed place or agency PE; many companies use an employer of record or a subsidiary instead.
    How many days create a service PE in India?
    It depends on the treaty; many use about 90 days in a 12-month period, and some count days differently or set other thresholds.
    What happens if a foreign company has a PE in India?
    Profits attributable to the PE are taxable in India at foreign-company rates, and the company must file Indian returns and meet transfer pricing rules.
