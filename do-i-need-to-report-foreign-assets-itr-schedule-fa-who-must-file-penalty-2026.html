Do I Need to Report Foreign Assets in My ITR? Schedule FA (2026): Who Must Report, What to Include, Penalty and How to Fix a Missed Disclosure, residents must, even with no tax due.

Yes, if you are resident and ordinarily resident in India for the year: every foreign asset you held at any time in the calendar year goes in Schedule FA of your ITR, even if it earned nothing and even if your income is below the taxable limit. NRIs and RNORs do not fill Schedule FA. Leaving an asset out can attract a Rs 10 lakh penalty under the Black Money Act, so a missed disclosure for AY 2026-27 should be fixed through a revised return or FAST-DS 2026 before 31 December 2026.

/ Who must report

Your residential status decides it.

Status for the yearSchedule FA?Why
Resident and ordinarily resident (ROR)Yes - every foreign assetTaxed on worldwide income, so foreign holdings must be disclosed
Resident but not ordinarily resident (RNOR)NoUsually the first two or so years after a long stay abroad
Non-resident (NRI)NoOnly Indian income is reported
  • Returning NRIs: Schedule FA starts in the first year you become ROR - this is where most omissions happen.
  • Even with no tax due: a resident who holds a foreign asset or has signing authority over a foreign account must file a return, even if income is below the basic exemption limit.
  • Joint holders and signatories: each resident holder reports the account; a parent added to a child's foreign account reports it too.

/ What to include

More than bank accounts.

  • Foreign bank and custodial accounts - savings, current, fixed deposits and brokerage accounts, including closed ones held at any time in the year.
  • Shares and securities - US stocks bought from India, ESOPs and RSUs (vested and held), mutual funds and bonds.
  • Cash-value insurance and annuities, pensions and retirement accounts - for example 401(k), IRA, UK pension, RRSP, superannuation.
  • Immovable property abroad - a house or flat, even if it is let out or self-used.
  • Financial interest in a foreign entity, trust interests and other capital assets.
  • Accounts with only signing authority - for example an employer's or relative's account you can operate.
  • Period: the calendar year ending 31 December before the assessment year (for AY 2026-27, 1 January to 31 December 2025), with values converted to rupees as the form's instructions require.

/ If you missed it

Fix it before 31 December 2026.

  • Penalty: sections 42 and 43 of the Black Money Act provide for a Rs 10 lakh penalty for not reporting a foreign asset; from 1 October 2024 it does not apply where the total value of foreign assets other than immovable property is up to Rs 20 lakh.
  • Revised return: for AY 2026-27, add Schedule FA (and any foreign income) in a revised return by 31 December 2026.
  • Earlier years or larger amounts: FAST-DS 2026 closes on 31 December 2026 and covers assets left out of earlier returns, within its limits.
  • Got an income tax email about foreign assets? It usually means foreign reporting data does not match your return - act on it before the deadline rather than ignoring it.
  • Schedule AL is different: it lists all assets (Indian and foreign) and applies only above Rs 1 crore of total income.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Do I need to report foreign assets in my ITR?
Yes, if you are resident and ordinarily resident in India for the year. Every foreign asset held at any time in the calendar year - bank and brokerage accounts, shares, ESOPs and RSUs, insurance, pensions, property and accounts you can sign on - goes in Schedule FA, even if it earned no income and even if your income is below the taxable limit.
Do NRIs have to fill Schedule FA?
No. Non-residents and residents who are not ordinarily resident (RNOR) do not fill Schedule FA. It starts in the first year you become resident and ordinarily resident, which for returning NRIs is often two or three years after moving back.
Which period does Schedule FA cover?
The calendar year ending 31 December before the assessment year. For the return for AY 2026-27 (financial year 2025-26), that is 1 January to 31 December 2025. Assets held at any time in that period are reported, including accounts closed during the year.
What is the penalty for not reporting foreign assets?
Sections 42 and 43 of the Black Money Act provide for a penalty of Rs 10 lakh for failing to report a foreign asset in the return. From 1 October 2024 the penalty does not apply where the total value of foreign assets other than immovable property is up to Rs 20 lakh. Undisclosed foreign income or assets can also be taxed under that Act, so omissions are best fixed before the department acts.
I forgot to add my foreign account in Schedule FA. What should I do?
For AY 2026-27, file a revised return with Schedule FA and any foreign income by 31 December 2026. For earlier years, or where the asset was never declared, FAST-DS 2026 - open until 31 December 2026 - may be the route. WhatsApp +91 78018 87130 with the list of foreign accounts and holdings and your ITR acknowledgement, and we tell you which route fits.
Do I report a foreign account if it has only a small balance?
Yes. Schedule FA has no minimum balance - every account held at any time in the calendar year is reported. The Rs 20 lakh relief only affects whether the Black Money Act penalty applies, not whether the asset has to be shown.
