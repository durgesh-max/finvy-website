GST Advance Ruling (2026): Who Can Apply, Questions Allowed Under Section 97(2), Form ARA-01, 90-Day Timeline, Binding Effect and Appeal to the AAAR, certainty before you charge the rate.

A GST advance ruling gives a written answer from the Authority for Advance Ruling on how GST applies to a transaction you are doing or plan to do - classification, rate, input tax credit, valuation, registration or whether something is a supply at all. The ruling is due within 90 days, binds you and your jurisdictional officer, and can be appealed to the Appellate Authority within 30 days.

/ The process

Sections 95 to 106 of the CGST Act.

PointRule
Who can applyA registered person, or a person seeking registration
Questions allowed (s.97(2))Classification; applicability of a notification; time and value of supply; admissibility of input tax credit; liability to pay tax; need for registration; whether a transaction is a supply
FormGST ARA-01, with a statement of facts and the applicant’s interpretation
Not admittedIf the question is already pending or decided in any proceeding in the applicant’s case
TimelineRuling within 90 days of receiving the application
Binding onOnly the applicant and the jurisdictional officer, while the law and facts stay the same
AppealTo the Appellate Authority for Advance Ruling within 30 days (extendable by 30) in Form GST ARA-02; decided within 90 days
  • State-specific: the AAR sits in each state, and a ruling binds only the applicant for that registration - rulings for other businesses are persuasive at most.
  • Void if obtained by fraud: a ruling obtained by fraud, suppression or misrepresentation of facts can be declared void.

/ When it is worth applying

And how to frame it.

  • Good candidates: new products or services with unclear classification, composite or mixed supplies, rate disputes, credit on large capital spending, and contract structures where the tax position decides the price.
  • Apply before a notice: once a proceeding on the same question starts in your case, the AAR cannot take it up.
  • Frame the question narrowly: one clear question per issue, tied to the actual contract or product, with your own interpretation and supporting law.
  • Plan for the outcome: an unfavourable ruling binds you too - consider the appeal route and how prices and invoices will change.
  • Alternatives: CBIC circulars, earlier rulings and professional opinions may answer simpler questions faster.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is an advance ruling under GST?
A written decision by the Authority for Advance Ruling on how GST applies to a transaction the applicant is doing or proposes to do, on questions such as classification, rate, input tax credit, valuation, registration or whether something is a supply.
Who can apply for a GST advance ruling?
Any registered person, or a person seeking registration, by filing Form GST ARA-01 with a statement of facts and their own interpretation of the law.
How long does a GST advance ruling take?
The Authority must pronounce its ruling within 90 days of receiving the application. An appeal to the Appellate Authority must be filed within 30 days, extendable by 30 days, and is decided within 90 days.
Is a GST advance ruling binding?
It binds only the applicant and the jurisdictional officer, and only while the law and facts remain the same. It is void if obtained by fraud or suppression of facts.
When can an advance ruling application be rejected?
When the question is already pending or has been decided in any proceeding in the applicant's case, or when it falls outside the questions listed in section 97(2).
