Deadline Desk: Every Income Tax, GST and ROC Due Date from 20 November 2026 to 31 January 2027 - For Indian Businesses, NRIs and Foreign-Owned Companies, what is due, what was extended, and what happens if you miss it.

The next ten weeks hold the biggest cluster of Indian deadlines in the year. 20 November: GSTR-3B for October. 21 November: income-tax returns for tax-audit cases, extended from 31 October by CBDT Circular 07/2026. 30 November: returns for transfer-pricing cases (not extended) and the last date to claim GST input tax credit for FY 2025-26. 15 December: advance tax. 31 December: belated and revised returns for AY 2026-27, GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C, the end of the AOC-4 fee waiver, FAST-DS 2026 foreign-asset declarations and the ODI annual performance report. 31 January 2027: the end of the MGT-7 fee waiver and Q3 TDS statements. Missing these means late fees, interest, lost input tax credit or refunds, and in some cases penalties or prosecution exposure. If any of them is yours, book a call and we will tell you what can still be filed, in what order, and what it will cost to fix.

/ NRIs and foreign-owned companies

What is most often missed.

  • NRIs: a belated return by 31 December 2026 is the last chance to claim refunds of TDS on NRO interest, rent and property sales for FY 2025-26; see which ITR for NRIs and NRI missed the deadline.
  • Returning NRIs: foreign assets missed in Schedule FA can be declared under FAST-DS 2026 by 31 December; see FAST-DS 2026.
  • Foreign-owned subsidiaries: transfer-pricing returns by 30 November, AOC-4 by 31 December and MGT-7 by 31 January within the fee waiver; see subsidiary compliance calendar.
  • Indian groups with overseas investment: ODI annual performance report by 31 December.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the ITR due date for audit cases for AY 2026-27?
21 November 2026, extended from 31 October by CBDT Circular 07/2026. Transfer-pricing cases were not extended and remain due on 30 November 2026.
What is the last date to claim GST input tax credit for FY 2025-26?
30 November 2026, or the date you file the FY 2025-26 annual return if earlier (section 16(4) of the CGST Act).
Has the AOC-4 or MGT-7 due date been extended?
MCA General Circular 05/2026 waives the additional fee if AOC-4 is filed by 31 December 2026 and MGT-7/7A by 31 January 2027 for FY 2025-26; filed later, the fee runs from the original due date.
What is the last date for a belated or revised ITR for AY 2026-27?
31 December 2026. After that only an updated return is possible, with additional tax and no refund.
What is due on 31 December 2026?
Belated and revised ITRs, GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C for FY 2025-26, AOC-4 within the fee waiver, FAST-DS 2026 declarations and the ODI annual performance report.
