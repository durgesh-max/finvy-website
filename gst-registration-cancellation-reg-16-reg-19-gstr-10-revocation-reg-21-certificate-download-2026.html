/ Businesses closing or restructuring · GST · Updated 2026-11-25

GST registration cancelled or to be cancelled, cancelling the GSTIN does not cancel what you owe.

A business that closes, transfers its business, falls below the GST threshold or changes its constitution can apply to cancel its GST registration in Form REG-16 on the GST portal; the officer issues the cancellation order in Form REG-19, generally within 30 days, and the GSTIN is suspended in the meantime. Within three months of the cancellation date or order (whichever is later) the business must file the final return, GSTR-10, and pay tax on input tax credit for stock held. There is no government fee to cancel. An officer can also cancel registration, for example for not filing returns, after a show-cause notice; that kind of cancellation can be revoked by applying in Form REG-21 within the time allowed.

/ Quick answers

What people search for.

  • How do I cancel GST registration? On the GST portal, file Form REG-16 with the reason, the date of closure and details of stock and tax on it; reply to any query, and the officer issues Form REG-19.
  • How do I download the GST cancellation certificate? The cancellation is an order in Form REG-19, not a separate certificate; download it from the notices and orders section of your GST portal login.
  • Is there a fee to cancel GST registration? No government fee. Late fees can apply to returns still pending, including the final return.
  • What is GSTR-10? The final return, due within three months of the cancellation date or the date of the order, whichever is later.
  • Can I cancel if returns are pending? File the pending returns first; the officer can refuse or delay cancellation until they are in.
  • Can a cancelled GSTIN be restored? If the officer cancelled it, apply for revocation in Form REG-21 within 90 days of the cancellation order after filing the overdue returns. A voluntary cancellation that has been approved generally cannot be revoked; apply for a new registration instead.

/ Before and after cancelling

Avoid surprises.

  • Stock and capital goods: pay an amount equal to the input tax credit on stock and capital goods held on the cancellation date (section 29(5)).
  • Liabilities continue: tax, interest and penalties for the period before cancellation can still be demanded; keep records for the full retention period.
  • Notices: respond to anything pending on the portal; see GST notice response.
  • Annual return: check whether GSTR-9 is due for the year of cancellation; see GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C.
  • Closing the company too? See closing a company in India.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How do I cancel my GST registration online?
File Form REG-16 on the GST portal with the reason, the date from which cancellation is sought and details of stock and tax payable on it. The officer may raise a query and then issues the cancellation order in Form REG-19, generally within 30 days.
How do I download the GST cancellation certificate?
There is no separate certificate: the cancellation order is Form REG-19, which you can download from the notices and orders section after logging in to the GST portal.
What is GSTR-10 and when is it due?
GSTR-10 is the final return after cancellation, due within three months of the date of cancellation or the date of the cancellation order, whichever is later. Late fees apply if it is filed late.
Is there a fee for GST cancellation?
No government fee is charged to cancel a GST registration, but late fees and interest on any pending returns, including the final return, must be paid.
Can a cancelled GST registration be revoked?
Where the officer cancelled it, you can apply for revocation in Form REG-21 within 90 days of the cancellation order (extendable by up to 180 days under rule 23), after filing pending returns and paying the tax, interest and late fees due. An approved voluntary cancellation generally cannot be revoked; you apply for a fresh registration.
My GST registration was cancelled suo moto. How do I activate it again?
Read the cancellation order (REG-19) for the reason and date. If it was cancelled for not filing returns, file every pending return up to the date of cancellation and pay the tax, interest and late fees; then apply for revocation in Form REG-21 on the GST portal within 90 days of the order (an extension of up to 180 more days can be sought on sufficient cause). The officer may ask questions in REG-23 before passing the revocation order in REG-22. If the 90 days and any extension have passed, the remaining routes are an appeal against the cancellation order or a fresh registration.
What should I send to get my GST registration restored?
WhatsApp us your GSTIN, the show cause notice (REG-17) and cancellation order (REG-19) if you have them, the list of returns not filed, and whether the business is still running.
