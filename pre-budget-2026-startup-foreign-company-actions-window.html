/ Why pre-Budget planning matters

The timing play.

  • Historical pattern: Finance Act (post-Budget) often introduces changes with effect from FY 2027-28 (next year).
  • Some changes retrospective (specific areas - usually clarificatory).
  • Pre-Budget action enables locking-in favourable position before any potential tightening.
  • Investment / transaction / election timing decisions have specific pre-Budget vs post-Budget trade-offs.
  • October 2026 - early February 2027 is the planning + execution window.

/ Startup pre-Budget actions

Before Budget 2026.

  • Section 115BAA opt-in via Form 10-IC: if planned, do before 31 October 2026 ITR for FY 2025-26. Irrevocable; locks 22% rate.
  • Section 115BAB opt-in via Form 10-ID: for new manufacturing - Form 10-ID with first-year ITR. Lock 15% rate.
  • DPIIT Startup India + Section 80-IAC application: before DPIIT policy changes in Budget.
  • ESOP grants pre-Budget: at current 409A/11UA valuation; before any potential Section 17(2) perquisite rule changes.
  • Section 56(2)(viib) legacy clean-up: for pre-abolition rounds - defend / regularise before any tightening clarification in Budget.
  • Section 54GB reinvestment: use current startup-friendly provisions.
  • Capital raise / M&A timing: completed transactions crystallise current tax position.

/ Foreign company pre-Budget actions

Before Budget 2026.

  • DPDP Act compliance readiness: Rules expected to finalise by Budget 2026. Prepare now vs scramble post-Rules.
  • FEMA voluntary compounding: historical breaches regularised before any Budget / policy tightening. Materially cheaper than reactive.
  • Rule 9B demat completion: 2-year post-mandate - complete remediation before any strict enforcement.
  • Transfer pricing documentation refresh: Form 3CEB benchmarking study updated for FY 2025-26. Potential Pillar 2 integration.
  • BEPS Pillar 2 preparation: for in-scope groups - operational now, GIR filing June 2027. Pre-Budget data readiness.
  • Section 115BAB opt-in via Form 10-ID: for new manufacturing - lock 15% rate before any potential future change.
  • FDI reform consultation feedback: engage with pre-Budget consultation if sectoral FDI expansion planned.

/ Individual + NRI pre-Budget actions

Specific moves.

  • Capital gain reinvestment: Section 54 / 54F / 54EC within windows. Current LTCG 12.5% framework (post Finance Act 2024).
  • Section 80C/D/G exhaustion: current limits + current rules.
  • NRI returning to India - RNOR optimisation: 2-3 year window + current treatment.
  • LRS planning Oct-March: current TCS 20% rate + specific exemptions (education loan, medical).
  • Section 139(8A) updated return: for prior AYs under current 24-month window.

/ Specific risks of waiting post-Budget

Why act now.

  • Rate changes: Budget may change 115BAA 22%, 115BAB 15%, LTCG 12.5%, or specific sector rates.
  • DTAA treaty renegotiations: US-India / Mauritius-India specific. Pre-Budget position may be grandfathered.
  • DPDP Rules notification: compliance obligations crystallise. Preparation required.
  • FDI sector reforms: specific sector may open or tighten; current rules apply to pre-reform transactions.
  • Specific anti-avoidance clarifications: GAAR / PPT / specific sector rules may tighten.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

When is Union Budget 2026-27 expected?
Early February 2027 - typically 1 February. Finance Act 2027 provisions typically effective from FY 2027-28 (next year). Some clarificatory changes retrospective. Pre-Budget planning window: October 2026 - early February 2027.
Should I opt into Section 115BAA before Budget 2026?
Yes if 115BAA is the right choice based on current analysis. Budget 2026 may change concessional corporate tax rate framework (unlikely but possible). Form 10-IC filing is irrevocable - once opted in, locked at 22% rate (effective 25.17%) regardless of future changes. Lock-in protective.
What's the biggest pre-Budget action for foreign companies?
FEMA voluntary compounding for historical breaches - under Section 13. Materially cheaper than being caught in RBI inspection + any potential policy tightening. 90-180 day window. Should be completed before Budget 2026.
Is DPDP Act compliance Budget-dependent?
DPDP Act is already enacted (August 2023). Rules expected to be notified around Budget 2026 timeframe. Pre-Budget compliance readiness means being prepared when Rules notify. 90-180 day window post-Rules notification for enforcement typically.
