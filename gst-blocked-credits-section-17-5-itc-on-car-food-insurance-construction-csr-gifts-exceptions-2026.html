GST Blocked Credits Under Section 17(5) (2026): ITC on Cars, Food, Insurance, Club Membership, Construction, CSR, Gifts and Free Samples - and the Exceptions, know the exceptions before you claim.

Section 17(5) of the CGST Act blocks input tax credit on certain purchases even when they are used in business - cars, food and beverages, insurance, club memberships, construction of buildings, CSR spending, and gifts or free samples, among others. Each block has its own exceptions, and claiming blocked credit is one of the most common reasons for GST notices and audit objections.

/ What is blocked, and when credit is still allowed

Section 17(5) item by item.

Blocked itemCredit still allowed when
Motor vehicles for carrying persons, seating up to 13 including driverUsed for further supply of such vehicles, transport of passengers, or driving training
Insurance, servicing, repair and maintenance of those vehiclesThe vehicle itself qualifies, or as specified for manufacturers and insurers
Food and beverages, outdoor catering, beauty treatment, health services, cosmetic surgeryUsed for an outward taxable supply of the same category, or as part of a composite or mixed supply
Renting of such vehicles, life and health insuranceSame-category outward supply, or the law obliges the employer to provide it to employees
Club, health and fitness centre membership; employee holiday travelThe law obliges the employer to provide it to employees
Works contract for constructing immovable propertyUsed for a further supply of works contract service; or for plant and machinery
Goods and services for constructing immovable property on own accountPlant and machinery (not buildings or civil structures)
Goods or services used for CSR obligations (from 1 October 2023)No exception
Gifts, free samples, goods lost, stolen, destroyed or written offNo exception
Personal consumptionNo exception
Tax paid on fraud or suppression demands, detention or confiscationNo exception
  • Construction after Safari Retreats: the Supreme Court read ‘plant or machinery’ broadly in October 2024, but the Finance Act, 2025 replaced it with ‘plant and machinery’ retrospectively from 1 July 2017 - credit on buildings let out on rent remains blocked.
  • Goods transport vehicles and vehicles seating more than 13 are not covered by the motor vehicle block.

/ Getting it right

Claims, reversals and notices.

  • Tag purchases at entry: classify vehicle, food, insurance, construction, gift and CSR purchases so blocked credit is not claimed in GSTR-3B.
  • Already claimed? Reverse the blocked credit with interest where it was used; paying before a show cause notice avoids penalty in non-fraud cases.
  • Free samples and gifts: credit on inputs used for them must be reversed.
  • Mixed use: where an item is partly for exempt or non-business use, proportionate reversal rules also apply.
  • Notices and audits: 17(5) claims are routinely checked in GSTR-9 / 9C reviews, scrutiny and department audits - keep a working of every exception you rely on.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What are blocked credits under GST section 17(5)?
Purchases on which input tax credit cannot be claimed even if used in business, such as cars seating up to 13, food and beverages, life and health insurance, club memberships, construction of buildings, CSR spending, gifts, free samples and personal consumption, each subject to specific exceptions.
Can I claim GST input tax credit on a car?
Not on a motor vehicle for carrying persons with seating capacity of 13 or less including the driver, unless it is used for further supply of such vehicles, transport of passengers or driving training. Goods vehicles and vehicles seating more than 13 are not blocked.
Is GST credit available on food and staff welfare?
Generally no for food and beverages, outdoor catering, health services, club memberships and holiday travel, unless they are used for an outward supply of the same kind or the law obliges the employer to provide them to employees.
Can I claim ITC on construction of a building used for business?
No. Credit on goods, services and works contracts for constructing immovable property on your own account is blocked, except for plant and machinery. The Finance Act, 2025 confirmed this retrospectively from 1 July 2017 after the Safari Retreats judgment.
What if I have already claimed blocked credit?
Reverse it with interest where it was used. Paying before a show cause notice avoids penalty in non-fraud cases; if a notice has already come, reply with the working of any exception you rely on.
