/ What the calculator does

The computation.

  • Inputs: Indian share acquisition date + cost per share, Indian company valuation at flip, US parent valuation offered, number of shares held, shareholder residence status.
  • Computes: implicit capital gain under Section 2(42A) + 55, holding period consideration under Section 2(42A), Section 112A LTCG at 12.5% (post Finance Act 2024) OR specific-rate alternatives.
  • Specific scenario alternatives: Section 47(viib) tax-neutral if qualifying + F-reorganisation + specific structuring to minimize crystallised gain.
  • FEMA ODI compliance side analysis.
  • US tax implications: Section 7874 inversion risk + specific US tax reporting.

/ Common flip tax mistakes calculator catches

Where founders trip.

  • Assuming flip is tax-neutral by default: often not - specific structuring required.
  • Section 2(42A) holding period reset: new US shares may have fresh holding period for Section 112A LTCG purpose.
  • Pre-money vs post-money valuation confusion in flip valuation.
  • Section 56(2)(x) buyer-side gift tax exposure in share swap structuring.
  • FEMA Rule 21 pricing guidelines compliance during flip (share swap must be at fair value).
  • US Section 7874 inversion rule - if Indian shareholders own 80%+ of US parent post-flip, specific US tax consequences.
  • Missing F-reorganisation structuring opportunity for existing Delaware LLC.

/ Post-calculator workflow

What founder does next.

  1. Review calculated capital gain + tax impact + alternative structuring.
  2. Discuss with CA (BQP) + US + Indian legal counsel on specific structuring.
  3. Pre-flip FEMA cleanup if historical breaches.
  4. Section 47(viib) qualifying structure design if applicable.
  5. US parent incorporation (Delaware C-Corp) + EIN + Mercury bank account.
  6. Indian shareholders exchange shares for US parent shares (specific documentation).
  7. FEMA Form ODI Part I filing (within 30 days) + specific pricing certificate.
  8. Section 2(42A) holding period reset documentation.
  9. Post-flip ongoing compliance: Form 3CEB TP + Form ODI APR + specific US + Indian reporting.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's the typical tax impact of India-to-US flip?
Depends on structure. Direct share-for-share exchange: Section 2(42A) + 112A LTCG 12.5% on implicit gain in Indian shareholders' hands. Section 47(viib) qualifying structure: potentially tax-neutral. F-reorganisation of existing Delaware LLC: specific US + Indian treatment. Calculator provides specific impact per structure.
Can flip be structured to be tax-neutral?
Potentially yes under Section 47(viib) with specific conditions - but complex + narrow qualification. Most practical flips crystallise some gain. Objective is to minimize + optimize timing. BQP structures 50+ flips - specific framework for each scenario.
What's Section 7874 US inversion risk?
If Indian shareholders of pre-flip Indian company own 80%+ of US parent post-flip: US treats the US parent as 'surrogate foreign corporation' + specific US tax consequences (inversion). Below 80% ownership: specific anti-abuse rules apply. 60%-80% ownership: specific limited US tax impact. Must plan ownership mix carefully.
Does flip require FEMA ODI compliance on Indian side?
Yes - Form ODI Part I filing within 30 days of flip + specific pricing certificate (Rule 21) + ongoing Form ODI Part II (APR) annual. BQP handles FEMA ODI stack - standard for 100+ flips executed.
