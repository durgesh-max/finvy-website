/ Businesses with GST demand, refund or cancellation orders · First appeal under section 107 · Updated 2026-11-27

GST Appeal in Form APL-01 (2026): Time Limit, Pre-Deposit (10%, Rs 20 Crore Cap), Format, Filing Online and the Stay on Recovery, three months, 10% and a well-argued ground.

If a GST officer passes an order you disagree with - a demand under section 73 or 74, a refund rejection, a cancellation of registration - your first remedy is an appeal to the Appellate Authority in Form APL-01 under section 107. It must be filed within three months of the order being communicated (one more month only with sufficient cause), and with a pre-deposit of the full admitted amount plus 10% of the disputed tax. Once the pre-deposit is paid, recovery of the rest of the demand is stayed while the appeal is pending.

/ The key rules

What section 107 requires.

PointRule
Who can appealAny person aggrieved by an order or decision of an adjudicating authority under GST
Time limitThree months from the date the order is communicated; the Appellate Authority can allow one more month for sufficient cause
FormAPL-01, filed online on the GST portal with the grounds of appeal and the order
Pre-depositFull admitted tax, interest, fine, fee and penalty, plus 10% of the disputed tax - capped at Rs 20 crore each under CGST and SGST (Rs 40 crore under IGST)
StayOnce the pre-deposit is paid, recovery of the balance is deemed stayed (section 107(7))
Next stepSecond appeal to the GST Appellate Tribunal under section 112, with a further pre-deposit

/ How to file

Building an appeal that holds.

  • Check the date of communication and count the three months from it; late appeals need a condonation request with reasons.
  • Work out the admitted and disputed amounts and pay the pre-deposit (through the electronic cash or credit ledger as allowed).
  • Draft the grounds: statement of facts, each ground of appeal with the legal basis - limitation, wrong section invoked, ITC eligibility, natural justice, calculation errors - and the relief sought.
  • Attach the evidence that was before the officer and explain any new evidence and why it was not produced earlier.
  • File APL-01 on the portal, keep the acknowledgement, and attend the personal hearing.
  • Penalty-only orders: the pre-deposit rules for appeals against orders that only impose a penalty were changed in 2025, so check the current requirement for your order before filing.

/ Common grounds

Where appeals succeed.

  • Demand raised after the limitation period, or section 74 invoked without real evidence of fraud or suppression.
  • Input tax credit denied on supplier default where you have invoices, payment and receipt of goods or services.
  • Order passed without considering the reply or without a personal hearing.
  • Arithmetical errors, double counting, or interest and penalty charged on amounts already paid.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is GST APL-01?
APL-01 is the form for filing a first appeal to the GST Appellate Authority under section 107 against an order or decision of a GST officer, such as a demand order, refund rejection or cancellation of registration. It is filed online on the GST portal with the grounds of appeal.
What is the time limit for a GST appeal in APL-01?
Three months from the date the order is communicated to you. The Appellate Authority can allow one further month if you show sufficient cause for the delay; beyond that the appeal cannot be admitted.
How much pre-deposit is required for a GST appeal?
The full amount of tax, interest, fine, fee and penalty you admit, plus 10% of the disputed tax - capped at Rs 20 crore each under CGST and SGST (Rs 40 crore under IGST). The rules for appeals against penalty-only orders were amended in 2025, so check the requirement for your order.
Does filing a GST appeal stop recovery of the demand?
Yes. Once the pre-deposit is paid and the appeal is filed, recovery of the balance of the demand is deemed stayed under section 107(7) until the appeal is decided.
Is there a format for a GST appeal?
APL-01 itself is a prescribed online form, but the statement of facts and grounds of appeal are drafted by you: the facts, each ground with its legal basis, the evidence relied on and the relief sought. Well-structured grounds on limitation, wrong section, ITC eligibility and natural justice carry most appeals.
