/ DPDP breach notification framework

The 72-hour window.

  • Section 8(6) DPDP Act 2023: Data Fiduciary must notify Data Protection Board (DPB) AND each affected data principal in the event of personal data breach.
  • Timing: 'without delay' - industry interpretation + GDPR analogy = 72 hours typically.
  • Specific Rules under finalisation (expected Budget 2026 notification) - exact format + timeline + specific content requirements.
  • Penalty: up to INR 200 crore for failure to notify. Up to INR 250 crore for inadequate security safeguards leading to breach.
  • Appeal: TDSAT within 60 days of DPB order.

/ 72-hour breach response workflow

Clock starts on discovery.

  1. Hour 0-6: Breach discovery + initial containment. Isolate affected systems + preserve forensic evidence + specific access controls.
  2. Hour 6-24: Incident response team assembled (CISO + Legal + Communications + Data Protection Officer if SDF + external cybersecurity + external legal counsel).
  3. Hour 12-24: Scope assessment - affected data principals + nature of data + specific risk assessment + specific attack vector.
  4. Hour 24-48: Legal notification preparation - DPB notification draft + data principal notification draft + specific regulatory parallel notifications (SEBI if listed, RBI if financial sector, specific sector regulator).
  5. Hour 48-72: Notifications sent - DPB + data principals + specific parallel regulators + specific media/public communications if material + specific contractual counterparts.
  6. Day 4-14: Specific remediation + specific data subject assistance + ongoing investigation + specific legal defence preparation.
  7. Week 2+: DPB investigation response + specific inquiry + specific adjudication defence.

/ Required breach notification content

What to include.

  • Nature of personal data breach + affected data categories.
  • Number of affected data principals (approximate).
  • Nature of personal data (sensitive / standard / specific categories).
  • Likely consequences of breach for data principals (identity theft risk, financial fraud risk, specific risks).
  • Measures taken by Data Fiduciary to address breach.
  • Measures recommended to data principals (password changes, specific monitoring, specific protective actions).
  • Contact point for data principals (Data Protection Officer if SDF, grievance officer).

/ Specific parallel compliance

Where DPDP overlaps with other regulators.

  • CERT-In directions (April 2022): 6-hour reporting to CERT-In for specific cybersecurity incidents (ransomware, specific data exfiltration, specific types). Separate + faster than DPDP 72-hour.
  • SEBI (if listed company): LODR Reg 30 + specific material event disclosure if specific threshold crossed.
  • RBI (if financial sector): specific banking + NBFC breach notification framework.
  • IRDAI (if insurance): specific breach notification.
  • TRAI / DoT (if telecom): specific breach notification.
  • GDPR (if EU data principals affected): GDPR 72-hour notification to EU supervisory authority. Specific parallel compliance for foreign-origin companies.
  • CCPA (if California residents): specific California breach notification law.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is DPDP 72-hour breach notification already mandatory?
DPDP Act 2023 Section 8(6) requires notification 'without delay'. Specific 72-hour window interpretation based on GDPR analogy + industry best practice. Specific Rules under finalisation (expected Budget 2026 notification) - exact timeline formally specified then. Prudent approach: 72-hour window regardless of final Rules.
What's the DPDP breach penalty exposure?
Up to INR 250 crore for failure to take reasonable security safeguards (Section 33 + Schedule). Up to INR 200 crore for failure to notify Data Protection Board. Separate penalties for children's data violations, SDF obligation failures. Appeal: TDSAT within 60 days.
Does CERT-In 6-hour reporting apply alongside DPDP 72-hour?
Yes - parallel compliance. CERT-In April 2022 directions: 6-hour reporting for specific cybersecurity incidents (ransomware, specific exfiltration). DPDP 72-hour: for personal data breach notification to Data Protection Board + data principals. Both apply if breach is both cyber incident + personal data breach.
Does BQP coordinate with foreign privacy counsel on DPDP + GDPR?
Yes - standard. For foreign-origin companies (US / UK / EU parent) with Indian subsidiary: GDPR 72-hour notification to EU supervisory authority + DPDP 72-hour notification in India + CCPA California notification if applicable. BQP + foreign privacy counsel + specific technical security + legal defence coordinated.
