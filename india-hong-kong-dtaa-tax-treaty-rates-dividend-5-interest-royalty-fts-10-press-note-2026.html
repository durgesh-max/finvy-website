/ Hong Kong companies and Indian founders · DTAA · Updated 2026-11-25

India-Hong Kong DTAA (2026): Tax Treaty Rates of 5% on Dividends and 10% on Interest, Royalties and Technical Fees, Anti-Abuse Rules and FDI Approval, low dividend tax, but substance and investment approval still decide the outcome.

India and Hong Kong signed their first comprehensive double tax avoidance agreement on 19 March 2018, and it entered into force on 30 November 2018. It caps Indian tax on dividends paid to a Hong Kong resident at 5% and on interest, royalties and fees for technical services at 10%, against India’s domestic 20% plus surcharge and cess; interest paid to the governments and specified bodies such as the Reserve Bank of India is exempt. The treaty has an anti-abuse article aimed at treaty shopping and conduit companies, so a Hong Kong entity needs real substance. Separately, investment into India with Hong Kong or Chinese beneficial ownership still needs government approval under Press Note 3. Hong Kong recipients claim the treaty rate with a Hong Kong tax residency certificate, Form 41 (formerly Form 10F) and a PAN.

/ Quick answers

What Hong Kong companies and Indian founders ask.

  • What is the dividend rate? 5% of the gross dividend for a Hong Kong resident beneficial owner; one summary shows 10%, so confirm against the treaty text.
  • Interest, royalties and technical fees? 10% of the gross amount each, with interest to the governments and specified bodies exempt.
  • Can I route Indian income through a Hong Kong company? Only with genuine substance; the anti-abuse article and India’s general anti-avoidance rules can deny benefits to conduit structures.
  • Does a Hong Kong investor need FDI approval? Yes, investment with Hong Kong or Chinese beneficial ownership needs prior approval; see Press Note 2 (2026) and land-border FDI.
  • From when does the treaty apply in India? It entered into force on 30 November 2018; confirm the first applicable year for withholding against the notification.

/ Claiming and structuring

Documents, substance and approvals.

  • Documents: a Hong Kong tax residency certificate from the Inland Revenue Department, Form 41 and a PAN or the documents allowed instead; see tax residency certificates.
  • Substance: board decisions, staff and real activity in Hong Kong support treaty claims and reduce anti-abuse risk.
  • Indian residents owning Hong Kong companies: overseas direct investment rules, Schedule FA reporting and place-of-effective-management risk apply.
  • Investment approvals: share issues, transfers and downstream investment with Hong Kong beneficial ownership need approval before money moves.
  • Credit in Hong Kong: check how Hong Kong treats Indian tax against its territorial system before assuming a credit.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What are the India-Hong Kong DTAA rates?
Indian tax on payments to a Hong Kong resident is capped at 5% on dividends and 10% on interest, royalties and fees for technical services, with interest to the governments and specified bodies exempt. Confirm the dividend rate against the treaty text, as one summary shows 10%.
When did the India-Hong Kong DTAA come into force?
It was signed on 19 March 2018 and entered into force on 30 November 2018. Check the notification for the first year its withholding rates apply in India.
Can a Hong Kong company invest in India without approval?
No. Investment with Hong Kong or Chinese beneficial ownership needs prior government approval under Press Note 3 of 2020; the 2026 easing for non-controlling stakes does not cover China and Hong Kong investors.
How does a Hong Kong company claim the treaty rate in India?
Give the Indian payer a Hong Kong tax residency certificate, Form 41 (formerly Form 10F) and a PAN or the documents allowed instead, and keep evidence of substance because the treaty has an anti-abuse article.
