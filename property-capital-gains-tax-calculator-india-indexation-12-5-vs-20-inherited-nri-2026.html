Property Capital Gains Tax Calculator India (2026): 12.5% vs 20% With Indexation, Inherited Property, 1 April 2001 Value and NRIs, see which rate costs you less.

Long-term gains on land and buildings sold in India are taxed at 12.5% without indexation. A resident individual or HUF can instead choose 20% with indexation for property acquired before 23 July 2024, and pays whichever is lower; NRIs pay 12.5%. For inherited property, use the previous owner's purchase date and cost; for property bought before 1 April 2001, use its value on that date. The calculator uses the cost inflation index up to 384 for 2026-27 (CBDT Notification 85/2026, 15 July 2026) and applies section 54 and 54EC reinvestment if you enter it.

    / The rules behind it

    And the index values.

    • Long-term: held more than 24 months; for inherited or gifted property, the previous owner's holding counts.
    • Rates: 12.5% without indexation; resident individuals and HUFs may opt for 20% with indexation on land or buildings acquired before 23 July 2024; plus 4% cess and any surcharge.
    • Indexed cost: cost x CII of the year of sale / CII of the year of purchase (2001-02 for property acquired earlier).
    • 1 April 2001: the fair market value on that date, not above the stamp duty value, can be used as cost.
    • Reliefs: section 54 (new residential house, up to INR 10 crore) and section 54EC (specified bonds within six months, up to INR 50 lakh).

    FAQ

    Common questions, answered.

    How is capital gains tax on property calculated in India?
    Long-term gains (held over 24 months) are taxed at 12.5% without indexation. Resident individuals and HUFs can choose 20% with indexation for land or buildings acquired before 23 July 2024, paying whichever is lower. Cess and any surcharge are added.
    What is the cost inflation index for 2026-27?
    384, notified by CBDT Notification No. 85/2026 dated 15 July 2026. It was 376 for 2025-26.
    How do I calculate capital gains on inherited property?
    Use the previous owner's purchase cost and date (or the 1 April 2001 value for older property). The holding period includes the previous owner's, so it is usually long-term.
    Can NRIs use indexation?
    No. NRIs pay 12.5% without indexation on long-term property gains; the 20% with-indexation option is for resident individuals and HUFs.
    Does the calculator include surcharge?
    No. It shows tax plus 4% cess before surcharge and any unused basic exemption, which depend on your total income.
