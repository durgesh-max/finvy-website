/ LLP partners, founders and foreign partners · LLP Act sections 23 to 25 · Updated 2026-11-28

Add or Remove a Partner in an LLP (2026): Form 4 Within 30 Days, Supplementary LLP Agreement in Form 3, Designated Partner Rules and Tax on Exit, two filings, thirty days.

When a partner joins or leaves an LLP, two filings follow: Form 4 to report the change in partners or designated partners, and Form 3 to file the supplementary LLP agreement - each within 30 days. The LLP must always keep at least two partners and two designated partners, one of them resident in India, and money or assets paid to an exiting partner can be taxed in the LLP’s hands.

/ The rules and filings

LLP Act 2008.

PointRule
Minimum partnersTwo partners, and two designated partners who are individuals
Resident designated partnerAt least one, in India for at least 120 days in the financial year
Form 4Notice of a partner or designated partner joining, leaving or changing details - within 30 days, with consent
Form 3Supplementary LLP agreement for the change (partners, profit share, contribution) - within 30 days
Designated partner IDDPIN / DIN needed before appointment
Leaving partnerAt least 30 days notice to the other partners, unless the agreement says otherwise
  • Sequence: sign the supplementary agreement, file Form 4 for the change, then file Form 3 with the agreement.
  • Stamp duty: the supplementary agreement attracts stamp duty that varies by state.

/ Foreign partners, exits and tax

Where it gets complicated.

  • Foreign or NRI partner: contribution by a non-resident follows FEMA rules for LLPs (the sector must permit foreign investment under the automatic route) and the related reporting; a foreign designated partner still needs a resident designated partner alongside.
  • Exit payouts: money or capital assets paid to a partner on reconstitution can be taxed in the LLP’s hands under sections 9B and 45(4) of the Income-tax Act - value the payout before agreeing it.
  • Capital accounts: settle the outgoing partner’s capital and current account in the books, and update bank signatories and GST records.
  • Falling below two partners: if only one partner remains for more than six months, that partner can become personally liable - add a partner promptly.
  • Late filings: Forms 3 and 4 attract additional fees per day of delay and keep the Registrar records out of date.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How do I add a new partner to an LLP?
Sign a supplementary LLP agreement admitting the partner, obtain the partner's consent (and DPIN if they will be a designated partner), file Form 4 with the Registrar within 30 days of the change, and file the supplementary agreement in Form 3 within 30 days.
How do I remove or replace a partner in an LLP?
The partner gives notice as the LLP agreement requires (at least 30 days if it is silent), the remaining partners settle the capital account, and the LLP files Form 4 for the cessation and Form 3 for the changed agreement, each within 30 days.
What is the difference between LLP Form 3 and Form 4?
Form 3 files the LLP agreement or changes to it. Form 4 notifies the appointment, cessation or change in details of a partner or designated partner.
Does an LLP need a resident designated partner?
Yes. At least one of the two or more designated partners must be resident in India, meaning in India for at least 120 days in the financial year.
Is there tax when a partner leaves an LLP?
Money or capital assets paid to a partner on reconstitution can be taxed in the LLP's hands under sections 9B and 45(4) of the Income-tax Act, so the payout should be structured and valued with advice.
