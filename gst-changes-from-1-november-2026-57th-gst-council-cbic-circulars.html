GST Changes From 1 November 2026: What the 57th GST Council and CBIC's 8 Circulars Mean for You, refunds, registration, scrap and discounts.

Following the 57th GST Council meeting on 8 October 2026, CBIC issued eight circulars dated 26 October 2026 and a set of notifications that mostly take effect on 1 November 2026. The headline changes: 90% provisional refunds for inverted duty structures, no INR 1,000 minimum for courier and postal export refunds, simpler handling of post-sale discounts, tighter rules for scrap trades, and limits on what officers can ask for at registration.

/ From 1 November 2026

Notifications.

  • Inverted duty refunds: businesses whose input tax exceeds output tax can get 90% of the refund provisionally, subject to risk assessment.
  • Small exporters: the INR 1,000 minimum for refunds of tax paid on exports through courier or post is removed.
  • Post-sale discounts: simplified credit-note provisions for discounts given by manufacturers to distributors and retailers.
  • Reverse charge on scrap: used cooking oil, plastic waste and scrap, tyre waste and scrap, and electrical and electronic waste supplied by unregistered persons to registered persons now fall under reverse charge (Notification 04/2026-Central Tax (Rate)).
  • Scrap dealers must register: suppliers of those items and of metal scrap (Chapters 72-81) lose the small-supplier registration exemption (Notification 06/2026-Central Tax).
  • GST TDS on scrap: registered buyers of those items from registered suppliers must deduct GST TDS (Notification 07/2026-Central Tax).
  • Rates: retreaded tractor tyres get a separate lower-rate entry; isabgol (psyllium seed) is exempt.

/ What to do this week

Action list.

  • Exporters with Rule 96(10) or Rule 89(4A)/(4B) disputes: file supplementary submissions citing Circular 264/10/2026 and claim refunds of amounts paid under protest.
  • Businesses with inverted duty structures: line up refund applications for the faster 90% provisional route.
  • Buyers of scrap and e-waste: update purchase processes for reverse charge and GST TDS from 1 November.
  • Service exporters paid into SNRR or similar rupee accounts: check refunds or demands that turned on the foreign-exchange condition.
  • Pending registration applications stuck on queries: cite Circular 257/03/2026.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What GST changes apply from 1 November 2026?
90% provisional refunds for inverted duty structures, no INR 1,000 minimum for courier/postal export refunds, simpler post-sale discount credit notes, reverse charge and GST TDS on specified scrap and e-waste, compulsory registration for scrap suppliers, and minor rate changes.
When was the 57th GST Council meeting?
8 October 2026, in New Delhi. CBIC issued eight implementing circulars (257 to 264) on 26 October 2026.
Can a GST officer ask for any document during registration?
No. Circular 257/03/2026 bars unwarranted document requests; deficiencies must be raised in FORM GST REG-03 and missed timelines result in deemed approval.
What is the pre-deposit for a GST appeal?
10% of the disputed tax. Circular 259/05/2026 says the refund after a favourable order is claimed through RFD-01, even if the department files a further appeal.
