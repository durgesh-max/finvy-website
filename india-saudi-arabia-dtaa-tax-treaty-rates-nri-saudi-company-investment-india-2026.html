India-Saudi Arabia Tax Treaty (2026): Rates on Dividends, Interest and Royalties, Technical Services, and What Saudi-Based NRIs and Saudi Companies Need, for Saudi-based NRIs and companies investing in or billing India.

Under the India-Saudi Arabia tax treaty, Indian dividends paid to a Saudi resident are taxed in India at up to 5%, and interest and royalties at up to 10%, instead of the domestic 20% to 30% plus surcharge and cess. To get these rates the recipient needs a Saudi tax residency certificate and Form 10F. Most commentators read the treaty as having no separate article on fees for technical services. A Saudi firm's technical services to India, without a permanent establishment here, are then generally treated as business profits. Payments that transfer know-how can still be royalties. This point needs case-by-case review. Saudi-based NRIs mainly use the treaty for NRO interest and dividends. NRE and FCNR interest is already exempt in India while they are non-resident, and Saudi Arabia does not tax individuals on that income.

/ For Saudi-based NRIs and companies

Practical points.

  • NRIs: give the bank a Saudi TRC and Form 10F for NRO interest at 10%; claim refunds of excess TDS in an Indian return; see which ITR for NRIs. Watch India's deemed-residence rule if Indian income exceeds INR 15 lakh; see deemed residence for Gulf NRIs.
  • Saudi companies investing in India: FDI route and sector caps apply; dividends from an Indian subsidiary can get 5%; see working with an Indian CA as a foreign company.
  • Saudi service providers: confirm whether fees are business profits (no Indian tax without a PE) or royalties (10%), and give Indian clients a no-PE declaration where appropriate.
  • Projects in India: track staff presence and project duration to manage permanent establishment risk.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the dividend tax rate under the India-Saudi Arabia treaty?
Up to 5% for a Saudi-resident beneficial owner with a tax residency certificate and Form 10F, compared with 20% plus surcharge and cess under Indian domestic law.
What tax applies on NRO interest for an NRI in Saudi Arabia?
Up to 10% under the treaty with a Saudi TRC and Form 10F, instead of up to 30% plus surcharge and cess; excess TDS can be claimed back in an Indian return.
Does the India-Saudi treaty cover fees for technical services?
Most sources say it has no separate article; such fees without a permanent establishment are generally business profits, unless they are really royalties for know-how. Review each contract.
Is NRE interest taxed for NRIs in Saudi Arabia?
No. NRE and FCNR interest is exempt in India while you are non-resident, and Saudi Arabia does not tax individuals on it.
Can a Saudi company invest in India?
Yes, through FDI subject to sector caps and approval routes; dividends from the Indian company can be taxed at the 5% treaty rate.
