Near you, or not.

  • Start with a health check: list the returns filed and pending, compare GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and GSTR-2B for the year, and check any portal notices.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What does a GST consultant do?
They check whether you need GST registration and apply for it, file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B every month or quarter, reconcile your input tax credit with GSTR-2B, file the LUT and refund claims for exports, prepare the GSTR-9 annual return, and reply to GST notices and show cause notices. For a cancelled or suspended registration they file the pending returns and the revocation application.
How does a GST registration consultant register my business, and what documents are needed?
The application (REG-01) is filed online on the GST portal with the PAN of the business, Aadhaar and photographs of the proprietor, partners or directors, proof of the principal place of business (electricity bill, property tax receipt, rent agreement or owner's consent letter), bank account details, and the constitution documents (partnership deed, LLP agreement or certificate of incorporation). Aadhaar authentication follows, and some applicants are asked to complete biometric verification at a GST Suvidha Kendra. The officer then approves the application or raises a query in REG-03, which is answered in REG-04 with documents. A consultant checks the documents before filing so that queries and rejections are less likely, and the work is done online, whether you are in Chennai, Delhi, Bangalore, Jaipur or abroad. I am searching for a GST consultant near me. Does location matter?
Not for the work. GST registration, returns, refunds and most notice replies are prepared from documents and filed online on the GST portal, so a call and shared documents are enough wherever you are. A personal hearing, where one is fixed, can usually be attended online or through an authorised representative.
Do I need GST registration?
For services, registration is generally required once aggregate turnover crosses Rs 20 lakh in a financial year in most states (lower in special category states); some cases need registration regardless of turnover, such as certain inter-state or e-commerce supplies. Exporters below the threshold often register to export under LUT and claim refunds. Check your own case before deciding.
What happens if I do not file GST returns on time?
A late fee runs every day (Rs 50, or Rs 20 for a nil return, up to the caps), 18% interest runs on tax paid late, the next return cannot be filed, e-way bills can be blocked and your buyers lose input tax credit. Continued non-filing can lead to a show cause notice in REG-17 and cancellation of the registration.
I got a GST notice. What should I do first?
Note the form (for example ASMT-10, DRC-01, DRC-01B, DRC-01C or REG-17), the period and the reply date, then gather the returns, invoices and reconciliations for that period. Reply within the time stated, with documents, even if only to explain and ask for time - an unanswered notice is usually decided against you.
How long do I have to reply to a GST show cause notice?
Reply in Form GST DRC-06 within the period stated in the notice, usually 30 days. If more time is needed, ask the officer in writing before the date passes; personal hearings can be adjourned only three times. An unanswered notice can be decided ex parte, and the demand, interest and penalty then have to be challenged in appeal instead. Read the notice for the section (73, 74 or 74A), the period and the amounts, collect the returns, invoices and reconciliations it relies on, and reply point by point. Book a call to have the reply drafted and filed on time.
What happens if an e-way bill expires before the goods reach?
An e-way bill is valid for one day for every 200 km (or part) for normal cargo. If the goods cannot reach in time, for example because of a vehicle breakdown, the generator or transporter can extend it on the e-way bill portal within eight hours before or after expiry, giving the reason and the current location; the total life is capped at 360 days from generation. Goods moving on an expired e-way bill are treated as moving without one and can be detained under section 129 until the tax and penalty are paid or security is furnished. Book a call if goods have been detained or a penalty order issued.
