NRI Property Management in India (2026): Managing Your Flat From Abroad, Power of Attorney, Rent, TDS, Tax Return and Repatriation, someone holds the keys; you still own the tax.

NRIs usually manage property in India through a trusted relative holding a registered power of attorney, or a property management company that finds tenants, collects rent and handles maintenance. Whoever manages it, the tax and FEMA duties stay with you: the tenant must deduct TDS of about 31.2% on rent paid to an NRI (from the first rupee), you file an Indian tax return to pay the right tax or claim the excess back, rent goes into your NRO account, and after-tax rent can be sent abroad with Forms 145 and 146. A lower deduction certificate (Form 128) can cut the TDS to your real tax, and a written, registered rent agreement protects you if a tenant does not pay or leave.

/ Quick answers

What NRI owners ask.

  • How can an NRI manage property in India? Through a family member or friend with a registered power of attorney, or a property management company under a written agreement. Either way, keep control of the bank account and the tax filings.
  • Does the tenant have to deduct TDS for an NRI landlord? Yes: 30% plus cess (about 31.2%) on the gross rent from the first rupee, deposited under the tenant's TAN and reported in Form 144.
  • How is NRI rental income taxed? As income from house property: rent minus municipal tax, minus a 30% standard deduction and home-loan interest, taxed at slab rates. Excess TDS comes back through your return.
  • Can I send rent abroad? Yes. Rent is current income; after tax it can be remitted from the NRO account with Form 145 and a CA's certificate in Form 146.
  • Who pays property tax and society charges? You, as owner; your manager can pay from your NRO account. Keep receipts: municipal tax paid is deductible from rent.

/ Checklist

Setting it up properly.

  • Registered rent agreement with the tenant's PAN, rent, deposit, notice period and police verification where required.
  • Rent into your NRO account, not a relative's or the manager's account, so income and TDS match your PAN.
  • Tenant's TDS: make sure the tenant has a TAN, deducts TDS and gives you the TDS certificate; check Form 26AS.
  • Lower deduction certificate: if your real tax is below 31.2%, apply in Form 128 before the year starts.
  • File your Indian return every year with rent, deductions and TDS credit.
  • Power of attorney: sign it abroad, notarise and apostille it (or attest at the Indian mission), then adjudicate it for stamp duty in India; limit its scope and revoke it when not needed.
  • Selling later: see NRI property sale and the country guides for the US, UK, Canada and Australia.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How can an NRI manage property in India?
Through a trusted relative or friend holding a registered power of attorney, or a property management company under a written agreement. Keep the rent flowing into your own NRO account and keep filing your own Indian tax return.
What TDS does a tenant deduct for an NRI landlord?
30% plus cess, about 31.2%, on the gross rent from the first rupee, using the tenant's TAN and the quarterly statement Form 144. A lower deduction certificate (Form 128) can reduce it.
What documents are needed for a rent agreement when the NRI owner lives abroad?
Usually the owner's PAN and passport or Aadhaar, proof of ownership such as the sale deed or latest property tax receipt, the tenant's PAN and identity and address proof, photographs, and the agreed rent, deposit, term and notice clauses. If you cannot sign in India, a family member or manager can sign under a power of attorney executed abroad and adjudicated for stamp duty in India, or you can use the state's e-registration where it allows remote signing. A lease longer than 11 months must be registered, and stamp duty depends on the state. Put the tenant's TDS obligation and your NRO account for rent in the agreement.
Do NRIs have to file an Indian return for rental income?
Yes, where Indian income exceeds the exemption or TDS is to be refunded. Rent is taxed as house property after a 30% standard deduction, and TDS deducted by the tenant is credited.
Can rent from Indian property be sent abroad?
Yes. After tax, rent can be remitted from the NRO account as current income, with Form 145 and a chartered accountant's certificate in Form 146.
Should rent be paid to my relative or property manager in India?
Better not. Have it paid into your NRO account so that the income, TDS and your PAN match, and so you keep control of the money.
