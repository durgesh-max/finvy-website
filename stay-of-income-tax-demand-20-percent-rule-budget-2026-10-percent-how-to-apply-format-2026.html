/ Individuals, companies and NRIs facing a high-pitched assessment · Recovery pending appeal · Updated 2026-11-27

Stay of Income Tax Demand (2026): How to Apply Pending Appeal, the 20% Rule, the Budget 2026 Cut to 10% and the Application Format, file the appeal, then stop the recovery.

When an assessment raises a large demand that you are appealing, the department can still recover it - attach bank accounts, adjust refunds - unless you get a stay of demand pending the appeal. The CBDT's guidelines generally grant a stay on payment of 20% of the disputed demand, and the Union Budget 2026-27 announced a reduction to 10% computed on the core tax. Courts have also held the percentage is not automatic: the officer must look at your case, including a strong prima facie case and financial hardship.

/ How the stay works

The rules in brief.

PointPosition
Who grants itThe Assessing Officer, on application, once an appeal against the order is filed; if refused, the Principal Commissioner or Commissioner can be approached
Standard conditionCBDT guidelines (2016, modified in July 2017): stay generally on payment of 20% of the disputed demand, with discretion to ask for more or accept less in specified cases
Budget 2026-27Announced a reduction to 10%, computed on the core tax demand - check whether it has been applied to your case before paying
CourtsHigh Courts have held the percentage is not a mandatory condition; the application must be decided on merits, considering prima facie case and hardship
Old or new ActDemands from proceedings under the Income-tax Act, 1961 follow its provisions; the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 April 2026

/ How to apply

What a stay application should contain.

  • File the appeal first (to the Commissioner (Appeals) or the next forum) and keep the acknowledgement.
  • Write to the Assessing Officer on the e-filing portal or by letter: the order and demand, the appeal reference, the grounds showing a prima facie case, and any financial hardship.
  • Offer what you can pay - the guideline percentage, or less with reasons - and ask for recovery to be kept in abeyance until the appeal is decided.
  • If refused or not decided, approach the Principal Commissioner or Commissioner, and if needed the Tribunal or the High Court.
  • Keep the order of stay safe and track any adjustment of refunds against the demand.

/ When a stay matters most

Typical situations.

  • High-pitched additions in scrutiny or reassessment, such as unexplained cash or property transactions.
  • Transfer pricing or international tax adjustments on companies with foreign parents.
  • NRIs whose refunds are being adjusted against a disputed demand.
  • Demands where the main issue is already decided in your favour in an earlier year or by a court.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is a stay of demand in income tax?
An order keeping recovery of a disputed income tax demand in abeyance while your appeal against the assessment is pending, so the department does not attach your bank accounts or adjust refunds against it in the meantime.
How much do I have to pay to get a stay of income tax demand?
Under the CBDT's guidelines a stay is generally granted on payment of 20% of the disputed demand, and the officer can ask for more or accept less in specified cases. The Union Budget 2026-27 announced a reduction to 10% computed on the core tax demand - check whether it applies to your demand. Courts have also held the percentage is not automatic.
Is the 20% deposit mandatory for a stay?
No. Several High Courts have held that the 20% figure in the CBDT guidelines is not a mandatory condition and that the officer must decide the stay application on its merits, including a strong prima facie case and financial hardship.
How do I apply for a stay of demand?
File the appeal, then apply to the Assessing Officer with the order, the demand, the appeal reference, your grounds and any hardship, offering the amount you can pay. If the officer refuses or does not decide, approach the Principal Commissioner or Commissioner, and if needed the Tribunal or the High Court.
Can the department adjust my refund against a disputed demand?
It can propose to adjust refunds against an outstanding demand, but you can object, and once a stay is granted the disputed amount should not be recovered while the appeal is pending. Respond to any adjustment intimation on the portal within the time given.
