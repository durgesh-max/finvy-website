/ Why FEMA breaches surface in due diligence

The common finds.

  • FC-GPR missed - foreign shareholder received shares without timely FEMA reporting (30-day window from allotment).
  • FC-TRS missed - share transfer involving non-resident without 60-day FEMA filing.
  • FLA Return missed - annual FEMA reporting of foreign liabilities / assets missed for multi-year window.
  • Form ODI APR missed - Indian parent's overseas subsidiary annual reporting missed.
  • Rule 21 pricing guideline breach - share issue below fair value to non-resident.
  • Press Note 3 (eased for non-controlling stakes within 10% by Press Note 2 (2026)) beneficial ownership issue - land-bordering country investment without prior approval.
  • Sector-cap breach - foreign investment exceeded sector FDI cap.
  • ECB reporting missed - External Commercial Borrowing not reported.

/ Impact on M&A deal

Why urgent.

  • Buyer's counsel flags FEMA breaches as material due diligence finding.
  • Deal pauses - closing cannot proceed until specific resolution.
  • Buyer may demand: specific indemnity + escrow + reduced consideration.
  • Deal timeline impact: 30-180 day delay typical.
  • Specific worst case: buyer walks away.

  •   — Voluntary compounding under Section 13 FEMA (90-180 days).
  •   — Specific indemnity structure with escrow.
  •   — Buyer accepts specific FEMA risk with price adjustment.

/ Voluntary compounding under Section 13

The fast fix.

  1. Comprehensive FEMA compliance history audit (5-year lookback typical).
  2. Breach identification + severity assessment + chronology drafting.
  3. Supporting document collection (FIRC, bank statements, board resolutions, share certificates).
  4. Compounding application drafting under Section 13 FEMA Act.
  5. Submission via AD-I category bank to RBI Compounding Authority.
  6. Hearing representation (physical or video) with CA partner attendance.
  7. Compounding order within 180 days: specifies fee + payment deadline.
  8. Fee payment via RTGS within 15 days of order.
  9. Compliance certificate issued - breach formally compounded.
  10. Buyer counsel accepts closure certificate as resolution.

/ Compounding fee typical range

What to budget.

  • FC-GPR / FC-TRS late filing (minor delay): INR 10,000-50,000 per instance.
  • FLA / APR non-filing (per year missed): INR 10,000-50,000 per year.
  • Pricing guideline breach: higher - up to multiples of pricing variance.
  • Sector cap / Press Note 3 breach: significant - can be 100%+ of amount involved; may require unwinding.
  • Multi-year / multi-instance breaches: consolidated application for cost efficiency.
  • Typical foreign-subsidiary cleanup: INR 50,000 - INR 10 lakh total depending on scope.
  • Materially cheaper than RBI inspection + 3x amount involved penalty.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How urgent is FEMA compounding pre-M&A?
Deal can pause 30-180 days until resolution. Buyer counsel typically demands specific compliance clean-up or specific indemnity/escrow structuring. Voluntary compounding (90-180 days) is fastest path to resolution. Alternative indemnity structuring may be acceptable in some cases but less clean.
What's typical FEMA compounding fee?
FC-GPR/FC-TRS late (minor): INR 10K-50K per instance. FLA/APR missed: INR 10K-50K per year. Pricing guideline breach: higher - up to multiples of variance. Sector cap/Press Note 3: significant - 100%+ of amount involved. Typical foreign-subsidiary cleanup: INR 50K-10 lakh total. Materially cheaper than RBI inspection.
Can compounding be done urgently in less than 90 days?
Standard timeline is 90-180 days (RBI's typical processing). Specific urgency can be flagged in application but RBI doesn't offer fast-track. Alternative: specific indemnity structuring with buyer may bypass compounding + close transaction. Compounding completed post-closing as condition precedent.
What if buyer walks away due to FEMA breaches?
Risk reduces by: (a) proactive compounding before deal talks, (b) complete compliance history + specific indemnity + escrow structure, (c) specific buyer counsel coordination. BQP engagement includes buyer counsel liaison + deal timing coordination. Specific pre-M&A audit by seller before marketing to buyers often avoids this scenario.
