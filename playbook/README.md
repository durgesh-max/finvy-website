# BQP Marketing Playbook — Execution Files

Everything in this folder is paste-ready. Nothing requires editing beyond adding your actual review-link / Formspree-endpoint / specific client-name details where marked.

**Execution rhythm (if you want a repeatable weekly cadence):**

- **Monday:** post the week's LinkedIn post from `01-linkedin-posts.md`.
- **Tuesday 10:00 IST:** publish the week's newsletter from `03-newsletter-drafts.md` (customised to the real regulatory updates that week).
- **Wednesday:** 2 Reddit / Quora substantive answers from `06-reddit-quora-answers.md` (never same answer twice).
- **Thursday:** 1-2 review requests from `02-review-sms-templates.md`.
- **Friday:** 1 press pitch from `04-press-pitches.md` OR 1 partnership outreach from `05-partnership-outreach.md` (alternate weeks).
- **Monthly:** Google Business Profile post from `07-gbp-execution.md`.

## What's NOT in this folder (because you asked)

- **Mailchimp/Buttondown signup setup** — you need to sign up (free), create a list, and I'll wire the signup form into the site. 15 minutes of your time, then say the word.
- **Formspree / Netlify Forms for lead capture** — you need to sign up, I'll wire the form endpoint into get-a-quote.html. 15 minutes.
- **GBP photo pack** — you need to shoot or source 10 photos (office interior, headshot, team, signage). Can't be synthesised.
- **Actual Reddit / Quora / LinkedIn posting** — must come from your account; copy-paste from the templates.

## Priority order (if you execute only part of this)

1. **Google Business Profile** (`07`) — single highest-ROI action. 30-minute setup, ranks you in local map pack. Do first.
2. **Review-acquisition SMS to past 50 clients** (`02`) — fuels GBP ranking. Start week 2 post-verification.
3. **12-week LinkedIn calendar** (`01`) — compounds. Start now.
4. **Weekly newsletter** (`03`) — direct distribution channel; starts the compound effect.
5. **Press pitches** (`04`) — one Inc42 / YourStory / ET backlink lifts domain authority materially.
6. **Partnership outreach** (`05`) — longer sales cycle but 10+ clients per successful partnership.
7. **Reddit / Quora** (`06`) — slow-burn; execute if time permits after priorities 1-5.
