Australian Accounting and Tax Practices Outsourcing to India (2026): TPB Offshoring Rules, Client Consent, APP 8 and a Safe Set-Up, the client consent comes first, the capacity second.

An Australian registered tax or BAS agent can outsource work to India, but under the Tax Practitioners Board's Code of Professional Conduct (item 6) it must not disclose client information to a third party without the client's consent. The TPB expects the consent to say what information is disclosed, to whom and where, where it is stored, whether AI tools are used, and any further outsourcing, and the Australian practice stays responsible for the work (TPB outsourcing and offshoring factsheet, December 2025). The Privacy Act's APP 8 also keeps the Australian entity accountable for personal information it discloses overseas.

/ What Australian practices typically outsource

And how to start.

  • Bookkeeping and reconciliations in Xero, MYOB or QuickBooks; BAS and IAS preparation for review.
  • Individual and company return preparation for agent review; workpapers and tax reconciliations.
  • SMSF accounts preparation and audit-ready files; year-end financial statements.
  • Season planning: individual returns are due by 31 October for self-lodgers, with later dates for clients on a registered agent's list, so work can be spread across the lodgement program.
  • Start with a 30 to 60 day pilot on a small client set, measured on turnaround and review points, before scaling.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can an Australian tax agent outsource work to India?
Yes, but under TPB Code item 6 client information cannot be disclosed to a third party without the client's consent, and the agent remains responsible for the work.
What must the client consent cover?
According to the TPB: what information will be disclosed, to whom and where, where it will be stored, whether AI tools may be used, and any further outsourcing by the provider.
Does the Privacy Act apply to offshoring?
Yes. APP 8 governs cross-border disclosure of personal information, and the Australian entity generally remains accountable for how the overseas recipient handles it.
What work do Australian practices usually outsource?
Bookkeeping, BAS and IAS preparation, individual and company return preparation for review, workpapers, year-end accounts and SMSF files.
