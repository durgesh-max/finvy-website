/ Key rules NRIs ask about

The short answers.

  • Do I have to file? Yes if your Indian income is above the basic exemption limit, and it is the only way to claim back excess TDS. The Rs 12 lakh tax-free limit (87A rebate) is for residents only.
  • Property sale TDS: the buyer deducts TDS on the price, not the gain: up to 14.95% on a long-term sale. NRIs pay 12.5% on long-term gains without the indexation option, so a lower TDS certificate usually saves a lot of locked-up cash.
  • Rent and interest: tenants and banks deduct TDS at high rates on payments to NRIs (about 31.2% on NRO interest unless a treaty rate applies); the excess comes back through the return.
  • NRE and NRO: NRE interest is tax-free while you are non-resident; NRO interest is taxable.
  • Deadlines now: a belated or revised return for AY 2026-27 can be filed until 31 December 2026; after that, only an updated return (ITR-U) with extra tax.

/ Working with a CA from abroad

No visit to India needed.

  • Online from start to finish: documents by WhatsApp or email, returns e-verified with Aadhaar OTP, net banking or a digital signature.
  • Any city: the income tax rules are national. What changes from city to city is property registration and stamp duty, handled at the local sub-registrar; a power of attorney signed abroad must be stamped in India within three months of reaching India.
  • What to send first: your country of residence, PAN, Form 168 (old 26AS) and AIS if you can download them, and documents for any sale or remittance.
  • Selling soon? Start before the sale agreement is signed so the lower TDS certificate is in place before the buyer pays.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Do I need a CA near me if I am an NRI?
No. NRI tax work is done online: documents by WhatsApp or email, the return e-verified with Aadhaar OTP, net banking or a digital signature. The tax rules are the same in every Indian city.
What does a CA for NRI do?
Files the Indian income tax return (ITR-2 or ITR-3), applies for a lower TDS certificate before a property sale, claims TDS refunds, certifies Form 146 (old 15CB) for repatriation, replies to notices and plans the return to India.
Which NRI tax deadlines fall before 31 December 2026?
Three matter most. A belated return for AY 2026-27 (financial year 2025-26) can be filed only until 31 December 2026 - after that, TDS refunds on rent, NRO interest or a property sale for that year are much harder to recover. A revised return for the same year is free until 31 December 2026 (a fee applies for revisions after that, up to 31 March 2027). And the FAST-DS 2026 window for residents to declare foreign assets left out of earlier returns also closes on 31 December 2026 - relevant to NRIs who have moved back. If you are selling property in early 2027, apply for the Form 128 lower deduction certificate before the sale. WhatsApp +91 78018 87130 with your PAN, Form 168 (old 26AS) and what you need to file, and we tell you what is still possible before 31 December.
Can an NRI file ITR without a CA?
Yes, on the income tax portal. A CA helps where there is a property sale, capital gains, treaty relief, foreign tax credit, a refund to claim or a notice.
I have property in Pune / Mumbai / Bengaluru. Does the city change the tax?
No. Income tax and TDS rules are national. Only property registration, stamp duty and local paperwork differ by state and city.
Can a CA help an NRI selling property in India?
Yes. The CA works out the capital gain, applies for a lower TDS certificate (Form 128) so the buyer deducts only your actual tax, plans section 54 / 54EC reinvestment, and handles the return and repatriation afterwards.
How does an NRI get a TDS refund?
By filing an Indian income tax return showing the actual tax; the excess TDS is refunded to an Indian bank account, with interest at 0.5% a month where it applies.
How do I track my income tax refund as an NRI, and what if it is stuck?
Log in to the income tax e-filing portal and check the status of the return for that year - it shows whether the return is processed and whether the refund was issued, failed or adjusted; the Protean (NSDL) refund status page shows the same once the refund banker has it. Refunds go only to an Indian bank account (usually your NRO account) that is pre-validated on the portal and linked to your PAN. The common reasons a refund is stuck: the return was never e-verified; the account is not validated, is closed, or the name does not match your PAN; the return is still being processed or is under scrutiny; or the department adjusted the refund against an old outstanding demand (you get an intimation first and can reply if the demand is wrong). Once the cause is fixed, a refund that failed can be reissued through a refund reissue request on the portal, and a long delay can be raised as a grievance. Where interest applies, it is 0.5% a month on the refund. WhatsApp +91 78018 87130 with your PAN, the assessment year and a screenshot of the refund status, or book a call.
