Form MR-1 Managerial Personnel Appointment India 2026, MD + WTD + Manager appointment ROC filing.

/ Form MR-1 core

Who files when.

  • Form MR-1: filed by company within 60 days of appointment of MD / WTD / Manager.
  • Section 196 Companies Act 2013 + Section 197 (managerial remuneration limits).
  • Covers: appointment, re-appointment, variation in terms, remuneration change.
  • MCA v3 portal filing.
  • Section 450 default penalty for late / non-filing.

/ Section 197 managerial remuneration limits

The ceilings.

  • Public company: aggregate remuneration to all directors including MD/WTD/Manager - maximum 11% of net profit (Section 197(1)).
  • Specific sub-limits: single MD/WTD max 5% of net profit; multiple MDs/WTDs max 10% aggregate; non-executive directors max 1% (with MD/WTD) or 3% (without).
  • Private company: no Section 197 limits (except specific exemptions carved out).
  • Loss / inadequate profit: Schedule V Part II provisions apply with specific slab-wise maximum.
  • Shareholder special resolution required for exceeding Section 197 limits.

/ Schedule V compliance

The specific framework.

  • Schedule V Companies Act: part I (eligibility conditions for appointment) + Part II (remuneration ceiling for loss / inadequate profit years).
  • Eligibility conditions: minimum age 21 + maximum age 70 (specific special resolution if above 70) + specific qualifications.
  • Prohibition on managerial appointment: specific disqualifications (undischarged insolvent, convicted of specific offences, specific IRDA/SEBI debarment).
  • Loss / inadequate profit remuneration limit: slab-wise maximum based on effective capital of company.
  • Approval sequence: Board → Nomination & Remuneration Committee (if applicable) → Shareholders (if Section 197 limits exceeded or Schedule V applicable).

/ Form MR-1 + related compliances

Integrated.

  • Form MR-1: within 60 days of appointment.
  • Form DIR-12: appointment of director (within 30 days).
  • Form MGT-14: for specific resolutions (Section 179(3) board resolutions for appointment of KMP including MD / CEO / CFO).
  • Section 134 Board Report: disclosure of managerial remuneration.
  • Section 197 details in financial statements + notes.
  • Separate CSR Committee / Audit Committee / Nomination & Remuneration Committee if applicable.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

When is Form MR-1 due?
Within 60 days of appointment of MD / WTD / Manager. Also for re-appointment, variation in terms, remuneration change. Section 196 Companies Act 2013 + Section 197 managerial remuneration limits.
Does Section 197 limit apply to Pvt Ltd?
No - Section 197 managerial remuneration limits (11% of net profit, etc.) apply only to public companies. Private companies are exempt from Section 197 limits (except specific carved-out scenarios). But Form MR-1 filing is still mandatory for private company MD/WTD/Manager appointment.
What's Schedule V compliance?
Companies Act Schedule V: Part I (eligibility conditions - age 21-70, qualifications, no specific disqualification) + Part II (remuneration ceiling for loss / inadequate profit years - slab-wise maximum based on effective capital). Applicable to public companies in loss years + specific private company scenarios.
