What Is Section 194Q and How to Comply (After TCS on Goods Was Scrapped), 0.1% TDS on purchases above INR 50 lakh.

Section 194Q requires a buyer with turnover above INR 10 crore in the preceding year to deduct 0.1% TDS on purchases of goods from a resident seller exceeding INR 50 lakh in a financial year. Since TCS on sale of goods under Section 206C(1H) was discontinued from 1 April 2025, Section 194Q is the only withholding on ordinary goods transactions.

/ The rule

Section 194Q at a glance.

  • Who deducts: a buyer whose turnover exceeded INR 10 crore in the preceding financial year.
  • When: on purchases of goods from a resident seller exceeding INR 50 lakh in the financial year.
  • Rate: 0.1% on the amount above INR 50 lakh, at credit or payment, whichever is earlier.
  • Seller without PAN: 5%.
  • Not applicable: imports from non-resident sellers, and transactions where TDS under another provision or TCS (other than on sale of goods) applies.

/ How to comply

Monthly and quarterly steps.

  1. At the start of each year, check whether your turnover in the previous year exceeded INR 10 crore.
  2. Track purchases per seller; deduct only on the amount above INR 50 lakh.
  3. Deduct at credit or payment, whichever is earlier, and deposit by the 7th of the following month.
  4. File the quarterly TDS statement and issue TDS certificates to sellers.
  5. Collect sellers' PAN up front to avoid the 5% rate.

/ What changed in 2025

Simpler than before.

  • No more coordination with sellers about 206C(1H) overlap: sellers no longer collect TCS on goods.
  • Section 206AB (higher rate for non-filers) was omitted from 1 April 2025.
  • From 1 April 2026, the provision sits in Section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

Sources: VJM Global: amendments from 1 April 2025; CalcGuru: 194Q vs 206C(1H) after the change; Income Tax Department: Section 206AB omitted.

Last updated: 30 October 2026. From 1 April 2026, TDS and TCS provisions sit in Sections 393 and 394 of the Income-tax Act, 2025; the rules described here carried over.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is Section 194Q?
TDS of 0.1% by a buyer with turnover above INR 10 crore on purchases of goods from a resident seller above INR 50 lakh in a year, on the amount above INR 50 lakh.
Does 194Q still interact with 206C(1H)?
No. TCS on sale of goods under Section 206C(1H) was discontinued from 1 April 2025, so 194Q is the only withholding on ordinary goods sales.
What if the seller has no PAN?
TDS is deducted at 5% instead of 0.1%.
Does 194Q apply to imports?
No. It applies to purchases from resident sellers.
