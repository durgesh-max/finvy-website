When Is TDS Applicable on NRI Sale of Property India, 12.5% LTCG / 30% STCG, lower deduction certificate, and no TAN needed from 1 October 2026.

Direct answer: a buyer must deduct TDS when purchasing property from an NRI seller (Section 393(2) of the Income-tax Act, 2025 for payments from 1 April 2026; earlier Section 195). Rates: 12.5% + surcharge + cess on long-term gains (property held more than 24 months) or 30% + surcharge + cess on short-term gains. From 1 October 2026, resident individual and HUF buyers can deposit this TDS under their own PAN through Form 141, without a TAN. NRI can obtain Lower Deduction Certificate (LDC) under Section 197 for reduced rate based on actual tax liability. Buyer deducts TDS at specified rate; NRI claims refund via ITR if excess.

/ Section 195 TDS framework

The core provision.

  • Section 195 Income Tax Act: buyer must deduct TDS on sum paid to NRI seller if chargeable to Indian tax.
  • Chargeability: Section 9 - capital gain on sale of Indian property is deemed to accrue in India.
  • Section 195(2) procedure: NRI can seek lower rate via Form 13 + specific LDC under Section 197.
  • Buyer's responsibility: compute TDS at specified rate + deposit by 7th of following month + Form 27Q quarterly + Form 16A to NRI.
  • Buyer's default: Section 201 'assessee in default' + specific interest + specific penalty.
  • NRI's recovery: Section 195 TDS reflected in Form 26AS + claimed as credit against actual tax liability via Indian ITR.

/ Standard TDS rates

Default without LDC.

  • LTCG property (held > 24 months): 12.5% + surcharge + 4% cess, without indexation.
  •   — The option of 20% with indexation for property bought before 23 July 2024 is available only to resident individuals and HUFs, not to NRIs.
  •   — Property purchased on/after 23 July 2024: 12.5% without indexation (default).
  •   — Without a lower deduction certificate, buyers usually deduct on the full sale price, which is often far more than the tax actually due on the gain.
  • STCG property (held ≤ 24 months): 30% + surcharge + 4% cess.
  • Surcharge: 15% if total income > INR 1 crore / 10% if INR 50 lakh - 1 crore / 7% if INR 5-10 crore + specific scenarios.
  • Cess: 4% health + education cess on tax + surcharge.
  • Section 206AA: if NRI lacks Indian PAN - higher rate 20%+ applies.

/ The LDC under Section 197 solution

Lower rate option.

  • NRI applies for Lower Deduction Certificate (LDC) via Form 13 on Income Tax e-filing portal.
  • Discloses: expected sale price + expected gain + expected tax liability + specific DTAA treaty rate if applicable.
  • AO issues LDC with specific lower rate (typically 10-15% on sale consideration = equivalent to actual tax on gain).
  • Timeline: 30-60 days typical for LDC issuance.
  • NRI gives LDC to buyer BEFORE registration.
  • Buyer deducts TDS at LDC rate (lower than the 12.5%/30% default).
  • NRI claims refund of excess only (instead of waiting for a refund of TDS deducted on the full price).
  • Materially better than default + specific liquidity benefit.

/ Common scenarios

What triggers TDS.

  • NRI selling self-owned residential property to Indian buyer: standard scenario - TDS at 12.5% (LTCG) or 30% (STCG) plus surcharge and cess; lower deduction certificate recommended.
  • NRI selling inherited property: cost of acquisition = cost of predecessor + specific indexation; LTCG typically; LDC essential for liquidity.
  • NRI selling property jointly held with Indian resident: TDS applicable only on NRI share - specific sale deed structuring + specific allocation.
  • NRI selling property to another NRI: Section 195 still applies if buyer in India + payment via Indian bank channels.
  • NRI selling commercial property: specific analysis - business income vs capital gain + specific depreciation recapture.
  • Section 54 / 54F reinvestment claim: NRI can plan reinvestment in another Indian residential property (54 for residential sale / 54F for other-asset sale) to avoid LTCG + specific LDC with reinvestment plan reflected.

/ Workflow for NRI selling Indian property

Step-by-step.

  1. Pre-sale planning (3-6 months before closing): valuation + cost base + indexation + specific DTAA + specific reinvestment analysis.
  2. LDC application (Form 13): 30-60 days to AO; file 90+ days before expected closing.
  3. Receive LDC: specific rate + specific supporting documentation.
  4. Share LDC with buyer: BEFORE sale deed execution.
  5. Sale deed execution: buyer deducts TDS at LDC rate (or default if no LDC).
  6. Buyer pays TDS: by 7th of following month + Form 27Q quarterly + Form 16A to NRI.
  7. NRI files ITR (31 July / 31 October): capital gain computation + TDS claim + specific reinvestment claim if applicable.
  8. Refund processing: if excess TDS - typically 2-6 months.
  9. FEMA repatriation (if needed): Form 15CA + 15CB + USD 1M NRO limit (if NRO) or NRE pathway + specific CA certificate.

/ Section 54 / 54F reinvestment strategy

The exemption path.

  • If NRI reinvests sale proceeds in another Indian residential property (Section 54 for residential sale / Section 54F for other-asset sale): LTCG EXEMPT.
  • Section 54 window: 1 year back / 2 years forward purchase / 3 years forward construction.
  • Section 54F window: same timeline but requires full NET CONSIDERATION reinvestment.
  • If investment delayed: Capital Gain Account Scheme (CGAS) deposit by 31 July ITR year.
  • Property must be in INDIA (not foreign).
  • Property not sold within 3 years (clawback).
  • LDC reflecting reinvestment plan: specific lower rate possible (even zero if full reinvestment planned + CGAS deposit confirmed).

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is TDS applicable when NRI sells Indian property?
Yes. The buyer must deduct TDS on the amount paid to an NRI seller (Section 393(2) of the Income-tax Act, 2025 for payments from 1 April 2026; earlier Section 195). Rates: 12.5% + surcharge + cess on long-term gains (held more than 24 months) or 30% + surcharge + cess on short-term gains. Resident individual and HUF buyers can use their PAN through Form 141 from 1 October 2026. NRI can obtain Lower Deduction Certificate (LDC) under Section 197 for reduced rate matching actual tax liability. LDC strongly recommended.
What's the LDC under Section 197?
Lower Deduction Certificate: NRI applies via Form 13 on Income Tax portal disclosing expected sale + gain + tax liability. AO issues LDC with specific lower rate (set to match the actual tax on the gain, instead of the 12.5%/30% default on the price). Timeline: 30-60 days. NRI shares with buyer BEFORE sale deed. Materially better than default + specific liquidity benefit.
Can NRI avoid LTCG via Section 54 reinvestment?
Yes - Section 54 (for residential sale) / 54F (for other-asset sale) reinvestment in another Indian residential property. 1 year back / 2 years forward purchase / 3 years forward construction. If investment delayed: CGAS deposit by 31 July ITR year. Full LTCG exempt if conditions met. Property must be in India + 3-year lock-in.
