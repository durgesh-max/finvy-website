/ Inheritance · UK, US, Canada, Australia · Updated 2026-11-07

Inheriting Money or Property From India While Living in the UK, USA, Canada or Australia (2026): Tax, Reporting and Transfer, what India and your country each need.

India has no inheritance or estate tax, and receiving an inheritance is not income for the heir. The UK, Canada and Australia do not tax the heir on receiving it either, and the US does not tax it, though a US person must report an inheritance from a non-US person above USD 100,000 on Form 3520. What is taxed is what the inheritance earns or gains afterwards. The money itself lands in an NRO account and can be sent abroad within USD 1 million a year with documents proving the inheritance, Form 15CA and a CA's Form 15CB.

/ Step by step

From the death certificate to your foreign bank.

  1. Get the death certificate and find the will, nominations and joint holdings.
  2. Get probate, a succession certificate or a legal heir certificate, depending on the asset and the city.
  3. Open an NRO account (and NRO demat if there are shares).
  4. Transmit deposits, shares and funds; mutate the property records.
  5. Sell what you do not want to keep (with a lower deduction certificate for property).
  6. Get Form 15CB, file Form 15CA and repatriate within USD 1 million per financial year.
  7. Report it where you live: Form 3520 in the US if over USD 100,000; T1135 in Canada if you keep assets above CAD 100,000.

Sources: Income-tax Act, 2025 (inheritances not income); FEMA (Acquisition and Transfer of Immovable Property in India) Regulations and RBI Master Direction on remittance facilities for NRIs; HMRC IHTM47020 (long-term UK residence test); IRS Form 3520 instructions; CRA Form T1135; ATO guidance on inherited assets. FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is there inheritance tax in India?
No. India has no inheritance or estate tax, and an inheritance is not taxable income for the heir. Income from it later (interest, rent, capital gains) is taxable.
Do I pay tax in the UK, US, Canada or Australia on money inherited from India?
Not on receiving it. The US requires Form 3520 if inheritances from non-US persons exceed USD 100,000 in a year. UK inheritance tax applies to the deceased's estate and reaches foreign assets only if they were a long-term UK resident.
How much inherited money can I take out of India?
Up to USD 1 million per financial year from your NRO account, with documents proving the inheritance, Form 15CA and a Chartered Accountant's Form 15CB.
Can an NRI inherit agricultural land in India?
Yes, NRIs and OCIs can inherit any immovable property, including agricultural land, farmhouses and plantations. Agricultural land can only be sold to a resident Indian citizen.
Do I need to report inherited Indian bank accounts abroad?
In the US, yes: FBAR above USD 10,000 combined and Form 8938 above its threshold. In Canada, T1135 if the total cost of foreign property exceeds CAD 100,000.

Yes: transmission paperwork with your Indian lawyer, Indian returns, Form 15CB and repatriation.
