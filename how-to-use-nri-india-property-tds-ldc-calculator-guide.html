/ What the calculator does

The computation.

  • Inputs: property purchase price + date, sale price + date, improvement cost, indexation choice (12.5% vs 20% with indexation post Finance Act 2024), Section 54 reinvestment plan.
  • Computes: indexed cost of acquisition + indexed cost of improvement + capital gain under both methods (12.5% without indexation vs 20% with indexation) + tax under each method + optimal choice.
  • Section 54 reinvestment benefit: if reinvesting in residential property, exemption applied.
  • LDC rate recommendation: effective tax rate as % of gross sale consideration - this is what NRI should request in Form 13 under Section 197.
  • Comparison: 20% default TDS (INR locked up) vs LDC rate (minimum needed).

/ Step-by-step usage

The workflow.

  1. Open bharatquantumprospera.com/nri-property-tds-ldc-calculator.html.
  2. Enter purchase price + date (year of acquisition).
  3. Enter sale price + sale date (planned).
  4. Enter improvement cost (any capex on property - renovation, etc.).
  5. Select indexation choice (pre-23-July-2024 acquisition offers choice).
  6. Enter planned reinvestment under Section 54 (if applicable).
  7. Calculator shows: capital gain, tax, LDC rate recommendation.
  8. Download / share results for buyer coordination.

/ Common calculator usage mistakes

Where NRIs trip.

  • Wrong indexation choice: Finance Act 2024 offers 12.5% without indexation OR 20% with indexation (whichever LOWER) for pre-23-July-2024 acquisitions. Post that date: only 12.5% without indexation. Choose correctly.
  • Missing improvement cost: renovation / addition / structural upgrade specifically added to acquisition cost per Section 55.
  • Section 54 reinvestment amount vs timing: must be residential property + within 2/3 year window.
  • Agricultural land + commercial - calculator is for residential property. For commercial / land / specific - use different framework.

/ Post-calculator workflow

What NRI does next.

  1. Review calculated LDC rate (typically 2-5% of sale consideration vs 20% default).
  2. File Form 13 on TRACES portal requesting LDC at calculated rate.
  3. Form 13 to Jurisdictional AO - 4-8 week processing.
  4. AO issues LDC certificate with specific TDS rate.
  5. Share LDC with buyer - buyer deducts specified rate (not 20% default).
  6. Buyer files Form 27Q quarterly reflecting LDC rate.
  7. NRI files Indian ITR with final tax computation + claims any residual refund.
  8. FEMA Form 15CA + 15CB for repatriation up to USD 1M/year.

What's the typical LDC rate the AO approves?
Varies: 2-5% of sale consideration for property with modest LTCG. 0.1-1% if Section 54 reinvestment planned (near-zero actual tax). Close to 12.5% if large gain with no reinvestment. AO's approval generally matches calculator-recommended rate if calculations are properly documented.
How long does LDC application + approval take?
4-8 weeks typical. Form 13 submission on TRACES + Jurisdictional AO review + LDC certificate issuance. Can be faster (2-4 weeks) if application well-documented. Plan property sale timing accordingly - LDC should be in hand before buyer deducts TDS.
Can I claim LDC after sale is done?
No - LDC must be in place BEFORE buyer makes payment / deducts TDS. If 20% default TDS already deducted on gross consideration: only remedy is refund via Indian ITR filing (6-18 month wait). Plan LDC application 2-4 months before planned sale date.
