How to Change NRI Status in Mutual Funds and Demat (2026): Resident to NRI When You Move Abroad, NRI to Resident When You Return, KYC, bank mandate, FATCA and demat steps, in the right order.

When you become an NRI you do not have to sell your mutual funds or shares, but you must update your status. First get your bank to redesignate your savings account as NRO, or open an NRE account. Then change your tax status to NRI with the registrars (CAMS and KFintech) or through MFCentral, update your FATCA/CRS declaration and overseas address, and switch the bank mandate to the NRO or NRE account. Your SIPs can continue from the new account. For shares, the demat and trading account must become an NRI account. Many brokers require a new NRI (NRO, non-PIS) demat: you transfer the shares into it and close the resident account. Moving shares between your own accounts is not a sale. Returning to India reverses the steps: resident status, a resident bank mandate and a resident demat. Some fund houses limit investors resident in the US and Canada, so check before investing more.

/ Points that trip people up

Before you submit.

  • Order matters: change the bank account first; a folio linked to a resident account after you become an NRI is a common reason for a status change being put on hold.
  • Online vs form: some online KYC modification routes work only for residents; NRIs may need the registrar’s change-of-status form with overseas address proof.
  • Repatriation: units bought with resident money are non-repatriable; new investments from NRE are repatriable.
  • US and Canada residents: several fund houses do not accept new investments from them; US persons also face PFIC rules on Indian funds; see PFIC explained.
  • Tax on redemptions: fund houses deduct TDS from NRI capital gains; file an Indian return to claim any excess; see NRI mutual fund tax.
  • Digital KYC from abroad: see NRI demat and digital KYC.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Do I have to sell my mutual funds when I become an NRI?
No. You update your tax status, FATCA details, address and bank mandate with the fund house or registrar; the units continue.
How do I change my status to NRI in mutual funds?
Redesignate your bank account as NRO (or open NRE), then submit a change of tax status to CAMS or KFintech or through MFCentral, with FATCA/CRS, overseas address proof and the new bank mandate.
How do I update NRI status in my demat account?
The demat and trading account must become an NRI account. Many brokers ask you to open an NRI (NRO non-PIS) demat, transfer your shares into it and close the resident account; some depository participants can change the status directly.
How do I change NRI status back to resident in mutual funds?
Convert your NRE/NRO account to a resident account, then submit a change of tax status to resident with the registrar or MFCentral, update FATCA and switch the bank mandate.
Can NRIs in the US or Canada continue SIPs?
Existing investments usually continue, but several fund houses do not accept new investments from US or Canada residents; check each fund house.
