India-UAE DTAA for NRIs in Dubai and the UAE (2026): Tax on NRO Interest, Mutual Funds, Shares, Rent and Property, and the UAE Tax Residency Certificate, the treaty cuts tax on interest and dividends; property and rent stay taxable in India.

For an NRI living in the UAE, the India-UAE tax treaty mainly helps on interest and dividends: Indian tax on NRO and other interest is capped at 12.5% (against 30% plus surcharge and cess under Indian law) and on dividends at 10% (against 20%). Rent and gains on Indian property stay fully taxable in India, and gains on shares of Indian companies remain taxable in India at Indian rates. Gains on Indian mutual fund units have been held not taxable in India for UAE residents by tax tribunals (Delhi ITAT, October 2024), because units are not shares. To use the treaty you need a UAE tax residency certificate, Form 41 (formerly 10F) and your PAN; the treaty treats you as UAE resident if you were in the UAE for at least 183 days in the calendar year. Because the UAE has no personal income tax on salaries, whatever India collects is the final cost: there is no foreign tax credit to offset it.

/ Residence

Who counts as a UAE resident.

  • Treaty test (Article 4): an individual is a UAE resident for the treaty if present in the UAE for at least 183 days in the calendar year concerned. Note that India's tax year runs April to March, so check both periods.
  • UAE tax residency certificate: issued online by the Federal Tax Authority under Cabinet Decision No. 85 of 2022 (in force from 1 March 2023), on a 183-day presence test or a 90-day test with additional conditions; a tenancy contract or title deed and an entry-exit report are usually needed.
  • India comes first: you must also be non-resident (or RNOR) under Indian law: under 182 days in India, and watch the 120-day rule if your Indian income exceeds INR 15 lakh. Indian citizens with Indian income above INR 15 lakh who are not liable to tax in any country can be deemed resident; see deemed residence for UAE-based Indians and the residential status calculator.

/ How to claim it

Paperwork that stops the over-deduction.

  • Get your UAE tax residency certificate for the relevant year.
  • File Form 41 (formerly Form 10F) electronically on the Indian income-tax portal; a PAN-less route exists, see Form 10F without PAN.
  • Give your bank the TRC, Form 41, a no-permanent-establishment declaration and a treaty declaration so NRO interest is taxed at 12.5%; give the same to fund houses and the company registrar for mutual funds and dividends.
  • If tax was already deducted at the higher rate, file your Indian return (ITR-2) and claim the refund; keep the TRC and day-count records for the assessment.
  • Selling property? Apply for a lower deduction certificate (Form 128, formerly 13) before the sale so TDS is based on the gain, not the sale price.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Does the India-UAE DTAA reduce tax on NRO interest?
Yes. Indian tax on interest paid to a UAE resident is capped at 12.5% (5% for banks), against 30% plus surcharge and cess under Indian law. Give your bank a UAE tax residency certificate, Form 41 (formerly 10F) and a treaty declaration.
Is money sent from Dubai to India taxed?
No. Sending your own UAE salary or savings to India is not income, so there is no Indian tax on the transfer itself and no TCS (TCS applies to money going out of India). Credit it to an NRE account to keep it freely repatriable; interest on the NRE account is tax-free for NRIs. Money sent to a parent or spouse is a gift from a relative, also not taxed in India. Banks and exchange houses charge their own fees and may apply GST on the currency conversion service.
Do I have to show money transferred from the UAE in my Indian tax return?
Not as income, if you are a non-resident. You file an Indian return only for Indian income (rent, NRO interest, capital gains) above the exemption or to claim a refund. If you become resident in India, foreign assets and bank accounts go in Schedule FA.
Are Indian mutual fund gains taxable for NRIs living in the UAE?
Tax tribunals (Delhi ITAT in October 2024, Cochin ITAT in 2019) have held that mutual fund units are not shares, so gains of a UAE resident are taxable only in the UAE under Article 13(5) of the treaty, which means no tax in practice. Fund houses usually still deduct tax, so the refund is claimed in the Indian return. The tax department can contest the position.
Is rent or a property sale in India taxable for a Dubai NRI?
Yes. Income and gains from Indian immovable property are taxable in India under the treaty. Long-term gains on property are taxed at 12.5%, and a lower deduction certificate can reduce the TDS on the sale to the actual tax.
Are gains on Indian shares taxable for UAE residents?
Yes. Gains on shares of Indian companies may be taxed in India under Article 13(4), at Indian rates: 20% short-term and 12.5% long-term above INR 1.25 lakh a year for listed equity.
How many days must I spend in the UAE to use the treaty?
The treaty treats an individual as UAE resident if present in the UAE for at least 183 days in the calendar year. The UAE tax residency certificate is issued on the UAE's own tests (183 days, or 90 days with conditions). You must also be non-resident or RNOR under Indian law.
Can I claim foreign tax credit in the UAE for Indian tax?
No. The UAE does not levy personal income tax on individuals' salaries or investment income, so tax paid in India is a final cost. That is why reducing Indian withholding through the treaty matters.
