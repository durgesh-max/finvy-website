How to Receive Payment From Foreign Clients in India (2026): Bank or Platform, Purpose Codes, FIRA/FIRC, GST and Tax, get paid properly once, and GST, FEMA and tax line up.

Freelancers, agencies and startups in India can receive foreign client payments by SWIFT wire into a bank account or through a payment platform that pays out to an Indian bank; either way the money must arrive as an export receipt with the right RBI purpose code. Common codes are P0802 (software implementation or consultancy), P0807 (off-site software exports) and P1006 (business and management consultancy); your bank confirms the code. Keep the FIRA (foreign inward remittance advice) or FIRC for each payment: it proves the export for GST and the bank. Under the 2026 FEMA export rules, service exports are declared in an EDF, which is deemed filed for individuals and for companies up to INR 10 lakh per service, and money should come in within nine months. No GST is charged on exports under an LUT, but the income is taxable.

/ Quick answers

What people starting to bill abroad ask.

  • How do I receive payment from a foreign client in India? Give the client your bank’s SWIFT details and account, or use a payment platform that collects in foreign currency and pays out to your Indian account. Tell the bank or platform the purpose of the payment.
  • Which purpose code for export of services? It depends on the service: P0802 for software implementation or consultancy, P0807 for off-site software exports, P1006 for business and management consultancy, P1005 for accounting and tax services, P1009 for engineering and technical services. Confirm with your bank.
  • What is FIRC or FIRA? The bank’s record that foreign currency came in for a stated purpose: FIRA is the per-transaction advice; a FIRC is the certificate some banks issue on request. Platforms issue their own equivalent. Keep them for GST and audits.
  • Do I pay GST on foreign client payments? No GST on a valid export of services: register (if above the threshold) and file an LUT to invoice without GST. Below INR 20 lakh a year you need not register.
  • Is the income taxable? Yes, as business or professional income in your Indian return; freelancers may use presumptive taxation where eligible.

/ Purpose codes

Common RBI codes for service receipts.

CodeUse it for
P0802Software implementation or consultancy (other than work declared as software exports)
P0807Off-site software exports: development, SaaS, maintenance delivered remotely
P0803Database and data processing charges
P1005Accounting, auditing, bookkeeping and tax consulting
P1006Business and management consultancy and public relations
P1007Advertising and market research
P1008Research and development services
P1009Architectural, engineering and other technical services

The wrong code can make the bank treat a payment as something else (a gift or a loan), which causes trouble with GST refunds and tax. Ask the bank to correct it promptly if the code is wrong.

/ Bank or platform

Choosing how to get paid.

  • Bank wire (SWIFT): best for larger invoices and companies; the bank issues FIRA and applies the purpose code; watch correspondent bank charges.
  • Payment platforms: convenient for freelancers and card payments from clients; they convert and pay out to your Indian account and issue an inward remittance advice. Money held in a platform balance abroad can be a foreign asset to report in Schedule FA; see platform balances and Schedule FA.
  • Invoice in foreign currency with the client’s name, country and the service; for GST, include the export endorsement under LUT; see GST on export of services.
  • FEMA: realise payment within nine months; the EDF is deemed filed for individuals and small receipts; see the EDF relief.
  • US clients usually ask for Form W-8BEN; see US company paying an Indian contractor.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How do I receive payment from international clients in India?
Through a SWIFT wire into your Indian bank account or a payment platform that collects in foreign currency and pays out to your Indian account. Make sure the payment is tagged with the correct RBI purpose code and keep the FIRA for each payment.
What is the purpose code for export of services?
It depends on the service: P0802 for software implementation or consultancy, P0807 for off-site software exports, P1006 for business and management consultancy, P1005 for accounting and tax services and P1009 for engineering and technical services. Your bank confirms the code.
What is the purpose code for software export?
P0807 is used for off-site software exports such as remotely delivered development and SaaS; P0802 is used for software implementation or consultancy. Ask your bank which applies to your invoice.
Do freelancers need a FIRC?
Freelancers should keep the bank's FIRA (foreign inward remittance advice) or a FIRC for each foreign payment. It proves the export for GST, supports zero-rating and is useful for income tax and audits.
Is GST payable on payments from foreign clients?
Not on a valid export of services. Below INR 20 lakh of turnover a year you need not register; above it, register and file an LUT to invoice without GST.
