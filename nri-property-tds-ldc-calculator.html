How this works.

Default TDS under section 393 (old 195): buyer withholds 12.5% + surcharge + 4% cess on the sale consideration (not the gain). Effective rate: 13% to 14.95% depending on the sale value (surcharge on long-term gains is capped at 15%). For a INR 2 crore sale, TDS can be about INR 29.9 lakh, and any excess over the actual tax comes back only as a refund after the return is filed and processed.

Lower TDS certificate (Form 128, old Form 13, under section 395, old 197): applied for online on TRACES well before the sale is registered. The Assessing Officer issues a certificate with a lower rate based on the expected tax on the actual gain, and the buyer deducts at that rate. How much it saves depends on your purchase cost and gain; the calculator above shows the gap on your numbers.

FAQ

NRI property sale TDS, answered.

Which section applies to TDS when an NRI sells property in India?
Section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (old section 195 of the 1961 Act), which covers payments to non-residents. The 1% rule for buying from a resident seller does not apply. The buyer deducts tax from the full sale price, deposits it, files the TDS return and gives the seller a TDS certificate in Form 131 (old Form 16A). To reduce the deduction, the NRI applies before the sale for a lower or nil TDS certificate in Form 128 (old Form 13) under section 395 (old section 197).
What surcharge applies to TDS on an NRI property sale?
For a long-term sale (property held more than 24 months) the base rate is 12.5% without indexation. Surcharge is nil up to Rs 50 lakh, 10% above Rs 50 lakh and up to Rs 1 crore, and 15% above Rs 1 crore (surcharge on long-term gains is capped at 15%), and 4% cess is added on top. That gives about 13%, 14.3% or 14.95% of the amount paid, and buyers usually apply the band by the sale price. For a short-term sale the base rate is 30% plus surcharge and cess, so the deduction is higher. Either way it is worked out on the price, not the gain.
Why is the TDS more than the tax I actually owe?
Because the buyer deducts on the full sale price, while tax is due only on the gain (sale price minus purchase cost and improvement cost). If the gain is small, or you reinvest under section 82 or 85 (old 54 or 54EC), most of the TDS is excess and comes back only as a refund after you file your Indian return. A Form 128 lower TDS certificate obtained before the sale avoids that.
Is the calculator result exact?
No. It is an estimate for planning. The actual TDS depends on the buyer's reading of the surcharge band, the holding period, and any certificate issued; the actual tax depends on your cost, improvement bills, exemptions and residency. Have a CA check your numbers before the sale agreement fixes the payment schedule.
What should I send to check the TDS on my property sale?
WhatsApp us the sale agreement or draft, the purchase deed with the purchase cost and date, bills for any improvement, your PAN and your country of residence.
