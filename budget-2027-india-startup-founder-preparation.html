Union Budget 2027 (1 February 2027): What NRIs, Founders and Foreign Companies Should Do Before It - Where the Law Stands Today, no forecasts: the current rules and a sensible checklist.

The Union Budget for FY 2027-28 is expected on 1 February 2027. Nobody outside the government knows what it will contain, and this page does not guess. What helps is knowing where the law stands today, so that you can judge any change on the day, and finishing the things that have fixed deadlines before then. The main baselines are these. The Income-tax Act, 2025 has applied since 1 April 2026. Companies pay 25.17%. Long-term capital gains are taxed at 12.5%. Buybacks are taxed as capital gains. Remittances above INR 10 lakh carry TCS of up to 20%. India has not enacted the global minimum tax. Changes announced in a Budget usually apply from 1 April, but some apply from Budget day or midnight, so if you already plan a sale, buyback or large remittance, decide the timing deliberately. Do not rush a transaction on a rumour.

/ What to do before 1 February 2027

A checklist that holds whatever the Budget says.

  • Close the fixed deadlines first: belated or revised return, FAST-DS declaration and GSTR-9 by 31 December 2026; see the deadline desk.
  • Property or share sales already planned: work out today’s tax, apply early for a lower deduction certificate if you are an NRI, and choose the completion date knowingly.
  • Buybacks, dividends and restructurings: board approvals, valuations and filings take weeks; start now if you want the choice of completing before the Budget.
  • Large remittances: use the right purpose code and remember TCS is creditable; see TCS on foreign remittances.
  • Foreign companies: keep transfer pricing, FEMA filings and payroll current, so a Budget change is the only new item on your list; see the compliance calendar.
  • On Budget day: check the effective date of each change before acting. We will publish what changed for NRIs, founders and foreign companies.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

When is Union Budget 2027?
By convention the Union Budget is presented on 1 February, so the Budget for FY 2027-28 is expected on 1 February 2027. The government announces the date.
What will Budget 2027 change for NRIs and startups?
Nobody outside the government knows before Budget day. It is more useful to know the current rules, such as the 12.5% long-term capital gains rate, TCS on remittances and the residence tests, and to judge any change against them.
Should I sell property or shares before the Budget?
Only if the sale makes sense anyway. Budget changes usually apply from 1 April, but some apply from Budget day, so decide the timing deliberately and do not rush on rumours.
Has India replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961?
Yes. The Income-tax Act, 2025 has applied since 1 April 2026, with new section and form numbers.
Is the global minimum tax in force in India?
No. India has not enacted Pillar Two. A domestic top-up tax could be introduced in a future Budget.
