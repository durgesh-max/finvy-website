LLP Registration in India (2026): Online Process on MCA, Government Fees, Documents, Designated Partners and NRI or Foreign Partners, one integrated form, then the LLP agreement within 30 days.

A limited liability partnership (LLP) is registered online with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs on the MCA V3 portal through a single form, FiLLiP, after each designated partner has a digital signature certificate. You need at least two designated partners, one of them resident in India, a registered office in India, and an LLP agreement filed in Form 3 within 30 days of incorporation. NRIs and foreign nationals can be partners, and foreign investment in an LLP is allowed under the automatic route in sectors where 100% FDI is automatic without performance conditions. After registration come the yearly filings: Form 11, Form 8 and the income tax return, with an audit once turnover or contribution crosses the limits.

/ Quick answers

What people registering an LLP ask.

  • How do I register an LLP online? Get DSCs for the designated partners, reserve the name through RUN-LLP or within FiLLiP, file FiLLiP on the MCA V3 portal with the documents, receive the certificate of incorporation with the LLPIN, then file the LLP agreement in Form 3 within 30 days.
  • How many partners are needed? At least two partners, and at least two designated partners who are individuals, with one designated partner resident in India (in India for at least 120 days in the financial year).
  • Can an NRI or foreigner be a partner? Yes. An NRI or foreign national can be a partner or designated partner; the LLP still needs one resident designated partner, and foreign contribution must follow FEMA rules for LLPs.
  • How long does it take? Usually one to three weeks once DSCs and documents are ready, depending on name approval and any resubmission the registrar asks for.
  • LLP or private limited company? An LLP is cheaper to run and has no dividend tax layer, but investors and ESOPs need a company; see LLP vs private limited.

/ After registration

The compliance an LLP must keep up.

  • Form 11 (annual return) by 30 May each year, listing partners and contributions.
  • Form 8 (statement of accounts and solvency) by 30 October; see Form 8 due date and late fee.
  • Income tax return: an LLP is taxed at 30% plus surcharge and cess, and partners’ share of profit is exempt in their hands; alternate minimum tax can apply.
  • Audit once turnover exceeds INR 40 lakh or contribution exceeds INR 25 lakh, plus a tax audit where income tax limits are crossed.
  • Changes in partners, office or agreement are filed with the registrar within the set time.
  • Later conversion to a company is possible; see converting an LLP to a company.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How do I register an LLP in India online?
Get a digital signature certificate for each designated partner, reserve the name through RUN-LLP or inside the FiLLiP form, file FiLLiP on the MCA V3 portal with partner and office documents, receive the certificate of incorporation with the LLPIN, and file the LLP agreement in Form 3 within 30 days of incorporation.
What documents are needed for LLP registration?
For each partner: PAN (or passport for a foreign national), address proof and a photograph, with foreign documents notarised or apostilled; for the registered office: a recent utility bill and the owner's no-objection or a rent agreement; plus DSCs for the designated partners, their consent and the subscription details.
What are the government fees for LLP registration?
MCA fees depend on the total contribution. The FiLLiP incorporation form is generally Rs 500 for contribution up to Rs 1 lakh, Rs 2,000 for Rs 1 lakh to Rs 5 lakh, Rs 4,000 for Rs 5 lakh to Rs 10 lakh and Rs 5,000 above Rs 10 lakh; name reservation through RUN-LLP is Rs 200; the LLP agreement (Form 3) has a separate small fee, and stamp duty on the agreement varies by state. Digital signatures for partners cost extra. Check the current MCA fee schedule when filing.
What is the full form of LLP?
Limited Liability Partnership: a separate legal entity registered under the LLP Act, 2008, in which partners have limited liability, at least two designated partners are needed and one must be resident in India.
How many partners does an LLP need, and must one be resident in India?
At least two partners and at least two designated partners who are individuals, with at least one designated partner resident in India, meaning in India for at least 120 days in the financial year.
Can an NRI or foreign national be a partner in an Indian LLP?
Yes. NRIs and foreign nationals can be partners or designated partners, as long as one designated partner is resident in India. Foreign contribution is allowed under the automatic route in sectors where 100% FDI is automatic without performance conditions, and must be reported under FEMA.
What compliance does an LLP have after registration?
Form 11 annual return by 30 May, Form 8 statement of accounts by 30 October, the income tax return, an audit once turnover exceeds INR 40 lakh or contribution exceeds INR 25 lakh, and filings for any change in partners, office or the LLP agreement.
