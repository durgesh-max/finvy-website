/ PAN Status Fix · Updated 2026-10-23

How to Fix PAN-Aadhaar Link + NRI/OCI Status Update India, deactivated PAN remediation.

NRI/OCI often finds their PAN marked 'inoperative' due to PAN-Aadhaar linking issue - despite NRIs being EXEMPT per CBDT Notification 37/2017. Common cause: PAN database shows holder as 'resident' from original Form 49A issuance when they were in India. Fix: update PAN status to NRI via Form 49AA + supporting documents. Here's the complete step-by-step remediation framework.

/ The exemption + the trap

Why it happens.

  • CBDT Notification 37/2017: NRIs EXEMPT from mandatory PAN-Aadhaar linking.
  • But PAN database must correctly reflect NRI status for exemption to apply.
  • Common scenario: person obtained PAN when resident (say 2010) + moved abroad post 2015 + PAN database still shows them as 'resident'.
  • Income Tax applies 'resident' status to PAN for Section 139AA linking requirement.
  • Linking deadline passed → PAN marked 'inoperative'.
  • NRI discovers when: Section 195 TDS at 20% (PAN-absent rate), bank KYC refresh fails, mutual fund KYC fails, Form 15CB signing fails, Indian ITR cannot be e-filed.

/ Form 49AA fix workflow

Step-by-step.

  1. Document preparation:
  2.   — Proof of NRI status: foreign passport + visa / green card / OCI card.
  3.   — Foreign address proof: utility bill / bank statement / lease agreement.
  4.   — Existing PAN card copy.
  5.   — Passport-size photo.
  6. Online filing: NSDL (now Protean) e-governance portal: tin-nsdl.com → PAN services → Changes or Correction → select 'Non-resident' category.
  7. Fill Form 49AA online with specific NRI details.
  8. Upload documents + pay fee (~INR 100-200 + courier).
  9. Submit for processing.
  10. If PAN already inoperative: filing Form 49AA simultaneously + requesting restoration. May require Jurisdictional AO contact.
  11. Processing: 15-30 days.
  12. Updated PAN: database status changes to 'Non-Resident'. Section 139AA exemption applies. PAN reactivated.

/ Post-fix verification

Ongoing readiness.

  • Verify PAN status on Indian income tax e-filing portal: shows 'Non-Resident' flag.
  • Confirm Section 195 TDS at treaty rate (not 20% default) with Indian payers.
  • Bank KYC refresh with updated NRI status.
  • Mutual fund + demat KYC with NRI status.
  • Form 10F electronic filing enabled (login works).
  • Form 15CB signing by Indian payer's CA succeeds.
  • Indian ITR e-filing works.

/ What if PAN remains stuck

Escalation.

  • Jurisdictional AO visit (if PAN jurisdiction in India + specific escalation).
  • CBDT grievance redressal portal + specific escalation path.
  • Alternative: fresh PAN via Form 49AA (not update) if specific fix not working - but then two PANs linked to same person (regularisation issue).
  • BQP handles complex NRI PAN status issues including specific jurisdictional problems.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Why is my (NRI) PAN inoperative despite being exempt?
PAN database still shows you as 'resident' from original Form 49A issuance. Section 139AA applies based on database status. CBDT Notification 37/2017 exemption needs correct NRI flag in database. Fix: Form 49AA update to NRI status.
How long does PAN NRI status update take?
15-30 days typical processing. Faster if application well-documented. Can be expedited via Jurisdictional AO contact in urgent cases. BQP coordinates for faster processing in urgent NRI situations.
What happens if PAN remains inoperative?
Section 195 TDS at 20% default on all India-source income (vs 10-15% treaty rate). Indian ITR cannot be e-filed. Form 10F electronic filing blocked. Bank/MF/demat KYC refresh fails. Form 15CB signing blocked. Materially blocks most financial operations - fix urgently.
Can I use alternate PAN while updating?
No - two PANs linked to same person creates regularisation issue. Must update existing PAN to NRI status. Cannot have multiple active PANs.
