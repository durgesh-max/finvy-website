/ Scope

What a BQP diligence covers.

  • Financial: quality of earnings, normalised EBITDA, net debt and debt-like items, working capital trends, revenue recognition and related-party transactions.
  • Direct tax: open assessments, pending notices and litigation, TDS defaults, transfer pricing exposure, and carry-forward losses that may lapse on a change in shareholding.
  • GST: return reconciliation, input tax credit eligibility, pending notices and export refund positions.
  • FEMA: FC-GPR and FC-TRS filings, pricing compliance, downstream investment, Press Note 3 (eased for non-controlling stakes within 10% by Press Note 2 (2026)) beneficial-ownership checks, and whether past breaches need compounding before closing.
  • Companies Act: share capital history, ROC filings, charges, director disqualification, and demat status of shares under Rule 9B.
  • Labour: provident fund and ESI compliance.

/ Deliverables

What you receive.

  • Red-flag report early in the engagement so deal teams can decide whether to proceed.
  • Full report quantifying exposures, with recommended price adjustments, specific indemnities, escrows and conditions precedent for the share purchase agreement.
  • Completion support: closing accounts, FEMA filings for the acquisition and post-deal integration of compliance.

/ Typical timeline

Planning the deal calendar.

A focused red-flag review on a small or mid-sized Indian target typically takes two to three weeks from data-room access; a full-scope diligence takes four to six weeks. Timelines depend on data-room quality and the number of entities.

What are the most common red flags in Indian targets?
Unreported or late FEMA filings for foreign shareholders, TDS defaults, GST input credit mismatches, open tax litigation, related-party transactions not at arm's length, and shares not yet dematerialised.
Can FEMA breaches be fixed before closing?
Usually, through late filing with fees or compounding with the RBI; the timing should be built into the conditions precedent.
Does BQP work alongside the buyer's lawyers?
Yes. Legal diligence stays with counsel; BQP covers financial, tax and regulatory areas and feeds findings into the SPA.
