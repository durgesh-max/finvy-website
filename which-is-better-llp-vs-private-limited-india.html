Which Is Better LLP vs Private Limited Company India, the direct comparison + decision framework.

Choose LLP for professional services (CA / lawyer / consultant partnerships), low-regulation ventures, small-business + family businesses with no equity fundraising intent. Specific decision factors: fundraising, tax regime, compliance load, foreign ownership, exit flexibility. Here's the complete framework.

/ Legal framework comparison

The entity types.

  • Private Limited Company: Companies Act 2013 + Companies (Incorporation) Rules + MCA regulations. Separate legal entity + limited liability + perpetual succession + specific board governance.
  • LLP: Limited Liability Partnership Act 2008 + LLP Rules. Separate legal entity + limited liability for partners + partnership-style flexibility + no board requirement.
  • Both: separate legal entity + limited liability for owners.
  • Different: internal governance + fundraising capacity + tax treatment + compliance load.

/ Fundraising capacity

The decisive factor.

  • Private Limited:
  •   — Can issue shares to investors (equity).
  •   — Can issue preference shares + CCPS + specific equity-linked instruments.
  •   — Can issue CCDs + OCDs + specific debt instruments.
  •   — Can grant ESOPs under SEBI SBEB.
  •   — Can accept foreign direct investment (FDI) + FEMA FC-GPR.
  •   — Standard VC/PE investment vehicle.
  •   — Can list on stock exchange (SME / main board) post conversion to Public Ltd.
  • LLP:
  •   — Capital contribution by partners only.
  •   — No shares / no equity instruments.
  •   — No ESOP mechanism.
  •   — FEMA FDI allowed since 2015 - but LLP cannot have more than 100 partners.
  •   — VC / PE generally does NOT invest in LLPs (prefer equity-shareable Private Ltd).
  •   — Cannot list on stock exchange.

/ Tax regime comparison

Corporate vs partnership tax.

  • Private Limited:
  •   — Base corporate tax: 25% (if turnover < INR 400 crore) OR 30%.
  •   — Section 115BAA 22% (optional, with specific conditions).
  •   — Section 115BAB 15% (optional, new manufacturing + specific conditions + specific date limits).
  •   — Surcharge + 4% cess on top.
  •   — MAT 15% / AMT if specific scenarios + 115BAA/BAB opt-in exempt MAT.
  •   — Dividend to shareholders: taxed in shareholder's hands at specific rates (115BBDA abolished post FY 2020-21).
  •   — Buyback: Section 115QA 20% buyback tax on company.
  • LLP:
  •   — Flat tax rate: 30% + surcharge + 4% cess.
  •   — No MAT equivalent (but AMT 18.5% applicable if specific thresholds).
  •   — Section 115BBD-equivalent for LLP: dividend / specific receipts specific.
  •   — Partner's share of profit: EXEMPT in partner's hands (Section 10(2A)).
  •   — Partner's remuneration + interest: taxable in partner's hands + deductible in LLP (per Section 40(b) limits).

/ Compliance load

Annual + statutory.

  • Private Limited:
  •   — Annual: AOC-4 (financial statements) + MGT-7 (annual return) + DPT-3 (deposits) + MSME-1 (if applicable).
  •   — Director: DIR-3 KYC annual + specific board meetings (minimum 4 per FY).
  •   — AGM: annual general meeting mandatory.
  •   — Audit: statutory audit mandatory regardless of turnover.
  •   — Specific registers: 15+ statutory registers maintained.
  •   — Rule 9B demat: mandatory for most Private Limited (except small).
  • LLP:
  •   — Annual: Form 11 (annual return) + Form 8 (statement of accounts).
  •   — Audit: ONLY if turnover > INR 40 lakh OR contribution > INR 25 lakh.
  •   — No specific AGM requirement.
  •   — Fewer statutory registers.
  •   — No Rule 9B demat requirement.
  •   — Partner: Designated Partner KYC (DPIN).
  •   — Materially lower compliance load than Private Limited.

/ Decision framework

Which to choose.

  • Choose Private Limited if:
  •   — VC / PE / angel fundraising planned.
  •   — ESOP scheme for employees planned.
  •   — Foreign subsidiary / foreign direct investment expected.
  •   — Future IPO / listing on SME / main board.
  •   — Multi-sector growth + specific equity-partner addition.
  •   — Section 115BAA 22% opt-in tax optimisation.
  •   — Section 115BAB 15% new manufacturing opt-in.
  • Choose LLP if:
  •   — Professional services firm (CA / lawyer / consultant partnership).
  •   — Family business + no equity fundraising intent.
  •   — Small-business + low compliance tolerance.
  •   — Specific real-estate / specific sector-specific advantage.
  •   — Partner-level tax efficiency (Section 10(2A) exempt share of profit).
  •   — No ESOP + no VC + no listing intention.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Which is better LLP vs Private Limited in India?
Private Limited for VC-backed startups, equity fundraising, foreign subsidiaries, ESOP schemes, future IPO. LLP for professional services firms (CA/lawyer/consultant partnerships), family businesses, small-business + low compliance intent. Decision depends on: fundraising, tax regime, compliance load, foreign ownership, exit flexibility.
Can LLP receive VC/PE investment?
LLPs can accept FDI since 2015 but VC/PE generally AVOIDS LLP structure because: (a) no shares - only capital contribution, (b) no CCPS / CCD / preferred equity instruments, (c) no ESOP mechanism, (d) cannot list on stock exchange, (e) 100-partner max limit. VC/PE strongly prefers Private Limited for standard equity-based investment.
What's the tax difference LLP vs Private Limited?
Private Limited: 25%/30% base (or Section 115BAA 22% / Section 115BAB 15% opt-in) + MAT 15% + dividend taxable in shareholders' hands. LLP: 30% flat + AMT 18.5% if applicable + Section 10(2A) exempts partner's share of profit + Section 40(b) regulates partner remuneration deduction.
Can LLP convert to Private Limited later?
Yes - LLP to Private Limited conversion possible under specific framework (specific NCLT + Section 366 Companies Act or specific LLP Act). Specific tax implications + specific capital gain. Alternatively: new Private Limited incorporated with specific business transfer + specific valuation + specific partnership. BQP advises optimal path based on specific scenarios.
