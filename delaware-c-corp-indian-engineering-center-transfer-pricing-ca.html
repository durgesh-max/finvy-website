/ Why Indian engineering captives need transfer pricing

The regulatory lens.

  • Indian ECC = Associated Enterprise (AE) of US parent under Section 92A.
  • All services rendered to US parent = International Transaction under Section 92B.
  • Must be at Arm's Length Price (ALP) under Section 92F.
  • Form 3CEB mandatory annual filing (30 November following FY) + Form 3CEB auditor's report.
  • Form 3CEAA Master File (MF) + Form 3CEAD Country-by-Country Report (CbCR) if MNE consolidated revenue EUR 750M+.
  • Specific Safe Harbour available: specific markup + specific conditions (Rule 10TD + 10TE).

/ Cost-plus benchmarking (TNMM)

The standard method.

  • Transactional Net Margin Method (TNMM) typical for captive services.
  • Operating profit margin (OP/Operating Costs) benchmarked against external comparables.
  • Comparables selected from Prowess / Capitaline / CMIE database: Indian IT services / BPO / R&D services companies with similar functions.
  • Industry-typical markup for software captive: 15-20% OP/OC range (industry median FY 2024-25 was 17-18%).
  • Industry-typical for pure R&D captive: 15-18% OP/OC.
  • Industry-typical for BPO back-office: 12-15% OP/OC.
  • Specific comparability adjustments: idle capacity, startup phase, specific contractual terms.

/ Safe Harbour Rules (SHR)

The simplification option.

  • Rule 10TD + 10TE: Safe Harbour Rules for specific captive services.
  • Software development services: 17% OP/OC if INR 100 crore+ operating cost; 18% if < INR 100 crore (approximate).
  • ITES/BPO: specific rates per category.
  • R&D services (contract): specific rate.
  • SHR benefit: no comparability analysis required + specific exemption from TP audit scrutiny for 5-year block.
  • SHR limitations: specific transaction thresholds + specific documentation + specific service categories only.
  • Opt-in: Form 3CEFA filed before specified date.

/ Advance Pricing Agreement (APA)

The certainty option.

  • Rule 10F-10T: APA framework.
  • Unilateral APA with CBDT: certainty on TP method + markup for 5 years + rollback 4 years = 9-year certainty total.
  • Bilateral APA: with CBDT + US IRS: certainty both sides + avoids double taxation.
  • Multilateral APA: multiple jurisdictions.
  • Timeline: APA typically 24-36 months for bilateral; 12-18 months for unilateral.
  • Fee: INR 10-20 lakh (specific scale).
  • Cost: materially cheaper than specific TP audit + MAP defence.

/ TP audit + MAP defence

If assessed adversely.

  • Transfer Pricing Officer (TPO) scrutiny: specific adjustment proposed if ALP differs from Indian ECC's reported margin.
  • Draft Assessment Order (DAO): taxpayer's objections + specific documentation defence.
  • Dispute Resolution Panel (DRP) within 30 days: specific adjudication.
  • Final Assessment Order: specific adjustment + Section 270A penalty.
  • CIT(A) appeal within 30 days (if DRP route not chosen).
  • ITAT appeal if specific adverse CIT(A) / DRP order.
  • Mutual Agreement Procedure (MAP) under US-India DTAA Article 27: competent authorities negotiate + avoid double taxation.
  • MAP timeline: 2-5 years.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's the typical markup for Indian software captive to US parent?
15-20% OP/OC under TNMM benchmarking. Industry median FY 2024-25: 17-18% for software development; 15-18% R&D; 12-15% BPO. Specific comparability adjustments for idle capacity / startup phase / specific contractual terms. Safe Harbour Rules offer 17-18% certainty.
Should we opt for Safe Harbour Rules?
Depends on: (a) actual operating margin position, (b) comparability analysis conclusion, (c) specific transaction thresholds, (d) specific service category eligibility. If actual margin at or above SHR rate: SHR offers audit exemption + simplicity. If actual margin below SHR rate: comparable benchmarking analysis may support lower + SHR not optimal.
What's the APA timeline + cost?
Unilateral APA: 12-18 months typical, INR 10-20 lakh fee. Bilateral APA (CBDT + IRS): 24-36 months, higher fees + double representation. 5 years prospective + 4 years rollback = 9 years certainty. Materially cheaper than TP audit + penalty + MAP defence over equivalent period.
Can TP adjustment lead to double taxation?
Yes - if Indian TPO adjusts upward (specific Indian income increased) + US IRS doesn't correspondingly adjust downward (specific US expense increased) = same income taxed both sides. MAP (Mutual Agreement Procedure) under US-India DTAA Article 27: competent authorities negotiate corresponding adjustment. 2-5 year timeline.
