Inherited Property in India (2026): Who Inherits, Documents, Selling It, Capital Gains Tax for Residents and NRIs, no inheritance tax, but the sale is taxed.

India has no inheritance or estate tax: receiving property from a parent or relative is not taxed. Tax arises only when the heir sells. The heir takes over the previous owner's purchase date and cost (fair market value on 1 April 2001 can be used for older property), so most inherited property is already long-term on day one. Long-term gains are taxed at 12.5% without indexation; a resident individual or HUF may instead choose 20% with indexation for land or buildings acquired before 23 July 2024, while NRIs pay 12.5%. Who inherits depends on the will, or without one, on personal law: under the Hindu Succession Act, daughters have the same rights as sons.

/ Documents to put the property in your name

Before you can sell.

  • Death certificate, and the will with probate where required, or a legal heir certificate (a succession certificate is used for bank balances, shares and other movable assets).
  • Mutation of the land or municipal records and the housing society transfer into the heirs' names.
  • If one heir is to keep the property, the others sign a registered release (relinquishment) deed; stamp duty depends on the state and is often concessional between family members.
  • If the heirs split several properties, a registered partition deed.
  • Heirs living abroad can sign through a power of attorney; see selling Indian property from abroad.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is there inheritance tax in India?
No. India has no inheritance or estate tax, and property received by inheritance is not taxed as income. Tax arises only when the heir sells.
How is capital gains tax worked out on inherited property?
The heir uses the previous owner's purchase cost and holding period (fair market value on 1 April 2001 for older property). Long-term gains are taxed at 12.5% without indexation; residents may choose 20% with indexation for land or buildings acquired before 23 July 2024.
Do daughters have equal rights in inherited property?
Yes, for Hindus, Sikhs, Jains and Buddhists. Since the 2005 amendment to the Hindu Succession Act, daughters are coparceners by birth with the same rights as sons, as confirmed by the Supreme Court in 2020.
Can an NRI inherit and sell property in India?
Yes. An NRI or OCI can inherit any property in India, sell it (agricultural land only to a resident Indian citizen) and repatriate up to USD 1 million per financial year.
What documents do I need to sell inherited property?
Death certificate, will and probate or legal heir certificate, mutation in the records, and release or partition deeds if the heirs are dividing the property.
Can I avoid tax on selling inherited property?
You can reduce or defer it by reinvesting the gain in a residential house, in capital gain bonds within six months, or under section 54F, within the time limits.
