    / How freelancers are taxed

    The rules behind the numbers.

    • Section 44ADA: 50% of gross receipts is treated as profit (declare more if actual profit is higher); available to specified professionals such as IT, design, consulting, legal, medical and engineering services, up to ₹75 lakh of receipts where cash receipts are within 5% (₹50 lakh otherwise). It is section 58 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 from tax year 2026-27.
    • Actual profit: receipts minus genuine business expenses, with books of account; a tax audit applies where receipts exceed ₹50 lakh without 44ADA.
    • Slab rates: new regime nil to ₹4 lakh, then 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25% and 30% above ₹24 lakh, with the 87A rebate making income up to ₹12 lakh tax-free for residents (marginal relief just above); old regime nil to ₹2.5 lakh (₹3 lakh at 60, ₹5 lakh at 80), 5%, 20% and 30% above ₹10 lakh, with deductions. The ₹75,000 standard deduction is for salary only.
    • TDS: Indian clients usually deduct 10% under section 194J on professional fees above ₹50,000 a year; it is credited against your tax, and any excess is refunded when you file.
    • Advance tax: if tax after TDS is ₹10,000 or more, pay it in advance; under 44ADA the whole amount is due by 15 March, otherwise in instalments by 15 June, 15 September, 15 December and 15 March.
    • GST: registration is needed once aggregate turnover crosses ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states); export receipts count, and exports go out zero-rated under a LUT.

    FAQ

    Common questions, answered.

    How is income tax calculated for freelancers in India?
    Profit is worked out either under section 44ADA (50% of gross receipts, no books) or as receipts minus expenses, other income is added, and tax is charged at new-regime or old-regime slab rates, less the 87A rebate for residents, plus 4% cess.
    Is there a tax calculator for freelancers in India?
    Yes. The calculator on this page estimates a resident freelancer's tax for 2026-27 under section 44ADA or actual profit, in either regime, with the 87A rebate, TDS and advance tax dates.
    How much can a freelancer earn tax-free in India?
    Under the new regime, taxable income up to Rs 12 lakh is tax-free for residents because of the 87A rebate. With section 44ADA at 50%, that means gross receipts up to about Rs 24 lakh if there is no other income.
    What is the limit for section 44ADA in 2026-27?
    Rs 75 lakh of gross receipts where cash receipts are within 5% of the total, and Rs 50 lakh otherwise.
    Do freelancers need to pay advance tax?
    Yes, if tax after TDS is Rs 10,000 or more. Under section 44ADA the full amount is due by 15 March; otherwise it is paid in four instalments by 15 June, 15 September, 15 December and 15 March.
    Which ITR does a freelancer file?
    ITR-4 for resident individuals using section 44ADA (within its conditions), and ITR-3 for those declaring actual profit or with income that ITR-4 does not cover.
