FAQ

Online CA consultation, answered.

How does an online consultation with a chartered accountant work?
Fill in the short form on this page (or WhatsApp +91 78018 87130) with your name, where you live, the topic and two or three times that suit you with your time zone. We reply within one working day to confirm a time, and the call takes place by phone or WhatsApp. You speak directly with CA Durgesh Chavda.
Is the first consultation free?
Yes. The first 20 minutes are free and carry no commitment. If you then want us to take on the work, we explain the scope, the documents needed, the timeline and the fee before anything starts.
What should I send before the call?
The document the question is about helps most: the notice PDF for an income tax or GST notice, the return or AIS for a refund or mismatch, the sale agreement and purchase cost for a property sale, the remittance details for a Form 145 / 146 question, or the company details for an India entry or due diligence question. Do not send passwords, OTPs or card numbers.
Can I consult a CA in India from outside India?
Yes. Calls are arranged across time zones, including the Gulf, Europe, the UK, the US, Singapore and Australia. Most NRI and foreign-company work, such as tax returns, refunds, notice replies, Form 146 certificates and company filings, is handled online through the government portals after the call.
Can the work be completed online after the consultation?
In most cases, yes. Income tax returns are filed and e-verified online, notice replies are submitted through e-Proceedings on the income tax portal or on the GST portal, Form 146 (old Form 15CB) is uploaded online by the CA, and company filings are made on the MCA portal. Where a signature, digital signature certificate or original document is needed, we tell you on the call.
