/ What breaks with deactivated DIN

The cascade.

  • ALL MCA eForm filings require active DIN of authorised signatory.
  • Deactivated DIN blocks: AOC-4 (financial statements), MGT-7 (annual return), DIR-12 (director appointments), PAS-3 (share allotment), PAS-6 (Rule 9B demat), MSME-1 (MSE dues), DPT-3 (deposits), SH-7 (share capital change), CHG-1/4 (charges), INC-22 (registered office change), MGT-14 (specific resolutions).
  • Also: cannot sign board resolutions on MCA records, cannot incorporate new company as director, Bank KYC refresh fails, Section 148A show-cause response e-filing blocked, specific FEMA FIRMS portal filings blocked.
  • Materially: entire MCA compliance stack frozen until DIR-3 KYC filed + DIN reactivated.

/ DIR-3 KYC Web vs eForm

Which to use.

  • DIR-3 KYC Web: for returning filers with UNCHANGED KYC details (name, DOB, nationality, email, mobile, address, PAN, Aadhaar). Login MCA v3 portal with DSC or MCA credentials. OTP verification. Submit. Faster.
  • DIR-3 KYC eForm: for FIRST-TIME filers OR any KYC detail changed from last filing. Download eForm, attach documents (PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, specific documents for foreign directors), self-attest, apply DSC, upload. Longer processing.

/ 24-72 hour reactivation workflow

The fast fix.

  1. Hour 0: Determine DIR-3 KYC Web vs eForm applicability.
  2. Hour 1: For Web - login MCA v3 portal + verify details + OTP + submit. Takes 15-30 minutes.
  3. Hour 1-4: For eForm - download eForm + attach docs + DSC + upload. Takes 2-4 hours including documentation.
  4. Hour 4-24: Late fee INR 5,000 paid via MCA portal.
  5. Hour 24-72: MCA processes filing + DIN marked ACTIVE on MCA portal.
  6. Post-reactivation: proceed with normal MCA filings (AOC-4, MGT-7, DIR-12, PAS-6, etc.).

/ For foreign directors + specific scenarios

Special cases.

  • Foreign director with foreign DSC: DSC must be valid Indian DSC (Class 3). If foreign DSC: specific DSC refresh needed.
  • DSC expired: new DSC issuance 2-3 days. BQP coordinates.
  • KYC details changed (address change, mobile change, email change): must use eForm (not Web) + specific documents.
  • Foreign director's apostilled documents: for first-time filer eForm - apostille takes 7-21 days typically. Plan ahead.
  • Multiple directorships: single DIR-3 KYC filing + reactivates DIN for all directorships simultaneously.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How urgently can DIN be reactivated?
24-72 hours after DIR-3 KYC filing processed by MCA. Faster if DIR-3 KYC Web applicable (returning filer, unchanged KYC). Slower if eForm applicable (first-time filer, changed KYC, document attachments needed). Weekend / holiday delays add specific days.
What's the DIR-3 KYC late fee?
INR 5,000 standard late fee irrespective of delay duration. Paid via MCA v3 portal. No specific multiplier for extended delay (unlike some MCA late fees). Capped at INR 5,000 - doesn't matter if DIN deactivated 1 month or 6 months.
Can I file AOC-4 before DIN reactivation?
No - all MCA eForm filings require active DIN of authorised signatory. Fix DIR-3 KYC first (24-72 hour reactivation); then proceed with AOC-4 + MGT-7 + DIR-12 + other filings. For year-end 29 November MGT-7 deadline: fix DIR-3 KYC urgently to leave buffer time.

How to avoid DIN deactivation next year?
DIR-3 KYC is annual mandatory. BQP annual director compliance calendar includes 15-30 day pre-deadline reminder + specific client engagement to file before 30 September. Prevents next year's deactivation. For director with multiple directorships: single DIR-3 KYC filing + specific prevention.
