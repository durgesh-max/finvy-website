            FAQ

            Common questions, answered.

            How much money can an NRI send from India abroad?
            NRE and FCNR balances freely; NRO balances and property sale proceeds up to USD 1 million per financial year with Form 15CA and Form 15CB.
            How much can my parents send me abroad?
            Up to USD 250,000 per parent per financial year under LRS. TCS of 20% applies on gifts above INR 10 lakh a year (2% for education or medical), refundable through their Indian return.
            Is money sent to India taxable?
            Not in India for the transfer itself. Gifts to relatives are tax-free for them; gifts from non-relatives are taxable once the year's total exceeds INR 50,000.
