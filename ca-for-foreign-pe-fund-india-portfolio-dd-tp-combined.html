/ Portfolio-level engagement benefits

vs individual portfolio.

  • Consistent compliance standard: all portfolio companies follow same practices on FEMA, TP, ESOP, DPIIT.
  • Cost efficiency: volume discount across multiple portfolio companies.
  • Faster DD for new acquisitions: BQP familiar with fund's standard DD framework; template-driven.
  • Coordinated reporting: fund-level portfolio reporting consolidated across all Indian investments.
  • Exit preparedness: pre-exit clean-up (FEMA, demat, Section 56 legacy) proactively.
  • Single India point of contact: fund partner interacts with one firm vs multiple.

/ Scope of portfolio engagement

The complete offering.

  • New acquisition DD: six-workstream DD on each new target. Standardised format for fund consumption.
  • Post-closing integration: 100-day plan + fund reporting alignment + control audit.
  • Ongoing portfolio tax: annual ITR + Form 3CEB (Form 48 from tax year 2026-27) TP + audit for each portfolio.
  • FEMA compliance: FC-GPR + FLA Return + Form ODI for each portfolio with foreign shareholder.
  • Transfer pricing: Form 3CEB + Master File + CbCR for in-scope portfolio.
  • Pre-exit clean-up: Rule 9B demat + Section 56(2)(viib) legacy + FEMA compounding + ESOP compliance.
  • Exit structuring: M&A tax + stamp duty + capital gain + exit TRC + Form 15CB (Form 146 from 1 April 2026) for foreign shareholder.
  • Fund-level reporting: quarterly portfolio compliance dashboard to fund partners.

/ Fund structures we support

US / UK / Singapore / UAE PE + VC.

  • US VC: typically Delaware LLC / LP; Indian investments via FEMA FDI; exit via M&A or IPO.
  • UK PE: UK Ltd GP + LP structures; Indian investments.
  • Singapore PE: Singapore Pte Ltd / VCC; Indian investments; India-Singapore DTAA pre-April-2017 grandfathering for pre-existing portfolio.
  • UAE PE / family office: ADGM / DIFC licensed; Indian investments; India-UAE DTAA 10% dividend.
  • Mauritius-based PE: Mauritius GBC + Indian investment; India-Mauritius DTAA pre-April-2017 grandfathering.
  • GIFT City IFSC AIF: Section 10(4D) exemption; LP-friendly structure.

/ Typical engagement model

The flow.

  1. Fund engagement letter signed - portfolio-level framework agreement.
  2. Portfolio inventory + current state audit for all existing portfolio companies.
  3. Compliance roadmap for each portfolio + fund-level dashboard.
  4. New acquisition DD request: 3-6 week turnaround standard; expedited for competitive processes.
  5. Ongoing annual compliance for portfolio.
  6. Exit preparedness audit on pre-defined criteria.
  7. Quarterly fund-level reporting to GP + Investment Committee.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Why engage BQP at fund-level vs individual portfolio-company level?
Fund-level engagement offers: consistent compliance standards across portfolio, cost efficiency through volume, faster DD on new acquisitions (BQP familiar with fund's framework), coordinated fund-level reporting, pre-exit clean-up proactively planned, single India point of contact for the fund. Suitable for funds with 5+ Indian portfolio companies.
Does BQP work with Big-4 at individual portfolio level?
Yes - complementary. Some portfolio companies may already have Big-4 statutory auditor; BQP operates as advisory + specialist CA alongside. Common structure: Big-4 for statutory audit + BQP for TP + FEMA + specialist advisory. Or BQP for all including statutory audit for smaller portfolio companies.
Can BQP support pre-exit clean-up across portfolio?
Yes - standard scope. Pre-exit clean-up audit identifies: FEMA breaches needing compounding, Rule 9B demat non-compliance, Section 56(2)(viib) legacy angel tax exposure, ESOP SBEB non-compliance, related party transaction gaps, tax notice history. Clean-up proactively reduces exit transaction friction + buyer DD findings.
How does fund-level engagement work commercially?
Framework engagement letter at fund level + specific scope letter per portfolio company or per workstream (new DD / ongoing compliance / exit). Volume-based fee framework; scoped per deliverable. Transparent + published rate card for routine deliverables. Fund partner has single point of contact + consolidated billing.
Does BQP coordinate with foreign counsel on fund-level matters?
Yes - standard. BQP's India-side work feeds into fund's US / UK / Singapore counsel on new acquisitions SPA, portfolio secondaries, exit M&A SPA, LP side letter obligations. Prior work with top US (V&E, Weil, Latham, Simpson), UK (Clifford Chance, Linklaters), Singapore (Allen & Gledhill, Rajah & Tann) counsel on cross-border fund-level matters.
Is BQP a strong fit for a fund with 5-30 Indian portfolio companies?
Yes - this is BQP's sweet-spot engagement size. Fund with 5-30 Indian portfolio companies benefits most from fund-level engagement economics + consistency.
