TDS on Payment to a Foreign Company: Calculator (2026) for Royalty, Technical Service Fees, Interest and Dividends - Domestic 20.8% vs Treaty Rates by Country, work out the Indian withholding tax before you remit.

When an Indian company pays a foreign company a royalty, a fee for technical services, interest or a dividend, it must deduct Indian tax before remitting. The domestic rate is 20% plus surcharge and cess, about 20.8% to 21.84%. If the recipient gives a tax residency certificate and Form 10F (Form 41 under the new Act), the tax treaty rate applies where it is lower: usually 10% (Singapore, Germany, the Netherlands, Japan, France, Ireland) or 15% (United States, United Kingdom, Australia). Some treaties, including the UAE and Saudi Arabia, have no article for technical services. Those fees are then normally taxable in India only if the foreign company has a permanent establishment here. If the contract is net of tax, the payment must be grossed up. The bank will also ask for Form 145 and Form 146 (formerly 15CA and 15CB). Pick the payment type and country below.

    / How it works

    Rates used and limits.

    • Domestic rate: 20% on royalty and technical service fees (since the Finance Act 2023), interest on foreign-currency loans and dividends, plus surcharge of 2% above INR 1 crore or 5% above INR 10 crore, plus 4% cess.
    • Treaty rate: applied to the gross amount without surcharge or cess; the lower of the treaty and domestic rates is used. Rates are from the Income Tax Department’s withholding-rate table.
    • Two-rate treaties: the calculator uses the general rate and shows when a lower one applies, for example 10% for equipment rentals under the US and UK treaties or for interest paid to banks.
    • No technical services article: under treaties such as the UAE and Saudi Arabia the fee is treated as business profits; see fees for technical services and the make-available test.
    • Section number: section 195 of the 1961 Act is section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025; see what changed.
    • Not modelled: a permanent establishment in India, lower rates on specific bonds or loans, the recipient having no PAN, GST, and anti-abuse tests.

    FAQ

    Common questions, answered.

    What is the TDS rate on payments to a foreign company from India?
    For royalty, technical service fees, interest on foreign-currency loans and dividends, the domestic rate is 20% plus surcharge and 4% cess (about 20.8% to 21.84%), or the lower tax treaty rate if the recipient provides a tax residency certificate and Form 10F.
    What is the TDS rate on technical service fees paid to a US company?
    Under the India-US treaty the rate is 15% (10% for ancillary services and equipment rentals) with a tax residency certificate and Form 10F, compared with about 20.8% under domestic law. Whether the service is taxable at all depends on the treaty's make-available test.
    Is TDS deducted on service fees paid to a UAE company?
    The India-UAE treaty has no separate article for technical services, so such fees are normally taxable in India only if the UAE company has a permanent establishment here. Get this confirmed before remitting without deduction.
    What does grossing up mean?
    If the contract says the foreign company must receive the invoice amount in full, the Indian payer bears the tax, and the payment is increased so that the amount after tax equals the invoice: gross = net / (1 - rate).
    Which forms are needed to remit the payment?
    Form 145 (formerly 15CA) filed online and, in most taxable cases, a chartered accountant's certificate in Form 146 (formerly 15CB), given to the bank before remittance.
