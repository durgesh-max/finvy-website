NRI Pensioners: Life Certificate from Abroad by 30 November 2026, and Is Your Indian Pension Taxable? (UAE, US, UK, Canada, Australia), keep the pension flowing and pay tax in the right country.

Pensioners must give their pension bank or authority a life certificate by 30 November 2026. Pensioners aged 80 and above could start from 1 October. A pensioner living abroad can usually do this with a paper life certificate signed at the Indian embassy or consulate, or in another form the pension bank accepts, or through the Jeevan Pramaan digital life certificate. Reports differ on whether the face-authentication app works outside India, so check with the bank. If the certificate is late, the December pension can be held. It resumes once the certificate is submitted, and the entitlement is not cancelled. On tax, Indian pension is taxable in India under Indian law even for a non-resident, and the bank may deduct TDS. Treaties can change this. Government pensions are generally taxable only in India. Under some treaties, for example India-US and India-UK, private-employer pensions are taxable only in the country where you live. To get treaty relief, give the bank your TRC and Form 10F, and file an Indian return to recover excess TDS.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the last date for the pensioner life certificate in 2026?
30 November 2026. Pensioners aged 80 and above could submit from 1 October.
How can a pensioner living abroad submit a life certificate?
Usually through a paper life certificate signed at the Indian embassy or consulate, another format the pension bank accepts, or the Jeevan Pramaan digital life certificate. Check whether the face-authentication app works from your country.
What happens if the life certificate is late?
The pension can be held from December; it is released once the certificate is submitted. The entitlement is not cancelled.
Is Indian pension taxable for an NRI?
Under Indian law, yes, because it arises in India. Treaties can change this: government pensions are generally taxable only in India, while under some treaties private pensions are taxable only in the country of residence.
How can an NRI pensioner stop excess TDS?
Give the bank a tax residency certificate and Form 10F to claim treaty relief, apply for a lower deduction certificate, and file an Indian return to claim refunds.
