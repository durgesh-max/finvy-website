/ Section 135 threshold

Who's in CSR scope.

  • Net worth of INR 500 crore+ OR
  • Turnover of INR 1,000 crore+ OR
  • Net profit of INR 5 crore+
  • Threshold assessed on previous FY basis.
  • Once in scope, CSR obligation continues until company falls below ALL thresholds for 3 consecutive FYs.

/ CSR obligation

The 2% rule.

  • Average net profit over 3 preceding FYs × 2% = CSR spend target.
  • If spending less than 2%: unspent balance in current FY must be transferred to specific Unspent CSR Account within 30 days of FY end; must be spent on CSR activities within 3 FYs.
  • Alternative: transfer to specific Fund (PM National Relief Fund, etc.) within 6 months of FY end.
  • If after 3 FYs unspent balance still remains: transfer to specific Fund + Section 135 Schedule VII compliance.
  • CSR committee: minimum 3 directors; small company exemption.

/ Schedule VII permissible activities

What qualifies as CSR.

  • Eradicating hunger, poverty, malnutrition.
  • Promoting education.
  • Promoting gender equality + empowering women.
  • Reducing child mortality + improving maternal health.
  • Combating HIV, malaria, specific diseases.
  • Environmental sustainability.
  • Protection of national heritage, art, culture.
  • Rural + sports development.
  • Specific disaster relief.
  • Rural / slum / sanitation / drinking water.
  • COVID-19 relief + public health initiatives.
  • Specific funds (PM National Relief Fund, specific education funds).

/ Form CSR-2 + annual reporting

The disclosure.

  • Form CSR-2 filed with ROC: specific company-level CSR disclosure.
  • Reports: 2% target + actual spend + CSR activities + implementing agency / recipient details.
  • Due 90 days from end of CSR financial year.
  • Section 135(5): if company fails to spend, Board's report must explain reasons.
  • Penalty: Section 135(7) + Section 450 default - 2x unspent amount or INR 1 crore (whichever less) + INR 50 lakh per officer in default.
  • Specific CSR mentions in Board's Report + Director's Report.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Which companies must comply with Section 135 CSR?
Net worth INR 500 crore+ OR Turnover INR 1,000 crore+ OR Net profit INR 5 crore+. Threshold assessed on previous FY basis. Once in scope, CSR obligation continues until company falls below ALL thresholds for 3 consecutive FYs.
What's the 2% CSR spending obligation?
Average net profit over 3 preceding FYs × 2% = CSR spend target. Must be spent on permissible Schedule VII activities. Unspent balance: transfer to Unspent CSR Account within 30 days of FY end; spend within 3 FYs. If still unspent: transfer to specific Fund. Penalty: 2x unspent amount or INR 1 crore (whichever less).
What activities qualify as CSR?
Schedule VII of Companies Act 2013: eradicating hunger/poverty, education, gender equality, child mortality, HIV/malaria, environmental sustainability, national heritage/art/culture, rural development, sports, disaster relief, rural sanitation/water, COVID-19 relief, specific government funds. Classification validated by CSR committee + statutory auditor.
What's Form CSR-2 and when is it due?
Form CSR-2 filed with ROC: specific company CSR disclosure (2% target + actual spend + activities + implementing agencies + recipients). Due 90 days from end of CSR financial year. Reflected in Board's Report + Director's Report.
What's the penalty for CSR non-compliance?
Section 135(7) + Section 450: 2x unspent CSR amount or INR 1 crore (whichever less) + INR 50 lakh per officer in default. Materially higher than most Companies Act penalties. ROC adjudication proceedings.
