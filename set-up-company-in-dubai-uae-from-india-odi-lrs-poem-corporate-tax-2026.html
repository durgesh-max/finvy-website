/ Indians going global · UAE · FEMA ODI · Updated 2026-11-14

Setting Up a Company in Dubai or the UAE From India (2026): FEMA ODI Route, LRS Limit, POEM Risk and UAE Corporate Tax, legal on both sides, if you do it in the right order.

An Indian resident can own a Dubai or UAE company, but it is overseas direct investment (ODI) under FEMA, not a simple LRS remittance: an individual can invest within the LRS limit of USD 250,000 a financial year, only in an operating company with a genuine business (not financial services) and not one that has its own subsidiary where the individual has control; an Indian company can invest up to 400% of its net worth. The investment is reported through your bank (Form ODI and a UIN), with an annual performance report by 31 December. If the company is run from India, it can be treated as Indian-resident (POEM) and taxed in India on its global profit. In the UAE, corporate tax is 0% up to AED 375,000 of profit and 9% above, and registration is compulsory.

/ The order of steps

Do FEMA first, then remit.

  • Choose the jurisdiction (mainland or free zone) and confirm the activity is a bona fide business.
  • Open the ODI file with your authorised dealer bank: Form ODI, valuation where needed and declarations; the bank reports it and a UIN is allotted.
  • Remit the capital through the bank under the ODI route, then incorporate and obtain the UAE licence.
  • Within the prescribed time, submit evidence of investment (share certificate) to the bank.
  • Every year: the annual performance report by 31 December, the foreign shares in Schedule FA of your Indian return, and UAE corporate tax registration and filing.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can an Indian resident open a company in Dubai?
Yes, as overseas direct investment under FEMA: within the LRS limit of USD 250,000 per financial year, in an operating company with a genuine business, reported through your bank with Form ODI.
Is setting up a Dubai company ODI or LRS?
ODI. LRS is the remittance limit that an individual's ODI must fit within; the investment itself must follow the Overseas Investment Rules and be reported.
Can my Indian company own a UAE subsidiary?
Yes, under Schedule I of the Overseas Investment Rules, with total financial commitment up to 400% of net worth and within the two-layer limit.
Will the UAE company be taxed in India?
Only if its place of effective management is in India, meaning the key decisions are in substance made from India. Then it is Indian-resident and taxed in India on its global income.
What is UAE corporate tax for a small company?
0% on taxable profit up to AED 375,000 and 9% above. Businesses with revenue up to AED 3 million can elect small business relief for tax periods ending on or before 31 December 2026.
What yearly filings does the Indian owner have?
The ODI annual performance report by 31 December, Schedule FA in the Indian return, and UAE corporate tax registration and returns.
