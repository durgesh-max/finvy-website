Sole Proprietorship Registration in India (2026): Documents, GST, Udyam, Shop Licence and How to Get a Current Account, there is no single certificate, so build the right set of registrations.

India has no separate law or certificate for registering a sole proprietorship: the business is simply the owner trading in their own name or a trade name, using the owner’s PAN. It is “registered” by taking the registrations the business needs, most commonly Udyam (free, online), GST (once turnover crosses ₹20 lakh for services or ₹40 lakh for goods-only in most states, or earlier in compulsory cases), and the state Shops and Establishments licence or a municipal trade licence. Two of these in the business name are usually what a bank asks for to open a current account.

/ Quick answers

What people search for.

  • How do I register a proprietorship? There is no single government registration. Take Udyam registration, GST if required, and the state shop or trade licence, then open a current account in the business name.
  • Is there a government website for proprietorship registration? No single one. Udyam is on the Udyam Registration portal, GST on the GST portal, and shop and trade licences on state or municipal portals.
  • Is GST compulsory for a sole proprietor? Only once aggregate turnover crosses the threshold (₹20 lakh for services, ₹40 lakh for goods-only in most states, lower in some special category states) or in compulsory cases such as inter-state supply of goods or selling through e-commerce platforms; see is GST registration mandatory?.
  • What documents are needed? The owner’s PAN and Aadhaar, a photograph, proof of the business address (rent agreement or ownership with NOC, and a utility bill), and a bank account; GST also asks for the place-of-business proof.
  • How is a proprietorship taxed? As the owner’s own income, in the owner’s return (ITR-3, or ITR-4 under the presumptive scheme for eligible small businesses).
  • Can an NRI own one? Yes, on a non-repatriation basis, outside barred activities; see can an NRI start a business in India.

/ Limits of a proprietorship

When to choose something else.

  • Unlimited liability: the owner is personally liable for all business debts.
  • No outside equity: investors cannot take shares; bringing in a partner means a partnership firm, an LLP or a company.
  • Continuity: the business is tied to the owner and does not continue as a separate entity.
  • Growth path: compare LLP registration and LLP vs private limited.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How do I register a sole proprietorship in India?
There is no single proprietorship registration. The business is identified through the registrations it takes: usually Udyam (free, online), GST once turnover crosses the threshold or in compulsory cases, and the state Shops and Establishments or municipal trade licence. A current account is then opened in the business name.
What documents are required for proprietorship registration?
The owner's PAN and Aadhaar, a photograph, proof of the business address such as a rent agreement or ownership document with an NOC plus a utility bill, and bank details. Each registration (GST, Udyam, shop licence) asks for its own set.
Is there a government website for proprietorship registration?
No single website. Udyam registration is on the Udyam portal, GST on the GST portal, and shop or trade licences on state or municipal portals.
Do I need GST registration as a sole proprietor?
Only when aggregate turnover exceeds the threshold (Rs 20 lakh for services, Rs 40 lakh for goods-only in most states, lower in some special category states) or in compulsory cases such as inter-state supply of goods or selling through e-commerce operators.
Which documents does a bank need for a proprietorship current account?
Banks usually ask for two proofs of the business in the proprietor's name, such as a GST certificate, Udyam certificate, shop and establishments licence, IEC or utility bills, along with the owner's KYC.
