/ Who must file Form 3CEB

Scope.

  • Any Indian company / entity with international transactions above INR 1 crore in aggregate in the FY.
  • International transaction = transaction between Associated Enterprises (26%+ shareholding or common control) crossing border + affects profit / income / loss / assets.
  • Effectively every Indian subsidiary of foreign parent (any related-party transaction triggers).
  • Specified Domestic Transactions also in scope: specific tax-holiday / 115BAB-type entities.
  • Due date: 30 November 2026 for AY 2026-27 (one month after standard tax audit).

/ Form 3CEB content

The 24 clauses.

  • Entity details + nature of business + Associated Enterprise relationship chain.
  • Each international transaction: nature + volume + value in INR + currency.
  • Transaction categories: goods, services, intangibles, financing, cost allocation, business restructuring.
  • Arm's-length method chosen per transaction: CUP / RPM / Cost Plus / TNMM / PSM / Other.
  • Comparable set + arm's-length range.
  • Material facts specific to the transaction.
  • Methodology justification.
  • CA signature + UDIN.

/ Underlying Section 92D documentation

What's maintained but not filed.

  • Section 92D + Rule 10D: comprehensive TP documentation to be maintained (not filed but produced on scrutiny).
  • Entity profile + industry analysis + functional analysis + economic analysis + benchmarking study.
  • Must be contemporaneous (prepared by Form 3CEB filing date, not after scrutiny).
  • Updated annually.
  • Preserved 8+ years.

/ Master File + CbCR (if applicable)

The BEPS layer.

  • Master File (Form 3CEAA): if group consolidated revenue > INR 500 crore + international transactions > INR 50 crore (or intangible > INR 10 crore). Due 30 November, with the TP-case ITR; Form 3CEB itself is due 31 October.
  • CbCR (Form 3CEAD): if ultimate parent of group with consolidated revenue > INR 6,400 crore (~EUR 750M, BEPS Action 13).
  • Form 3CEAC: secondary notification by Indian subsidiary if ultimate parent country has no CbCR regime.
  • Due dates vary - typically 12-15 months from FY-end.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

When is Form 3CEB due for AY 2026-27?
31 October 2026 for Form 3CEB, one month before the 30 November 2026 ITR due date for TP audit cases.
What's the INR 1 crore threshold for Form 3CEB?
Aggregate international transactions (goods + services + intangibles + financing + cost allocation + business restructuring with Associated Enterprises) above INR 1 crore in the FY. Effectively every foreign-subsidiary Indian company triggers.
What's the penalty for missing Form 3CEB?
Section 271BA: INR 1,00,000 penalty for non-filing or incomplete Form 3CEB. Section 234F ITR late fee. ITR filing blocked until Form 3CEB linked.
Does Form 3CEB alone suffice or do I need Section 92D documentation?
Both. Form 3CEB is the auditor's certificate filed with ITR. Section 92D documentation (entity profile + industry analysis + functional analysis + benchmarking) is maintained + produced on scrutiny. Rule 10D specifies contents. Must be contemporaneous.
When is Master File + CbCR required?
Master File (Form 3CEAA) if group consolidated revenue > INR 500 crore + aggregate international transactions > INR 50 crore (or intangible-related > INR 10 crore). CbCR (Form 3CEAD) for ultimate parent of group with consolidated revenue > INR 6,400 crore (~EUR 750M). Due 30 November for Master File; 12-15 months for CbCR.
