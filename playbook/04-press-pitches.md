# Press Pitches — Paste Ready Emails for Reporter Outreach

**Target:** 1 backlink per month from a DA 60+ Indian publication (Inc42, YourStory, Economic Times, Mint, Business Standard, Moneycontrol, Entrepreneur India, Forbes India).

**Rule:** reporters are busy. One concise ask. Open with the specific angle, close with the specific help you can provide. No preamble, no attached PDFs on first contact.

**Response-rate expectation:** 10-20% on cold pitch. Follow up once after 7 days. Don't follow up twice.

**Find reporter emails:** publication's About / Contact page + LinkedIn + Rocket Reach + sometimes `[firstname]@[publication].com` works. Twitter/X DM is a valid alternative for younger reporters.
