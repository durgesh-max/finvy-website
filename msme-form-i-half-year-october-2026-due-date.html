/ The core rule

Who must file + when.

  • Form MSME Form I: half-yearly return of outstanding dues to Micro + Small Enterprise (MSE) suppliers that are over 45 days outstanding as of half-year end.
  • Applicability: every company (not LLP, firm, individual) that has outstanding MSE supplier dues over 45 days.
  • Half-year periods: April-September + October-March.
  • Due dates: 31 October for the April-September half-year and 30 April for the October-March half-year. So April-September 2026 period: due 31 October 2026. October 2026-March 2027 period: due 30 April 2027.
  • MCA Rules 2019: Specified Companies (Furnishing of Information about Payment to Micro and Small Enterprise Suppliers) Order 2019 under Section 405 Companies Act.

/ What goes in the return

The data points.

  • Company CIN + PAN + registered office + applicable period.
  • Each MSE supplier: PAN + Udyam number + total outstanding amount + aging bucket (46-60 days / 61-90 days / 91-180 days / 180+ days).
  • Reason for delay (if specified).
  • Interest payable under Section 15 MSMED (3x RBI bank rate compounded monthly).

/ Section 43B(h) interaction

The Income Tax trap.

  • Section 43B(h) Income Tax Act disallows deduction for MSE expenses paid beyond 45 days. See our 43B(h) Year 2 guide.
  • Form MSME Form I identifies the same universe of overdue payments.
  • Tax auditor's Form 3CD Clause 22 cross-references MSME Form I data.
  • Inconsistency between MSME Form I disclosure + Form 3CD disclosure attracts specific scrutiny.

/ Compliance checklist

What to do now.

  1. Compile list of MSE suppliers (verified via Udyam certificate).
  2. Extract outstanding dues as of 30 September 2026 - specifically those over 45 days from acceptance date.
  3. Age bucket analysis (46-60 / 61-90 / 91-180 / 180+ days).
  4. Compute Section 15 MSMED interest at 3x RBI bank rate compounded monthly.
  5. Reason-for-delay documentation.
  6. Reconcile with Section 43B(h) tax audit disclosure (Form 3CD Clause 22).
  7. File MSME Form I by 31 October 2026 via MCA v3 portal.
  8. Pay MSE suppliers before year-end 31 March 2027 to minimise Section 43B(h) disallowance.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

When is MSME Form I due for April-September 2026?
31 October 2026 for the April-September 2026 half-year (30 April for October-March). For October 2026-March 2027 period: 30 April 2027. Form filed via MCA v3 portal under Specified Companies (Furnishing of Information) Order 2019.
Which companies must file MSME Form I?
Every company (Pvt Ltd / Public Ltd) with outstanding dues to Micro + Small Enterprise suppliers beyond 45 days as of half-year end. LLPs, firms, individuals NOT in scope. Based on acceptance date of goods/services + 45-day MSMED Act 2006 payment window.
Does MSME Form I apply if we always pay MSE suppliers on time?
If no MSE dues outstanding beyond 45 days at half-year end: no filing obligation. However, some practitioners file 'nil' return for compliance signal. Prudent approach: file nil return + maintain audit trail of all MSE invoice payments.
What's the penalty for not filing MSME Form I?
Section 450 Companies Act default penalty: up to INR 10,000 for first default + INR 1,000 per day of continuing default. Also Section 450 for every officer in default. More materially: inconsistency with Section 43B(h) tax audit disclosure attracts tax officer scrutiny.
How is MSE supplier identified for MSME Form I?
Via Udyam certificate - supplier's Udyam Registration Number specifies category (Micro / Small / Medium). MSME Form I covers only Micro + Small. Medium Enterprises are NOT in scope (same as Section 43B(h)). Collecting Udyam certificate at supplier onboarding is foundational compliance.
