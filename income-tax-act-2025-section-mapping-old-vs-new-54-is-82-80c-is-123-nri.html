Income-tax Act 2025 Section Mapping, 54 is now 82.

From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act and renumbered its sections. Section 54 is now section 82, 54EC is 85, 54F is 86, 80C is 123, 87A is 156, 115BAC is 202, 112A is 198, 90 is 159 and 195 is 393(2). Residential status stays in section 6. Search the table below.

/ Which Act applies

The rule of thumb.

  • The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 April 2026, to tax year 2026-27 onwards. "Previous year" and "assessment year" are replaced by a single "tax year".
  • Income of FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27), including returns, audits and capital gains on sales up to 31 March 2026, stays under the 1961 Act and its section numbers.
  • Most rules carried over with new numbers; check rates and thresholds separately, since Budgets change those.
  • Several new numbers reuse old ones for a different topic: new section 92 is income from other sources (old 56), and new section 197 is long-term capital gains (old 112), not the lower TDS certificate.

/ For NRIs and foreign companies

The sections you will meet most.

  • Selling property in India: exemptions in sections 82 (was 54), 85 (was 54EC) and 86 (was 54F); TDS on the sale by the buyer under section 393 (was 195).
  • Residential status: still section 6.
  • Treaty relief: section 159 (was 90), with Form 41 (was 10F).
  • Payments to non-residents: TDS under section 393(2) (was 195), reported in Form 144 (was 27Q).
  • Listed shares and mutual funds: sections 196 (was 111A) and 198 (was 112A).

For forms, see old vs new income tax form numbers.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the new section for Section 54 under the Income-tax Act, 2025?
Section 82. Sections 54EC and 54F become sections 85 and 86.
What is Section 80C called in the new Income-tax Act?
Section 123, read with Schedule XV.
What is the new section for 115BAC (new tax regime)?
Section 202. The Section 87A rebate is now section 156.
What replaced Section 195 TDS?
Section 393(2) of the Income-tax Act, 2025.
Is residential status still Section 6?
Yes, residential status remains in section 6.
Which Act applies to FY 2025-26 income?
The Income-tax Act, 1961. The 2025 Act applies from tax year 2026-27 (from 1 April 2026).
