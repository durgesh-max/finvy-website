/ Who must file DPT-3

Companies in scope.

  • All companies (Pvt Ltd + Public Ltd) EXCEPT government companies, banks, NBFCs, insurance, housing finance.
  • Nil DPT-3 even if no deposits/loans - specific compliance signal.
  • Position reported as at 31 March of each year.
  • Due by 30 June of the same year.

/ What's reported

The categories.

  • Deposits (Rule 2(1)(c) Companies (Acceptance of Deposits) Rules 2014): loans from public are deposits. Pvt Ltd generally cannot accept deposits from public.
  • Exempted deposits: loans from directors + promoters + other companies + banks + financial institutions. These are exempted from deposit rules but reportable in DPT-3.
  • External Commercial Borrowings (ECB).
  • Debentures (non-convertible + convertible).
  • Advance received from customers above specific threshold.

/ Compliance signal

Why DPT-3 matters.

  • Shows company's external borrowing / loan structure to regulators + lenders + auditors.
  • Pre-M&A diligence: buyer reviews DPT-3 history.
  • Pre-IPO: SEBI reviews DPT-3 for public offering compliance history.
  • Non-filing: Section 450 default penalty.
  • Form 3CD Clause 22 tax audit disclosure cross-references DPT-3.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is DPT-3 nil return mandatory?
Yes - even if company has no deposits / loans, nil DPT-3 must be filed as compliance signal. Non-filing attracts Section 450 default penalty.
What's the due date for DPT-3?
30 June each year, reporting position as at 31 March of same year. So DPT-3 for position as at 31 March 2026 due by 30 June 2026.
Is director loan reported in DPT-3?
Yes - loan from director is 'exempted deposit' under Rule 2(1)(c). Not a 'deposit' from public but reportable in DPT-3. Both the amount + terms disclosed.
