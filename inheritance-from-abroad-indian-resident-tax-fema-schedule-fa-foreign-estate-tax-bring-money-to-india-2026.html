/ Indian residents with parents or relatives abroad · Inherited foreign money, shares and property · Updated 2026-11-28

Inheritance From Abroad for Indian Residents (2026): Is It Taxable in India, FEMA Rules for Inherited Foreign Assets, Schedule FA Reporting, Foreign Estate Tax and Bringing the Money Home, no tax on receiving it, but plenty to report.

India has no inheritance tax, and money or assets you inherit from a relative abroad are not taxed as a gift. FEMA lets you keep foreign assets inherited from a non-resident, and inheritance does not use your LRS limit. But as a resident you must report those assets in Schedule FA of your return, pay Indian tax on the income they earn and on any gain when you sell - and the estate may face tax in the other country first.

/ Abroad first, then India

The estate, probate and moving money.

  • Foreign estate or inheritance tax: the deceased’s country may tax the estate before distribution; India’s tax treaties cover income taxes, so such estate tax is generally not creditable in India.
  • Probate abroad: foreign banks and brokers usually need probate or an equivalent court order before releasing assets to heirs.
  • Bringing money to India: through banking channels into your Indian account; keep the estate documents and bank advice as the source of funds.
  • Retirement accounts and insurance: inherited pensions, 401(k) / IRA balances and policies have their own rules in the other country and in India - check before withdrawing.
  • Property abroad: you can keep or sell inherited foreign property; rent and sale gains are reportable and taxable in India for a resident.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Do I need to pay tax in India on an inheritance from abroad?
No. India has no inheritance tax, and assets received by inheritance are excluded from the gift tax rule. Tax arises only on income the inherited assets earn and on gains when you sell them.
Can an Indian resident keep foreign assets inherited from a relative abroad?
Yes. FEMA allows a person resident in India to hold foreign assets inherited from a person resident outside India, and the Overseas Investment Rules let a resident individual acquire foreign securities by inheritance without limit. It does not use the LRS limit.
Do I have to report inherited foreign assets in my income tax return?
Yes, if you are a resident and ordinarily resident. Foreign assets go in Schedule FA and foreign income in Schedule FSI. Not reporting can lead to penalties under the Black Money Act.
Is foreign estate or inheritance tax creditable in India?
Generally not. India's tax treaties cover income taxes, so estate or inheritance tax paid on the estate abroad is usually not available as a credit in India. Tax paid abroad on income from the assets can be claimed as foreign tax credit.
How do I bring inherited money from abroad to India?
Through normal banking channels into your Indian bank account, keeping the probate or estate documents and bank advice as proof of the source of funds.
