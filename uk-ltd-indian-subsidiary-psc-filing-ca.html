/ UK PSC framework recap

What UK Ltd must disclose.

  • UK Small Business, Enterprise and Employment Act 2015 + Companies Act 2006 Part 21A.
  • UK Ltd must identify + register People with Significant Control (PSC).
  • PSC = individual who: (a) directly/indirectly holds > 25% shares, (b) directly/indirectly holds > 25% voting rights, (c) directly/indirectly can appoint/remove majority of board, (d) has significant influence/control over company/trust.
  • PSC register maintained at UK Companies House + annual confirmation statement (CS01).
  • Failure penalties: criminal offence + unlimited fine on company + directors.

/ India SBO framework (Section 90)

What Indian subsidiary must disclose.

  • Companies Act 2013 Section 90 + Companies (Significant Beneficial Owners) Rules 2018 (amended 2019).
  • Indian subsidiary must identify SBO: individual holding (directly/indirectly) at least 10% beneficial interest (shares/voting rights/distribution rights/right to exercise significant influence).
  • Form BEN-1: declaration by SBO to Indian company (within 30 days of becoming SBO).
  • Form BEN-2: company's return to RoC (within 30 days of receiving BEN-1).
  • Form BEN-3: register maintained at Indian subsidiary's registered office.
  • Form BEN-4: notice by company to person suspected to be SBO.
  • Form MGT-6: Section 89 declaration if registered member and beneficial owner differ.

/ UK PSC vs India SBO reconciliation

The matching exercise.

  • UK PSC threshold: 25% (more lenient).
  • India SBO threshold: 10% (stricter).
  • Indian subsidiary with Indian-resident SBO = must file Form BEN-1/2 regardless of UK PSC position.
  • UK PSC for UK Ltd = must be identified + registered regardless of India SBO position.
  • Mismatch risk: (a) UK PSC filed + India SBO not filed = Indian compliance gap, (b) Indian SBO filed + UK PSC not filed = UK compliance gap, (c) different beneficial owners disclosed = regulatory inconsistency flags.
  • Both regimes have strict penalties.

/ Common structuring triggers

Scenarios creating complexity.

  • UK HoldCo + Indian OpCo: founder's holding in UK HoldCo + Indian OpCo requires parallel disclosure.
  • Trust structure: trust as UK Ltd shareholder + Indian beneficial ownership rules on trustees + protectors + beneficiaries.
  • Nominee structure: UK Ltd shares held in nominee name + beneficial Indian owner - Section 89 Form MGT-6 declaration mandatory in India.
  • SPV layering: multiple layer structure obscuring beneficial ownership - Indian SBO rules look through for 10% threshold; UK PSC also looks through.
  • FDI compliance: UK investment in Indian subsidiary requires FEMA FC-GPR + Press Note 3 (if specific beneficial-ownership from land-bordering countries); specific exclusion for UK as non-land-bordering.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Does Indian subsidiary SBO filing conflict with UK PSC disclosure?
Different thresholds: UK PSC 25% / India SBO 10%. Indian subsidiary with 10%+ beneficial Indian owner must file Form BEN-1/2 regardless of UK PSC position. Both regimes operate parallel. BQP ensures consistency to avoid regulatory inconsistency flags.
What's the India SBO filing deadline?
Form BEN-1 (SBO declaration to company): within 30 days of becoming SBO. Form BEN-2 (company to RoC): within 30 days of receiving BEN-1. For existing structures where SBO identified post-rule: BEN-1 within 30 days from identification. Annual review required.
Can Press Note 3 affect UK-India investment?
Press Note 3 affects investment where beneficial ownership is situated in or citizen of land-bordering country (China, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Afghanistan, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar). UK direct investment: not affected. However if UK Ltd is itself owned by beneficial Chinese/Pakistani party: PN3 triggered + prior government approval required.

Partner network of UK solicitors / accountants for UK-side filings. For UK Ltd parents of Indian subsidiaries. WhatsApp +91 78018 87130.
Can founder of UK HoldCo + Indian OpCo be in SBO/PSC register of both?
Yes - standard scenario. If founder holds 10%+ in Indian OpCo (directly or via UK HoldCo): Indian SBO. If founder holds 25%+ in UK HoldCo: UK PSC. Both regimes capture the founder in parallel registers. Must file both; disclosures should be consistent. BQP ensures identical beneficial ownership statement both sides.
