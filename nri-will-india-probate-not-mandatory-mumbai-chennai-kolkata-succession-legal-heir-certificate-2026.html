/ NRIs · Wills · Inheritance · Updated 2026-11-20

NRI Will and Inheritance in India (2026): Probate No Longer Compulsory in Mumbai, Chennai and Kolkata, Separate India Will, Succession and Legal Heir Certificates, Tax and Repatriation, what changed in 2025 and how NRIs should plan Indian assets.

NRIs can make a will for their Indian assets in India, and registering it is optional. Many keep a separate India will alongside the will in their country of residence, because a foreign will or foreign probate may need ancillary probate before Indian banks and registrars act on it. The Repealing and Amending Act, 2025 removed the rule that made probate compulsory for wills in Mumbai, Chennai and Kolkata. Probate is still available and often still requested by sub-registrars, banks and property buyers. Without a will, heirs use a succession certificate for bank balances, shares and other debts and securities, or a legal heir certificate from the revenue office, depending on the asset and the institution. India has no inheritance tax. A later sale is taxed using the previous owner's cost and holding period, and NRI heirs can remit inherited money abroad from an NRO account within USD 1 million per financial year after tax.

/ Tax and repatriation for NRI heirs

What happens after inheritance.

  • No inheritance tax: receiving property or money by inheritance is not taxed in India.
  • Income after inheritance: rent, interest and dividends are taxable to the heir; see rent to NRIs.
  • Selling inherited property: cost and holding period of the previous owner carry over; long-term gains at 12.5% for NRIs; buyer deducts TDS unless a lower deduction certificate is obtained; see inherited property rules and sale.
  • Repatriation: inherited assets go through the NRO account; remittance up to USD 1 million per financial year with Forms 145/146 (formerly 145/146 (formerly 15CA/15CB)); see repatriating money.
  • Home country: the US, UK, Canada and Australia have their own estate, inheritance or capital gains rules and reporting for foreign inheritances; plan with both sides.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can an NRI make a will in India?
Yes. An NRI can make a will for Indian assets; it needs two witnesses, and registration is optional. Many NRIs keep a separate India will consistent with their foreign will.
Is probate still mandatory in Mumbai, Chennai and Kolkata?
No. The Repealing and Amending Act, 2025 removed the compulsory probate requirement for wills in those cities, though registrars, banks and buyers may still ask for probate, especially for property.
What is the difference between a succession certificate and a legal heir certificate?
A succession certificate is issued by a civil court for debts and securities such as bank balances and shares; a legal heir certificate is an administrative document from the revenue office used for pensions and similar transfers.
Does a foreign will work in India?
It can, but Indian banks and registrars often require ancillary probate of a foreign will or foreign probate order, which is why many NRIs keep a separate India will.
Is inheritance taxed in India for NRIs?
No inheritance tax applies; income from inherited assets and gains on their later sale are taxable, using the previous owner's cost and holding period.
