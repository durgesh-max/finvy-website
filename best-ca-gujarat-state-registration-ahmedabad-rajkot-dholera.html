/ Core Gujarat state registrations

Mandatory.

  • Gujarat Shops & Establishments Act: within 30 days of commencement.
  • Professional Tax: employer registration + monthly deduction + annual return. Max INR 2,400 per employee per year.
  • Gujarat Labour Welfare Fund (LWF): half-yearly contribution.
  • Gujarat Factories Act: for establishments with 10+ workers using power or 20+ without power.
  • GPCB (Gujarat Pollution Control Board): CTE / CTO for specific industries.
  • GIDC (Gujarat Industrial Development Corporation) land allocation if GIDC-based.

/ Gujarat-specific industrial advantages

Why Gujarat wins.

  • Dholera Special Investment Region (DSIR): dedicated 920 sq km investment region - semiconductor cluster + solar + specific industrial focus. Tata-PSMC fab + Micron ATMP located here.
  • Gujarat Semiconductor Policy 2022: state capex subsidy on top of central ISM. Up to 25% state subsidy + land at concessional rate + state SGST reimbursement + electricity rebate.
  • GIFT City (Gujarat International Finance Tec-City): dedicated IFSC. 500+ licensed entities (AIFs, banks, insurance, broker-dealers, bullion exchange). Section 10(4D) tax exemption.
  • Gujarat Industrial Policy 2020: comprehensive state support across manufacturing + technology + services. Capex subsidy + SGST reimbursement + electricity rebate.
  • Vibrant Gujarat: biennial investment summit; specific MOU + state facilitation.
  • Mundra + Pipavav ports: dedicated port + logistics ecosystem.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Why is Gujarat the semiconductor + manufacturing hub?
Dholera Special Investment Region (DSIR) + Gujarat Semiconductor Policy 2022 (up to 25% state capex subsidy) + Vibrant Gujarat industry support + Mundra + Pipavav ports + Ahmedabad logistics. Tata-PSMC fab + Micron ATMP located here. Combined central ISM + state subsidy stack is among most aggressive globally.
What's GIFT City and how is it different from Gujarat state?
GIFT City (Gujarat International Finance Tec-City) is a dedicated IFSC (International Financial Services Centre) in Gandhinagar. Regulated by IFSCA (not state GSTAT). Section 10(4D) tax exemption + specific sector licensing. Separate regulatory + tax framework but geographically in Gujarat state.
What state incentives does Gujarat Industrial Policy 2020 offer?
Comprehensive support: capex subsidy 10-25% based on sector + investment commitment, SGST reimbursement 50-100% for specified period, electricity rebate 1-3 rupees per kWh for specific duration, stamp duty + registration exemption, specific policy sub-additions for manufacturing / IT / cleantech.
