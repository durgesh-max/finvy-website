GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C for FY 2025-26: Mandatory vs Optional Tables After Notification 05/2026, Due 31 December 2026, Late Fee by Turnover, what you must fill this year and what you can still skip.

The GST annual return GSTR-9 and the reconciliation statement GSTR-9C for FY 2025-26 are due on 31 December 2026. No extension has been announced. Notification No. 05/2026-Central Tax of 26 October 2026 keeps last year’s optional tables optional for FY 2025-26 onwards and adds no new tables. The relaxations withdrawn from FY 2024-25 stay withdrawn: Table 6E (credit on imports), Table 7 (reversals, row by row) and Tables 12 and 13 (credit reversed or claimed in the next year) must be filled. GSTR-9 is required above INR 2 crore turnover and GSTR-9C above INR 5 crore. The late fee is INR 50, 100 or 200 a day depending on turnover. The return cannot be revised, and filing it early closes the window to claim FY 2025-26 input tax credit, which otherwise runs to 30 November 2026.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the due date for GSTR-9 for FY 2025-26?
31 December 2026 for both GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C, unless the government extends it. No extension had been announced as of 21 November 2026.
Which GSTR-9 tables are optional for FY 2025-26?
Under Notification 05/2026-Central Tax the earlier relaxations continue: exempt and nil-rated supplies can be combined in Table 5D, Tables 5H to 5K are optional, inputs and input services can be combined in Table 6, and Tables 15, 16 and 18 are optional.
Are Tables 12 and 13 of GSTR-9 mandatory?
Yes, where applicable. The option to skip Tables 12 and 13, and the shortcuts in Tables 6E and 7, were withdrawn from FY 2024-25 and have not been restored for FY 2025-26.
What is the late fee for GSTR-9?
INR 50 a day for turnover up to INR 5 crore and INR 100 a day for INR 5 to 20 crore, each capped at 0.04% of turnover in the State; INR 200 a day above INR 20 crore, capped at 0.5%.
Who must file GSTR-9C?
Taxpayers with aggregate turnover above INR 5 crore. It is self-certified; Tables 5B to 5N and Table 14 remain optional.
