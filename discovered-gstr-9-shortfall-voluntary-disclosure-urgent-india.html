/ Why voluntary disclosure makes sense

The economics.

  • Section 73 (non-fraud, voluntary): 10% penalty of tax OR INR 10,000 whichever higher. 3-year lookback from DRC-03 filing date. Interest at 18% per annum.
  • Section 74 (fraud / wilful suppression, discovered via notice): 100% penalty of tax + interest 18% + possible prosecution. 5-year lookback.
  • Difference: Section 73 vs 74 = 90% penalty saving + 2-year shorter lookback window.
  • Timing: must be BEFORE service of ASMT-10 (Section 61 scrutiny notice) OR DRC-01 (Section 73/74 demand notice) OR GST audit notice.
  • Window closes once Department serves specific notice.

/ Common GSTR-9 shortfall triggers

Where to look.

  • GSTR-1 vs 3B turnover mismatch - under-reported in 3B.
  • Section 17(5) blocked ITC claimed - must reverse with interest.
  • Section 16(4) time-barred ITC claimed beyond window.
  • Credit notes not accounted - affecting output tax.
  • Reverse charge mechanism (RCM) supplies not paid.
  • Place of supply errors - CGST+SGST vs IGST wrong.
  • Export under LUT bond without actual export compliance.
  • Advance received from customers above specific threshold not GST'd.
  • Specific sector classification error.

/ Form DRC-03 voluntary payment workflow

Step-by-step.

  1. Pre-filing audit identifies specific shortfalls + aggregates by month/year.
  2. Compute exact tax + interest at 18% per annum.
  3. 10% penalty / INR 10,000 (whichever higher) addition.
  4. Prepare Form DRC-03 on GST portal: specific month/year, specific HSN, specific reason, computation.
  5. Pay via Challan GST-04 (or Challan PMT-06).
  6. File Form DRC-03 on GST portal with payment reference.
  7. Receive acknowledgement + closure.
  8. Timing: BEFORE ASMT-10 / DRC-01 / audit notice served.

/ Specific advantages of DRC-03 route

Why act now.

  • 90% penalty saving vs Section 74 reactive compliance.
  • No specific legal battle - closes exposure permanently.
  • Clean compliance history - critical for pre-M&A due diligence + pre-IPO.
  • Avoid specific prosecution risk under Section 132 CGST (specific thresholds + wilful suppression).
  • Preserve ITC + specific credit for subsequent periods.
  • GSTR-9 can be filed with correct position.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Why voluntary disclosure before notice?
90% penalty saving. Section 73 (non-fraud, voluntary): 10% penalty / INR 10,000 whichever higher. Section 74 (reactive post-notice fraud/suppression): 100% penalty + 5-year lookback + specific prosecution risk. Specific action window closes once ASMT-10 / DRC-01 / GST audit notice served.
What's the deadline for DRC-03 voluntary disclosure?
No specific statutory deadline - but window closes once Department serves specific notice (ASMT-10 Section 61 scrutiny, DRC-01 Section 73/74 demand, GST audit notice). Must be filed BEFORE such service. Practical: before 31 December 2026 GSTR-9 FY 2024-25 deadline + before FY 2025-26 department focus on current year.
Can DRC-03 be filed for multiple years + multiple issues?
Yes - single DRC-03 can cover specific month/year/issue; multiple DRC-03s for different periods/issues. Each DRC-03 has specific tax + interest + penalty. For complex multi-year shortfalls: multiple DRC-03s with aggregated computation.
What's interest rate on DRC-03?
18% per annum simple interest from due date of GST return where shortfall occurred to date of DRC-03 payment. Specific calculation per Section 50 CGST Act. For 3-year lookback with 18% interest: can be material + 3-year compound effect approx 65%+ addition to tax.
