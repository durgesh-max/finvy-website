/ PAN Status Fix · Updated 2026-10-23

NRI PAN Status Update (2026): How to Change Your PAN to NRI or OCI Status, Check It Online and Fix an Inoperative PAN, deactivated PAN remediation.

NRI/OCI often finds their PAN marked 'inoperative' due to PAN-Aadhaar linking issue - despite NRIs being EXEMPT per CBDT Notification 37/2017. Common cause: PAN database shows holder as 'resident' from original Form 49A issuance when they were in India. Fix: update PAN status to NRI via a PAN change request + supporting documents. Here's the complete step-by-step remediation framework.

/ The exemption + the trap

Why it happens.

  • CBDT Notification 37/2017: NRIs EXEMPT from mandatory PAN-Aadhaar linking.
  • But PAN database must correctly reflect NRI status for exemption to apply.
  • Common scenario: person obtained PAN when resident (say 2010) + moved abroad post 2015 + PAN database still shows them as 'resident'.
  • Income Tax applies 'resident' status to PAN for Section 139AA linking requirement.
  • Linking deadline passed → PAN marked 'inoperative'.
  • NRI discovers when: TDS is deducted at the higher section 206AA rate, a refund is held back, or a bank or mutual fund KYC refresh flags the PAN.

/ PAN status-update fix workflow

Step-by-step.

  1. Document preparation:
  2.   — Proof of NRI status: foreign passport + visa / green card / OCI card.
  3.   — Foreign address proof: utility bill / bank statement / lease agreement.
  4.   — Existing PAN card copy.
  5.   — Passport-size photo.
  6. Online filing: NSDL (now Protean) e-governance portal: tin-nsdl.com → PAN services → Changes or Correction → select 'Non-resident' category.
  7. Fill a PAN change request online with specific NRI details.
  8. Upload documents and pay the correction fee shown on the portal.
  9. Submit for processing.
  10. If PAN already inoperative: filing a PAN change request simultaneously + requesting restoration. May require Jurisdictional AO contact.
  11. Processing time varies; keep the acknowledgement number.
  12. Updated PAN: database status changes to 'Non-Resident'. Section 139AA exemption applies. PAN reactivated.

/ Post-fix verification

Ongoing readiness.

  • Verify PAN status on Indian income tax e-filing portal: shows 'Non-Resident' flag.
  • Confirm with Indian payers that TDS is back at the normal or treaty rate (not the higher section 206AA rate).
  • Bank KYC refresh with updated NRI status.
  • Mutual fund + demat KYC with NRI status.
  • Form 10F (Form 41 from 1 April 2026) electronic filing enabled (login works).
  • Pending refunds are released on processing.
  • Indian ITR e-filing works.

/ What if PAN remains stuck

Escalation.

  • Jurisdictional AO visit (if PAN jurisdiction in India + specific escalation).
  • CBDT grievance redressal portal + specific escalation path.
  • Alternative: a fresh PAN application (not update) if specific fix not working - but then two PANs linked to same person (regularisation issue).
  • BQP handles complex NRI PAN status issues including specific jurisdictional problems.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Why is my (NRI) PAN inoperative despite being exempt?
PAN database still shows you as 'resident' from original Form 49A issuance. Section 139AA applies based on database status. CBDT Notification 37/2017 exemption needs correct NRI flag in database. Fix: update the NRI status through a PAN change request or the jurisdictional assessing officer (49AA is only for foreign citizens; from 1 April 2026 PAN forms are numbered 93-96).
How long does PAN NRI status update take?
Processing time varies and is not fixed by any rule. A complete application with clear proof of non-resident status avoids back-and-forth; keep the acknowledgement number and follow up with the Jurisdictional AO if the status does not change.
Is there a last date to update my PAN status to NRI?
No deadline is fixed for changing a PAN's residential status. The cost of waiting is practical: while the PAN wrongly shows you as a resident without an Aadhaar link it can become inoperative, so refunds are held, no refund interest is paid for that period and tax is deducted at the higher rate. Update it as soon as you become non-resident, and before a property sale, a large NRO withdrawal or filing a return claiming a refund.
How do I check my PAN status online as an NRI?
Use Verify Your PAN on the income tax e-filing portal: it shows whether the PAN is active or inoperative. If it is inoperative even though you are non-resident, the residential status in the PAN records needs correcting through a PAN change request or the jurisdictional assessing officer. Keep the acknowledgement and check again after the update is processed.
What happens if PAN remains inoperative?
Under CBDT Circular 3/2023, while a PAN is inoperative: refunds are not issued, no interest is paid on refunds for that period, and tax is deducted or collected at the higher rate (under section 206AA / 206CC, at least 20% or the normal rate if higher), so treaty rates are lost in practice. The return can still be filed. Banks, brokers and mutual funds may also flag the account at KYC refresh. Updating the PAN to non-resident status restores normal treatment going forward.
Can an NRI get an Aadhaar card?
Yes. An NRI with a valid Indian passport can enrol at any Aadhaar enrolment centre in India on any visit; the 182-day stay rule that applies to residents does not apply to NRIs. Aadhaar is optional for NRIs, and an NRI does not need it to keep a PAN operative if the PAN records show non-resident status.
What documents does an NRI need for Aadhaar?
A valid Indian passport, which UIDAI accepts as proof of identity, address and date of birth. If you want an address other than the one in the passport, bring any other address proof UIDAI accepts from residents. You also give an email address, and you can book an appointment at a centre through the UIDAI website.
Can an NRI in the USA or UAE apply for Aadhaar from abroad?
No. Enrolment needs biometrics at an Aadhaar centre in India, so it has to be done during a visit. OCI cardholders and foreign passport holders are not NRIs for Aadhaar and follow the resident-foreigner route, which needs 182 days of stay in India.
Can I use alternate PAN while updating?
No - two PANs linked to same person creates regularisation issue. Must update existing PAN to NRI status. Cannot have multiple active PANs.
