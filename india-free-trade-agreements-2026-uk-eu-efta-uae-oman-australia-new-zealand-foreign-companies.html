/ Foreign companies · Trade agreements · Updated 2026-11-20

India's Trade Agreements in 2026 (UK, EU, EFTA, UAE, Oman, Australia, New Zealand): What They Mean for Foreign Companies Sourcing, Manufacturing or Selling in India, status, benefits and the compliance that unlocks them.

India's trade-agreement network expanded quickly in 2025-26. The India-EFTA TEPA (Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein) took effect in October 2025, with an aspiration of USD 100 billion of EFTA investment over 15 years. The India-UK CETA, signed in July 2025, took effect in July 2026, alongside a social security convention for posted workers. The India-Oman CEPA was signed in December 2025. The India-New Zealand FTA was signed in April 2026. The India-EU FTA was concluded on 27 January 2026 and is awaiting signature and ratification. These sit alongside the India-UAE CEPA (since May 2022) and the India-Australia ECTA (since December 2022). For a foreign company, the value is lower duties on goods moving between India and the partner country, easier services and mobility terms, and a stronger case for manufacturing or sourcing in India for export. The duty benefit only applies if the goods meet the rules of origin and carry a valid certificate of origin, which is where most claims fail.

/ Making the benefit real

Where companies go wrong.

  • Rules of origin: each agreement sets value-addition or tariff-shift tests; keep cost and bill-of-materials records that prove origin.
  • Certificates of origin: issued through the designated agencies; errors lead to duty demands and penalties on audit.
  • Customs and GST in India: concessional duty on imports needs correct classification and documentation; GST still applies; see IEC, GST and customs.
  • Manufacturing in India for export: combine agreement benefits with India's manufacturing incentives; see China plus one: moving to India.
  • Services and people: mobility chapters and social security conventions reduce cost for posted staff; see expat tax in India.
  • Tax treaties are separate: trade agreements do not change income-tax treaty rates; see the Qatar and Oman treaty updates.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Which countries have free trade agreements with India in 2026?
In force: UAE (CEPA), Australia (ECTA), EFTA states (TEPA), the UK (CETA, from July 2026), and reportedly Oman and New Zealand; plus older agreements such as those with ASEAN, Japan and South Korea. The India-EU FTA was concluded in January 2026 and awaits signature.
Is the India-EU FTA in force?
Not yet. It was concluded on 27 January 2026; signature and ratification were pending as of November 2026.
What does the India-UK trade agreement change for UK companies?
Lower tariffs on many goods, services and mobility commitments, and a social security convention that helps posted workers avoid double contributions.
How do companies claim lower duty under an FTA?
By meeting the agreement's rules of origin and presenting a valid certificate of origin, with records that prove origin if customs audits the claim.
Do trade agreements change income-tax rates?
No. Income-tax treaty rates on dividends, interest and royalties are set by separate tax treaties.
