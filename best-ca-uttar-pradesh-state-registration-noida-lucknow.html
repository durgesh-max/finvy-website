/ Core UP state registrations

Mandatory.

  • UP Shops & Establishments Act.
  • UP Professional Tax: re-introduced in recent years.
  • UP Labour Welfare Fund.
  • UP Factories Act.
  • UPPCB (Uttar Pradesh Pollution Control Board).
  • NOIDA / Greater Noida / YEIDA Authority specific if based in these areas.
  • Specific Nagar Nigam Trade Licence (Lucknow Nagar Nigam, Kanpur Nagar Nigam, Varanasi Nagar Nigam).

/ UP industrial advantages

What UP offers.

  • UP Industrial Investment & Employment Promotion Policy: comprehensive support.
  • UPSIDA (UP State Industrial Development Authority): industrial parks across state.
  • Yamuna Expressway Industrial Development Authority (YEIDA): dedicated industrial development near Jewar Airport (Noida International Airport, operational 2024+).
  • Jewar Airport ecosystem: specific logistics + manufacturing + supply chain for aviation + export-oriented.
  • Specific sectors: electronics manufacturing (Noida IT corridor), food processing (specific clusters), leather (Kanpur / Agra), textile (Varanasi silk + others).
  • UP Electronics Manufacturing Cluster (EMC): specific electronics focus.
