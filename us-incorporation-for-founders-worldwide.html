/ What US incorporation covers

From entity choice to annual filings.

  • Choosing between a Delaware C-Corp (for venture capital and stock options) and an LLC (for bootstrapped or services businesses).
  • Formation, registered agent, EIN without an SSN, and US bank account guidance.
  • Founder stock, vesting and 83(b) elections; cap table set-up for a first round.
  • Annual US filings: Delaware franchise tax and annual report, Form 1120 or 5472, BOI-related checks, and state registrations.
  • Home-country compliance: FEMA ODI for Indian residents, transfer pricing once a home-country team bills the US entity, and India-to-US flips.

C-Corp or LLC?
A Delaware C-Corp if you plan to raise venture capital or grant stock options; an LLC for a bootstrapped or services business where pass-through taxation suits the owners.
Can I get an EIN without an SSN?
Yes. Foreign founders obtain an EIN from the IRS without an SSN; BQP prepares and files the application.
Do Indian residents need RBI approval to own a US company?
Under the automatic route, no prior approval is needed, but the investment must be reported under FEMA overseas direct investment rules through an authorised bank.
What does it cost?
