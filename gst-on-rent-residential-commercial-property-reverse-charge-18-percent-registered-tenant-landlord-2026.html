/ Landlords, tenants and businesses · GST · Updated 2026-11-25

GST on Rent (2026): Residential vs Commercial Property, 18% Rate, Reverse Charge for Registered Tenants and Landlord Registration, who pays depends on who is registered.

GST on rent is 18%, but whether it applies, and who pays it, depends on the type of property and on whether the landlord and the tenant are GST-registered. Renting a house or flat to an unregistered person for living in is exempt. Renting a residential dwelling to a GST-registered tenant is taxed under reverse charge, so the tenant pays. For commercial property, a registered landlord charges 18% on the invoice; if the landlord is unregistered and the tenant is registered, the tenant pays under reverse charge (since 10 October 2024), unless the tenant is under the composition scheme.

/ Quick answers

What landlords and tenants ask.

  • Is there GST on residential rent? Not when the tenant is an unregistered individual using it as a home. If the tenant is GST-registered, the tenant pays 18% under reverse charge.
  • A proprietor rents a flat to live in. GST? Renting to a registered proprietor in a personal capacity for his or her own residence is carved out; renting in the business’s name or for business use is under reverse charge.
  • Is there GST on commercial rent? Yes, 18%. A registered landlord charges it; if the landlord is unregistered and the tenant is registered (and not under composition), the tenant pays under reverse charge.
  • When must a landlord register? When aggregate turnover from taxable supplies, including commercial rent, exceeds ₹20 lakh in a year (₹10 lakh in special category states). Exempt residential rent is part of aggregate turnover but is not taxed.
  • Can the tenant claim input tax credit? A registered tenant using the premises for business can generally claim credit for GST paid, including under reverse charge, subject to the usual conditions.
  • Is TDS on rent worked out on rent plus GST? No. Where GST is shown separately on the invoice, TDS on rent is deducted on the rent excluding GST; see TDS on rent for FY 2026-27.

/ Practical points

Getting it right.

  • Tenants paying under reverse charge issue a self-invoice, pay the GST in cash (it cannot be paid from credit) and report it in GSTR-3B; the credit can then be taken where eligible.
  • Startups and companies renting a flat for a founder or as a guest house are registered tenants: reverse charge applies even if the flat is only lived in.
  • NRI landlords letting commercial property: an unregistered NRI landlord’s registered tenant pays under reverse charge; once taxable rent crosses the threshold the NRI must register. Income tax on the rent is separate; see NRI rental income and TDS.
  • Mixed use: a residential flat used as an office is rent for non-residential use; check the arrangement and the agreement.
  • Annual return: rent and reverse charge entries flow into the annual return; see GSTR-9 for FY 2025-26.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is GST applicable on rent of residential property?
Not when it is rented to an unregistered person as a residence. If the tenant is GST-registered and rents it for business or in the business's name, the tenant pays 18% GST under reverse charge. A registered proprietor renting in a personal capacity for his or her own home is carved out.
What is the GST rate on commercial rent?
18%. A registered landlord charges it on the invoice. If the landlord is unregistered and the tenant is a regular registered taxpayer, the tenant pays it under reverse charge, which applies from 10 October 2024.
Do composition taxpayers pay reverse charge on commercial rent?
No. From 16 January 2025, a tenant under the composition scheme is excluded from reverse charge on rent paid to an unregistered landlord for commercial property.
When does a landlord need GST registration?
When aggregate turnover from taxable supplies, such as commercial rent, crosses Rs 20 lakh in a financial year (Rs 10 lakh in special category states).
Is TDS on rent deducted on the GST amount too?
No. Where GST is shown separately on the invoice, TDS on rent is worked out on the rent amount excluding GST.
