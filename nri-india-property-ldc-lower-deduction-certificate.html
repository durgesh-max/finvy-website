/ NRI Guide · LDC · Updated 2026-10-15

NRI property sale, LDC saves you from a 20% TDS trap.

NRI selling Indian property: Section 195 requires buyer to deduct 20% TDS on sale consideration (not just gain). On INR 3 crore sale, that's INR 60 lakh locked up as TDS even if actual capital gain tax is INR 5 lakh. Lower Deduction Certificate (LDC) under Section 197 fixes this — apply to Indian Tax Officer, get a certificate specifying actual tax amount, buyer deducts only that amount. 4-8 week process. Here's the complete LDC playbook.

/ The 20% TDS problem

Why LDC exists.

Section 195 default: when buyer pays NRI seller for Indian property, buyer must deduct TDS at:

  • 20% + applicable surcharge + 4% cess on LTCG (property held > 24 months).
  • 30% + applicable surcharge + 4% cess on STCG (property held <= 24 months).
  • TDS applies on gross sale consideration — not on gain. So even if your gain is modest or nil, 20% of sale amount withheld.
  • TDS deposited by buyer with Indian government; NRI claims refund later in Indian ITR.

The problem: on INR 3 crore property with INR 50 lakh gain, actual tax under Section 112 (LTCG) at 12.5% without indexation = INR 6.25 lakh. But Section 195 TDS = 20% of INR 3 crore = INR 60 lakh. INR 54 lakh overpaid; locked in TDS pool until Indian ITR refund (6-18 months). Huge cash flow trap.

Section 197 LDC solution: NRI applies to Indian Tax Officer for certificate specifying lower TDS rate matching actual tax liability. Buyer then deducts the lower amount.

/ When LDC is worth the effort

Decision criteria.

  • Property sale value > INR 50 lakh: 20% TDS = INR 10 lakh. Worth 4-8 weeks of application effort.
  • Gain is less than sale value: standard for property sold with indexed cost or Section 54 reinvestment planned. Material TDS saving.
  • Timing allows 4-8 weeks: LDC takes typically 4-8 weeks (sometimes faster, sometimes longer). If buyer wants to close within 2 weeks, LDC may not be ready.
  • Section 54 / 54F reinvestment planned: capital gain will be fully / partially exempt → actual tax very low → LDC essentially zero rate → massive TDS saving.

For sale under INR 50 lakh or urgent closing (no LDC time), accept 20% TDS and claim refund in Indian ITR.

/ LDC application workflow

Step by step.

  1. Pre-requisite documentation:
  2.   — Property purchase deed (showing cost + date).
  3.   — Property sale agreement / draft (showing sale value + buyer details).
  4.   — NRI's PAN + proof of NRI status (passport + visa / OCI).
  5.   — Capital gain calculation with indexation + Section 54 reinvestment plan if applicable.
  6.   — Last 2-3 years Indian ITR of NRI (shows capital gain treatment history).
  7. File Form 13 on TRACES portal (traces.tdscpc.gov.in):
  8.   — NRI logs in with PAN + password.
  9.   — Navigate to 'Statement/Payment → Request for Lower / No Deduction Certificate'.
  10.   — Fill Form 13 online: Section 195, nature of payment (immovable property sale), payer details (buyer PAN), amount, requested TDS rate.
  11.   — Upload supporting documents.
  12.   — Submit with DSC or Aadhaar OTP (if NRI has either).
  13. Assigned to Jurisdictional AO: application moves to Assessing Officer having jurisdiction over NRI's PAN.
  14. AO review: may ask for clarifications, additional documents. Response via portal.
  15. LDC issued: specifies the applicable TDS rate (e.g., 1% of sale consideration vs 20% default). Valid for the specific transaction.
  16. Share LDC with buyer: buyer deducts the lower rate; deposits reduced TDS with government; files Form 27Q (Form 144 from 1 April 2026; all new form numbers) showing LDC reference.
  17. Final tax reconciliation: NRI files Indian ITR; any residual tax paid/refunded.

/ What LDC rate the AO typically approves

Realistic expectations.

  • LTCG on property held long term with indexation: AO calculates actual tax on indexed capital gain + applicable surcharge + cess, then LDC specifies that amount as % of sale consideration. Typically 2-5% of sale value for modest gain.
  • LTCG with Section 54 reinvestment plan: if NRI commits to buy new residential property within 2 years (or construct within 3 years), capital gain exempt → LDC may specify very low rate (0.1-1% of sale).
  • Section 54EC bonds: INR 50 lakh in REC / NHAI / PFC / IRFC bonds within 6 months of sale → INR 50 lakh of gain exempt → LDC reflects remaining tax.
  • Section 54F: full LTCG exemption on sale of long-term asset (non-residential) if net consideration invested in one residential property → LDC near zero.
  • No reinvestment, substantial gain: LDC rate close to actual tax rate (12.5% on indexed gain).

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is Lower Deduction Certificate (LDC) for NRI property sale?
A certificate issued by Indian Tax Officer under Section 197 Income Tax Act, specifying a TDS rate lower than Section 195 default (20% on LTCG / 30% on STCG), based on NRI's actual tax liability on the sale. Issued via Form 13 application on TRACES portal. Buyer deducts the LDC-specified rate instead of default - saves significant cash flow for NRI.
How long does LDC take?
4-8 weeks typically from Form 13 submission to LDC issuance. Can be faster (2-4 weeks) if application is well-documented + AO is responsive, or slower (8-12 weeks) if AO raises queries or jurisdictional transfer required. Plan the property sale timeline accordingly; LDC should be in hand before closing.
What's the typical LDC rate approved by AO?
Varies: 2-5% of sale consideration for property with modest LTCG; 0.1-1% if Section 54 reinvestment planned; close to 0% if full Section 54F exemption; close to 12.5% if large gain with no reinvestment. AO calculates actual tax under Section 112 (post Finance Act 2024 LTCG rules) and expresses as % of sale value for LDC.
Can I apply for LDC after the sale is done?
No - LDC must be in place BEFORE buyer makes payment / deducts TDS. Once 20% default TDS is deducted and deposited, the only remedy is refund via Indian ITR filing (6-18 month wait). LDC is prospective - apply well in advance.
Does the buyer need to do anything special with LDC?
Buyer references the LDC in Form 27Q TDS return + mentions it in the TDS challan. Buyer deducts the lower rate specified in LDC, deposits only that amount, files Form 27Q quarterly, issues Form 16A to NRI showing LDC rate. Standard procedure for buyer; most Indian buyers are familiar if advised at execution.
