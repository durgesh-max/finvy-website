/ Common notice types + response windows

The playbook.

  • Section 143(1) intimation: computer-generated intimation of return processed. If demand: 30 days to pay or respond. If refund: no action needed. If adjustment: review + specific response.
  • Section 143(2) scrutiny: ITR selected for detailed assessment. 15-30 day response for first notice + 6-month assessment window.
  • Section 148 reassessment: formal reassessment proceeding. 30 days to file return + specific response + Section 147 full assessment.
  • Section 148A(b) show-cause: pre-reassessment show cause. 7-30 days to respond + opportunity to quash before Section 148.
  • Section 139(9) defective return: ITR has defects. 15 days to rectify defects.
  • Section 142(1) info call: specific documents / information requested by AO. 15-30 days typical.
  • Section 245 adjustment: refund adjustment proposed against demand. 30 days to object.
  • Section 154 rectification: proposed rectification of mistake. 30 days to object.
  • Section 156 demand notice: tax demand notification. 30 days to pay OR 15 days to object + Section 220 interest.

/ Immediate action in first 24-72 hours

Preserve + plan.

  1. Acknowledge notice via Income Tax portal (incometax.gov.in - Login - Pending Actions).
  2. Note exact deadline (specified in notice).
  3. Download complete notice + supporting documents.
  4. Identify specific AY / specific income head / specific issue flagged.
  5. Preserve all records: ITR + Form 26AS (Form 168 from tax year 2026-27) + AIS/TIS + supporting documents + bank statements + agreements.
  6. Engage CA immediately - response window typically tight (7-30 days).
  7. Specific risk assessment: Section 148 reassessment / Section 270A penalty exposure / specific appeal implications.

/ Section 143(1) intimation response

The common first-contact.

  • Computer-generated intimation after ITR processing (typically 3-6 months after ITR filing).
  • Three outcomes:
  •   — Refund: Form 143(1) intimates refund + specific bank credit timeline (typically 2-6 weeks).
  •   — Demand: Form 143(1) intimates specific additional tax demand (interest 234A/B/C + specific adjustment).
  •   — No change: ITR accepted as filed - no action needed.
  • Response window for demand: 30 days to pay OR file rectification request under Section 154 OR appeal CIT(A) under Section 246A.
  • Common issues: TDS mismatch, Section 234A/B/C interest computation, specific disallowance (self-attested).
  • Rectification under Section 154: if specific apparent-on-face error.
  • Appeal: if substantive disagreement.

/ Section 143(2) scrutiny response

The detailed assessment.

  • ITR selected for scrutiny under Section 143(3).
  • CASS (Computer Aided Scrutiny Selection) OR manual selection by AO.
  • 15-30 day response for first notice.
  • 6-month assessment window + multiple hearings.
  • Documents demanded: full reconciliation of ITR + Form 26AS + AIS + supporting documents.
  • Oral representation + specific document submission.
  • Section 143(3) order within 6 months + specific additions / disallowances.
  • CIT(A) appeal within 30 days of adverse order.
  • Section 270A penalty 50-200% on additions.

/ Section 148 / 148A response

The reassessment track.

  • Section 148A(b) show-cause: AO must issue BEFORE Section 148 formal notice (post Finance Act 2021 amendment).
  • Response window: 7-30 days typical.
  • Response strategy: documentary refutation + legal position + specific exhibits.
  • Section 148A(d) order: AO decides whether to proceed with Section 148 OR drop.
  • If AO drops: no reassessment. Case closed.
  • If AO proceeds: Section 148 notice + 30 days to file return + Section 147 full assessment.
  • Writ in High Court if Section 148A procedure defective (specific case-law + specific grounds).
  • Specific 4-year vs 10-year lookback limit assessment.

/ Common response mistakes + how to avoid

What to watch.

  • Not acknowledging notice via portal (deadlines keep running).
  • Missing response deadline (AO proceeds with specific adverse presumptions).
  • Not engaging CA (DIY response often substandard).
  • Not preserving records (specific documentary refutation becomes difficult).
  • Not understanding risk cascade (143(2) can lead to 148, 148A to 148, specific appeal implications).
  • Not raising specific legal grounds (jurisdictional / procedural / substantive).
  • Not considering specific appeal path (CIT(A) / ITAT / High Court / writ) at outset.
  • Not managing Section 270A penalty defence (classification 50% vs 200% matters materially).

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How to respond to Income Tax notice India?
First identify notice type (143(1) / 143(2) / 148 / 148A / 139(9) / 142(1) / 245 / 154 / 156). Each has distinct response window + consequences. Acknowledge on portal + preserve records + engage CA within 24-48 hours + respond within specified deadline (typically 7-30 days). Default: no response = AO proceeds with specific adverse presumptions. BQP urgent response team.
What's the typical response deadline for Income Tax notice?
Varies by notice type. Section 143(1) demand: 30 days. Section 143(2) scrutiny first response: 15-30 days + 6-month assessment window. Section 148A(b): 7-30 days. Section 148: 30 days to file return. Section 139(9) defective: 15 days. Section 142(1): 15-30 days. Section 245: 30 days. Section 154: 30 days. Section 156: 30 days to pay / 15 days to object.
Can Section 148A show-cause be quashed before Section 148 reassessment?
Yes - Section 148A(b) response is quashing opportunity. Well-drafted response can achieve Section 148A(d) order dropping the proceeding (no Section 148 formal notice). For procedurally defective 148A: writ in High Court can quash (several High Court rulings have struck down defective 148A). Specific legal grounds + specific case law critical.
What happens if I don't respond to Income Tax notice?
AO proceeds with only AO's version considered. Specific adverse presumptions. Section 148 reassessment if 148A unresponded. Section 143(3) ex-parte scrutiny order if 143(2) unresponded. Section 270A penalty 50-200%. Section 234A/B/C interest. Recovery proceedings under specific sections. Specific CIBIL impact for individuals.
