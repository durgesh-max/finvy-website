/ IPO readiness workstreams

Six parallel streams.

  • Financial restatement to Ind AS: 3-5 year audited financials in Ind AS 115 / Ind AS 102 / Ind AS 116 / Ind AS 109 compliance. Specific adjustments: revenue recognition (ASC 606 analog), lease accounting (ASC 842 analog), SBC, financial instruments.
  • SEBI ICDR Regulations compliance: 3-year promoter continuity, promoter / promoter group identification, public shareholding dilution, lock-in requirements.
  • DRHP (Draft Red Herring Prospectus) drafting: with Merchant Banker + Legal Counsel. 300+ page document; detailed company + industry + risk factor + financial disclosure.
  • Vendor coordination: Merchant Banker + Legal Counsel + RTA + Depository + Printer + Advertising + PR + Investor Roadshow organiser.
  • Price band + pricing strategy: book-building (Main Board) or fixed-price (SME) decision + market timing.
  • Post-listing compliance: LODR Regulations 2015 + quarterly / half-yearly / annual filings + corporate governance + disclosure obligations.

/ SME IPO vs Main Board decision

The strategic choice.

SME IPO (NSE Emerge / BSE SME):

  • Smaller issue size (INR 3-25 crore typical).
  • Pre-IPO net worth INR 1-3 crore minimum.
  • Lower disclosure burden.
  • Fixed-price route (not book-building).
  • 50% minimum promoter lock-in 3 years + 50% for 1 year.
  • Later migration to Main Board after 2 years.
  • Appropriate for revenue INR 20-200 crore, EBITDA INR 3-30 crore.

Main Board IPO (NSE / BSE):

  • Larger issue size (INR 100+ crore typical).
  • Pre-IPO net worth higher thresholds + profitability track record.
  • Book-building route.
  • Full SEBI ICDR + LODR compliance.
  • Anchor investor allotment 60% of QIB portion.
  • Appropriate for revenue INR 200+ crore, EBITDA INR 30+ crore.

/ Common pre-IPO issues

Where readiness surfaces gaps.

  • Related party transactions (Section 188 Companies Act) - specific disclosure + arm's-length verification.
  • Section 56(2)(viib) legacy angel tax - historical rounds pre-abolition reviewed.
  • FEMA breaches - FC-GPR / FLA / APR missed filings - compounding before IPO.
  • Rule 9B demat non-compliance - mandatory pre-IPO.
  • Promoter + Promoter Group accurate identification - SEBI ICDR specific definition.
  • ESOP compliance (SEBI SBEB Regulations).
  • Internal Financial Controls (ICFR) audit sign-off.
  • Material litigation disclosure + indemnity structuring.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's typical pre-IPO engagement timeline?
12-24 months for Main Board; 6-12 months for SME IPO. Phases: IPO readiness assessment (1-2 months), Ind AS restatement + 3-year audited track record (3-6 months), DRHP drafting + regulatory filings (3-6 months), roadshow + pricing + listing (2-4 months). Longer if pre-IPO corporate restructuring required (FEMA cleanup, demat, legacy angel tax).
Can my startup IPO on SME platform vs Main Board?
SME platform (NSE Emerge / BSE SME) for smaller issues (INR 3-25 crore), revenue INR 20-200 crore, EBITDA INR 3-30 crore. Main Board for larger issues (INR 100+ crore), higher revenue + profitability. Choice depends on readiness, growth plan, promoter liquidity needs, market timing. BQP comparative analysis part of scope.
What's the typical Ind AS restatement adjustment?
Common adjustments: ASC 606 → Ind AS 115 revenue recognition (performance obligation + variable consideration), ASC 842 → Ind AS 116 lease (operating lease to right-of-use asset + lease liability), ASC 718 → Ind AS 102 SBC fair-value based, ASC 326 → Ind AS 109 expected credit loss, deferred tax under Ind AS 12. Materially different financial position vs Indian GAAP in many cases.
What's SEBI ICDR 3-year promoter continuity rule?
Promoter should have held their current shareholding for 3 years prior to filing DRHP (with specific exceptions). Promoter identification per SEBI ICDR Reg. 2(1)(oo). Promoter group includes immediate relatives + specified holding structures. Dilution restrictions during 3-year window. Critical for IPO timing - any recent major share transfer by promoter affects eligibility.
What are the biggest pre-IPO compliance gaps BQP sees?
Top 5: (1) FEMA breaches - missed FC-GPR / FLA / APR, needs compounding. (2) Rule 9B demat non-compliance - mandatory for IPO. (3) Section 56(2)(viib) legacy angel tax - historical rounds pre-abolition. (4) Related party transaction documentation + arm's-length. (5) ESOP compliance under SEBI SBEB Regulations. BQP pre-IPO readiness audit identifies all of these.
