/ Step 1: Define what you need

Clarity first.

Foreign company engaging Indian CA typically needs one or more of:

  • India entity setup (WOS / Branch / Liaison) or operational compliance for existing Indian subsidiary.
  • Due diligence on Indian target company (M&A / investment).
  • Form 15CA/15CB (Forms 145/146 from 1 April 2026) CA certificate for foreign remittance.
  • OIDAR GST registration + monthly compliance.
  • Transfer pricing (Form 3CEB [Form 48 from tax year 2026-27]) + benchmarking study.
  • Annual audit coordination (statutory + tax + TP).
  • Section 195 TDS advisory + opinion letter.
  • Profit repatriation (dividend / royalty / FTS / buyback).
  • India market entry / research / supplier verification.
  • FEMA compounding of past breaches.
  • India IP registration (trademark / patent).
  • EOR India or own-subsidiary hiring setup.

Define needs in a one-paragraph email - BQP can advise on specific scope.

/ Step 3: Scoping call

30 minutes.

What to discuss on the scoping call:

  • Specific business situation + context.
  • Specific deliverables you need.
  • Timeline + specific deadlines.
  • Format preferences (PDF / Word / Excel / Google Workspace / Microsoft 365).
  • Communication preferences (email / WhatsApp / weekly call cadence).
  • Specific regulatory / compliance context (prior audits, pending notices, ongoing transactions).
  • Co-ordination with your foreign counsel / Big-4 global auditor / US CPA (if applicable).

BQP partner CA Durgesh Chavda personally conducts scoping calls - not junior staff.

/ Step 4: Engagement letter + onboarding

Signed remotely.

  1. Receive scoped quote email with engagement letter attached (PDF).
  2. Review scope + fee + timeline + deliverables.
  3. Sign electronically (DocuSign / HelloSign / Adobe Sign) + email back.
  4. BQP counter-signs + returns.
  5. Shared workspace setup (Google Drive / Dropbox / Microsoft 365 / Box - your preference).
  6. Document request list issued (what we need from you).
  7. Delivery begins.

/ Step 5: Delivery cadence

Weekly status + ad-hoc.

  • Weekly 30-min status call (Zoom at your time zone).
  • Deliverables shared via workspace in your preferred format.
  • Email / WhatsApp for ad-hoc questions.
  • Partner-level access: no junior-staff gatekeeping.
  • Co-ordination with your foreign counsel if cross-border matter.
  • Co-ordination with your US CPA / UK accountant if dual-tax matter.
  • Co-ordination with Big-4 group auditor if parent uses Big-4.

/ Step 6: Final deliverable + post-engagement

Closure + continuity.

  • Final deliverables shared + signed off.
  • 30-day Q&A window on deliverables.
  • Ongoing retainer available if continuing need (annual compliance, quarterly MIS, periodic Form 15CB).
