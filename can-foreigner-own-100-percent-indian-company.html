Press Note 3 affects beneficial ownership from land-bordering countries (China, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Afghanistan, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar). / Specific sectoral caps + government approval route

The exceptions.

  • Banking (private): 74% cap (49% automatic + 25% approval).
  • Insurance: 74% cap (automatic for specific sub-sectors; approval beyond).
  • Defence: 100% but 74% automatic + beyond needs government approval; sensitive sub-sectors stricter.
  • Pension + annuity: 74% cap.
  • Multi-brand retail trading: 51% + government approval + specific conditions.
  • Print media (news): 26% cap + approval route; magazine 100% specific.
  • Broadcasting (news channels): 49% + specific limits.
  • Telecom: 100% automatic post 2021 (previously 74%+approval).
  • Satellite: specific sector-specific limits.
  • Atomic energy / specific sensitive: prohibited for private FDI.
  • Lottery / gambling / chit funds: prohibited.
  • Tobacco products: prohibited for FDI in manufacturing.
  • Real estate business + farm house: prohibited (except specific development projects).

/ Press Note 3 (April 2020) impact

Beneficial ownership rule.

  • Press Note 3 (April 2020) + Rule 6(a) of FEM (Non-Debt Instruments) Rules 2019: any FDI from entity INCORPORATED IN or where BENEFICIAL OWNER is located in / citizen of land-bordering country requires prior government approval.
  • Land-bordering countries: China, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Afghanistan, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar.
  • Beneficial ownership threshold: specific 10%+ beneficial ownership (parallel to SBO rules).
  • Not just direct investment - also acquisition of control + specific transfer scenarios.
  • Approval process: Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) + specific inter-ministerial review + specific national security angle.
  • Timeline: 60-180 days typical for approval.

/ FEMA compliance workflow for 100% foreign ownership

The steps.

  1. Confirm sector + 100% automatic route applicability (or government approval route).
  2. Press Note 3 beneficial ownership screening (land-bordering country check).
  3. Incorporate Indian company (Private Ltd typical for foreign subsidiaries) + MCA registration + Section 139A PAN.
  4. Open AD-I category bank account.
  5. Foreign shareholder's remittance via SWIFT: inward FDI.
  6. Specific pricing guideline compliance: valuation at fair market value (FMV) by specific merchant banker / CA.
  7. Form FC-GPR filing within 30 days of share allotment via FIRMS portal.
  8. Form FLA Annual Return (July 15 following FY-end).
  9. Section 90 beneficial ownership (SBO): Form BEN-1 by foreign shareholder + Form BEN-2 by company.
  10. Specific OPI (Overseas Portfolio Investment) if portfolio investment path.
  11. Ongoing: annual compliance + Form MGT-7 + AOC-4 + specific transfer pricing if related party.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can a foreigner own 100% of an Indian company?
Yes - in most sectors under automatic FDI route (no prior approval needed). Specific sectoral caps: banking 74%, insurance 74%, multi-brand retail 51%+approval, print media (news) 26%+approval, defence beyond 74% needs approval. Press Note 3 (April 2020): investment from land-bordering countries (China, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Afghanistan, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar) requires government approval regardless of sector.
Which sectors are prohibited for 100% foreign ownership?
Prohibited: atomic energy, lottery / gambling / chit funds, tobacco products (manufacturing), real estate business / farm house (except specific development projects). All other sectors: either 100% automatic, or cap-restricted automatic, or government approval route.
How long does FDI setup take for foreign investor?
Automatic route: 4-6 weeks typical (incorporation + MCA + PAN + GST + banking + FC-GPR filing + specific compliance). Government approval route (sector cap / Press Note 3): 90-180 days additional for DPIIT approval before incorporation. BQP standard engagement delivers 100% automatic route setup in 4-6 weeks.
What's Press Note 3 impact on foreign ownership?
Press Note 3 (April 2020): Any FDI from entity incorporated in / beneficial owner from land-bordering country (China, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Afghanistan, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar) requires prior government approval regardless of sector + regardless of amount. Approval: DPIIT via FIFP portal + 60-180 day timeline + inter-ministerial national-security review.
