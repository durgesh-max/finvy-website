/ All questions

Grouped by topic.

Taking money out of India

How much money can an NRI transfer from India abroad in a year?
NRE and FCNR balances can be sent freely with no limit. NRO balances (rent, dividends, pension, old savings) and property sale proceeds can be sent up to USD 1 million per financial year (April to March), with Form 15CA and a Chartered Accountant's Form 15CB.

Can I move money from my NRO account to my NRE account?
Yes. It counts as repatriation within the USD 1 million limit and needs the same Form 15CA and 15CB paperwork. Once in NRE it is freely repatriable.

What are Form 15CA and Form 15CB?
Form 15CB is a Chartered Accountant's certificate on the nature of the payment and the tax position; Form 15CA is the remitter's online declaration based on it. Under the Income-tax Act, 2025 they are Form 146 and Form 145 from 1 April 2026.

How long does a Form 15CB take?
Usually within 24 hours once the CA has the documents: source of money, tax paid, PAN and bank details. The bank then remits, often the same or next working day.

Can I do the repatriation from abroad without travelling to India?
Yes. Form 15CB is signed digitally, Form 15CA is filed online and most banks accept the remittance request by secure message or email.

Can I send money from India to buy a house abroad?
An NRI can use NRE funds freely or NRO funds within USD 1 million a year. A resident in India can buy property abroad under LRS within USD 250,000 a year, with TCS of 20% above INR 10 lakh.

Sending money to India

Is money I send to my parents in India taxable?
No. Gifts from a relative are not taxable for the receiver in India, whatever the amount, and India does not tax the transfer itself. Keep the bank's inward remittance advice.

Is a gift to a friend in India taxable?
For the friend, yes, once gifts from non-relatives exceed INR 50,000 in the financial year; then the whole amount is taxable as their income.

Is there a US tax on sending money to India?
Only the 1% federal remittance excise tax from 1 January 2026, and only on transfers paid in cash, money order or cashier's cheque. Transfers from a US bank account or card are not covered.

Should I send money to my NRE or NRO account?
Money earned abroad usually goes to NRE: interest is tax-free in India and it stays freely repatriable. NRO is for Indian income; its interest is taxable in India.

Parents sending money abroad

How much can my parents send me abroad?
Up to USD 250,000 per parent per financial year under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme.

What TCS applies when parents send money abroad?
From 1 April 2026: 20% of the amount above INR 10 lakh a year for gifts or maintenance; 2% above INR 10 lakh for self-funded education or medical treatment; nil for education paid from a loan by a specified lender; a flat 2% on overseas tour packages.

Is TCS refundable?
Yes. It is a prepaid tax credited to the parent, claimed in their Indian return or adjusted against salary TDS.

Where you live

Is NRE interest taxable in the UK, US, Canada or Australia?
Yes, for residents of those countries. It is tax-free only in India. Declare it on your return there.

Do I need to report my Indian bank accounts to the IRS?
Yes, if you are a US person: FBAR if your foreign accounts together exceeded USD 10,000 at any time in the year, and Form 8938 above USD 50,000 at year-end or USD 75,000 at any time (single, living in the US).

Do I need Form 3520 for money from my parents in India?
If you are a US person and gifts or bequests from non-US persons exceed USD 100,000 in the year. It is a report, not a tax, but the late-filing penalty is steep.

Has the UK remittance basis ended?
Yes, from 6 April 2025. UK residents are taxed on worldwide income and gains, with four years of relief for new arrivals who were non-resident for the previous 10 tax years. Former non-doms can bring older foreign income and gains in at 12% in 2025-26 and 2026-27 (15% in 2027-28) under the Temporary Repatriation Facility.

Do I need Form T1135 in Canada for my Indian accounts?
Yes, if the total cost of your foreign property (Indian deposits, shares, funds, rented property; not personal-use property) exceeded CAD 100,000 at any time in the year.

Is money from my parents in India taxable in Australia?
No, Australia has no gift tax. Keep proof that it is a genuine gift (bank records and a gift note), because the ATO looks at offshore money described as gifts or loans (TA 2021/2).

Do I need to report Indian accounts in Germany, the Netherlands or Ireland?
Residents are taxed on worldwide income, so Indian interest and gains go on your return there with a credit for Indian tax. Indian banks share account data with European tax authorities under the Common Reporting Standard.

Property and inheritance

Why is TDS deducted on the full price when an NRI sells property?
Without a lower deduction certificate the buyer deducts tax on the whole sale price. Apply on Form 128 (formerly Form 13) before signing, so TDS matches the real tax on the gain.

Do I pay tax twice when I sell Indian property while living abroad?
Both countries usually tax the gain, but the Indian tax is credited against the foreign tax under the treaty, so you pay roughly the higher of the two.

Is there inheritance tax in India?
No. India has no inheritance or estate tax, and an inheritance is not income for the heir. The money can be repatriated within USD 1 million a year with proof of inheritance and Form 15CA/15CB.

Can an NRI inherit agricultural land?
Yes, by inheritance NRIs and OCIs can hold any Indian property, including agricultural land; it can only be sold to a resident Indian citizen.

Returning to India and FAST-DS

What happens to my NRE account when I move back to India?
Once you become resident, NRE and NRO accounts must be redesignated as resident accounts; foreign currency can be held in an RFC (resident foreign currency) account.

Do I have to declare my UK pension, ISA, 401(k), RRSP or super in India?
Yes, in Schedule FA of your Indian return from your first year as resident and ordinarily resident. RNOR years generally need no Schedule FA.

What is FAST-DS 2026?
A one-time scheme for foreign assets missing from Schedule FA. Assets built from income earned while non-resident usually fall in Category 2: a flat INR 1 lakh fee for a value up to INR 5 crore. Undisclosed foreign income is Category 1 at 60%.

What is the FAST-DS deadline?
Declarations close on 31 December 2026.
