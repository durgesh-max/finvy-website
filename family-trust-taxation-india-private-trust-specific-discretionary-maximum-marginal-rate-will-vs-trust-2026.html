/ Families, business owners and NRIs planning succession · Private family trusts · Updated 2026-11-29

Family Trust Taxation in India (2026): How Private Trusts Are Taxed, Specific vs Discretionary Trusts, the Maximum Marginal Rate, Gifts to a Trust and Will vs Trust, the deed decides the tax.

A private family trust is taxed through its trustees, but the rate depends on how the deed is drafted. If the beneficiaries and their shares are fixed (a specific trust), income is taxed at the beneficiaries’ own rates; if the trustees decide who gets what (a discretionary trust), income is generally taxed at the maximum marginal rate, with limited exceptions. Settling property on a trust for relatives is not taxed as a gift, and India has no inheritance tax.

/ How a family trust is taxed

Sections 160 to 164 of the Income-tax Act, 1961.

TypeHow income is taxed
Specific (determinate) trustAt the beneficiaries' applicable rates - in the trustees' hands as representative assessees or directly in the beneficiaries' hands
Discretionary trustGenerally at the maximum marginal rate, with limited exceptions (some will trusts and trusts for dependent relatives with low incomes)
Trust with business incomeGenerally at the maximum marginal rate, with limited exceptions
Revocable transfer to the trustIncome taxed in the settlor's hands
Trust benefiting the settlor's spouse or son's wifeIncome can be clubbed with the settlor
  • Setting up: a settlor transfers property to trustees for named beneficiaries under a trust deed governed by the Indian Trusts Act, 1882; the trust takes a PAN and files returns through its trustees.
  • Gifts to the trust: property received by a trust created solely for the benefit of the settlor’s relatives is not taxed as a gift.
  • New Act: the same rules continue in the Income-tax Act, 2025 from tax year 2026-27, under new section numbers.

/ Will vs trust

Which suits the family?

  • Will: takes effect only on death; depending on where it is made and for whom, probate may be needed before assets pass.
  • Trust: works during the settlor’s lifetime; assets in the trust pass under the deed, giving continuity and control over when beneficiaries receive them.
  • Tax neutrality: India has no inheritance or estate tax, so the choice is mostly about control, continuity, protection and the income-tax rate on trust income.
  • Transfer costs: moving property into a trust can attract state stamp duty and registration - check before transferring real estate.
  • NRI beneficiaries or trustees: FEMA rules on transfers to and distributions for non-residents need checking along with the tax position.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is a family trust taxable in India?
Yes. A private family trust files returns through its trustees. Income of a specific trust, where beneficiaries and shares are fixed, is taxed at the beneficiaries' rates; income of a discretionary trust is generally taxed at the maximum marginal rate, with limited exceptions.
What is the tax rate for a discretionary family trust?
Generally the maximum marginal rate. Exceptions include certain trusts created by will and trusts set up for relatives dependent on the settlor where the beneficiaries' incomes are below the taxable limit.
Is transferring property to a family trust taxed as a gift?
Not where the trust is created solely for the benefit of the settlor's relatives - such receipts are exempt from tax on gifts. Stamp duty and registration on transferring real estate are separate and depend on the state.
Will or trust - which is better in India?
A will takes effect only on death and may need probate depending on where it is made and for whom. A trust works during the settlor's lifetime and lets assets pass under the deed with more control. India has no inheritance tax, so the choice turns mainly on control, continuity and the tax rate on trust income.
Is income from a family trust clubbed with the settlor?
It can be. Income from a revocable transfer is taxed in the settlor's hands, and income from assets settled for the benefit of the settlor's spouse or son's wife can be clubbed with the settlor.
