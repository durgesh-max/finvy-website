Setting Up a Global Capability Centre (GCC) in India in 2026: Entity, 15.5% Safe Harbour and Compliance Checklist, a captive team with fewer tax arguments.

A Global Capability Centre is a company's own engineering, operations or analytics team in India, usually run as a wholly owned Indian subsidiary that charges the parent cost plus a margin. Two 2026 developments make it easier to justify: from tax year 2026-27 a single transfer pricing safe harbour of 15.5% on operating expenses covers software, ITeS, KPO and contract R&D for transactions up to INR 2,000 crore, approved for five years; and the uncertainty over the USD 100,000 H-1B fee has pushed more US companies to build teams in India instead. The set-up itself is a sequence: entity, bank and FDI reporting, intercompany agreement, safe harbour election, then payroll and ongoing compliance.

/ Set-up sequence

From decision to first payroll.

  1. Choose the model: start with an employer of record or contractors for a pilot team, or go straight to a subsidiary; see EOR vs contractor vs subsidiary.
  2. Incorporate a private limited company: 100% foreign ownership is allowed under the automatic route for IT and IT-enabled services; see setting up a wholly owned subsidiary.
  3. Capitalise and report: bring in share capital through banking channels at not less than fair value and file Form FC-GPR within 30 days of allotment.
  4. Intercompany agreement: a services agreement with the parent setting out the functions, cost base and mark-up, signed before invoicing starts.
  5. Elect the safe harbour where it fits, so the cost-plus margin is accepted for five years.
  6. Register and run: GST with a letter of undertaking for zero-rated exports, payroll registrations, and STPI or SEZ registration if the facilitation suits the business.

The 2026 safe harbour changes the maths for mid-size GCCs

A single 15.5% cost-plus margin for IT, ITeS, KPO and contract R&D, now available up to INR 2,000 crore and approved for five years, removes most transfer pricing disputes for a new centre. We set up the entity, the intercompany agreement and the safe harbour filing: WhatsApp CA Durgesh Chavda.

Outside the safe harbour, the centre needs a transfer pricing study, an annual accountant’s report and documentation; see transfer pricing for foreign MNCs and a US company’s Indian engineering centre.

/ Points that cause trouble later

Plan them in.

  • Permanent establishment: keep contract authority with the parent and the Indian company's role documented, so the parent does not create a taxable presence in India.
  • GST classification: make sure the services qualify as exports and watch the intermediary question for support functions.
  • SEZ: the old SEZ income-tax holiday is closed to units that began after 31 March 2020; SEZ and STPI still give customs and procedural benefits.
  • Employee stock plans: parent RSUs granted to Indian employees create perquisite tax, withholding and cross-charge questions.
  • Annual cycle: audit, corporate tax return, transfer pricing report, the FLA return by 15 July and ROC filings.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the transfer pricing safe harbour for GCCs in India from 2026?
From tax year 2026-27, a single safe harbour covers software development, ITeS, KPO and contract R&D: a 15.5% margin on operating expenses for transactions up to INR 2,000 crore, approved for five years once elected. It replaces the earlier 17-24% margins and INR 300 crore limit.
Can a US company own 100% of an Indian GCC?
Yes. IT and IT-enabled services are under the automatic route for 100% foreign direct investment. The Indian company reports the investment on Form FC-GPR within 30 days of allotting shares.
Should we start with an employer of record or a subsidiary?
An employer of record suits a small pilot team; a subsidiary suits a planned centre with a growing team. Many companies start with an EOR and move to a subsidiary once the team passes a handful of people.
Does the H-1B fee matter for an India GCC decision?
It has added to the case for building teams in India. The USD 100,000 fee announced in September 2025 was extended in September 2026 and has faced conflicting court rulings, so check the current position with US immigration counsel.
Do GCCs still get SEZ tax holidays?
Not for units that started after 31 March 2020. SEZ and STPI registration can still offer customs and procedural benefits.
