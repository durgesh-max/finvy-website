/ When Big-4 is the right choice

The clear scenarios.

  • Listed Indian company with INR 1,000 crore+ turnover needing statutory audit + global network support.
  • Fortune 500 Indian subsidiary needing full ERP + consolidation + global reporting integration.
  • Pre-IPO (Main Board, INR 1,000 crore+ issue) where SEBI expects Big-4 financial auditor.
  • Specific sector requiring Big-4 brand (listed-company lender / rating-agency / investor confidence).
  • Multinational merger / acquisition requiring 24-country integrated DD / tax structuring.
  • Complex regulatory investigation / forensic matter requiring global network.
  • Pillar 2 compliance for mega-cap group (consolidated revenue USD 10B+).

/ When BQP is the right choice

The sweet spot.

  • Startup / SMB / mid-market (INR 10-1,000 crore revenue) wanting specialist advisory + transparent fee + partner-level attention.
  • Foreign company entering India (US / UK / EU / Singapore / UAE) wanting remote-first delivery + integrated CA + CS + tax + legal under one firm.
  • Foreign PE / VC fund with 5-30 Indian portfolio companies needing fund-level engagement + consistent compliance standards.
  • NRI / US green card holder wanting cross-border India-US dual-tax coordination.
  • Mid-market M&A buyer wanting India target DD + SPA input + buyer counsel coordination (3-6 week turnaround).
  • Specialist matters: Form 15CB urgent, Section 148 response, FEMA compounding, DPDP compliance, Rule 9B demat remediation, PLI scheme application.
  • SME IPO + Main Board IPO under INR 500 crore issue size.

/ Combined engagement model

When both firms work together.

  • Common structure for foreign-parent Indian subsidiary: Big-4 as group auditor (US / UK HQ parent) + BQP as Indian specialist (TP + FEMA + ESOP + specific advisory).
  • Pre-IPO (Main Board): Big-4 as auditor + BQP as pre-IPO specialist advisor (SEBI ICDR + Ind AS restatement + DRHP input).
  • Cross-border M&A: Big-4 as global DD lead + BQP as India specialist DD (tax + FEMA + Rule 9B + 56(2)(viib) legacy).
  • BEPS Pillar 2: Big-4 as group Pillar 2 advisor + BQP as India QDMTT + Form 3CEAE/F/G specialist.
  • Portfolio PE / VC: Big-4 at specific larger portfolio companies + BQP at smaller mid-market portfolio.

No conflict - complementary. BQP regularly works alongside Big-4 teams on cross-border mandates.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Should my startup engage BQP or Big-4?
Depends on stage + budget + ambition. Pre-Series B / mid-market: BQP typically better fit - specialist attention, scoped fee, partner-level, faster. Series C+ / unicorn / pre-IPO Main Board: often Big-4 for brand + global network, BQP for specific specialist areas. Combined engagement common.
Is BQP's fee materially different from Big-4?
Yes - typically 50-70% lower for comparable scope due to lower overhead + partner-direct delivery model. Scoped engagement letter means fixed fee + no hourly surprises. For mid-market mandates, this adds up to significant savings while maintaining ICAI-qualified CA competence.
Does BQP coordinate with Big-4 on cross-border matters?
Yes - standard. BQP's work-product feeds into Big-4 group audit + global tax structuring + Pillar 2 compliance. Co-ordinated with Big-4 teams on specific mandates: Indian subsidiary audit + global group audit; India DD + global DD; India Pillar 2 QDMTT + group Pillar 2 compliance. No conflict - complementary.
What if my Indian subsidiary needs Big-4 brand for lender comfort?
Common. Structure: Big-4 as statutory auditor for lender comfort + BQP as advisor for TP + FEMA + specialist matters. Both firms co-ordinate on information flows. BQP's work-product goes into Big-4 statutory audit workpapers.
For urgent matters (Form 15CB, Section 148 response), BQP is better?
Typically yes. BQP's 24-48 hour urgent response time is faster than most Big-4 standard engagement cycles. For time-sensitive specialist matters (Form 15CB CA certificate for VC funding wire, Section 148A show-cause response, FEMA deadline), BQP's partner-direct model + specialist depth works better.
