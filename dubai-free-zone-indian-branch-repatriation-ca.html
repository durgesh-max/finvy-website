/ UAE tax regime recap

9% + Pillar 2.

  • UAE Corporate Tax (CT) effective 1 June 2023 (first FY: 1 June 2023 to 31 May 2024 for 31 May FY-end).
  • 9% CT on business profits above AED 375K.
  • 0% CT on first AED 375K (small business relief).
  • Specific free zones (Qualifying Free Zone Person) continue to enjoy 0% CT on 'Qualifying Income' subject to conditions.
  • Non-qualifying income (specific mainland dealings, specific thresholds) at 9%.
  • BEPS Pillar 2 QDMTT in UAE from FY 2024: 15% minimum effective rate for MNEs with EUR 750M+ consolidated revenue.

/ Indian branch of Dubai entity

PE + tax exposure.

  • Liaison Office (LO): representational only - no business activity. No tax liability if genuine LO. RBI approval + annual Form LO reporting + CA certificate.
  • Project Office (PO): specific project execution. Business income taxable in India as PE profit at 40% + surcharge + cess + Section 195 TDS on remittances.
  • Branch Office (BO): specific permitted activities (export / import / consultancy specific). Business income taxable at 40% + surcharge + cess.
  • India-UAE DTAA Article 5 PE definition + Article 7 business profits attributable to PE.
  • Transfer pricing: Form 3CEB mandatory for AE transactions between Dubai parent + Indian branch.

/ FEMA repatriation framework

Moving profits out.

  • LO: no profits (representational) - no repatriation issue.
  • PO: project completion → PO closes + residual balances repatriated.
  • BO: branch profits remittable after corporate tax paid in India (40%+) + Form 15CA/15CB + AD bank.
  • Repatriation requires: audited financial statements + Form 15CB CA certificate + Form 15CA e-filing + specific supporting documentation.
  • Dubai parent receives remittance: UAE 9% CT on business income from Indian branch (specific DTAA relief for Indian tax paid).
  • FTC: UAE CT allows credit for Indian corporate tax paid (specific limitations + treaty).

/ India-UAE DTAA + CEPA considerations

The treaty overlay.

  • India-UAE DTAA (operational since 1993; MLI covered agreement).
  • Residence tie-breaker: specific tests applied.
  • Dividend: 10% treaty rate (reduced from 20% domestic).
  • Interest: 5-12.5% depending on specific scenarios + bank exemptions.
  • Royalty / FTS: 10%.
  • Capital gain: taxable in state of residence subject to specific carve-outs.
  • India-UAE CEPA (Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement operational since 2022): specific trade facilitation + specific movement of professionals.
  • GAAR + MLI PPT apply to UAE DTAA benefits.

/ Common structuring triggers

Scenarios.

  • UAE free zone trading company + Indian LO for market representation: standard, no Indian tax, annual Form LO filing.
  • UAE engineering firm + Indian PO for specific project: project profits taxable in India at 40%+; corresponding adjustment UAE side.
  • UAE consultancy + Indian BO for consultancy delivery: branch business profits in India + transfer pricing + specific DTAA relief.
  • UAE holding + Indian subsidiary (not branch): specific different framework - FEMA FC-GPR + dividend repatriation + Section 195 TDS.
  • UAE free zone + Indian branch dealing with mainland UAE: specific QFZP conditions assessment required.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's UAE 9% CT impact on Dubai parent of Indian branch?
9% UAE CT on Dubai parent's business income (including Indian branch profits). India-UAE DTAA provides FTC for Indian corporate tax paid - typically credit available up to UAE CT on same income. Dubai parent net tax: UAE CT - credit for Indian tax. QFZP status (if applicable) may preserve 0% CT on qualifying income.
Does Indian LO have Indian tax exposure?
No if genuine liaison (representational only - no business activity, no sales, no contracts). Must comply: RBI approval + annual Form LO + CA certificate. If Indian tax officer finds LO conducting business activity: deemed PE + Section 9(1)(i) business connection + branch taxation at 40%+.
What's the repatriation process from Indian branch to Dubai?
Audit financial statements + file ITR + pay Indian corporate tax 40%+ + prepare Form 15CB CA certificate + file Form 15CA e-filing + AD bank processes remittance. BQP standard turnaround 2-4 weeks post ITR + Form 15CB execution.
Does BEPS Pillar 2 affect UAE free zone + Indian branch?
Yes if MNE consolidated revenue EUR 750M+. UAE QDMTT from FY 2024: 15% minimum effective rate. India QDMTT + IIR from FY 2025-26. QFZP 0% UAE CT may trigger specific top-up calculation. BQP + UAE partner Pillar 2 assessment + specific jurisdictional analysis.
