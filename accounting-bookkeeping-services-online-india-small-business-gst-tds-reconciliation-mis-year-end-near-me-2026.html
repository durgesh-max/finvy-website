Monthly, quarterly and at year-end.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What does an accounting and bookkeeping service include?
Recording sales, purchases, expenses and bank entries every month, reconciling bank accounts, matching purchase invoices with GSTR-2B for input tax credit, identifying and depositing TDS, preparing monthly or quarterly MIS (profit, cash flow and dues), and closing the books at year-end so the financial statements are ready for audit and the income tax return.
I am searching for accounting and bookkeeping services near me. Does location matter?
Not for the work. Invoices, bank statements and the accounting file are shared online, and questions are settled on a call or WhatsApp, so the service works wherever the business is.
Do small businesses and proprietors need proper books?
A business whose income or turnover crosses the limits in the income tax law must keep books of account, and any business registered for GST must keep records that support its returns. Even below the limits, monthly books make GST, TDS and the income tax return far easier and help with loans and investors.
How long must accounting records be kept?
Companies preserve books for eight years, and GST records are kept for 72 months from the due date of the annual return for the year. Keep invoices, bank statements and the accounting file together for at least that long.
Can you bring pending months up to date?
Yes. Pending months are entered and reconciled with GST returns and bank statements first, so any mismatch is found before the year-end, and then the monthly routine starts.
