NRIs, OCI Cardholders and Agricultural Land in India (2026): Buying, Inheriting, Selling, Tax and Repatriation, inherit yes, buy no, sell only to a resident Indian.

An NRI or OCI cardholder cannot buy or receive as a gift agricultural land, a farmhouse or plantation property in India, but can inherit it. Inherited farmland can be sold or gifted only to a person resident in India who is an Indian citizen, not to another NRI or OCI (FEMA Non-debt Instruments Rules, 2019, rule 24). For tax, rural agricultural land (outside municipal limits and beyond 2, 6 or 8 km depending on the town's population) is not a capital asset, so its sale is not taxed; urban agricultural land is taxed like other land, at 12.5% on long-term gains for NRIs. Sale proceeds of inherited property can be repatriated up to USD 1 million a financial year from the NRO account.

/ Tax when you sell

Rural is outside the capital gains net.

LandHow it is taxed
Rural agricultural land: outside municipal or cantonment limits and beyond 2 km (town population 10,000 to 1 lakh), 6 km (1 lakh to 10 lakh) or 8 km (over 10 lakh), measured aeriallyNot a capital asset: no capital gains tax
Urban agricultural land (inside those limits)Capital asset: long-term after 24 months, taxed at 12.5% without indexation for NRIs; short-term gains at slab rates
Urban land used for agriculture by you or your parents for 2 years before saleSection 54B relief if the gain is reinvested in other agricultural land within the time allowed

Rural or urban decides the tax.

If the land is outside municipal limits and beyond the 2, 6 or 8 km band, it is not a capital asset and the gain is not taxed at all. Get this checked before the buyer deducts TDS on the full price. WhatsApp CA Durgesh Chavda.

  • Population is taken from the last published census, and every nearby municipality counts, including newly notified ones.
  • A buyer paying an NRI must deduct tax unless a lower or nil deduction certificate is obtained; for rural land with no taxable gain, apply before the sale. Try the NRI property TDS calculator.
  • Income from farming or leasing the land (agricultural income) is exempt from income tax.

/ Inherited the land? The order of steps

From succession to repatriation.

  • Establish title: will and probate where required, or legal heir or succession documents, then mutation of the land records in your name.
  • Decide: keep (and lease through a local arrangement), or sell or gift to a resident Indian citizen.
  • Before selling: confirm rural or urban status and get a lower or nil TDS certificate if needed.
  • Repatriate: sale proceeds go to your NRO account; up to USD 1 million per financial year can be sent abroad with a CA certificate and bank forms (Forms 145/146, formerly 15CA/15CB), with proof of inheritance.
  • Report abroad: declare the sale in your country of residence as its rules require.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can an NRI buy agricultural land in India?
No. Under rule 24 of the FEMA Non-debt Instruments Rules, 2019, NRIs and OCI cardholders can buy any immovable property except agricultural land, farmhouses and plantation property.
Can an NRI or OCI inherit agricultural land?
Yes. They can inherit any immovable property, including agricultural land, from a person resident in India, or from a non-resident who acquired it lawfully.
Who can an NRI sell inherited agricultural land to?
Only to a person resident in India who is an Indian citizen. Agricultural land, farmhouses and plantations cannot be sold or gifted to another NRI or OCI.
Can my parents gift me agricultural land if I am an NRI?
No. Agricultural land, farmhouses and plantation property cannot be acquired by an NRI or OCI by gift; inheritance is the only route.
Is the sale of agricultural land taxable for an NRI?
Rural agricultural land is not a capital asset, so there is no capital gains tax. Urban agricultural land (within municipal limits or the 2, 6 or 8 km band) is taxed at 12.5% on long-term gains for NRIs, with section 54B relief possible on reinvestment in agricultural land.
How much of the sale money can I take abroad?
Up to USD 1 million per financial year from the NRO account, with a CA certificate and bank forms, and proof of inheritance.
