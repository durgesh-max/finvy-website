Private Limited Company Registration in India (2026): Online Process on MCA (SPICe+), Company Name Check, Documents, Directors and Timeline, one form, but the name and documents decide how fast it goes.

A private limited company is registered online on the MCA portal through the SPICe+ form: Part A reserves the name and Part B incorporates the company, with the memorandum, articles and AGILE-PRO-S filed alongside, and PAN and TAN issued with the certificate of incorporation. You need at least two directors (one resident in India) and two shareholders, digital signatures for the signatories and a registered office address; there is no minimum capital. Most incorporations take one to three weeks, and the main delays come from rejected names and document mismatches.

/ Company name check and SPICe+ process

Step by step on the MCA portal.

  • Check the name: search existing company and LLP names on the MCA portal and the trade marks register; avoid names identical or too similar to existing ones and restricted words under the incorporation rules.
  • SPICe+ Part A: reserve the name (one or two choices); an approved name stays reserved for 20 days, within which Part B must be filed.
  • SPICe+ Part B: enter capital, directors (DINs are allotted through the form), subscribers, registered office and declarations.
  • Linked forms: e-MoA (INC-33), e-AoA (INC-34) and AGILE-PRO-S (INC-35) for EPFO, ESIC, optional GSTIN, professional tax in some states and the company bank account.
  • Certificate of incorporation: issued with the CIN, PAN and TAN once the Registrar approves.
  • Already registered? Anyone can check a company's status free on the MCA master data search; see how to verify an Indian company.

/ After incorporation

The first-year compliance.

  • Commencement of business: file INC-20A within 180 days after subscribers pay for their shares; see INC-20A.
  • First auditor: the board appoints it within 30 days; see ADT-1 and auditor appointment.
  • Bank account, registers and share certificates: open the account, maintain statutory registers and issue share certificates.
  • Directors’ KYC: DIR-3 KYC for each director; see DIR-3 KYC.
  • Startup benefits: apply for free DPIIT recognition; see Startup India registration.
  • GST: register when you cross the threshold or need it for interstate or online sales; see GST registration thresholds.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How do I register a private limited company in India?
File the SPICe+ form on the MCA portal: Part A to reserve the name and Part B to incorporate, with the e-MoA, e-AoA and AGILE-PRO-S. The certificate of incorporation comes with the CIN, PAN and TAN.
How do I check if a company name is available in India?
Search existing company and LLP names on the MCA portal and the trade marks register, then apply through SPICe+ Part A; an approved name is reserved for 20 days.
How can I check whether a company is registered in India?
Use the free MCA master data search with the company name or CIN to see its status, directors, capital and filing history.
How many directors and shareholders does a private limited company need?
At least two directors, one of whom must be resident in India (in India for at least 182 days in the financial year), and at least two shareholders.
How long does company registration take in India?
Usually one to three weeks, depending on name approval, digital signatures and document checks; foreign directors' apostilled documents can add time.
Can an NRI or foreign national register a company in India?
Yes. NRIs and foreign nationals can be directors and shareholders, subject to FDI rules and at least one resident director; foreign capital must be reported to the RBI.
