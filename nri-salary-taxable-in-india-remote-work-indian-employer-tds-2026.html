Is an NRI's Salary Taxable in India? (2026): Work Done Abroad vs in India, Remote Work for Indian Employers, TDS and Residence Tests, where you work decides, not where you are paid.

For an NRI, salary is taxable in India only for work physically done in India. Indian law treats salary as earned in India when the services are rendered in India, wherever it is paid and in whatever currency. Salary for work done wholly outside India is generally not taxed in India even if it is paid into an Indian account; tribunals have held this, and the CBDT clarified it for seafarers' salary credited to NRE accounts (Circular 13/2017). Days you work from India, including remote work during visits, are taxable and the employer deducts TDS. First check you really are non-resident: the 182-day test, the 60-day and 120-day tests, and deemed residence for Indian citizens with Indian income above INR 15 lakh.

Check this before anything else.

Indian citizen or person of Indian origin visiting with Indian income above INR 15 lakh120 days in the year (with 365 in four years) makes you resident (usually RNOR)

/ What to do

Practical steps.

  • Give your Indian employer a written declaration of non-resident status and that services are rendered outside India, with your travel dates, so TDS is not deducted on salary for foreign work.
  • Keep a day-count log and boarding passes for every year; residence is tested each financial year.
  • If you work from India for some days, expect TDS on that part; split salary by days if the employer agrees.
  • If TDS was deducted on salary for foreign work, claim it back in your Indian return.
  • Your country of residence may tax the salary; India's treaties (dependent personal services article) and foreign tax credits prevent double tax.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is NRI salary taxable in India?
Only for work physically done in India. Salary for services rendered wholly outside India is generally not taxable in India for a non-resident, even if paid into an Indian account.
Under which section is NRI salary exempt?
There is no specific exemption section; salary for services rendered outside India simply does not accrue in India under the source rule (section 9(1)(ii) of the 1961 Act, carried into the 2025 Act), so a non-resident is not taxed on it.
I work remotely from abroad for an Indian company. Is my salary taxable?
Generally not, if you are non-resident and do all the work outside India. Give the employer a declaration with travel dates; days worked from India are taxable.
What TDS applies to NRI salary?
Salary for work done in India is subject to TDS at slab rates like any salary. Salary for work done outside India should not attract TDS if the employer is satisfied it is not taxable in India.
Is salary credited to an NRE account taxable?
Not merely because it is credited there; the CBDT clarified for non-resident seafarers that salary for services outside India credited to an NRE account is not taxable.
