NRO to NRE Transfer (2026): Is It Allowed, the USD 1 Million Limit, Tax, Form 145 and 146 (Old 15CA and 15CB) and Documents, what the bank asks for and how to get it done.

Yes, an NRI can transfer money from an NRO account to an NRE account. The limit is USD 1 million per financial year (April to March), and it is shared with any money you send abroad directly from the NRO account. The transfer itself is not taxed. What the bank checks is that Indian tax on the money behind it (rent, interest, sale proceeds) has been paid. For that it asks for Form 145, filed online, and usually a Chartered Accountant’s certificate in Form 146. These replaced Form 15CA and Form 15CB from 1 April 2026. Once the money is in the NRE account it can be sent abroad freely, and NRE interest is tax-free in India while you are a non-resident. Since June 2026 the RBI’s deposit regulations list this transfer expressly, so the rule is the same at every bank; only the bank’s own request form differs.

/ Step by step

How the transfer is done.

  • 1. Identify the source: rent, interest, dividends, pension, sale of property or shares, inheritance, or savings from before you left India. The bank and the CA both ask.
  • 2. Settle the tax: check that TDS was deducted or tax was paid, and file the Indian return where one is due. If TDS on NRO interest was more than your treaty rate, claim the refund in the return; see the NRO interest refund calculator.
  • 3. CA certificate (Form 146): the Chartered Accountant checks the source, the tax paid and the treaty position, and uploads the certificate.
  • 4. Form 145: filed on the income-tax portal by you, using the certificate details.
  • 5. Bank request: give the bank its NRO-to-NRE transfer form, the FEMA declaration, Form 145, Form 146 and proof of source.
  • 6. Credit to NRE: the bank moves the rupees to your NRE account. From there you can keep them in India or send them abroad.

Documents usually asked for: PAN, passport and visa or residence proof, NRO statement showing the credits, proof of source (sale deed, rent agreement, will or succession papers, maturity advice), proof of tax (TDS certificates, Form 26AS or AIS, return acknowledgment, challans), and the bank’s own form.

/ Practical points

What people get wrong.

  • The limit is shared: NRO to NRE transfers and remittances abroad from NRO in the same April-to-March year add up towards USD 1 million. A large sale can be split across 31 March.
  • Not taxed does not mean no paperwork: banks treat the transfer like a remittance abroad, because NRE money is freely repatriable.
  • Property sale money: the buyer’s TDS and your capital gains return come first. See selling Indian property from abroad.
  • Inherited money: India has no inheritance tax, but the bank wants the will or succession documents. See inheriting money or property from India.
  • Why move to NRE and not straight abroad: NRE keeps the money in rupees in India, with tax-free interest and no limit on sending it out later. If you need the money abroad now, remit directly from NRO; the paperwork is the same.
  • Your home country: interest earned in India may still be taxable where you live, even when India does not tax it.
  • SNRR accounts: the same June 2026 amendment also allows transfers from NRO to a Special Non-Resident Rupee account within the same limit.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can I transfer money from an NRO account to an NRE account?
Yes. An NRI can transfer from their NRO account to their NRE account within USD 1 million per financial year. RBI's June 2026 amendment to the FEMA deposit regulations lists it as a permitted debit to NRO and credit to NRE.
What is the NRO to NRE transfer limit?
USD 1 million per financial year, April to March. The limit is shared with money sent abroad directly from NRO balances, property sale proceeds and inheritance.
Is transfer of funds from NRO to NRE taxable?
No. The transfer itself is not income. Indian tax must have been paid on the income behind the balance, such as rent, interest or capital gains, and the bank checks this before moving the money.
Is Form 15CA and 15CB required for NRO to NRE transfer?
Banks generally ask for both. From 1 April 2026 they are Form 145, filed online, and Form 146, the Chartered Accountant's certificate.
Can I transfer from NRE to NRO?
Yes, freely, with no limit and no CA certificate. The money then follows NRO rules, so sending it abroad later falls under the USD 1 million limit and paperwork.
Is the process different at ICICI, HDFC, SBI, Axis or Kotak?
The FEMA rule and the tax forms are the same at every bank. Each bank has its own request form and declaration, and some ask for extra proof of source.
