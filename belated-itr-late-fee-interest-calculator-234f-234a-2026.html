/ The rules

FY 2025-26 returns (1961 Act).

  • Late fee, Section 234F: INR 5,000; INR 1,000 if total income is INR 5 lakh or less.
  • Interest, Section 234A: 1% for every month or part of a month from the day after the due date until filing, on tax unpaid, rounded down to the nearest INR 100.
  • Last date: 31 December 2026 for belated and revised returns.
  • Losses: business and capital losses cannot be carried forward from a belated return.
  • After 31 December: only an updated return (ITR-U) with 25% to 70% additional tax, and no refunds.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the late fee for filing ITR after the due date?
INR 5,000 under Section 234F, or INR 1,000 if total income is INR 5 lakh or less.
How is Section 234A interest calculated?
1% for each month or part month from the day after the due date until the return is filed, on the tax still unpaid.
What is the last date for a belated return for FY 2025-26?
31 December 2026.
Is there a late fee if my income is below the taxable limit?
No late fee applies if you were not required to file and are filing voluntarily.
