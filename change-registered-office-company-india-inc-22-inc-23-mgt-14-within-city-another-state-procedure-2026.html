How to Change the Registered Office of a Company in India (2026): Within the City, Same ROC, Another ROC or Another State - INC-22, INC-23 and MGT-14, the route depends on how far you move.

Moving a company's registered office is a filing with the Registrar of Companies, and the route depends on the distance: a board resolution and Form INC-22 within the same city; a special resolution as well when you leave the city but stay under the same ROC; and the Regional Director's approval on Form INC-23 when the move crosses into another ROC's area or another state. After the ROC records the change, GST, PAN and TAN records, bank accounts and licences must be updated too.

/ Four routes

Pick the one that matches the move.

MoveApproval neededFiling
Within the same city, town or village (local limits)Board resolutionINC-22 within 30 days of the change
Outside local limits, same ROCBoard resolution and special resolution of shareholdersMGT-14 for the special resolution, then INC-22
To another ROC's area in the same stateSpecial resolution and confirmation by the Regional DirectorINC-23 to the Regional Director; order filed with the ROC; INC-22
To another stateSpecial resolution altering the memorandum, and approval by the Regional DirectorPublic notice and notice to creditors and others; INC-23; order filed with the ROCs; INC-22
  • Documents for every route: proof of the new address (sale deed, lease or rent agreement), a recent utility bill, and a no-objection letter from the owner if the place is not owned by the company.
  • Interstate moves take longest: the application must show that creditors and employees are not prejudiced, and objections can be raised before the Regional Director decides.
  • The name board and stationery: the new registered office address must appear on the company's letterheads, invoices and outside the office.

/ After the ROC records the change

Update everything else.

  • GST: amend the principal place of business in the GST registration; a move to another state needs a new GST registration there.
  • Income tax: update the address on PAN and TAN records; the jurisdictional assessing officer can change.
  • Banks and FEMA: inform the bank; a foreign-owned company also updates its bank and reporting records.
  • Licences and registrations: shop and establishment, professional tax, import-export code, Udyam and any sector licences.
  • Books and registers: keep statutory registers at the registered office unless a resolution allows another place.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How do I change the registered office within the same city?
The board passes a resolution approving the new address and the company files Form INC-22 with the ROC within 30 days of the change, with proof of the new address, a recent utility bill and the owner's no-objection letter if the place is rented.
Do I need shareholder approval to change the registered office?
Only when the office moves outside the local limits of the city, town or village. A move within local limits needs only a board resolution; a move outside them needs a special resolution of shareholders, filed in Form MGT-14, and then INC-22.
What is Form INC-23?
It is the application to the Regional Director to confirm a shift of the registered office to an area under another ROC in the same state, or to another state. The Regional Director's order is filed with the ROC, and INC-22 is filed for the new address.
Can a company move its registered office to another state?
Yes. It needs a special resolution altering the memorandum, a public notice and notice to creditors and others, an application on INC-23 to the Regional Director, and, once approved, the order filed with the ROCs and INC-22 for the new address. A new GST registration is needed in the new state.
What has to be updated after the registered office changes?
The GST registration, PAN and TAN records, bank accounts, letterheads and invoices, and licences such as shop and establishment, professional tax, import-export code and Udyam.
