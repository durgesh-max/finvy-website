/ Tax Audit Post-Filing · Updated 2026-10-22

Tax audit filed, November follow-ups to execute.

The tax audit report (Section 44AB [section 63 of the 2025 Act]) for AY 2026-27 was due 21 October 2026 (extended from 30 September by CBDT Circular 07/2026); audit-case ITRs are due 21 November 2026. Form 3CEB (Form 48 from tax year 2026-27) is due 31 October 2026, with transfer-pricing-case ITRs due 30 November 2026. Post-filing, several follow-up actions are critical: ITR coordination (ITR-3 / ITR-5 / ITR-6 due 31 October / 30 November / 31 December), 43B(h) Year 2 handling, Clause 44 reconciliation review, specified financial transaction documentation. Here's the November-December follow-up playbook.

/ Post-Filing Reconciliation

What to verify.

  • Form 3CA/3CB signed + e-filed: tax auditor's audit report signed and e-filed on income tax portal.
  • Form 3CD + 44 Clauses verification: specific Clause 20 (Section 40(a) / 40A payments), Clause 22 (43B / 43B(h) MSE), Clause 32 (specified financial transactions), Clause 34 (Chapter XVII-B TDS), Clause 44 (GST reconciliation).
  • CA signature + membership verification: ICAI UDIN (Unique Document Identification Number) generated for the audit report.
  • Supporting workpapers: in audit firm's file (preserved 8 years).

/ Clause 22 Section 43B(h) specific review

The year-2 critical clause.

  • Clause 22 expanded (post Finance Act 2023): specific line items for Section 43B(h) MSE disallowance + amounts paid in subsequent FY (reversal from prior year).
  • Common error: not identifying Micro + Small enterprise suppliers; treating all suppliers as outside 43B(h) scope. Udyam certificate collection becomes compliance signal.
  • Reconciliation: 43B(h) disallowance in current year + 43B(h) reversal from prior year payment = net 43B(h) impact on current-year taxable income.
  • Documentation: Udyam certificates for each MSE supplier + payment date tracking + 45-day calculation per invoice.

/ Clause 44 GST reconciliation

The GST-Income Tax interface.

  • Clause 44 requires reconciliation of Income Tax turnover with GST turnover (as per GSTR-1 + GSTR-9).
  • Common differences: credit notes / debit notes timing, year-end sales returns, GSTR-1 vs 3B gaps, exempt supplies not in Income Tax but in GST, specific adjustments like discounts.
  • Narrative explanation for each unreconciled item.
  • Feeds into GSTR-9 Reconciliation (31 December 2026) + GSTR-9C Part II / III.

/ Form 3CEB TP audit (30 November 2026)

The next deadline.

  • Form 3CEB applicable for any Indian company with international transactions above INR 1 crore (effectively every foreign-subsidiary subsidiary of Indian group).
  • Deadline: 30 November 2026 for AY 2026-27.
  • Content: all international transactions listed with each AE + nature + value + arm's-length method + benchmarking.
  • Underlying documentation (Section 92D): entity profile + industry analysis + functional analysis + economic analysis + benchmarking study. Not filed but produced on scrutiny.
  • Master File (Form 3CEAA) for groups above INR 500 crore + CbCR (Form 3CEAD) for ultimate parent of groups above EUR 750M.

/ ITR filing coordination

The November-December window.

  • ITR-5 / ITR-6 / ITR-3 (tax audit cases): due 21 November 2026 (extended from 31 October by CBDT Circular 07/2026) or 30 November 2026 if TP audit applicable.
  • ITR must reflect Form 3CD disclosures (43B(h), Section 40(a), Chapter XVII-B TDS, specified financial transactions).
  • Advance tax Q3 (15 December 2026) coordination.
  • Belated return window: 31 December 2026.
  • Updated return window (Section 139(8A)): up to 31 March 2027 for AY 2026-27, with additional tax.

/ Common November post-filing attention

What to check.

  • Form 3CA/3CB status on portal: accepted / rejected / pending.
  • UDIN verification against ICAI records.
  • Form 3CD Clause 22 (43B(h)) + Clause 44 (GST reconciliation) + Clause 34 (TDS) coherence.
  • Specified financial transaction disclosure (Clause 32) matching Form 61A reports.
  • Pending Section 148A(b) notices from November-December 2026 reassessment season - urgent response.
  • Advance tax Q3 (15 December) computation based on finalised audited financials.
  • GSTR-9 FY 2025-26 preparation (due 31 December 2026).
  • Last updated: 2026-10-22.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What follow-ups are critical after tax audit filing on 31 October?
Form 3CA/3CB status verification on portal, UDIN check against ICAI records, Form 3CD Clause-by-Clause coherence (especially 22, 32, 34, 44), coordination with ITR filing (30 November TP cases, 31 October standard), 43B(h) MSE reversal tracking, Clause 44 GST reconciliation feeding into GSTR-9 (December 2026), advance tax Q3 (15 December), Form 3CEB TP audit (30 November).
Is Form 3CEB TP audit also due on 31 October?
No - Form 3CEB has its own deadline: 30 November 2026 for AY 2026-27 (one month after standard tax audit). Mandatory for any Indian subsidiary / company with international transactions above INR 1 crore. Benchmarking study + comparability analysis underlying + Master File + CbCR for in-scope groups.
What's Clause 44 of Form 3CD?
Clause 44 requires reconciliation of Income Tax turnover with GST turnover (as per GSTR-1 + GSTR-9). Line-by-line explanation of differences: credit notes timing, exempt supplies, year-end adjustments, discounts. Feeds into GSTR-9 reconciliation (December 2026). Common area of discrepancy requiring narrative.
How does Section 43B(h) show up in Form 3CD?
Clause 22 (expanded post Finance Act 2023): specific line items for amounts disallowed under 43B(h) in current year (MSE payment beyond 45 days) + amounts paid in current year where disallowance was reversed from prior year. Documented with Udyam certificates + invoice-level 45-day calculation.
What happens if Form 3CA/3CB wasn't filed by 31 October?
ITR filing blocked - cannot e-file ITR without Form 3CA/3CB linked on portal. Late filing fee under Section 234F: INR 5,000 (reduced to INR 1,000 for small income). Section 271B penalty for late tax audit: 0.5% of turnover or INR 1,50,000 whichever less. Belated filing possible but with penalties.
