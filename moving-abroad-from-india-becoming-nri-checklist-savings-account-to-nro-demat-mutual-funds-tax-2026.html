/ Becoming an NRI · Bank accounts · Investments · Tax · Updated 2026-11-20

Moving Abroad from India (2026): Becoming an NRI Checklist - Can You Keep Your Savings Account? NRO Conversion, Demat, Mutual Funds, PPF, EPF and Tax, what to change in India when you move to the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Singapore or the Gulf.

No, an NRI cannot keep an ordinary resident savings account. Under the FEMA deposit rules, your savings account, fixed deposits and recurring deposits must be redesignated as NRO accounts once you become non-resident, which usually means once you leave India for a job, business or an indefinite stay. An NRO account can be held jointly with a resident relative. NRO interest is taxed in India with TDS. Most people also open an NRE account, which is tax-free in India and fully repatriable, for money they earn abroad. Also tell your broker, fund houses and insurer about the change of status: demat accounts must become NRI accounts, and mutual fund folios need NRI status, an NRO or NRE bank mandate and FATCA details. An existing PPF continues to maturity but cannot be extended. For tax, an Indian citizen leaving for employment abroad stays resident in the year of departure only if in India for 182 days or more. File the Indian return for that year.

/ Tax in the year you leave

Residential status and filing.

  • Leaving for employment: an Indian citizen who leaves for a job abroad is resident that year only if in India for 182 days or more; the 60-day test does not apply. See the residential status calculator.
  • Gulf moves: if your Indian income exceeds INR 15 lakh and you are not taxed anywhere, the deemed-resident rule can apply; see UAE NRIs and the INR 15 lakh rule.
  • Your new country: most countries tax you on worldwide income once resident; a split year or part-year status may apply. Indian income can be taxed in both countries, with treaty credit.
  • Filing: file the Indian return for the year of leaving and for any later year with Indian income above the exemption limit or with TDS to recover; see which ITR an NRI files.
  • Coming back later: plan for RNOR years and foreign asset reporting; see returning NRI and RNOR.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can an NRI keep a normal savings account in India?
No. Under the FEMA deposit rules, resident savings accounts, fixed deposits and recurring deposits must be redesignated as NRO accounts when the holder becomes non-resident.
Is it mandatory to convert a savings account to NRO?
Yes. Continuing to operate a resident account after becoming non-resident is a FEMA contravention and penalties can apply. Banks redesignate it on request with proof of your move.
Can an NRO account be joint with a resident?
Yes. An NRO account can be held jointly with a resident.
Do I need to update my demat and mutual funds when I move abroad?
Yes. Demat and trading accounts must be converted to NRI accounts, and mutual fund folios need NRI status, an NRO or NRE bank mandate, an overseas address and FATCA details. Some fund houses restrict US and Canada residents.
Am I an NRI in the year I leave India for a job?
An Indian citizen leaving for employment abroad is resident in that year only if in India for 182 days or more; the 60-day test does not apply.
