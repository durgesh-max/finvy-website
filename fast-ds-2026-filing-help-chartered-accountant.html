FAST-DS 2026 Filing Help From a Chartered Accountant: Category, Form 1 and Payment Before 31 December, declare foreign assets the right way, once.

FAST-DS 2026 lets eligible taxpayers declare foreign assets and income they left out of their Indian returns, pay either 60% (Category 1) or a flat INR 1 lakh (Category 2), and get immunity from tax, penalty and prosecution under the Black Money Act. Getting the category, residential status and valuation right matters: a declaration with a false material particular is invalid (section 134(3)) and nothing paid is refundable (section 138). / Who we help

The situations we see most.

  • Employees holding RSUs or ESPP shares of a foreign employer that were taxed through Indian payroll but never shown in the foreign-assets schedule.
  • Returning NRIs with US 401(k)/IRA, UK pensions, foreign bank or brokerage accounts not reported after becoming resident and ordinarily resident.
  • Students and professionals with old foreign bank accounts, deposits or crypto accounts left open after returning to India.
  • Taxpayers who received the Income Tax Department's email about foreign assets in their AIS.
  • Current NRIs who were resident when the foreign income arose or the asset was acquired (they are eligible under section 131(1)(a)(ii)).

/ What we do

End to end, fixed scope.

  1. Review your AIS (foreign assets information), past returns and residential status year by year.
  2. Classify each asset and income item: no action, revised or updated return, Category 1, or Category 2, and check the INR 1 crore and INR 5 crore limits and the exclusions in section 140.
  3. Value each asset as on 31 March 2026 in the prescribed manner.
  4. Prepare and file Form 1 on the e-filing portal and verify it electronically.
  5. Track the order of amount payable, arrange payment in time, file the payment intimation and obtain the certificate.

What to send us: a list of foreign accounts and assets with opening dates, statements, the source of funds for each, your Indian returns for the relevant years, your AIS and the dates you lived abroad.

/ Timeline

From declaration to certificate.

  • Declaration (Form 1): filed electronically on the e-filing portal by 31 December 2026 (sections 132 and 134).
  • Order of amount payable: issued within one month from the end of the month in which you declare (section 135(1)).
  • Payment: within two months from the end of the month in which you receive the order; or within a further two months with simple interest of 1% a month or part of a month (section 135(2) and (3)).
  • Intimation of payment: within the extended period (section 135(4)).
  • Certificate: an order certifying payment within one month from the end of the month of the intimation, conclusive as to the matters stated (section 135(5) and (6)).

Source: Finance Act, 2026, Chapter IV (sections 130 to 144), the Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers Disclosure Scheme, 2026, as published by the Income Tax Department. Forms, valuation and payment mechanics are in the FAST-DS Rules. Last updated: 4 November 2026.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can a chartered accountant file FAST-DS for me?
Yes. We review your residential status and records, determine the category, value the assets, file Form 1, and manage payment and the final certificate. WhatsApp +91 78018 87130 or email durgesh@bharatquantumprospera.com with a short note of your foreign accounts or assets and the years you were resident. Fixed-scope quote within 48 hours.
What is the last date for FAST-DS 2026?
31 December 2026 for filing the declaration. Payment follows the order issued after the declaration.
How much does FAST-DS cost in tax?
Category 1: 60% of the value of undisclosed foreign assets (as on 31 March 2026) and undisclosed foreign income, if the total is INR 1 crore or less. Category 2: a fee of INR 1 lakh for assets bought from explained income but not reported in the foreign-assets schedule, if the asset value is INR 5 crore or less.
Is the amount paid under FAST-DS refundable?
No. Section 138 of the scheme says no amount paid is refundable, so check the category before filing.
What immunity does FAST-DS give?
A valid declaration with payment gives immunity from further tax, penalty and prosecution under the Black Money Act for the declared income or asset, for years up to 2025-26 (section 139).
