US Incorporation for Bangladeshi Founders

For Bangladeshi founders building US-facing businesses, incorporating in Delaware unlocks US VC funding, US enterprise customers, payment rails (Stripe, etc.), and an eventual US exit. We are a CA-led advisory firm running US company formation end-to-end for Bangladeshi founders — from entity selection through ongoing US-Bangladesh compliance.

Why incorporate in the US

For Bangladeshi founders, here's why.

Strategic reasons:

  • US VC funding access
  • US enterprise customer trust
  • Stripe/Mercury/Brex payment rails
  • Clean cap table for ESOPs and preferred stock
  • QSBS (Section 1202) — up to $10M tax-free gain
  • US IP protection (USPTO)
  • Easier global expansion from US parent
  • Tax-efficient exit

How we help

End-to-end for Bangladeshi founders.

What's included:

  • Entity selection — Delaware C-Corp vs LLC vs Wyoming
  • State of incorporation choice with reasoning
  • Certificate of Incorporation filing
  • EIN registration (no SSN needed)
  • Registered agent setup
  • Founders' stock + 83(b) elections
  • US bank account intro (Mercury/Brex/Wise)
  • Bangladesh-side coordination: RJSC Bangladeshi Pvt Ltd, NBR tax, Delaware C-Corp for US enterprise sales and Stripe access
  • Year-1 + ongoing US filings (1120, 5472)
  • BOIR / CTA reporting
