/ Core West Bengal state registrations

Mandatory.

  • West Bengal Shops & Establishments Act: within 30 days of commencement. Online via WB Labour Department.
  • WB Professional Tax: employer registration + monthly deduction + annual return.
  • WB Labour Welfare Fund.
  • WB Factories Act for establishments meeting worker + power thresholds.
  • WBPCB (West Bengal Pollution Control Board): CTE / CTO.
  • Kolkata Municipal Corporation Trade Licence (for Kolkata-based specific categories).

/ West Bengal industrial advantages

What WB offers.

  • Industrial Policy 2017-22 + 2022+: specific state support for manufacturing + specific sectors.
  • WBIDC (West Bengal Industrial Development Corporation): land allocation + specific industrial parks.
  • Specific sectors: jute (traditional), tea (specific regulatory), leather (Bantala Leather Complex), engineering + IT (Kolkata Salt Lake Sector V), specific manufacturing clusters.
  • Kolkata IT ecosystem: Salt Lake Sector V + New Town + specific IT parks. Specific state IT policy support.
  • Haldia port + Kolkata port: specific port + logistics advantages for East India + Southeast Asia trade.
