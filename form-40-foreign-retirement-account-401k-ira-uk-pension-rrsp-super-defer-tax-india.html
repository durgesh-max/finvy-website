/ Returning NRIs · Foreign retirement accounts · Updated 2026-11-10

Form 40 (Formerly Form 10-EE): Defer Indian Tax on Your 401(k), IRA, UK Pension, RRSP or Australian Super After Moving Back, section 158 of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

When you become resident in India, growth inside a foreign retirement account can be taxable in India every year, even though the US, UK, Canada or Australia taxes it only when you withdraw. The relief lets you align the two: income from the account is taxed in India when it is taxed abroad on withdrawal. From tax year 2026-27 it sits in section 158 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 and rule 74 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026, and you claim it by filing Form 40 electronically by the return due date. Up to FY 2025-26 the same relief was section 89A, claimed in Form 10-EE. The official Form 40 FAQ lists the USA, the UK, Canada and Australia as notified countries at present.

/ Who it helps

Typical cases.

  • Returning from the US with a 401(k) or IRA that keeps growing while you live in India.
  • Returning from the UK with a workplace pension or SIPP.
  • Returning from Canada with an RRSP.
  • Returning from Australia with superannuation: now listed as notified in the Form 40 FAQ, which matters because super was outside the old section 89A list.

The relief is about when income is taxed, not whether. You still report the account every year in Schedule FA, and withdrawals are taxed in India in the year they are taxed abroad, with credit for foreign tax under the treaty.

/ Before you file

Check these first.

  1. Your residential status for the year: in RNOR years foreign income is generally not taxed in India, so the relief may not be needed yet.
  2. That the account was opened while you were resident in the notified country.
  3. Every retirement account you hold in that country: the option covers all of them and cannot be revoked later.
  4. That the return due date has not passed: miss it and the relief is lost for that year.
  5. Whether earlier years were reported. If the account was never in Schedule FA, look at FAST-DS before 31 December 2026.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is Form 40 in the Income-tax Rules, 2026?
The form to opt for relief on income from a retirement account in a notified country, under section 158 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 and rule 74. It replaces Form 10-EE, which was used under section 89A.
Is Form 10-EE still used?
For income up to FY 2025-26 under the Income-tax Act, 1961, yes. From tax year 2026-27 the relief is claimed in Form 40.
Which countries are notified for the foreign retirement account relief?
The official Form 40 FAQ lists the USA, the UK, Canada and Australia at present. Under the old section 89A, the notified countries were the USA, the UK and Canada.
When must Form 40 be filed?
Electronically by the due date for filing the income-tax return for the year. Missing it means losing the relief for that year.
Can I withdraw the Form 40 option later?
No. Once exercised it cannot be revoked for that tax year and applies to all subsequent tax years.
Does Form 40 mean my 401(k) is tax-free in India?
No. It defers Indian tax so that income is taxed when it is taxed abroad on withdrawal. You still report the account in Schedule FA every year.
