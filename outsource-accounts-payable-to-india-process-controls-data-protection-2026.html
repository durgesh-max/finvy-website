Outsourcing Accounts Payable to India (2026): What Gets Outsourced, Controls That Stop Payment Fraud, Data Protection and Tax Points, for foreign companies, CPA firms and accounting practices.

Accounts payable (AP) is one of the easiest finance processes to outsource to India because it is rule-based and runs in your existing system: the Indian team captures and codes invoices, matches them to purchase orders and receipts, routes them for approval, maintains vendor records, reconciles supplier statements and prepares the payment run and month-end accruals. The key is control: you keep payment approval and release, and any change to a vendor's bank details, and the team works inside your accounting system with role-based access rather than on copies of your data. Data protection follows your country's rules (for UK and EU clients, an international data transfer agreement or standard contractual clauses). On the Indian side, the provider's services to you are generally exports, invoiced without Indian GST under a Letter of Undertaking.

/ Controls and data protection

What a careful client asks for.

  • Segregation of duties: the people who enter invoices cannot release payments or change vendor bank details.
  • Vendor bank changes verified by a call to a known contact, never on the strength of an email (the most common payment fraud).
  • Access: named user accounts in your system, multi-factor authentication, no local copies of data, access removed on exit.
  • Audit trail: every entry and approval logged in your system; monthly review by a Chartered Accountant.
  • UK and EU clients: a data processing agreement and an international transfer tool (UK IDTA or Addendum, EU standard contractual clauses); see UK GDPR and IDTA checklist.
  • US CPA firms: confidentiality terms, and IRC section 7216 consent where tax return information is involved; see section 7216 checklist.

/ Tax and contract points

Cross-border basics.

  • The Indian provider's fees to a foreign client are generally an export of services: invoiced without Indian GST under a Letter of Undertaking, paid in foreign currency.
  • For US clients, fees for services performed in India are not US-source income, so no US withholding normally applies; the provider gives a Form W-8BEN-E.
  • If your company has a team in India of its own, check permanent establishment risk; an outsourced service provider is different from your own employees in India. See PE risk with India teams.
  • Agree a scope document: volumes, turnaround times, cut-offs, systems, exception handling and who signs off.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What accounts payable work can be outsourced to India?
Invoice capture and coding, two- or three-way matching, approval routing, vendor master maintenance, supplier statement reconciliation, payment run preparation and month-end accruals and ageing.
Should the outsourced team release payments?
No. Best practice is for the client to keep payment approval and release, and approval of any vendor bank change, while the outsourced team prepares the run.
Is outsourcing AP to India compliant with UK GDPR?
It can be, with a data processing agreement and an international transfer tool such as the UK IDTA or the UK Addendum to EU standard contractual clauses, plus a transfer risk assessment.
Will Indian GST be added to the invoice?
Usually not. Services provided to a foreign client and paid in foreign currency are generally zero-rated exports invoiced under a Letter of Undertaking.
Does a US company withhold tax on fees paid to an Indian AP provider?
Normally no, because fees for services performed outside the US are not US-source income; the provider gives a Form W-8BEN-E.
