Selling Property in India While Living Abroad: Power of Attorney, TDS and Repatriation, Step by Step (2026), sign once abroad, get the money out cleanly.

You can sell a flat or plot in India without flying back. Sign a specific power of attorney abroad, have it notarised and apostilled (or attested at the Indian mission), and get it stamped in India within three months of it arriving there. On the tax side, the buyer will deduct TDS on the full sale price at non-resident rates unless you first obtain a lower deduction certificate; the money lands in your NRO account, and up to USD 1 million a financial year can be sent abroad with Form 15CA/15CB.

/ The power of attorney

Do it in this order.

  1. Draft a specific POA for this property and sale (not a vague general one), naming the attorney and the powers: negotiate, sign the agreement and sale deed, receive payment into your NRO account, appear before the sub-registrar.
  2. Sign it abroad before a notary, then have it apostilled if the country is a Hague Apostille Convention member (US, UK, Canada, Australia and others), or attested by the Indian embassy or consulate.
  3. Courier it to India and stamp it within three months of it first being received in India; the Collector of Stamps adjudicates the duty. Late stamping attracts a penalty.
  4. Register it where required: many states require a registered POA for selling immovable property. Your lawyer confirms the rule for the property's state.

The biggest avoidable cost is excess TDS

Without a lower deduction certificate the buyer deducts tax on the whole sale price, not on your gain, and the refund waits for your return. Apply before the deal is signed: WhatsApp CA Durgesh Chavda.

/ The tax steps

Start before the buyer pays.

  • Work out the gain: sale price less indexed or actual cost, depending on when you bought; see capital gains for NRIs selling property.
  • Lower or nil TDS certificate: apply to the Income Tax Department so the buyer deducts tax on the gain, not the full price. Without it, TDS on the whole consideration can be far more than your actual tax.
  • Buyer's TDS: from 1 October 2026 a buyer can deduct and deposit TDS on a purchase from a non-resident using PAN, without a TAN; see the new buyer route.
  • Exemptions: reinvestment in another Indian residential property or specified bonds can reduce the tax; plan it before you sell.
  • Your return: file in India for the year of sale to claim any refund of excess TDS, and report the sale in your country of residence with credit for Indian tax under the treaty.

/ Getting the money out

From NRO to abroad.

  • Sale proceeds are paid into your NRO account.
  • You can remit up to USD 1 million per financial year from NRO balances, including property sale proceeds, after tax.
  • The bank needs Form 15CA (and a CA's Form 15CB where applicable), now numbered Forms 145 and 146 under the Income-tax Rules, 2026.
  • Keep the sale deed, TDS certificates and proof of the original purchase; banks ask for them.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can an NRI sell property in India without coming to India?
Yes. Sign a specific power of attorney abroad, have it notarised and apostilled or attested at the Indian mission, stamp it in India within three months of receipt, and register it where the state requires. The attorney then signs the sale deed.
How long is a power of attorney executed abroad valid for stamping in India?
It must be stamped within three months of first being received in India under section 18 of the Indian Stamp Act. After that a penalty applies.
How much TDS does the buyer deduct when buying from an NRI?
TDS at non-resident rates on the sale consideration unless the NRI obtains a lower or nil deduction certificate, which lets the buyer deduct on the actual gain instead.
How much of the sale money can I send abroad?
Up to USD 1 million per financial year from your NRO account, after tax, with Form 15CA and, where applicable, a CA's Form 15CB.
Does the buyer need a TAN to buy from an NRI?
Not from 1 October 2026: the buyer can deduct and deposit TDS using PAN.
