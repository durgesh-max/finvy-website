FEMA Late Submission Fee Calculator (2026): FC-GPR, FC-TRS, Form DI, Form FC, ECB 2, FLA and APR - INR 7,500 Plus 0.025% a Year, what a late RBI filing costs, and when it becomes compounding.

A late FEMA filing can usually be regularised by paying a late submission fee, if it is filed within three years of the due date. For forms that report money moving, such as FC-GPR, FC-TRS, Form DI, Form FC and ECB 2, the fee is INR 7,500 plus 0.025% of the amount for each year of delay. For returns that report no flow, such as the FLA return and the Annual Performance Report, it is a flat INR 7,500. The delay is counted in months, with part months rounded up. The fee cannot exceed the amount reported and is rounded up to the nearest INR 100. After three years the fee route closes and the delay has to be compounded with the RBI. The fee is small next to what a pending filing blocks: the next funding round, a share transfer, an exit or a remittance to the parent. Enter the form, amount and dates below.

    / How the fee works

    Formula, limits and due dates.

    FilingNormal due dateLate fee
    FC-GPR (shares issued to a non-resident)30 days from allotmentINR 7,500 + 0.025% × amount × years
    FC-TRS (transfer between resident and non-resident)60 days from the transfer or payment, whichever is earlierINR 7,500 + 0.025% × amount × years
    Form DI (downstream investment)30 days from allotmentINR 7,500 + 0.025% × amount × years
    Form FC (overseas investment)With the remittance, through the bankINR 7,500 + 0.025% × amount × years
    FLA return15 July each yearINR 7,500 flat
    Annual Performance Report (overseas investment)31 December each yearINR 7,500 flat
    • Years of delay: counted in months, part months rounded up, then divided by 12 to two decimals. Five months and three days is six months, or 0.50 years.
    • Ceiling: the fee cannot be more than the amount reported in the form.
    • Per form: each late form has its own fee.
    • Payment: within 30 days of the fee advice. If it lapses, the delay runs to the date of the fresh application.
    • Three years: beyond three years from the due date the fee route is not available; see FEMA compounding for foreign-owned companies.
    • What the fee does not cure: shares issued below fair value, a sector that needed approval, or money received in the wrong account. Those are separate contraventions.
    • ECB forms: Form ECB 2 uses the formula. Form ECB 1 is reported to be a flat-fee return from 1 April 2026; confirm with the bank.

    FAQ

    Common questions, answered.

    What is the late submission fee under FEMA?
    For returns that report a flow of money, such as FC-GPR, FC-TRS, Form DI, Form FC and ECB 2, it is INR 7,500 plus 0.025% of the amount for each year of delay. For returns that report no flow, such as the FLA return and the Annual Performance Report, it is a flat INR 7,500.
    How is the delay counted for the FEMA late submission fee?
    In years, with the delay rounded up to the nearest month and expressed to two decimals. The fee is capped at 100% of the amount reported and rounded up to the nearest INR 100.
    What is the late fee for FC-GPR?
    INR 7,500 plus 0.025% of the amount received for the shares for each year of delay. For INR 1 crore filed five months late, the fee is about INR 8,600.
    What if a FEMA filing is more than three years late?
    The late submission fee is available only for three years from the due date. After that the delay must be compounded through an application to the RBI.
    How soon must the late submission fee be paid?
    Within 30 days of the fee advice. If it is not paid in time the advice lapses, and the delay is then counted up to the date of the fresh application.
