PPF for NRIs (2026): Can You Continue Your PPF After Becoming an NRI? Maturity, Premature Closure, Extension and Repatriation, continue to maturity, no new account, no extension.

An NRI cannot open a new PPF account, but a PPF opened while you were resident in India can continue until its 15-year maturity; you can keep depositing up to INR 1.5 lakh a year on a non-repatriation basis. The five-year extension after maturity is not available to NRIs, so plan to close the account at maturity. If you want out earlier, premature closure after five years is allowed when the reason is a change in residency status, with the interest reduced by one percentage point. The money is paid into your NRO account and can be sent abroad within the USD 1 million a year NRO limit with Forms 145/146 (formerly 15CA/15CB). PPF interest stays tax-free in India, but your country of residence may tax it.

PPF is tax-free in India, not necessarily where you live.

The US, UK, Canada and Australia can tax PPF interest each year, and the account must be reported abroad. Moving the money out goes through NRO and Forms 145/146. WhatsApp CA Durgesh Chavda.

/ Points to watch

Where NRIs get caught out.

  • Interest after maturity: sources and banks differ on whether an NRI's matured but unclosed account keeps earning interest; some NRI accounts were flagged under the Ministry of Finance’s August 2024 regularisation of irregular small-savings accounts. Confirm with your bank or post office and close on maturity unless told in writing otherwise.
  • Tell the bank you are NRI: update your residential status and KYC; link the PPF to an NRO account for credits.
  • Returning to India: once you are resident again you can open a new PPF, and a resident can use the extension facility.
  • US residents: PPF is generally treated as a foreign account for FBAR and Form 8938, and the interest is reported on the US return; the US-India treaty does not make it tax-free in the US. See FBAR.
  • Moving the money: see repatriating from NRO (USD 1 million, Forms 145/146).

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can an NRI continue a PPF account?
Yes, if it was opened while resident. It can run until its 15-year maturity, with deposits up to INR 1.5 lakh a year on a non-repatriation basis. NRIs cannot open a new PPF.
Can an NRI extend PPF after 15 years?
No. The five-year block extension is available to residents only, so NRIs should close the account at maturity.
Can an NRI close PPF before maturity?
Yes, after five complete years, if the reason is a change in residency status. Interest is reduced by one percentage point for the period.
Can PPF money be sent abroad?
Yes. It is credited to the NRO account and can be remitted within the USD 1 million per financial year NRO limit, with Forms 145/146 (formerly 15CA/15CB).
Is PPF interest taxable for NRIs?
Not in India; PPF interest and maturity are exempt. The country of residence may tax it: the US, UK, Canada and Australia generally do, and the account must be reported there.
