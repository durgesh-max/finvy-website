/ NRIs · Travel · Customs · Tax · Updated 2026-11-20

Visiting India in December 2026 as an NRI: Baggage Rules 2026 (Gold and Duty-Free Limits), Cash Declaration, Gifts to Family, Property Work and Tax Checklist, what to carry, what to declare, and what to get done while you are in India.

NRIs flying to India for the holidays or a family wedding have new rules this year. Under the Baggage Rules, 2026 (in force from 2 February 2026) the general duty-free allowance is INR 75,000. If you have lived abroad for over a year, you can bring gold jewellery duty-free by weight: 40 grams for women and 20 grams for others. Coins and bars are not covered, and anything above the limit must be declared and taxed. Declare foreign currency notes above USD 5,000, or total foreign exchange above USD 10,000, on the Currency Declaration Form; you can file in advance on the ATITHI app. Gifts to relatives are tax-free in their hands; gifts from non-relatives above INR 50,000 are taxable. Use the trip to finish paperwork that needs you in person: a property sale or power of attorney, bank and PAN updates, and NRO-to-abroad remittances. Count your days, because a long stay can change your residential status.

/ Gifts, property and banking

Make the trip count.

  • Gifts to family: cash, jewellery or transfers to relatives (parents, siblings, spouse, children and their spouses, and others defined in the law) are not taxed in their hands; gifts from non-relatives above INR 50,000 in a year are; see NRI gifts to relatives.
  • Selling property: apply for a lower deduction certificate before signing, or the buyer deducts TDS on the full price; see selling property from abroad and TDS and LDC calculator.
  • Buying property: pay from NRE or by remittance to keep the price repatriable; see can an NRI buy property.
  • Power of attorney: signing in India avoids apostille delays later.
  • Banking: update KYC, convert savings accounts to NRO if not done, and arrange NRO repatriation with Forms 145/146 (formerly 145/146 (formerly 15CA/15CB)); see repatriating money.

/ Tax deadlines around your trip

December is busy.

  • 15 December 2026: advance tax if your Indian tax after TDS exceeds INR 10,000.
  • 31 December 2026: belated or revised return for FY 2025-26 to claim TDS refunds; FAST-DS 2026 for returning NRIs with unreported foreign assets.
  • Residency: 182 days in India in the financial year (or 120 days if Indian income exceeds INR 15 lakh and the 365-day test is met) makes you resident; see residential status calculator.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How much gold jewellery can an NRI bring to India duty-free in 2026?
Under the Baggage Rules, 2026, passengers resident abroad for over a year can bring gold jewellery up to 40 grams (women) or 20 grams (others) duty-free; coins and bars are not covered and excess must be declared with duty.
What is the duty-free allowance for NRIs flying to India?
INR 75,000 for residents and Indian-origin tourists arriving by air or sea, raised from INR 50,000 by the Baggage Rules, 2026.
How much foreign currency can I bring into India?
There is no limit, but foreign currency notes above USD 5,000 or total foreign exchange above USD 10,000 must be declared on the Currency Declaration Form.
Are gifts from an NRI to family in India taxable?
No, gifts to relatives are not taxed in their hands; gifts from non-relatives above INR 50,000 in a year are taxable to the recipient.
Can a December visit make me resident in India?
Only if your total days in India in the financial year reach 182, or 120 for Indian citizens and PIOs with Indian income above INR 15 lakh who meet the 365-day test.
