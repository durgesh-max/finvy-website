China Plus One: Moving Sourcing or Manufacturing to India (2026) - Sourcing, Contract Manufacturing or Your Own Factory, Incentives, Tax and Checks, for foreign brands, importers and manufacturers.

Companies adding India to a China-heavy supply chain usually move in three stages. First they source from Indian suppliers, then they use an Indian contract manufacturer, then they build their own factory through an Indian subsidiary. Each stage needs more diligence and gives more control. The numbers that decide the business case: a new manufacturing company pays 22% corporate tax plus surcharge and cess (25.168%), because the old 15% rate closed to companies starting production after 31 March 2024. Production-linked incentive (PLI) schemes cover 14 sectors. Duty on imported inputs can be deferred by manufacturing in a bonded warehouse (MOOWR) or in an SEZ. Two checks come first: whether your group has beneficial owners in China or another land-border country, which needs Indian government approval to invest; and whether your supplier or partner is genuine and can deliver quality and volume.

/ Incentives, duty and tax

What changes the landed cost.

  • Corporate tax: 25.168% effective under section 115BAA for a new manufacturing company; the 15% concessional regime closed to companies starting manufacturing after 31 March 2024.
  • PLI schemes: production-linked incentives in 14 sectors (for example electronics, pharmaceuticals, auto components, textiles, specialty steel, solar modules) with eligibility thresholds and investment and sales targets; check whether your sector's window is still open.
  • Imported inputs: manufacturing in a customs bonded warehouse (MOOWR) defers duty on imported inputs and removes it for exported output; SEZ units get duty-free inputs (the SEZ income-tax holiday is closed to units starting after 30 June 2020).
  • GST: exports are zero-rated; input tax credit on domestic purchases is refundable for exporters.
  • State incentives: capital subsidies, stamp duty and electricity concessions vary by state; compare two or three states before choosing land.
  • Your market: check current tariffs and rules of origin for the US, EU or UK, which decide whether goods qualify as Indian.

/ Checks before you commit

Where projects go wrong.

  • Supplier and partner diligence: company records, financials, GST and export history, litigation, factory audit. See verify an Indian company and supplier verification.
  • Land-border ownership: investment by an entity whose beneficial owner is in China or another land-border country needs government approval; small non-controlling stakes were eased in 2026. See Press Note 2 (2026).
  • Contract terms: IP ownership, tooling, quality, exclusivity, audit rights and arbitration.
  • Permanent establishment: your engineers supervising a contract manufacturer for long periods can create a taxable presence; see permanent establishment.
  • Import-export set-up: import-export code, customs and GST registration; see IEC, GST and customs.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the China plus one strategy for India?
Adding India as a second supply base alongside China, usually by sourcing from Indian suppliers first, then contract manufacturing, then building an own factory through an Indian subsidiary.
What tax does a new manufacturing company pay in India?
22% plus surcharge and cess (25.168% effective) under section 115BAA. The 15% regime for new manufacturers closed to companies starting manufacturing after 31 March 2024.
Are there incentives for manufacturing in India?
Yes: production-linked incentive schemes in 14 sectors, duty deferral on imported inputs through MOOWR or SEZ units, GST refunds for exporters, and state-level subsidies.
Can a company with Chinese shareholders invest in India?
It needs Indian government approval if a beneficial owner is in China or another country sharing a land border with India; small non-controlling stakes were eased in 2026.
Should we contract-manufacture or build our own plant?
Contract manufacturing is faster and needs less capital; an own plant gives control over quality and IP. Many companies start with a contract manufacturer and build later.
