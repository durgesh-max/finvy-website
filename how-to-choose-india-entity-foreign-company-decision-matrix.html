/ Foreign Entity Decision Matrix · Updated 2026-10-23

How to Choose India Entity for Foreign Company, WOS vs Branch vs Liaison vs JV vs LLP.

Foreign company entering India chooses between 5 entity structures: Wholly-Owned Subsidiary Pvt Ltd (WOS), Branch Office, Liaison Office, Project Office, LLP, Joint Venture with Indian partner. Each has distinct FDI route, tax rate, repatriation flexibility, compliance burden, operational scope. BQP decision matrix framework for foreign-client India entry. Here's the complete 2026 framework.

/ The 5-question decision tree

Pick based on foreign company context.

Q1: Will you have Indian employees + Indian customers + revenue in India?

  • Yes → WOS (Pvt Ltd) - the default choice. 90%+ of foreign entrants.
  • No → continue to Q2.

Q2: Only representation / market research / exploration in India (no sales / no services)?

  • Yes → Liaison Office (RBI prior approval + narrow permitted activities + no revenue).
  • No → continue to Q3.

Q3: Specific project-based engagement with Indian client (government contract, specific infrastructure)?

  • Yes → Project Office (time-bound + specific project only).
  • No → continue to Q4.

Q4: Specific narrow operational scope (export/import, consultancy, R&D)?

  • Yes + parent wants direct accountability (branch's acts bind parent) → Branch Office (RBI prior approval + 40% branch tax + narrow permitted activities).
  • No → continue to Q5.

Q5: Sector-restricted FDI OR specific partnership strategic value with Indian partner?

  • Yes → JV (Joint Venture) with Indian partner (Pvt Ltd structure with Indian equity partner).
  • No → WOS (Pvt Ltd) 100% foreign-owned via automatic FDI route.

LLP alternative: specifically for small operations in automatic-FDI sectors + pass-through tax preference. Not common for foreign parents.

/ Why WOS wins 90% of foreign-entrant decisions

The default rationale.

  • Lower tax: 115BAA 22% effective 25.17% vs Branch 40%+ (branch tax rate for foreign company).
  • No RBI prior approval: FDI automatic route in most sectors - 30-45 day setup vs 2-4 months for Branch / Liaison.
  • Operational flexibility: can enter any lawful business + hire employees + sign long-term customer contracts + raise local debt + eventual IPO.
  • Exit flexibility: sell shares to Indian / foreign buyer + buyback + voluntary winding up.
  • Liability shield: WOS liability stays with WOS; doesn't automatically extend to foreign parent.
  • Lower post-closure overhead: WOS is standalone entity; closure doesn't trigger foreign-parent tax issues.
  • Section 115BAA 22% + no MAT: better than most alternative Indian structures.

/ When Non-WOS makes sense

The specific 10% exceptions.

  • Branch Office: specific narrow operational scope (export/import, consultancy, R&D). Parent prefers direct accountability. Specific sector tradition (airlines, insurance, specific).
  • Liaison Office: pure representation / market exploration without any revenue plan. Must upgrade to WOS/Branch when commercial activity begins.
  • Project Office: specific project with Indian government / infrastructure client. Auto-closes at project end.
  • JV with Indian partner: sector-restricted FDI (defence 74% + Indian partner), specific strategic value from Indian partner (government relationships, specific supply chain, local market knowledge).
  • LLP: small operations + automatic FDI sectors + pass-through tax preference. Rare for foreign parents.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Why do 90% of foreign entrants choose WOS?
Lower tax (115BAA 22% vs Branch 40%+), no RBI prior approval (FDI automatic route - 30-45 day setup vs 2-4 months Branch/Liaison), operational flexibility (any lawful business + employees + customers), exit flexibility (sell/buyback/winding up), liability shield (WOS liability stays with WOS). Default decision for most foreign companies entering India for business.
When does Branch Office make sense?
Narrow operational scope matching permitted activities (export/import, consultancy, R&D, specific agency, buying/selling agent). Parent prefers direct accountability + specific sector tradition. Short-medium term presence. Parent OK with branch 40%+ tax rate vs 22% WOS. 2-4 month RBI prior approval cycle acceptable.
Can a foreign company own 100% of Indian WOS?
Yes - via FDI automatic route in most sectors. Prohibited sectors: lottery, chit funds, real estate dealing, atomic energy. Capped sectors: multi-brand retail 51%, print media 26%, defence 74%, insurance 74% (post 2024). Press Note 3 (eased for non-controlling stakes within 10% by Press Note 2 (2026)) restriction: investment from land-bordering country (China, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar, Afghanistan) requires prior government approval regardless of sector.
Does foreign company need resident director?
Yes - Section 149(3) Companies Act 2013: every Indian company (WOS or JV) must have at least one director resident in India (182+ days in preceding FY). BQP arranges nominee resident director if foreign parent has no India-resident person. CA-qualified nominee + indemnity + replacement workflow + DIR-3 KYC (every three years).
What's typical WOS setup timeline for foreign parent?
30-45 days end-to-end: DSC/DIN (3 days) + name reservation (2 days) + SPICe+ incorporation (10 days) + bank account (14 days) + FC-GPR filing after capital remittance (variable). Longest step: foreign document apostille (7-21 days depending on country).
