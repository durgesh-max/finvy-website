Can an OCI Cardholder Have an NRI Account? NRE, NRO and FCNR Accounts for NRIs and OCIs (2026): Tax, Repatriation and What Changes When You Return, yes, while you live outside India.

Yes. An OCI cardholder living outside India can open the same accounts as an NRI: NRE, NRO and FCNR(B) (FEMA (Deposit) Regulations, 2016). NRE and FCNR interest is tax-free in India for non-residents and the money is fully repatriable; NRO interest is taxable, with the bank deducting 30% plus surcharge and cess unless you give treaty documents, and repatriation from NRO is capped at USD 1 million per financial year. If you move back to India and become resident, the bank must redesignate the accounts as resident accounts.

NRO interest losing 30% to TDS?

If you live in a treaty country, a tax residency certificate and Form 41 bring the deduction down to the treaty rate, often 10% or 15%. WhatsApp CA Durgesh Chavda.

/ Points to watch

Common mistakes.

  • Indian income such as rent must be credited to the NRO account, not NRE.
  • Interest that is tax-free in India is usually still taxable in your country of residence (for example, the US, UK, Canada and Australia tax worldwide income).
  • Your tax residence and FEMA residence are tested separately; spending long periods in India can change both.
  • On return to India, NRE and NRO accounts are redesignated as resident accounts; FCNR deposits can run to maturity, and you may be RNOR for a few years.
  • US persons also report Indian accounts on FBAR and FATCA forms.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can an OCI cardholder open an NRE or NRO account?
Yes. OCI cardholders resident outside India can open NRE, NRO and FCNR(B) accounts, like NRIs.
Is NRE interest taxable in India?
No, while the account holder is non-resident. It may still be taxable in the country where you live.
How much TDS is deducted on NRO interest?
30% plus surcharge and cess by default; the treaty rate applies if you give the bank a tax residency certificate, Form 41 and PAN.
How much can I send abroad from an NRO account?
Current income such as rent and dividends after tax, plus up to USD 1 million per financial year of other balances, with the CA certificate and bank forms.
What happens to NRE and NRO accounts when I move back to India?
They must be redesignated as resident accounts once you become resident; FCNR deposits can continue to maturity.
