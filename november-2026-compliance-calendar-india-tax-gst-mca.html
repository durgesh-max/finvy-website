/ Already passed or extended

So you do not panic.

  • AOC-4 (FY 2025-26): due 30 October, but the additional fee is waived if filed by 31 December 2026 (MCA General Circular 05/2026).
  • LLP Form 8: due 30 October 2026, no extension.
  • Form 3CEB and MSME Form I: due 31 October 2026.
  • DIR-3 KYC: no longer annual; once every three years by 30 June.

/ Coming in December

  • 15 December: third advance tax instalment (75%).
  • 31 December: belated and revised ITRs for FY 2025-26; FAST-DS foreign asset disclosure; GSTR-9/9C for FY 2025-26; AOC-4 fee waiver ends.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What are the main compliance deadlines in November 2026?
21 November: ITR for audit cases. 29 November: MGT-7/MGT-7A and PAS-6. 30 November: ITR for transfer pricing cases, last date to claim FY 2025-26 GST input tax credit, and pensioners' life certificates. GST changes from the 57th GST Council take effect on 1 November.
Is the MGT-7 deadline extended?
The due date stays 29 November 2026, but MCA General Circular 05/2026 waives the additional fee for FY 2025-26 returns filed by 31 January 2027.
What is the last date to claim GST input tax credit for FY 2025-26?
30 November 2026, or the date you file the FY 2025-26 annual return if that is earlier.
When must pensioners submit the life certificate in 2026?
By 30 November 2026. Pensioners aged 80 and above could submit from 1 October; others from 1 November.
