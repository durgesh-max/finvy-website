Can a Foreign Company or Foreigner Buy Property or Land in India? (2026) FEMA Rules for Branches, Subsidiaries, Liaison Offices and Foreign Nationals, who can buy, who can only lease, and what to file.

A foreign company cannot buy property in India directly. It can buy only through a presence here. A branch office or project office may buy property that is necessary for its permitted activity, and must report the purchase to the RBI in Form IPI within 90 days. A liaison office cannot buy; it can lease premises for up to five years. An Indian subsidiary is an Indian company, so it can buy an office, factory or land for its business like any other company. It cannot trade in real estate, and farming and agricultural land are restricted. A foreign national living abroad who is not of Indian origin cannot buy property in India, but can take a lease of up to five years or inherit from a resident. NRIs and OCI cardholders can buy residential and commercial property but not agricultural land. Buyers from certain neighbouring countries need prior RBI approval.

/ Practical points

Before you sign.

  • Lease first: a lease of up to five years needs no FEMA approval and suits most new entrants.
  • Subsidiary route: most foreign groups that want to own premises do it through an Indian subsidiary; see the India entry structure chooser.
  • Branch route: the branch itself needs RBI approval to exist; see RBI approval for a place of business. Sending sale proceeds abroad later needs RBI approval.
  • Real estate business: foreign investment is not allowed in buying and selling land or property for profit; construction-development, and leasing of completed property, are treated differently.
  • Agricultural land: foreign companies and foreign nationals cannot buy it; state land laws add their own limits, even for Indian companies.
  • Listed countries: entities and citizens of Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Afghanistan, China, Iran, Nepal and Bhutan, among others, need prior RBI approval.
  • Tax on purchase: buyers deduct TDS when buying from an NRI seller; see buying property from an NRI.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can a foreign company buy property in India?
Not directly. A foreign company can buy only through a branch or project office in India, for property needed for its permitted activity, or through an Indian subsidiary, which is treated as an Indian company.
Can a liaison office buy property in India?
No. A liaison office cannot acquire immovable property; it can take premises on lease for up to five years.
What is Form IPI?
A declaration to the Reserve Bank of India that a branch or other office of a foreign entity must file within 90 days of acquiring immovable property in India.
Can a foreign company buy agricultural land in India?
No. Foreign companies and foreign nationals cannot buy agricultural land, and an Indian subsidiary faces foreign investment and state land-law restrictions on agricultural land.
Can a foreigner own land in India?
A foreign national of non-Indian origin living outside India cannot buy immovable property, but can lease for up to five years or inherit from a resident. A foreign national resident in India generally can, subject to RBI approval for citizens of certain countries.
