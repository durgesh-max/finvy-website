FAST-DS for Returning NRIs: Foreign Accounts and Pensions Not Reported After Coming Back, and whether current NRIs can use it.

Many returning NRIs kept foreign bank accounts, brokerage accounts, pensions or property but did not list them in the foreign-assets schedule once they became resident and ordinarily resident. Assets built from foreign income earned while non-resident fall in Category 2 of FAST-DS (fee INR 1 lakh, value up to INR 5 crore). Interest, dividends or rent on them that were not offered to tax in resident years are Category 1 (60%). A current NRI can also use the scheme if he or she was resident when the income arose or the asset was acquired. Declarations close on 31 December 2026.

/ Where returning NRIs stand

Year by year.

  • RNOR years: foreign income is generally not taxable and the foreign-assets schedule is not required, so there is usually nothing undisclosed for those years.
  • First resident and ordinarily resident year onwards: foreign assets must be listed in the schedule and worldwide income taxed. Missing these is what FAST-DS cures.
  • Eligibility: a person resident in the year, or a non-resident/RNOR who was resident when the income arose or the asset was acquired (section 131(1)(a)).

/ Steps

What to do now.

  1. Work out your residential status for each year since you returned (use our residential status calculator).
  2. List each foreign asset with its value as on 31 March 2026 and the source of funds.
  3. Check the AIS foreign-assets information on the e-filing portal.
  4. Classify each item (eligibility checker) and file Form 1 by 31 December 2026.

Source: Finance Act, 2026, Chapter IV (sections 130 to 144), the Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers Disclosure Scheme, 2026, as published by the Income Tax Department. Forms, valuation and payment mechanics are in the FAST-DS Rules. Last updated: 4 November 2026.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

I returned to India and kept my US accounts. Do I need FAST-DS?
If you have been resident and ordinarily resident and did not list them in the foreign-assets schedule, yes, usually under Category 2 for assets built while non-resident (INR 1 lakh fee), with any untaxed income from them under Category 1.
Can a current NRI file FAST-DS?
Yes, if he or she was resident in India in the year the income arose or the asset was acquired (section 131(1)(a)(ii)).
Did I need to report foreign assets in RNOR years?
No. The foreign-assets schedule is for residents who are ordinarily resident; RNOR years are generally outside it.
