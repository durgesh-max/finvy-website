/ How it works

Two categories.

  • Category 1: foreign income or assets never taxed in India, up to INR 1 crore in aggregate. Pay 30% tax plus an equal additional amount: 60% of the value.
  • Category 2: assets already taxed, or acquired while non-resident, but not shown in Schedule FA, up to INR 5 crore. Flat fee of INR 1 lakh.
  • Assets are valued at fair market value as on 31 March 2026.
  • A valid declaration gives immunity from tax, penalty and prosecution under the Black Money Act for the declared items.
  • For comparison, the Black Money Act charges 30% tax and a penalty of up to three times that tax if undisclosed assets are found, and prosecution is possible.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How much do I pay under FAST-DS 2026?
Category 1 (untaxed foreign income or assets up to INR 1 crore): 60% of the value. Category 2 (taxed-but-unreported assets up to INR 5 crore): a flat INR 1 lakh.
What is the last date for FAST-DS 2026?
31 December 2026.
Which date is used to value foreign assets?
Fair market value as on 31 March 2026.
What happens if I don't declare?
If found later, undisclosed foreign assets are taxed under the Black Money Act at 30% plus a penalty of up to three times the tax, and prosecution is possible.
