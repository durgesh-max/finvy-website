FAST-DS 2026: Declare Undisclosed Foreign Assets by 31 December 2026, the one-time window for small taxpayers.

FAST-DS 2026 (Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers Disclosure Scheme) lets eligible taxpayers declare foreign income or assets they left out of their Indian returns and get immunity under the Black Money Act. Declarations opened on 16 August 2026 and close on 31 December 2026. It is aimed at returning students, young professionals, technology employees and former NRIs who still hold overseas accounts, shares, ESOPs or property. / The two categories

Which one applies to you.

Category 1Category 2
What it coversForeign income or assets never offered to tax in IndiaAssets already taxed, or acquired while you were a non-resident, but not shown in the return's foreign-asset schedule
Value limitUp to INR 1 crore in aggregateUp to INR 5 crore
Cost30% tax plus an equal additional amount: 60% of the valueFlat fee of INR 1 lakh

Foreign assets are valued at fair market value as on 31 March 2026. CBDT's own illustration: an undisclosed foreign account of INR 60 lakh plus INR 20 lakh of related income gives INR 80 lakh; at 60% the amount payable is INR 48 lakh.

/ What you get

Immunity and finality.

  • Protection from further tax, penalty and prosecution under the Black Money (Undisclosed Foreign Income and Assets) and Imposition of Tax Act, 2015, for the asset or income declared.
  • The declared amount is not added again to your total income under the Income-tax Act or the Black Money Act.
  • Black Money Act penalties outside the scheme can be far higher, and prosecution is possible, so the scheme is materially cheaper for most eligible people.

/ Who should look at this now

Common cases.

  • You returned to India and are now resident, but your US 401(k), UK ISA, foreign bank account or RSUs are not in Schedule FA.
  • You studied or worked abroad and kept a foreign account open after moving back.
  • You hold ESOPs or RSUs of a foreign employer that vested while you were resident in India.
  • You were resident in a year but filed as a non-resident, so foreign assets were never reported.
  • You inherited or received a gift of foreign assets and did not report them.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is FAST-DS 2026?
A one-time scheme for small taxpayers to declare foreign income or assets missed in Indian returns, with immunity under the Black Money Act. It opened on 16 August 2026 and closes on 31 December 2026.
How much does FAST-DS cost?
Category 1 (untaxed foreign income or assets up to INR 1 crore): 30% tax plus an equal additional amount, 60% in total. Category 2 (already-taxed assets up to INR 5 crore that were not reported): a flat INR 1 lakh fee.
Who is FAST-DS meant for?
Returning students, young professionals, technology employees and former NRIs with overseas accounts, shares, ESOPs or retirement savings that were not reported after they became resident in India.
What happens after 31 December 2026?
Declarations cannot be filed after that date. Undisclosed foreign assets found later fall under the Black Money Act, with higher tax, penalties and possible prosecution.
