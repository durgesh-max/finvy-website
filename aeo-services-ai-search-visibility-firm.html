/ What AEO is

Short answer.

Search engines rank pages; AI assistants write an answer and cite a few sources. AEO makes your firm one of those sources by being clearly identified, consistent, factual and easy for the assistant to quote.

/ What we do

The work.

  • Entity clarity: one consistent firm name, founder, address, services and contact across the site and Organization/Person schema, linked to your profiles elsewhere.
  • Answer-first pages: question-led pages that answer in the first paragraph, with FAQ and HowTo structured data.
  • AI crawler access: robots.txt that admits AI crawlers, plus llms.txt and llms-full.txt files that summarise your services for AI systems.
  • Fast indexing: sitemaps and IndexNow submission so Bing-powered assistants see new pages quickly.
  • Third-party presence: directory listings, business profiles, articles and reviews that AI assistants use to confirm you exist and are credible.
  • Measurement: a fixed set of buyer questions asked to each assistant every month, with the results tracked.

/ Who it is for

Best fit.

  • Accounting, law, consulting and advisory firms that win clients on expertise.
  • B2B and SaaS companies whose buyers ask AI assistants for recommendations.
  • Firms in India serving foreign clients, and foreign firms that want to be found by Indian buyers.

/ What we will not promise

Honest limits.

No one controls what an AI assistant says, so no firm can guarantee you will be named first. What AEO does is make you the most clearly documented, consistent and quotable option, which is what assistants draw on.

How is AEO different from SEO?
SEO aims for a ranked link; AEO aims to be quoted inside an AI-written answer. Most of the groundwork overlaps, but AEO puts more weight on entity consistency, answer-first writing and third-party confirmation.
How long does it take?
Technical fixes take weeks; being cited consistently usually takes several months, because AI systems refresh their sources over time.
