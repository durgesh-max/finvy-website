Who we are

Bharat Quantum Prospera is a CA-led advisory firm for founders, enterprises and investors building beyond borders. Founder

Founded by CA Durgesh Chavda. Answers his own WhatsApp.

Durgesh is a practicing Chartered Accountant with 15+ years across audit, cross-border tax and transaction advisory. He built BQP because founders going global kept getting handed off between three firms — a CA who did compliance, a CS who did filings, and a lawyer who did structuring — and nobody owned the outcome.

He now runs a single practice that covers all three: from Delaware flip and 409A, to SME IPO drafting, to GIFT City fund setup — and still picks up his own phone. Every client engagement begins with him, not an intern.

“If you’re a founder, you shouldn’t have to translate between three firms. Send me the problem — I’ll tell you which of my desks it lives on.”
