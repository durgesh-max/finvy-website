/ Canadian ULC primer

The hybrid entity.

  • ULC (Unlimited Liability Corporation): Canadian corporate form in NS, AB, BC.
  • Canadian tax: taxed as corporation.
  • US tax (via check-the-box election): often disregarded entity (passed through to US parent / member) OR partnership (multi-member).
  • Hybrid characterisation enables specific US-Canada tax planning: Canadian tax deductions pushed to US parent + specific repatriation structures.
  • Used by US multinationals with Canadian subsidiaries + specific IP structures.

/ Indian software captive + ULC chain

The flow.

  • US parent → Canadian ULC → Indian software captive (as Indian subsidiary of ULC).
  • Indian captive renders software development services to Canadian ULC (immediate parent).
  • Indian captive income: transfer-pricing-benchmarked cost-plus to ULC.
  • ULC business income: Indian service revenue minus Indian cost base = ULC profit → Canadian corporate tax (specific federal + provincial rates total ~26-27%).
  • ULC distributes to US parent: hybrid characterisation treats this as transparent US pass-through.
  • US parent + CRA + CBDT: tri-party compliance.

/ Indian compliance for software captive

The India side.

  • Indian captive = AE of ULC under Section 92A.
  • Transfer pricing Form 3CEB mandatory.
  • TNMM cost-plus with 15-20% OP/OC typical for software captive.
  • Safe Harbour Rules (SHR) optional: Rule 10TD + 10TE.
  • Section 195 TDS on service receivables from ULC: typically 2% equalisation levy or specific treaty / specific DTAA relief under India-Canada DTAA.
  • FEMA FDI reporting: ULC's investment in Indian captive via FC-GPR.
  • Annual MCA compliance: AOC-4 + MGT-7 + DIR-3 KYC + specific board resolutions.

/ Canada side considerations

ULC-level compliance.

  • Canadian corporate tax on ULC's net income (service revenue from Indian captive minus specific operating costs).
  • Specific CRA transfer pricing documentation (Form T106 for cross-border transactions above CAD 1M).
  • India-Canada DTAA + FTC for Indian tax paid.
  • Specific BEPS Pillar 2 if MNE consolidated revenue EUR 750M+.
  • Specific CRA scrutiny of ULC + offshore planning.

/ India-Canada DTAA framework

The treaty.

  • India-Canada DTAA (operational since 1998; MLI covered agreement).
  • Residence tie-breaker: specific tests applied.
  • Dividend: 15% treaty rate (specific exceptions for substantial holdings).
  • Interest: 15% treaty rate (specific exceptions for banks).
  • Royalty / FTS: 10-20% depending on specific scenarios.
  • Business profits: taxable only via PE (Article 5).
  • Capital gain: taxable in residence state subject to specific real estate / substantial holding carve-outs.
  • Specific MLI PPT + GAAR scrutiny for ULC-style hybrid structures.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Why do US multinationals use Canadian ULCs?
ULC = hybrid entity: taxable corporation in Canada + fiscally transparent in US (via check-the-box). Enables specific US-Canada tax planning: Canadian tax deductions push to US + specific repatriation structures. Common with US multinationals having Canadian + Indian subsidiaries.
Does India-Canada DTAA apply to Canadian ULC?
ULC is Canadian resident for Canadian tax purposes - DTAA treats as Canadian resident. India-Canada DTAA Article 4 residence tie-breaker + Article 5 PE + Article 7 business profits + specific withholding rates apply. MLI PPT + India GAAR apply - substance file for ULC essential.
Can Indian software captive opt for Safe Harbour Rules?
Yes - Rule 10TD + 10TE for software development services. 17-18% OP/OC rate depending on operating cost scale. Safe Harbour offers audit exemption for 5-year block. Opt-in Form 3CEFA. Alternative: TNMM benchmarking study + specific comparability analysis. BQP evaluates optimal choice.
What's BEPS Pillar 2 impact on ULC chain?
If MNE consolidated revenue EUR 750M+: GloBE rules apply. QDMTT in Canada (effective FY 2024) + India QDMTT (FY 2025-26). IIR (Income Inclusion Rule) + UTPR (Undertaxed Payments Rule). ULC hybrid tax planning may trigger specific minimum tax top-ups. BQP + Canadian partner Pillar 2 assessment.
