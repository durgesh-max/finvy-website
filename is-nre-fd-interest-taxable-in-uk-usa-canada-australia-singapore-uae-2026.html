Is NRE FD Interest Taxable in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Singapore or UAE? (2026) Country-by-Country Answer, tax-free in India, but often taxable where you live.

NRE fixed deposit interest is tax-free in India while you are a non-resident, but your country of residence may still tax it. USA, UK, Canada and Australia tax their residents on worldwide income, so NRE interest is normally taxable there in full. Because India charges no tax on it, there is no foreign tax credit to set off. Two exceptions: new UK arrivals who qualify for the four-year foreign income and gains (FIG) regime from April 2025, and temporary residents of Australia, who are generally not taxed on foreign income. Singapore tax residents are generally exempt on foreign-sourced income, and the UAE has no personal income tax, so NRE interest is usually not taxed in either. NRO interest is different: it is taxed in India (TDS 30% plus surcharge and cess, or the treaty rate, usually 15% and 12.5% for the UAE), and that Indian tax is creditable abroad.

Tax-free in India is not tax-free everywhere.

Because India does not tax NRE interest, there is no Indian tax to offset abroad, so the full interest is usually taxed where you live. We give you an India-side statement of NRE, NRO and FCNR interest, TDS and treaty rates for your overseas return. WhatsApp CA Durgesh Chavda.

/ Practical points

Avoid the common mistakes.

  • Report the interest as it accrues where your country taxes accrued interest (for example cumulative FDs may still need annual reporting in the US, Canada and Australia); check the local rule.
  • Use the right exchange rate for each country's rules (annual average or transaction-date rates).
  • Keep the bank's interest certificate for NRE, NRO and FCNR separately; NRO TDS certificates (Form 16A) support the foreign tax credit.
  • Missed reporting abroad? Most countries have voluntary disclosure routes; fix it before the tax office asks, since banks report Indian accounts under CRS (and FATCA for US persons).

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is NRE FD interest taxable in India?
No, while you are a non-resident (a person resident outside India under FEMA). It becomes taxable once you return and become resident, so accounts should be converted on return.
Is NRE FD interest taxable in the UK?
Usually yes for UK residents, because the remittance basis ended on 6 April 2025. Qualifying new arrivals who were non-resident for the previous 10 years can claim four years of relief under the FIG regime. The personal savings allowance applies to all savings interest.
Is NRE FD interest taxable in the USA?
Yes for US citizens, green-card holders and US tax residents, who are taxed on worldwide income. Report it on Schedule B and file FBAR and Form 8938 where the thresholds are met.
Is NRE FD interest taxable in Canada or Australia?
Yes for residents of Canada and Australia, who are taxed on worldwide income. Australian temporary residents are generally not taxed on foreign investment income. Canadians also check Form T1135 if foreign property exceeds CAD 100,000.
Is NRE FD interest taxable in Singapore or the UAE?
Generally no. Singapore exempts foreign-sourced income received by resident individuals (except through a partnership), and the UAE has no personal income tax.
