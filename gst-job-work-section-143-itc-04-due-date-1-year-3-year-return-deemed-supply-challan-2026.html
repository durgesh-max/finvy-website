/ Manufacturers, brand owners and job workers · GST job work · Updated 2026-11-28

GST on Job Work (2026): Section 143, Sending Goods Without Tax, the One-Year and Three-Year Limits, Deemed Supply, Challans and Form ITC-04 Due Dates, track every challan until the goods come home.

A registered manufacturer can send inputs and capital goods to a job worker without paying GST, under a challan, as long as inputs come back within one year and capital goods within three years. If they do not, the goods are treated as supplied on the day they were sent, and tax is payable with interest. Movements are reported in Form ITC-04 - half-yearly if turnover exceeds INR 5 crore, annually otherwise.

/ The job work rules

Section 143 and rule 45.

PointRule
Sending goodsInputs and capital goods can go to a job worker without tax, under a challan
Return limit - inputsOne year from the date sent
Return limit - capital goodsThree years from the date sent
Outside the limitsMoulds, dies, jigs, fixtures and tools
Not returned in timeDeemed supply by the principal on the day the goods were sent; tax with interest
Supply from job worker's placeAllowed if the job worker is registered, or the premises are declared as the principal's additional place of business
Waste and scrapJob worker can supply directly if registered; otherwise the principal supplies it
Input tax creditPrincipal can take credit on inputs and capital goods sent, including those delivered directly to the job worker
  • ITC-04 due dates: principals with aggregate turnover above INR 5 crore file half-yearly - by 25 October (April-September) and 25 April (October-March); others file annually by 25 April.
  • Challans: issue a challan for each movement, both ways, with the details the rules require, and keep it with the goods.

/ Staying compliant

Where principals get caught.

  • Ageing report: track every challan by date; flag inputs nearing one year and capital goods nearing three years.
  • Deemed supply: where the limit has passed, pay tax with interest and treat the goods as supplied - it is cheaper to fix early than in an audit.
  • Losses at the job worker: account for process loss, wastage and scrap consistently in challans and ITC-04.
  • Job worker side: the job work service itself is taxable for the job worker if registered; goods received are not the job worker’s stock.
  • Audits and notices: ITC-04 and challan records are routinely matched with stock in GST audits.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is job work under GST?
Processing or working on goods belonging to another registered person (the principal). Under section 143, the principal can send inputs and capital goods to the job worker without paying GST, under a challan.
What is the time limit for goods sent for job work?
Inputs must be brought back, or supplied from the job worker's place, within one year, and capital goods within three years. Moulds, dies, jigs, fixtures and tools are outside these limits.
What happens if job work goods are not returned in time?
They are treated as supplied by the principal on the day they were originally sent, and GST is payable with interest from that date.
What is the due date for ITC-04?
Principals with aggregate turnover above INR 5 crore file ITC-04 half-yearly, by 25 October for April-September and by 25 April for October-March. Others file annually by 25 April.
Can the job worker sell finished goods directly?
Yes, from the job worker's premises, if the job worker is registered or the principal has declared those premises as an additional place of business.
