/ What's included in CA cost

The scope breakdown.

  • Pre-incorporation: name availability check + specific DSC for subscribers + specific DIN for directors + specific MoA + AoA drafting.
  • Incorporation: SPICe+ Part A + Part B form + specific attachments + specific signatures + MCA filing.
  • Statutory registrations: Section 139A PAN + Section 203A TAN + specific professional tax + specific ESIC/EPFO + specific state-specific registrations.
  • GST registration: compulsory for most businesses (CGST Section 22 + specific thresholds / specific voluntary).
  • Banking: AD-I category bank account opening + specific KYC documentation.
  • Post-incorporation compliance: first board meeting + specific statutory registers + first ITR filing + first MGT-7 / AOC-4.
  • Additional for foreign subsidiary: FEMA FC-GPR + FLA + SBO Form BEN-1/2 + Press Note 3 screening + specific pricing compliance.

/ Government fees component

Fixed statutory costs.

  • MCA SPICe+ filing fee: depends on authorised capital.
  • Stamp duty: set by the state of the registered office and the authorised capital.
  • DSC: one Class 3 digital signature per director/subscriber, from a licensed certifying authority.
  • DIN: allotted through SPICe+ for directors who do not already hold one.
  • PAN: applied for through SPICe+.
  • TAN: applied for through SPICe+.
  • GST registration: free (no government fee).
  • Specific professional tax: state-specific.
  • Specific trade licence: specific local body depending on business + location.
