/ India-Netherlands DTAA recap

The historical + current.

  • Historical India-Netherlands DTAA (1988) offered specific favourable rates: 10% dividend / 10% interest / 10% royalty + specific capital gain carve-outs.
  • Dutch BV was popular treaty-shopping vehicle for Indian inbound investment.
  • Specific 'Dividend Washing' and 'Capital Gain Routing' structures via Dutch BV were common pre-GAAR.
  • MLI PPT (effective dates per MLI positions): applies to India-Netherlands DTAA.
  • India GAAR (effective 1 April 2017): applies broadly.
  • Specific renegotiation + protocols: specific updated provisions.

/ Treaty-shopping risk analysis

The flag conditions.

  • Dutch BV with Indian-origin / US-origin / specific-origin UPE and no genuine Netherlands business = high treaty-shopping risk.
  • MLI PPT: deny treaty benefit if 'reasonable to conclude' obtaining benefit was one of principal purposes.
  • India GAAR Section 95-102: deny tax benefit if arrangement = Impermissible Avoidance Arrangement.
  • Dutch BV without substance = presumption of treaty shopping.
  • Specific documentation + commercial rationale critical for defence.

/ Economic substance requirements

What makes Dutch BV defensible.

  • Local employees: 2-5+ Dutch-resident employees (depending on size/activity).
  • Local premises: physical office in Netherlands (not virtual).
  • Local decision-making: board meetings in Netherlands + Dutch-resident directors majority.
  • Local expenses: specific operating costs - salaries, rent, utilities, specific contracts (typical EUR 300K-500K annually minimum).
  • Local banking: operational bank account(s) in Netherlands.
  • Local books: accounting records + financial statements prepared + audited under Dutch GAAP / IFRS.
  • Dutch corporate tax: paid at 25.8% (standard) on net taxable income.
  • Specific function: regional holding + treasury + specific sub-regional operational role beyond pure tax conduit.

/ Dutch participation exemption + substance

The holding benefit.

  • Dutch Participation Exemption (deelnemingsvrijstelling): 100% exemption on dividend + capital gain from qualifying participation (typically 5%+ shareholding).
  • Powerful tool for holding Indian subsidiaries + specific EU subsidiaries.
  • Dutch BV's dividend from Indian subsidiary: Dutch tax 0% (participation exemption).
  • Dutch BV's capital gain on Indian subsidiary sale: 0% Dutch tax (participation exemption).
  • Specific conditions: 5%+ holding + subsidiary in business + specific not-tax-haven subsidiary.
  • Combined with Dutch 25.8% CT on non-participation income + specific IP box (9% effective rate for qualifying IP).

/ MLI PPT + GAAR defence file

The documentation.

  • Substance file: evidence of local employees + premises + decision-making + banking + tax + accounting.
  • Commercial rationale memo: specific business reasons for Dutch BV beyond tax (EU regional access + specific operational advantage + specific industry positioning).
  • Decision-making file: board minutes + specific decisions + specific signatures.
  • Transfer pricing: Form 3CEB for Indian subsidiary + specific intra-group service pricing + specific royalty pricing + specific interest pricing.
  • TRC (Netherlands Belastingdienst) + Form 10F electronic filing + Section 90(4) DTAA relief claim in India.
  • Specific annual review + specific commercial rationale update.
  • Specific 3-7 year retention.

/ BEPS Pillar 2 + 2024 updates

The current regime.

  • Netherlands implemented BEPS Pillar 2 from 1 January 2024 (Wet minimumbelasting 2024).
  • QDMTT + IIR + UTPR per OECD GloBE rules.
  • 15% minimum effective rate for MNEs with EUR 750M+ consolidated revenue.
  • Netherlands 25.8% CT typically satisfies - but specific low-tax income + specific tax incentives may trigger top-up.
  • India QDMTT from FY 2025-26 - parallel compliance both sides.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is Netherlands BV still viable for Indian stepdown post-MLI + GAAR?
Yes - but only with genuine substance + commercial rationale. Pure tax-conduit Dutch BV = high denial risk under MLI PPT + GAAR. Defensible structure needs: 2-5+ Dutch employees + physical office + local decision-making + Dutch CT paid + specific commercial function + annual substance review. Operating budget EUR 300K-500K+ annually.
What's the Dutch participation exemption benefit?
100% Dutch tax exemption on dividend + capital gain from qualifying participation (5%+ shareholding + specific business subsidiary + specific not-tax-haven subsidiary). Dutch BV holding Indian subsidiary: 0% Dutch tax on dividend from India + 0% Dutch tax on capital gain on sale of Indian subsidiary shares. Powerful holding benefit.
Does 2024 Protocol change India-Netherlands DTAA?
Specific updates. MLI positions + specific residence tie-breakers + specific updated rates + specific anti-abuse rules.
