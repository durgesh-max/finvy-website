/ Common notice types + response windows

The playbook.

  • Section 143(1) intimation: computer-generated intimation of return processed. If demand: 30 days to pay or respond. If refund: no action needed. If adjustment: review + specific response.
  • Section 143(2) scrutiny: ITR selected for detailed assessment. Reply within the time in the notice; the assessment order is due within 12 months from the end of the financial year in which the return was filed.
  • Section 148 reassessment: formal reassessment proceeding. 30 days to file return + specific response + Section 147 full assessment.
  • Section 148A show cause notice: pre-reassessment show cause. Reply within the time stated in the notice + opportunity to have it dropped before Section 148.
  • Section 139(9) defective return: ITR has defects. 15 days to rectify defects.
  • Section 142(1) info call: specific documents / information requested by AO, by the date in the notice.
  • Section 245 adjustment: refund adjustment proposed against demand. 30 days to object.
  • Section 154 rectification: proposed rectification of mistake. 30 days to object.
  • Section 156 demand notice: tax demand notification. 30 days to pay OR 15 days to object + Section 220 interest.

/ Immediate action in first 24-72 hours

Preserve + plan.

  1. Acknowledge notice via Income Tax portal (incometax.gov.in - Login - Pending Actions).
  2. Note exact deadline (specified in notice).
  3. Download complete notice + supporting documents.
  4. Identify specific AY / specific income head / specific issue flagged.
  5. Preserve all records: ITR + Form 26AS (Form 168 from tax year 2026-27) + AIS/TIS + supporting documents + bank statements + agreements.
  6. Engage a CA as soon as the notice arrives - reply windows are short.
  7. Specific risk assessment: Section 148 reassessment / Section 270A penalty exposure / specific appeal implications.

/ Section 143(1) intimation response

The common first-contact.

  • Computer-generated intimation after ITR processing (typically 3-6 months after ITR filing).
  • Three outcomes:
  •   — Refund: Form 143(1) intimates refund + specific bank credit timeline (typically 2-6 weeks).
  •   — Demand: Form 143(1) intimates specific additional tax demand (interest 234A/B/C + specific adjustment).
  •   — No change: ITR accepted as filed - no action needed.
  • Response window for demand: 30 days to pay OR file rectification request under Section 154 OR appeal CIT(A) under Section 246A.
  • Common issues: TDS mismatch, Section 234A/B/C interest computation, specific disallowance (self-attested).
  • Rectification under Section 154: if specific apparent-on-face error.
  • Appeal: if substantive disagreement.

/ Section 143(2) scrutiny response

The detailed assessment.

  • ITR selected for scrutiny under Section 143(3).
  • CASS (Computer Aided Scrutiny Selection) OR manual selection by AO.
  • Reply within the time stated in the notice.
  • Assessment order due within 12 months from the end of the FY of filing + hearings.
  • Documents demanded: full reconciliation of ITR + Form 26AS + AIS + supporting documents.
  • Oral representation + specific document submission.
  • Section 143(3) order within 6 months + specific additions / disallowances.
  • CIT(A) appeal within 30 days of adverse order.
  • Section 270A penalty 50-200% on additions.

/ Section 148 / 148A response

The reassessment track.

  • Section 148A show cause notice: AO must issue it BEFORE the Section 148 notice (within 3 years from the end of the AY, or 5 years where escaped income is Rs 50 lakh or more, for notices from 1 September 2024).
  • Response window: as stated in the notice.
  • Response strategy: documentary refutation + legal position + specific exhibits.
  • Section 148A(d) order: AO decides whether to proceed with Section 148 OR drop.
  • If AO drops: no reassessment. Case closed.
  • If AO proceeds: Section 148 notice + 30 days to file return + Section 147 full assessment.
  • Writ in High Court if Section 148A procedure defective (specific case-law + specific grounds).
  • Check whether the notice is within the three-year / five-year limit.

/ Common response mistakes + how to avoid

What to watch.

  • Not acknowledging notice via portal (deadlines keep running).
  • Missing response deadline (AO proceeds with specific adverse presumptions).
  • Not engaging CA (DIY response often substandard).
  • Not preserving records (specific documentary refutation becomes difficult).
  • Not understanding risk cascade (143(2) can lead to 148, 148A to 148, specific appeal implications).
  • Not raising specific legal grounds (jurisdictional / procedural / substantive).
  • Not considering specific appeal path (CIT(A) / ITAT / High Court / writ) at outset.
  • Not managing Section 270A penalty defence (classification 50% vs 200% matters materially).

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How to respond to Income Tax notice India?
First identify notice type (143(1) / 143(2) / 148 / 148A / 139(9) / 142(1) / 245 / 154 / 156). Each has distinct response window + consequences. Acknowledge on portal + preserve records + engage a CA early + respond within the deadline stated in the notice. Default: no response = AO proceeds on the department's information.
What's the typical response deadline for Income Tax notice?
Varies by notice type. Section 143(1) demand: 30 days. Section 143(2) scrutiny: as stated in the notice (the assessment order is due within 12 months from the end of the financial year in which the return was filed). Section 148A show cause: as stated in the notice. Section 148: file the return within the period stated in the notice. Section 139(9) defective: 15 days. Section 142(1): as stated in the notice. Section 245: 30 days. Section 156 demand: 30 days to pay.
Where can I check an income tax notice online?
Log in to the income tax e-filing portal and open Pending Actions, then e-Proceedings (and the Worklist) to see notices, their response dates and the option to reply. Notices are also emailed to your registered email address with an SMS alert; you can confirm a notice is genuine using the portal's authenticate notice service with its DIN.
What is the password for an income tax notice PDF?
Usually your PAN in lower case followed by your date of birth in DDMMYYYY format, with no spaces (for example abcde1234f15081990); companies and firms use the date of incorporation. A few notices have been reported to use the PAN in capitals, so try that if the first format fails.
What is the time limit for income tax notices?
It depends on the notice: a 143(1) intimation is issued within nine months from the end of the financial year in which the return was filed; a 143(2) scrutiny notice within three months from the end of the financial year in which the return was filed; and reassessment notices under section 148 have their own longer limits linked to the amount of income involved. Each notice states its own reply deadline.
What is a section 133(6) notice?
A section 133(6) notice is a request for information, often sent when the department sees high-value transactions in your AIS, such as large deposits, property deals or share trades, that do not appear to match your return. It is not an assessment or demand. Reply on the e-filing portal under e-proceedings or compliance with the explanation and documents asked for; ignoring it can attract a penalty for each day of default under section 272A(2)(c) and may lead to scrutiny or reassessment.
What is a section 142(1) notice and how do I reply to it?
A section 142(1) notice (1961 Act numbering, carried into the 2025 Act) asks you either to file a return you have not filed, or, during an assessment, to produce accounts, documents or information the officer lists. Reply on e-Proceedings within the time in the notice: file the return if that is what is asked, or upload a short covering letter answering each point with the documents as annexures, and ask for an adjournment on the portal if you need more time. Ignoring it can lead to a best-judgment assessment and a penalty of Rs 10,000 for each failure (old section 272A(1)(d)).

We check the DIN and deadline, list what each point needs and draft the reply.
Can Section 148A show-cause be quashed before Section 148 reassessment?
Yes - the reply to the section 148A show cause notice is the chance to stop it. A well-drafted reply can lead to an order under section 148A(3) dropping the proceeding (no Section 148 formal notice). For procedurally defective 148A: writ in High Court can quash (several High Court rulings have struck down defective 148A). Specific legal grounds + specific case law critical.
What happens if I don't respond to Income Tax notice?
AO proceeds with only AO's version considered. Specific adverse presumptions. Section 148 reassessment if 148A unresponded. Section 143(3) ex-parte scrutiny order if 143(2) unresponded. Section 270A penalty 50-200%. Section 234A/B/C interest. Recovery proceedings under specific sections. Specific CIBIL impact for individuals.
