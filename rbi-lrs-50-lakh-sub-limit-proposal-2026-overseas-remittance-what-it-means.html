RBI's Proposed INR 50 Lakh LRS Sub-Limit (2026): What Is Reported, What Is Not Changing, and How to Plan Your Overseas Remittances, a proposal, not yet a rule - plan, do not panic.

Since 27 October 2026, news reports citing sources have said the RBI has proposed a yearly sub-limit of INR 50 lakh on certain remittances under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS). Business travel and pilgrimages are the examples mentioned. The sub-limit would sit inside the overall USD 250,000 per person per financial year, and education and medical remittances are reported to be kept outside it. As of 20 November 2026 there is no RBI circular, notification or start date, so the current rules still apply. LRS covers resident individuals. NRIs moving their own NRO money abroad use the separate USD 1 million facility. If you plan to remit large amounts this year for an overseas property, investments or gifts to children abroad, review the timing, the purpose codes and TCS now, and watch for the final rule.

/ What to do now

Sensible steps while the rule is pending.

  • Do not rush or split artificially: the overall limit is per person; remitting in another family member’s name must reflect a genuine gift and their own limit.
  • Use the right purpose code: education and medical payments should be coded and documented as such; reports suggest they may stay outside any sub-limit.
  • Large planned transfers: for an overseas property or investment planned this financial year, review timing and documents with your bank now.
  • TCS: 20% TCS above INR 10 lakh on gifts and investments is not a final cost; claim it against your tax or as a refund. See TCS on foreign remittances and how to claim TCS back.
  • Students abroad: see parents funding study abroad.
  • Business investments abroad: an overseas company or subsidiary may fall under ODI rather than LRS; see FEMA ODI compliance.
  • NRIs: money in NRO accounts goes abroad under the USD 1 million facility with Forms 145 and 146; see repatriation from NRO.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Has the RBI capped LRS remittances at INR 50 lakh?
Not yet. Reports since 27 October 2026 say the RBI has proposed a yearly INR 50 lakh sub-limit on certain remittances, but no circular, notification or effective date had been issued as of 20 November 2026.
Which remittances would the INR 50 lakh sub-limit cover?
Reports mention business travel and pilgrimages as examples, with education and medical remittances kept outside; the final scope will depend on the RBI's rule.
Is the USD 250,000 LRS limit changing?
Reports say the overall USD 250,000 per person per financial year limit is likely to stay; the INR 50 lakh figure is described as a sub-limit within it.
Does the LRS sub-limit affect NRIs?
LRS applies to resident individuals. NRIs repatriating their own NRO funds use the separate USD 1 million per financial year facility.
Should I send money abroad now before the rule changes?
Plan genuine needs sensibly rather than rushing: use the correct purpose codes, keep documents, and remember TCS above INR 10 lakh can be claimed back in your return.
