/ The crypto tax + regulatory stack

Four layers.

  • Section 115BBH 30% VDA tax: flat rate on gain from any Virtual Digital Asset. No loss offset (loss cannot be set off against other VDA gain or any other income). Only deduction: cost of acquisition.
  • Section 194S 1% TDS on crypto transfer: paid by exchange / crypto service provider on sale consideration above INR 10,000 per transaction. Form 26QE / 26QF reporting.
  • Offshore structuring: many Indian Web3 startups structure HQ in Dubai (VARA licensed), Singapore (VCC), Delaware C-Corp. Rationale: regulatory clarity, exchange licence availability, LP-friendly tax, international LP access.
  • FEMA ODI compliance: Indian founder's investment in offshore crypto HQ requires Form ODI Part I filing + annual Form ODI Part II (APR) + specific restrictions on crypto-adjacent businesses under FEMA.
  • GST 18% on crypto services: trading fees, exchange services, staking rewards.

/ Dubai VARA structuring

The current favourite.

Dubai VARA (Virtual Asset Regulatory Authority) licensed entities offer:

  • Regulatory clarity for crypto exchange + custody + advisory + token issuance.
  • UAE 9% corporate tax (post 2024 CT regime).
  • Favourable India-UAE DTAA (10% dividend withholding).
  • Growing crypto hub ecosystem.
  • Workaround for India's Section 115BBH friction.

Structure: Dubai VARA entity as main operating entity + Indian subsidiary for Indian operations + Indian founder's equity in Dubai entity.

/ FEMA + Section 2(22)(e) risks

The traps.

  • FEMA ODI compliance: Indian investment in overseas entity (including through founder's personal LRS or Indian company's ODI route) requires regulatory compliance. Form ODI Part I + APR annual.
  • Section 2(22)(e) deemed dividend: if offshore entity gives loan / advance to Indian related party (common round-tripping risk), deemed dividend treatment.
  • Related party transactions: Section 92 TP compliance if Indian subsidiary of foreign crypto HQ.
  • Pre-GAAR / Principal Purpose Test (PPT): if offshore structure is primarily tax-driven, treaty / FEMA benefits may be denied.
  • Press Note 3 (eased for non-controlling stakes within 10% by Press Note 2 (2026)) beneficial ownership: investment from land-bordering country requires prior approval.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Why do Indian Web3 startups structure offshore?
Section 115BBH 30% + Section 194S 1% TDS + no loss offset make Indian crypto operations friction-heavy. Offshore HQ in Dubai (VARA licensed), Singapore (VCC), Delaware (C-Corp) offers: regulatory clarity, lower / simpler tax, LP-friendly structure for VC funding, international market access. Standard structure for Series A+ crypto startups.
What's Dubai VARA licensing?
Virtual Asset Regulatory Authority (Dubai, UAE) - comprehensive crypto regulatory framework (2022 onwards). Covers exchange, custody, broker-dealer, advisory, token issuer activities. Licensed entities operate under clear regulatory regime vs India's current uncertainty. UAE 9% corporate tax + favourable DTAA. 6-12 month licensing timeline.
Does Section 115BBH apply if I hold crypto in offshore wallet?
If Indian tax resident (Section 6), yes - global crypto income taxable in India under 115BBH 30%. Non-resident status (NRI) exempts from Section 115BBH for offshore holdings. For Indian founder of offshore crypto HQ: Indian compensation + capital gain on Indian activity taxable; offshore gain subject to Indian tax if resident.
How does Section 194S TDS work for Indian exchange?
Indian crypto exchange / service provider must deduct 1% TDS on sale consideration above INR 10,000 per transaction. Form 26QE / 26QF reporting. TDS credit to seller via Form 26AS (Form 168 from tax year 2026-27). Multi-exchange activity: aggregate TDS reconciliation needed in ITR Schedule VDA.
What's CARF for Indian crypto?
Crypto Asset Reporting Framework - OECD-led global automatic exchange of information for crypto. India implementation pending (likely 2027). Impact: foreign crypto exchanges will auto-share Indian user transaction data with Indian tax authority (similar to CRS for banking). Compliance preparation: ITR Schedule VDA accuracy + transaction history preservation.
