/ Property owners, landlords and NRIs · Income tax · Updated 2026-11-25

Tax on Rental Income in India 2026 (Income From House Property): Calculation, 30% Deduction, Home Loan Interest, New vs Old Regime and Loss Set-Off, rent, minus taxes, minus 30%, minus interest.

Income from house property is worked out as annual value (broadly the rent, or expected rent) minus municipal taxes paid, minus a flat 30% standard deduction, minus interest on a home loan. For a let-out property the interest has no ceiling in either regime, but under the new regime a loss from house property cannot be set off against salary or other income. For a self-occupied home the annual value is nil and, only under the old regime, interest up to ₹2 lakh a year is deductible. These deductions were in section 24 of the 1961 Act and are in section 22 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 from tax year 2026-27.

/ Quick answers

What owners search for.

  • How is house property income calculated? Gross annual value, less municipal taxes actually paid, gives net annual value; deduct 30% of that and the interest on borrowed capital.
  • What is the 30% deduction? A flat standard deduction on net annual value for let-out (and deemed let-out) property, whatever you actually spend on repairs. It does not apply to a self-occupied home with nil annual value.
  • How much home loan interest can I claim? Self-occupied: up to ₹2 lakh a year, old regime only. Let-out: the full interest in either regime, subject to the loss rules below.
  • Can I set off a house property loss? Old regime: up to ₹2 lakh a year against other income, the rest carried forward for eight years against house property income. New regime: not against other income.
  • What about interest before construction finished? It is deducted in five equal instalments starting from the year the construction is completed, within the limits above.
  • Two homes, both self-occupied? Up to two houses can be treated as self-occupied with nil annual value; a third is treated as deemed let out.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How is income from house property calculated?
Take the gross annual value (actual or expected rent), deduct municipal taxes paid to get the net annual value, then deduct a flat 30% of the net annual value and the interest on any home loan. The result can be income or a loss.
How much tax do I pay on rental income in India?
Rental income is added to your other income and taxed at your slab rate after deductions: municipal taxes paid, a flat 30% of the net annual value, and home loan interest. For example, annual rent of Rs 3 lakh with Rs 10,000 municipal tax gives a net annual value of Rs 2.9 lakh; after the 30% deduction (Rs 87,000) Rs 2.03 lakh is taxable before any loan interest. Tenants paying rent above Rs 50,000 a month deduct TDS, and you claim it in your return.
Is home loan interest deductible in the new tax regime?
For a let-out property, yes, in full, but a resulting loss cannot be set off against salary or other income. For a self-occupied home, interest is not deductible in the new regime; up to Rs 2 lakh is allowed only in the old regime.
What is the 30% standard deduction on house property?
A flat deduction of 30% of the net annual value of a let-out or deemed let-out property, allowed regardless of actual repair costs. It does not apply to a self-occupied house with nil annual value.
How much house property loss can be set off?
Under the old regime, up to Rs 2 lakh a year against other income, with the balance carried forward for eight years against house property income. Under the new regime, a house property loss cannot be set off against other income.
Which section covers house property deductions now?
Section 24 of the Income-tax Act, 1961 for FY 2025-26 and earlier; from tax year 2026-27, section 22 of the Income-tax Act, 2025.
