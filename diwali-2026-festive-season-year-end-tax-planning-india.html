/ The 5-month planning window

November to March.

  • November 2026: Diwali 10 Nov. Form 29B MAT verification. Form 3CEB (Form 48 from tax year 2026-27) TP audit (31 October). MSME Form I (31 October). MGT-7 (29 November; fee waived until 31 January 2027). PAS-6 (29 November). AOC-4 (if not already Oct 30).
  • December 2026: Q3 advance tax (15 Dec, 75% cumulative). Form ODI APR (31 Dec). GSTR-9 FY 2025-26 (31 Dec). Belated / revised ITR (31 Dec).
  • January 2027: Q3 TDS returns (31 Jan). Specific sector returns.
  • February 2027: Union Budget 2026-27 (expected 1 Feb). Pre-Budget investment / capital gain timing decisions.
  • March 2027: Q4 advance tax (15 Mar, 100% cumulative). Section 80C/80D/80G last-minute investment. Section 54 reinvestment completion. 31 Mar FY end.

/ Capital gain reinvestment planning

Section 54 / 54F / 54EC.

  • Section 54 (residential-to-residential): LTCG on residential property reinvested in residential property - exempt. 2-year purchase OR 3-year construction window.
  • Section 54F (section 86 of the 2025 Act) (any long-term asset-to-residential): LTCG on non-residential asset reinvested in ONE residential property - exempt. Net consideration (not just gain) must be invested.
  • Section 54EC (section 85 of the 2025 Act) (bonds): LTCG invested in REC / NHAI / PFC / IRFC bonds within 6 months - INR 50 lakh limit. Simple + lower-risk.
  • Finance Act 2024 LTCG 12.5% framework interaction - optimise choice.
  • Capital Gain Account Scheme (CGAS) deposit before 31 July 2027 ITR deadline if actual investment delayed.

/ Section 80C / 80D / 80G last-mile

The deduction optimisation.

  • Section 80C INR 1.5 lakh: ELSS mutual fund, PPF, EPF, NPS, Life Insurance Premium, Tuition Fees. Deadline 31 March 2027.
  • Section 80CCD(1B) NPS additional INR 50K: for NPS Tier-I.
  • Section 80D health insurance: self (INR 25K / 50K senior) + parents (INR 25K / 50K senior).
  • Section 80G donations: specific NGOs with 80G certificate + 100%/50% deduction. Document transfer before 31 March.
  • Section 80TTA / 80TTB savings interest exemption.
  • Section 80E education loan interest - no upper limit on amount, 8 years.

/ New vs Old regime decision

AY 2027-28 planning.

  • New regime (default): nil tax up to INR 12 lakh with the 87A rebate (INR 12.75 lakh for salaried). Lower rates. No most deductions except Section 80CCD(2) employer NPS + standard deduction INR 75K.
  • Old regime: higher rates + full deduction suite (80C, 80D, 80G, HRA, home loan interest, etc.).
  • Decision varies by individual deduction profile.
  • Can switch each year for non-business income; business income has specific lock-in.
  • Use BQP's new vs old regime calculator at bharatquantumprospera.com/new-vs-old-tax-regime-calculator.html.

/ Startup / company specific year-end

Beyond individual.

  • Form 10-IC Section 115BAA opt-in (if desired).
  • Form 10-ID Section 115BAB opt-in for new manufacturing.
  • Pre-FY-end bonus + ESOP exercise + related perquisite tax planning.
  • MSE supplier payment before 45-day Section 43B(h) trigger.
  • Section 194R business perquisite TDS year-end reconciliation.
  • Form 3CEB TP + Master File + CbCR for in-scope groups.
  • GSTR-9 + GSTR-9C pre-filing audit (31 December 2026 deadline).

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's the Diwali to March 31 tax planning window?
5 months of intensive year-end planning. November-December: compliance deadlines (TP audit, MGT-7, PAS-6, MSME-1, Q3 advance tax, GSTR-9, Form ODI APR). February: pre-Budget. March: last-mile Section 80C/80D/80G + Section 54 reinvestment + Q4 advance tax. Final decisions before FY ends 31 March 2027.
Should I buy ELSS before Diwali or in March?
Both work. Diwali-based ELSS SIP spreads cost. March-based lump-sum optimises timing based on actual annual tax position. For most individuals: SIP throughout year + top-up in March to hit INR 1.5 lakh Section 80C limit.
How to time Section 54 reinvestment?
If LTCG realised in current FY: reinvestment window is 2 years forward (purchase) / 3 years forward (construction). Deposit in Capital Gain Account Scheme (CGAS) by 31 July 2027 if actual investment delayed. Must complete investment within 2/3 year window from original sale date.
New or Old regime for AY 2027-28?
Varies by deduction profile. For individuals with substantial deductions (HRA + home loan interest + Section 80C + 80D + 80G), old regime typically better. For simpler profile (minimum deductions), new regime often better due to lower rates and nil tax up to INR 12 lakh. Use BQP calculator at bharatquantumprospera.com/new-vs-old-tax-regime-calculator.html.
What's the biggest Diwali-season compliance miss BQP sees?
Deactivated DINs from earlier missed DIR-3 KYC (blocking all MCA filings). Fix urgently with INR 5,000 late fee + 24-72 hour reactivation. Also: PAS-6 half-year (29 November) + MSME Form I (31 October) often missed.
