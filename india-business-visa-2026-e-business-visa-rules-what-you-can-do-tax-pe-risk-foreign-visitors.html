/ Foreign companies · Visiting India · Updated 2026-11-24

India Business Visa (2026): e-Business Visa Rules, What You Can and Cannot Do, and the Tax Side for Foreign Visitors, meetings yes, a job in India no.

Foreign founders, executives and staff visiting India for meetings, sales, site visits, setting up a company or hiring usually travel on an e-Business visa, applied for online on the official Indian visa portal. It is generally valid for one year with multiple entries, each stay capped at 180 days; fees depend on nationality. A business visa does not allow employment in India: someone who will work for an Indian entity or be paid in India needs an employment visa. Using a tourist visa for business can lead to deportation and a re-entry ban. The tax side matters too: salary for days worked in India can be taxable in India unless a short-stay or treaty exemption applies, long or frequent visits can create a permanent establishment for the foreign company, and foreign directors of an Indian company need a DIN.

/ Quick answers

What foreign visitors ask.

  • What can I do on an India business visa? Attend meetings and trade fairs, negotiate and sign contracts, explore or set up a business, recruit staff, inspect suppliers and give technical support to an Indian business you deal with.
  • What can I not do? Take up employment with an Indian entity or draw a salary in India; that needs an employment visa.
  • How long is it valid? The e-Business visa is generally valid for one year from grant with multiple entries, and each stay can last up to 180 days.
  • How do I apply? Online on the official Indian government visa portal, with a passport valid for at least six months, a photo and usually a business card and details of the Indian company you will visit. Avoid unofficial sites that charge extra.
  • How much does it cost? The fee depends on your nationality and is shown on the official portal when you apply.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What can I do in India on a business visa?
Attend meetings and trade fairs, negotiate and sign contracts, explore or set up a business, recruit staff, inspect suppliers and give technical support to an Indian business you deal with. You cannot take up employment in India or be paid by an Indian entity; that needs an employment visa.
How long is an India e-Business visa valid?
Generally one year from grant, with multiple entries, and each stay can last up to 180 days. Registration with the Foreigners Regional Registration Office is needed if a business stay goes beyond 180 days.
How do I apply for an India business visa?
Online on the official Indian government e-Visa portal, with a passport valid for at least six months, a photo, and usually a business card and details of the Indian company you will visit. The fee depends on your nationality; avoid unofficial sites that add charges.
Can I visit India on a tourist visa for business meetings?
No. Business activity needs a business visa; using a tourist visa for business can lead to deportation and a ban on re-entry.
Will I pay Indian tax on my salary during a business trip?
Often not for short trips: Indian law exempts employees of a foreign company that has no business in India for stays of 90 days or less, and most tax treaties exempt stays under 183 days when a foreign employer pays and bears the salary. Longer stays, work for an Indian entity or a permanent establishment change that.
