TDS on Salary 2026-27: Section 392 (Old Section 192), Limit, Rates, Calculation and Form 130, spread the year's tax evenly across the months.

TDS on salary is deducted by the employer each time salary is paid, at the average rate of income tax on the employee’s estimated taxable salary for the year; there is no fixed TDS rate on salary. From tax year 2026-27 the provision is section 392 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (section 192 of the 1961 Act). Under the default new regime, no tax is payable, and so no TDS is due, on salary up to about ₹12.75 lakh (₹12 lakh of taxable income after the ₹75,000 standard deduction, using the section 87A rebate). The annual TDS certificate is now Form 130, replacing Form 16, and the employer’s quarterly return is Form 138, replacing Form 24Q.

/ Quick answers

What people search for.

  • What is the new section for TDS on salary? Section 392 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, in force from 1 April 2026, replacing section 192.
  • What is the TDS on salary rate? There is no flat rate. The employer works out the tax on the year’s estimated salary under the regime the employee chooses and deducts it evenly across the remaining months.
  • What is the TDS on salary limit? TDS applies only if estimated taxable income exceeds the level at which tax becomes payable: in the new regime, taxable income above ₹12 lakh (salary above about ₹12.75 lakh), with marginal relief just above; in the old regime, above the basic exemption after deductions.
  • Which regime does the employer use? The new regime by default, unless the employee tells the employer they want the old regime for TDS.
  • What certificate do employees get? Form 130 (the successor to Form 16), first issued by 15 June 2027 for tax year 2026-27.
  • Can other income be taken into account? Yes. Employees can declare other income and losses under house property so the employer adjusts TDS; TDS from a previous employer in the same year must also be reported to the new employer.

Section 87A (section 156 of the 2025 Act) gives a rebate of up to ₹60,000, so tax is nil up to ₹12 lakh of taxable income. Full rates and surcharge: tax rates in India 2026-27; compare regimes with the new vs old regime calculator.

/ How the monthly TDS is worked out

The employer's steps.

  • Estimate annual salary: basic, allowances, perquisites and bonus expected for the year.
  • Apply the regime: new regime with ₹75,000 standard deduction, or old regime with ₹50,000 standard deduction plus exemptions such as HRA and declared deductions.
  • Compute tax: slab tax, less rebate, plus cess; divide by the number of salary payments left in the year and deduct monthly, adjusting as actual figures change.
  • Collect proofs: employees on the old regime submit evidence of rent, investments and loans so the employer can allow them.
  • Deposit and report: the employer deposits TDS and files the quarterly return in Form 138; see TDS return filing.
  • Issue Form 130: by 15 June after the tax year; employees then file their return, see ITR filing.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the new section for TDS on salary?
Section 392 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, which applies from 1 April 2026 and replaces section 192 of the Income-tax Act, 1961.
What is the rate of TDS on salary?
There is no fixed rate. The employer deducts tax at the average rate applicable to the employee's estimated taxable salary for the year, under the new regime by default or the old regime if the employee opts for it.
What is the TDS on salary limit for 2026-27?
Under the new regime, no TDS is due if taxable income is up to Rs 12 lakh, which with the Rs 75,000 standard deduction means salary up to about Rs 12.75 lakh, because the section 87A rebate makes the tax nil. Above that, TDS applies, with marginal relief just above the threshold.
What replaces Form 16?
Form 130 is the salary TDS certificate under the Income-tax Act, 2025. For tax year 2026-27 it is due by 15 June 2027.
Which return does the employer file for salary TDS?
The quarterly TDS return for salary is Form 138, which replaces Form 24Q from tax year 2026-27.
