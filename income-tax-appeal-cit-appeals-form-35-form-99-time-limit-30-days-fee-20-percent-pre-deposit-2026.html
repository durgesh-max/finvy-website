/ Taxpayers disputing an assessment · Income tax · Updated 2026-11-25

Income Tax Appeal to CIT(A) (2026): Time Limit 30 Days, Form 35 / Form 99, Fees, 20% Pre-Deposit for Stay and Appeal Status, file within 30 days, then deal with the demand.

The first appeal against an income tax assessment or penalty order goes to the Commissioner of Income Tax (Appeals) or the Joint Commissioner (Appeals), and must be filed online within 30 days of receiving the order. Under the Income-tax Act, 1961 the appeal is filed in Form 35; under the Income-tax Act, 2025 the form is Form 99 (rule 167 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026), so use the form that applies to the year of the order. The fee is ₹250, ₹500 or ₹1,000 depending on assessed income. Filing an appeal does not stop the demand: to keep recovery on hold while the appeal is pending, taxpayers usually pay 20% of the disputed demand and apply for a stay, under CBDT instructions.

/ Quick answers

What people search for.

  • What is the time limit for an income tax appeal? 30 days from the date the order is served. A late appeal can be admitted if you show sufficient cause for the delay, with a condonation request.
  • Which form? Form 35 for orders under the 1961 Act; Form 99 for appeals against orders under sections 356 or 357 of the 2025 Act. Both are filed on the e-filing portal with a digital signature or EVC.
  • What are the CIT(A) appeal fees? ₹250, ₹500 or ₹1,000 depending on the assessed income band (sources report the band limits differently, so check the portal); ₹250 for appeals not about assessed income. Pay by challan before filing.
  • Do I need to pay a pre-deposit? There is no statutory pre-deposit to file. To stay recovery of the demand while the appeal is pending, CBDT instructions generally expect 20% of the disputed demand to be paid, with the rest stayed.
  • How do I check appeal status? In the e-filing portal under the appeals section of pending actions and e-proceedings; appeals are heard in a faceless manner through notices on the portal.
  • What if I lose at CIT(A)? The next appeal is to the Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (ITAT), generally within 60 days of the appellate order.

/ Filing a strong appeal

What to prepare.

  • Grounds of appeal: short, specific grounds against each addition or disallowance, plus a statement of facts.
  • Evidence: documents filed before the assessing officer; new evidence is admitted only in limited circumstances, so explain why it was not filed earlier.
  • Demand: request a stay from the assessing officer, paying the expected pre-deposit, so refunds are not adjusted and recovery does not start.
  • Hearings: appeals are handled faceless; reply to each portal notice by the date given and ask for a video hearing if needed; see notice reply format.
  • Scrutiny background: see scrutiny notices.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the time limit to file an income tax appeal?
30 days from the date the assessment or penalty order is served. A delayed appeal can still be admitted if the Commissioner (Appeals) is satisfied there was sufficient cause, so file a condonation request with reasons.
Which form is used for an income tax appeal in 2026?
Form 35 for orders under the Income-tax Act, 1961, and Form 99 under the Income-tax Act, 2025 (rule 167 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026) for appeals against orders under sections 356 or 357. Both are filed on the e-filing portal.
What is the fee for an appeal to CIT(A)?
Rs 250, Rs 500 or Rs 1,000 depending on assessed income, and Rs 250 for appeals not involving assessed income, paid by challan before filing. Confirm the band on the portal.
Is a 20% pre-deposit required to file an income tax appeal?
No pre-deposit is needed to file. To stay recovery of the disputed demand while the first appeal is pending, CBDT instructions generally expect 20% of the disputed demand to be paid, with the balance stayed.
Where does the next appeal go after CIT(A)?
To the Income Tax Appellate Tribunal, generally within 60 days of receiving the CIT(A) order.
