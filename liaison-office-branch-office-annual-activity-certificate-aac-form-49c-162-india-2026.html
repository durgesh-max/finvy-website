Annual Activity Certificate for a Liaison or Branch Office in India (2026): 30 September Deadline, Form 49C / Form 162 and Annual Compliance, what a foreign company's Indian office must file each year.

Every liaison office (LO), branch office (BO) and project office of a foreign company in India must file an Annual Activity Certificate (AAC) for each financial year (April-March) by 30 September. It is certified by a Chartered Accountant, filed with the office's authorised dealer (AD) bank and with the Director General of Income Tax (International Taxation), and accompanied by the audited financial statements, including a receipt and payment account. The AAC confirms that the office carried on only the activities the RBI permitted. A liaison office also files an annual statement with the income-tax department (Form 49C under the 1961 rules, replaced by Form 162 under the Income-tax Act, 2025 from FY 2026-27), and a branch office files an income-tax return as a foreign company.

/ What the AAC confirms

Why the bank and the tax department want it.

  • That the office carried on only the permitted activities (for a liaison office: representing the parent, promoting trade, technical or financial collaboration and acting as a communication channel; no commercial or trading income).
  • That expenses were met from inward remittances from the parent (liaison office) and that permissions, such as validity and approved activities, were respected.
  • That the accounts were audited and the office's bank accounts were used as permitted.
  • Validity: a liaison office approval is usually for three years (two years for some sectors) and can be extended through the AD bank, which looks at the AAC record.

/ Problems we see

And how they are fixed.

  • Years of AACs not filed: file the pending years with audited accounts, and regularise any FEMA contravention through the AD bank or compounding; see FEMA compounding.
  • The office did commercial work (invoicing Indian customers): a liaison office cannot earn income, and this can create a permanent establishment and tax exposure for the parent; see permanent establishment.
  • Validity lapsed without extension: apply through the AD bank, or plan closure or conversion to a subsidiary; see liaison vs branch vs subsidiary.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the due date for the Annual Activity Certificate of a liaison office?
30 September each year, for the financial year ended 31 March, filed with the AD Category-I bank and the Director General of Income Tax (International Taxation).
Who certifies the Annual Activity Certificate?
A Chartered Accountant, usually the auditor of the office, together with the audited financial statements including a receipt and payment account.
Does a branch office also file an AAC?
Yes. Branch, liaison and project offices all file an AAC. A branch office also files an income-tax return as a foreign company.
What is Form 49C?
The annual statement a liaison office files with the income-tax department about its activities. Under the Income-tax Act, 2025 it is replaced by Form 162 from FY 2026-27.
What happens if AACs were not filed?
Pending years should be filed with audited accounts; the delay is a FEMA contravention that may need regularisation or compounding, and it can hold up extension of the office's validity or its closure.
