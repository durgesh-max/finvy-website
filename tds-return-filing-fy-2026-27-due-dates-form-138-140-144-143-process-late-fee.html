TDS Return Filing FY 2026-27: Due Dates, New Forms 138, 140, 144 and 143, Step-by-Step Process and Late Fee, new form numbers, same quarterly rhythm.

From tax year 2026-27, TDS returns are filed under the Income-tax Act, 2025 in new forms: Form 138 for salary (was 24Q), Form 140 for other payments to residents (was 26Q), Form 144 for payments to non-residents (was 27Q) and Form 143 for TCS (was 27EQ). Quarterly returns are due on 31 July, 31 October, 31 January and 31 May; the October-December 2026 return is due on 31 January 2027. Tax deducted must be paid by the 7th of the next month (March deductions by 30 April). A late return attracts a fee of ₹200 a day, so file on time even if the details need correcting later.

/ Quick answers

What deductors search for.

  • What is the TDS return due date for FY 2026-27? 31 July 2026 (April-June), 31 October 2026 (July-September), 31 January 2027 (October-December) and 31 May 2027 (January-March). TCS returns for January-March are reported as due on 15 May; check the notified date.
  • Which form do I use? Form 138 for salary, Form 140 for other resident payments such as contractors, professionals, rent and interest, Form 144 for non-residents and Form 143 for TCS.
  • What about the January-March 2026 quarter? It was the last filed in the old 24Q, 26Q and 27Q under the 1961 Act.
  • Is there a fee to file? Filing online yourself has no government fee; the late fee is ₹200 for every day of delay.
  • How do I file online? Prepare the return in the return preparation utility, validate it with the file validation utility, and upload it on the income tax e-filing portal with your TAN login (or through a facilitation centre).
  • What certificates follow? Form 130 (was Form 16) for salary and Form 131 (was Form 16A) for other deductions, downloaded after the return is processed.

/ Step by step

Each quarter.

  • 1. Deduct and pay: deposit TDS by the 7th of the next month (30 April for March), and allocate each challan to the right form and section code so the return can match it.
  • 2. Collect deductee details: PAN, amounts, dates and section codes; for non-residents, treaty documents such as the TRC and Form 41.
  • 3. Prepare and validate: use the return preparation utility and the file validation utility to create the upload file.
  • 4. Upload: on the e-filing portal with the TAN login, verified electronically, before the due date.
  • 5. Issue certificates: Form 130 for salary after the year ends, Form 131 quarterly for other payments.
  • 6. Correct if needed: file a correction statement for wrong PANs, amounts or challan mismatches flagged after processing.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What are the TDS return due dates for FY 2026-27?
31 July 2026 for April-June, 31 October 2026 for July-September, 31 January 2027 for October-December and 31 May 2027 for January-March. TCS returns for January-March are reported as due on 15 May.
Which forms replaced 24Q, 26Q and 27Q?
From tax year 2026-27: Form 138 replaced 24Q (salary), Form 140 replaced 26Q (other resident payments), Form 144 replaced 27Q (non-residents), and Form 143 replaced 27EQ (TCS).
What is the late fee for filing a TDS return late?
Rs 200 for every day of delay until the return is filed. Interest on late payment of TDS and penalties for not filing are separate.
How do I file a TDS return online step by step?
Pay TDS by the 7th of the next month and allocate challans, collect deductee PANs and section codes, prepare the return in the return preparation utility, validate it with the file validation utility, and upload it on the e-filing portal with your TAN login before the due date.
When was the last TDS return filed in the old forms?
The January-March 2026 quarter, filed by 31 May 2026, was the last in 24Q, 26Q and 27Q under the 1961 Act.
