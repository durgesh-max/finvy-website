Old vs New Income Tax Form Numbers, 15CA is now 145.

From 1 April 2026, the Income-tax Rules, 2026 renumbered almost every income tax form. Form 15CA is now Form 145, Form 15CB is Form 146, Form 10F is Form 41, Form 13 is Form 128, Form 27Q is Form 144 and Form 26AS is Form 168. Search the table below for any form.

/ Which numbers apply when

The rule of thumb.

  • The Income-tax Act, 2025 and the Income-tax Rules, 2026 apply from 1 April 2026. Transactions and filings for tax year 2026-27 onwards use the new numbers.
  • Returns, audits and transfer pricing reports for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27), due in 2026, are still under the 1961 Act and use the old forms, such as Form 3CEB and Form 3CD.
  • Foreign remittances made from 1 April 2026 are reported in Form 145, with a CA certificate in Form 146 where required.
  • Due dates and the basic purpose of most forms did not change; the numbers and section references did.
  • Old forms remain relevant for corrections and proceedings for earlier years.

/ For NRIs and foreign companies

The forms you are most likely to meet.

  • Sending or receiving money across borders: Form 145 (was 15CA) and Form 146 (was 15CB).
  • Claiming treaty rates: Form 41 (was 10F), plus a tax residency certificate.
  • Selling property in India as an NRI: Form 128 (was 13) for a lower TDS certificate; the buyer deducts TDS using PAN in Form 141 from 1 October 2026.
  • Paying a non-resident: TDS return in Form 144 (was 27Q).
  • Foreign company or foreign individual needing a PAN: Form 96 or Form 95 (was 49AA).
  • Indian subsidiary of a foreign group: transfer pricing report in Form 48 (was 3CEB) from tax year 2026-27.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the new number for Form 15CA and 15CB?
From 1 April 2026, Form 15CA is Form 145 and Form 15CB is Form 146 under the Income-tax Rules, 2026.
What is the new number for Form 10F?
Form 41. Non-residents use it to give the information needed to claim tax treaty (DTAA) benefits.
What replaced Form 27Q?
Form 144, the quarterly TDS return for payments to non-residents.
What is Form 128?
The application for a lower or nil TDS certificate, which replaced Form 13. NRIs selling property commonly use it.
Is Form 3CEB still used?
Yes, for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27). From tax year 2026-27 the transfer pricing report is Form 48.
What is the new number for Form 26AS?
Form 168, the annual tax statement.
