/ Tax rates · India · Updated 2026-11-24

Tax Rates in India 2026-27: Income Tax Slabs, Company Tax, Capital Gains, GST and NRI Rates, every main percentage on one page.

For tax year 2026-27, individuals in India pay income tax at 0% to 30%: under the default new regime, nil up to INR 4 lakh, then 5%, 10%, 15%, 20% and 25% in INR 4 lakh steps, and 30% above INR 24 lakh, plus 4% cess. Residents with taxable income up to INR 12 lakh pay nothing after the rebate (INR 12.75 lakh for salaried people after the INR 75,000 standard deduction). Domestic companies pay about 25.17% under the concessional regime, foreign companies' Indian branches 35% plus surcharge and cess, and GST is mostly 5% or 18%, with 40% on luxury and sin goods. Long-term gains on listed shares are taxed at 12.5% above INR 1.25 lakh a year and short-term at 20%. Budget 2026 did not change the individual slabs.

  • Rebate: residents with taxable income up to INR 12 lakh (new regime) or INR 5 lakh (old regime) pay no tax; NRIs do not get the rebate.
  • Standard deduction for salary and pension: INR 75,000 (new) or INR 50,000 (old).

  • Old regime keeps deductions such as investments, health insurance and home loan interest. Compare with the new vs old regime calculator.

/ NRIs and foreign investors

Rates that apply when you live abroad.

  • Same slabs as residents for Indian income, but no rebate, so tax starts above INR 4 lakh (new regime) or INR 2.5 lakh (old).
  • Tax deducted on NRO interest and rent paid to an NRI: 30% plus cess (about 31.2%), which a return can bring down to your actual tax.
  • Dividends to non-residents: 20% under Indian law, often lower under a tax treaty with a residency certificate.
  • NRE and FCNR interest is tax-free for NRIs.
  • Property sale by an NRI: the buyer deducts tax on the gain at 12.5% (long term) plus surcharge and cess; see NRI property sale.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the income tax percentage in India?
0% to 30% for individuals, plus 4% cess. Under the default new regime for tax year 2026-27: nil up to INR 4 lakh, 5% up to 8 lakh, 10% up to 12 lakh, 15% up to 16 lakh, 20% up to 20 lakh, 25% up to 24 lakh and 30% above. Residents with taxable income up to INR 12 lakh pay nothing after the rebate.
Did Budget 2026 change the income tax slabs?
No. Budget 2026 kept the individual slabs for tax year 2026-27 the same as the year before under both regimes.
What is the corporate tax rate in India?
About 25.17% for domestic companies under the concessional regime (22% plus surcharge and cess), 25% or 30% under the regular regime, and 35% plus surcharge and cess for a foreign company's branch.
What are the GST rates in India?
Since 22 September 2025, most goods and services are taxed at 5% or 18%, with 40% on luxury and sin goods and some items exempt or at 0%.
Do NRIs pay the same tax rates as residents?
The slabs are the same, but NRIs do not get the rebate up to INR 12 lakh, banks and tenants deduct about 31.2% on NRO interest and rent, and NRE and FCNR interest is tax-free.
Is there a separate tax slab for senior citizens under the new regime?
No. The new regime uses the same slabs for every age: nil up to INR 4 lakh and the INR 12 lakh rebate for residents. Only the old regime gives resident senior citizens a higher tax-free limit: INR 3 lakh from age 60 and INR 5 lakh from age 80, plus deductions such as up to INR 50,000 on deposit interest. Non-resident seniors get neither the higher limits nor the rebate.
Do senior citizens aged 75 or more need to file a tax return?
Not if they are resident, aged 75 or more, and their only income is pension and interest from the same specified bank, which deducts the tax after they submit a declaration. Anyone with other income, or an NRI, still files.
