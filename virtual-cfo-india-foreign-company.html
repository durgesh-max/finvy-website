/ Core vCFO scope

What a fractional CFO delivers.

  • Monthly MIS (Management Information Statement): P&L + Balance Sheet + Cash Flow + segment-wise analysis + KPI dashboard. Delivered by 15th of each month for prior month.
  • Financial model / 13-week cash flow: rolling 13-week cash forecast + 3-year 3-statement model updated quarterly.
  • Budget vs actual: monthly variance analysis with narrative explanation of material deviations.
  • Board / investor reporting pack: quarterly board deck with 5-year revenue plan, cohort analysis, CAC/LTV, burn rate, runway.
  • Fundraising preparation: data room, pitch deck finance pages, Q&A support, financial due diligence response.
  • Audit coordination: year-end audit management with external auditors; audit-ready financials.
  • Treasury: FX hedging advisory, idle cash deployment, banking relationship management.
  • Tax provision: deferred tax calculation, effective tax rate analysis, uncertain tax position review (ASC 740 for US GAAP; Ind AS 12).
  • Business partnering: pricing advisory, M&A support, strategic initiative modelling.

/ When vCFO is the right fit

The decision.

vCFO fits:

  • Revenue INR 5 crore - INR 500 crore (or USD 500K - 60M).
  • Growing > 30% YoY where finance infrastructure can't keep up.
  • Post-Series A / Series B startup needing institutional-grade reporting.
  • PE / VC portfolio company between formal CFO hires.
  • Pre-IPO company building audit-ready finance function.
  • Foreign-parent Indian subsidiary needing consolidation support to parent CFO.

Full-time CFO is right when:

  • Revenue > INR 500 crore or complex multi-entity structure.
  • Daily operational finance decisions needed.
  • Significant M&A pipeline / capital markets activity.

/ Remote delivery

How 20-40 hours/month plays out.

  • Weekly 1-hour review call with CEO/founders (Zoom).
  • Monthly 2-hour deep dive on P&L, cash flow, KPIs.
  • Quarterly board pack preparation + presentation (if board exists).
  • Ongoing async support via Slack / email on specific decisions.
  • Access to accounting system (QuickBooks / Zoho Books / Tally / NetSuite) read-only.
  • Direct liaison with India accounting team / Indian bookkeeper for close process.

Deliverables shared in client's preferred format (Google Workspace / Microsoft 365 / Dropbox).

/ Industry-specific vCFO specialisations

What matters per sector.

  • SaaS: ARR / MRR / NRR / GRR / CAC / LTV / Rule of 40 / magic number / burn multiple.
  • D2C / E-commerce: AOV / CAC payback / contribution margin / inventory turns / return rate.
  • Marketplace / platform: GMV / take rate / cohort unit economics / seller / buyer LTV.
  • Manufacturing: gross margin by SKU / plant utilisation / inventory / working capital days.
  • Fintech / lending: NIM / loan book quality / provisioning / regulatory capital.
  • Services / consultancy: utilisation / realisation / bill rate / project profitability.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is a Virtual CFO?
Fractional CFO service - CFO-level finance leadership delivered on a part-time / monthly retainer basis instead of a full-time hire. Typical engagement 20-40 hours/month covering monthly MIS, financial modelling, cash-flow management, FP&A, fundraising prep, board reporting, audit coordination, treasury.
Can a Virtual CFO work remotely for a foreign company?
Yes - this is the standard delivery model. Weekly video calls, async communication via email/Slack, read-only access to the client's accounting system, shared workspace for deliverables. BQP delivers vCFO from India for US / UK / EU / Singapore / UAE SMBs and startups.
What's the typical commitment?
6-24 months engagement typical. Monthly retainer scope-based: fewer hours for stable businesses needing reporting only, more hours for growing businesses needing modelling + fundraising + strategic finance work. Can scale up to full-time CFO transition when the business reaches that stage.
How does vCFO differ from an outsourced accountant?
Accountant handles transaction recording, bookkeeping, tax filing - operational. vCFO handles strategic finance - business partnering, decision support, investor communication, financial strategy, treasury, M&A support. vCFO sits above accountant in the finance hierarchy.
Can BQP provide both vCFO and accounting services together?
Yes - integrated engagement is cost-efficient and gives one point of accountability. BQP delivers the operational accounting layer (bookkeeping, tax, GST, ROC compliance) PLUS the vCFO strategic layer. Common engagement for foreign-parent Indian subsidiaries and Indian startups.
