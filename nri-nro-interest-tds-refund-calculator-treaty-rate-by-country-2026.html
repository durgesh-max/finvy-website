NRO Interest TDS Refund Calculator for NRIs (2026): Bank TDS vs Treaty Rate vs Slab Tax, by Country, see how much of your NRO TDS you can claim back.

Indian banks deduct TDS of 30% plus surcharge and cess (31.2% for most NRIs) on NRO interest. Your actual tax is often far lower: either the treaty rate for your country (12.5% for the UAE, 15% for the US, UK, Canada, Australia and Singapore, 10% for Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Oman) if you have a tax residency certificate and Form 10F (Form 41 from 1 April 2026), or slab tax if your Indian income is small. On the new regime for FY 2025-26, the first INR 4 lakh is tax-free. The difference is a refund, but only if you file. For FY 2025-26 the last date is 31 December 2026, through a belated or revised return; an updated return after that cannot claim refunds. Enter your figures to estimate the gap.

    / How it works

    The rules behind the numbers.

    • Bank TDS: 30% plus surcharge and cess on NRO interest paid to non-residents, unless you give the bank a TRC and Form 10F for the treaty rate.
    • Treaty rate: applies to the gross interest and is final; no surcharge or cess is added.
    • Slab tax: FY 2025-26 new-regime slabs (nil to INR 4 lakh, then 5% to 30%) plus 4% cess; non-residents do not get the section 87A rebate.
    • Lower of the two: you pay the lower of treaty tax and domestic tax; file a return to claim the excess.
    • NRE and FCNR interest: exempt for non-residents; do not include it.

    FAQ

    Common questions, answered.

    How much TDS do banks deduct on NRO interest?
    30% plus surcharge and cess, which is 31.2% for most NRIs, unless you provide a tax residency certificate and Form 10F for the treaty rate.
    Can NRIs get a refund of TDS on NRO interest?
    Yes, if the actual tax (the lower of treaty tax and slab tax) is less than the TDS; claim it by filing an Indian return. For FY 2025-26, a belated or revised return is possible until 31 December 2026.
    What is the treaty rate on NRO interest for the UAE, US and UK?
    12.5% for UAE residents and 15% for US and UK residents (non-bank recipients), with a TRC and Form 10F.
    Do NRIs get the INR 12 lakh tax-free limit?
    No. The section 87A rebate that makes income up to INR 12 lakh tax-free under the new regime is only for residents; NRIs still get the nil band up to INR 4 lakh.
    Is NRE interest included?
    No. NRE and FCNR interest is exempt for non-residents and should not be entered.
