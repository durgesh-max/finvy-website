India-UK CETA Implementation 2026, 95%+ trade lines now at preferential rates.

India-UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) was concluded in 2024-2025 and became operational in 2026. Preferential tariffs on 95%+ of trade lines; rules of origin framework; services + investment chapters. Significant impact on India-UK trade and investment flows. Here's what's actually live and what Indian + UK companies should do now.

/ What's operational now

The core gains.

  • Goods tariffs: preferential rates on 95%+ of tariff lines. Specific sectoral gains:
  •   — India exports: textiles, leather, gems + jewellery, pharma, auto components, agri (basmati rice, specific spices).
  •   — UK exports: scotch whisky (tariff phase-down from 150% to ~75% over 10 years), premium automobiles, machinery, specific food + beverage.
  • Services: specific sector-wise commitments on movement of professionals (Mode 4), financial services, legal services (limited), telecom.
  • Investment: bilateral investment protections framework; sector-specific commitments.
  • Rules of Origin (ROO): Certificate of Origin required at import to claim CETA preferential rate; product-specific rules (regional value content + tariff shift).
  • Dispute resolution: specific mechanism for ISDS limited; state-to-state dispute settlement.

/ For Indian exporters to UK

What to update.

  • Review tariff schedule for specific HSN - preferential rate vs MFN rate.
  • Obtain Certificate of Origin from DGFT-designated agency.
  • Confirm ROO satisfaction: regional value content / tariff shift / wholly obtained criteria.
  • Pricing re-strategy: preferential rate may give price advantage over non-CETA competitors.
  • Supply chain restructuring: ensure Indian value-add qualifies under ROO.
  • Combined with India-UAE CEPA + Australia ECTA: Indian exporters now have strong FTA coverage.

/ For UK companies entering India

The investment angle.

  • CETA investment chapter provides protection framework for UK investors in India.
  • Sector-specific commitments on financial services, telecom, legal (limited scope), professional services.
  • Services Mode 4: Indian professionals' easier access to UK; UK professionals' Indian access.
  • Combined with India's 2024 Space FDI reform + Telecom FDI 100% automatic + sector liberalisations: UK companies have strong India market access.
  • DTAA India-UK already in place for tax relief; CETA builds trade / investment layer.

/ Compliance actions

What to do now.

  1. Indian exporters: register with DGFT; obtain Certificate of Origin via designated agency; confirm ROO; apply preferential rate at UK import.
  2. UK importers: receive Certificate of Origin from Indian supplier; claim preferential rate at UK customs; HMRC compliance.
  3. UK exporters: review Indian tariff schedule; obtain UK Certificate of Origin; apply preferential rate at Indian import.
  4. Indian importers: receive UK Certificate of Origin; claim preferential rate at Indian customs via Bill of Entry CETA preferential tariff code; verify ROO satisfaction.
  5. Services providers: review Mode 4 commitments for professional category; verify qualification recognition framework.
  6. Investors: review CETA investment chapter protections before signing JV / M&A documentation.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is India-UK CETA fully operational in 2026?
Preferential tariff framework became operational in 2026 for 95%+ of trade lines. Full phase-in continues over 5-10 years for specific tariff reductions (e.g., scotch whisky phase-down from 150% to ~75% over 10 years). Services + investment chapters also operational with sector-specific commitments.
How do I claim CETA preferential rate at import?
Obtain Certificate of Origin from exporting country's designated authority (DGFT-designated agency in India; UK Chamber of Commerce / designated body in UK). Submit with import documentation; declare preferential tariff code on Bill of Entry (India) / Customs declaration (UK). Verify Rules of Origin (ROO) satisfaction - product-specific rules apply (regional value content, tariff shift, or wholly obtained).
What's the biggest sectoral winner from India-UK CETA?
On India export side: textiles + leather + gems/jewellery + pharma + auto components + agri. On UK export side: scotch whisky (phased reduction from 150% tariff), premium automobiles, machinery, specific F&B. Services Mode 4 helps Indian IT / professional services access to UK + UK professional services access to India.
Does CETA affect India-UK DTAA?
No - DTAA (Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement) and CETA (Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement) are separate instruments. DTAA covers direct tax treaty relief (dividend, royalty, FTS, capital gain). CETA covers trade (tariffs) + services + investment protection. Both continue to apply.
