DIN Deactivated for DIR-3 KYC? How to Reactivate It in 2026, and unblock your company's MCA filings.

A DIN marked 'Deactivated due to non-filing of DIR-3 KYC' cannot sign any MCA form, so AOC-4, MGT-7 and other filings stop. Many DINs were deactivated for missing the annual KYC under the old rules. Reactivation means filing the pending KYC with the INR 5,000 fee. Under the 2026 rules, KYC is then due only once every three years, plus within 30 days of any change in contact details.

/ What is blocked

While the DIN is deactivated.

  • The director cannot sign MCA forms: AOC-4, MGT-7/7A, DIR-12, PAS-3, PAS-6, MSME Form I, DPT-3, CHG-1 and others.
  • The director cannot be appointed to another company.
  • The company's own deadlines keep running, so late fees on AOC-4 and MGT-7 build up at INR 100 per day each.

/ How to reactivate

Steps.

  1. Log in to the MCA portal and check the DIN status and the KYC period shown as pending.
  2. Make sure the registered mobile number and email are working, since filing uses OTPs; if they changed, update them as part of the KYC.
  3. File the pending DIR-3 KYC and pay the INR 5,000 fee.
  4. Once the DIN shows as active, file the company's pending forms, starting with AOC-4 and MGT-7.
  5. Note your next KYC due year under the three-year cycle and set a reminder before 30 June of that year.

/ Under the 2026 rules

What changes after reactivation.

  • KYC is due once every three consecutive financial years, by 30 June of the due year (G.S.R. 943(E), effective 31 March 2026).
  • Mobile, email or address changes must be reported within 30 days.
  • Full explanation: DIR-3 KYC now once every three years.

Sources: InstaFinancials; TaxAJ; Ebizfiling.

Last updated: 29 October 2026. Sources differ on how the three-year cycle is counted for each DIN; confirm your next due year on the MCA portal.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How do I reactivate a deactivated DIN?
File the pending DIR-3 KYC on the MCA portal and pay the INR 5,000 fee. The DIN becomes active once the filing is processed.
Can a director with a deactivated DIN sign AOC-4 or MGT-7?
No. The DIN must be active before the director can sign any MCA form.
Do I have to file DIR-3 KYC every year after reactivation?
No. From 31 March 2026 KYC is due once every three financial years, by 30 June, plus within 30 days of any change in contact details.
