/ How it is calculated

The rules behind the numbers.

  • Formula: monthly wages × 15 ÷ 26 × years of service.
  • 50% wage rule: from 21 November 2025, basic pay plus DA must be at least 50% of total remuneration. If allowances exceed 50%, the excess is added back to wages for gratuity, PF and other statutory benefits.
  • Eligibility: permanent employees after 5 years of continuous service (not needed on death or disablement); fixed-term employees after 1 year, paid pro rata.
  • Rounding: for permanent employees, a part-year of more than six months counts as a full year.
  • Tax: gratuity of government employees is fully exempt; for others, exemption is limited to INR 20 lakh over the employee's lifetime.
  • The calculator gives an estimate; employer policies can be more generous.

/ For employers

What the codes change.

  • Salary structures with low basic pay now cost more in gratuity and PF; review CTC structures and provisions.
  • Fixed-term contracts of one year or more now carry a gratuity liability.
  • Foreign companies with Indian subsidiaries or employer-of-record arrangements should re-run actuarial provisions under Ind AS 19.

Sources: Aditya Birla Capital; Outlook Money.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How is gratuity calculated under the new labour codes?
Monthly wages x 15/26 x years of service, where wages must be at least 50% of total remuneration. Fixed-term employees qualify after one year, permanent employees after five.
What is the 50% wage rule for gratuity?
Basic pay plus DA must be at least half of total pay. If allowances are more than 50%, the excess is treated as wages for gratuity and PF, which raises the gratuity amount.
Do fixed-term employees get gratuity after one year?
Yes. Under the Code on Social Security, a fixed-term employee becomes eligible after one year of service, paid pro rata.
How much gratuity is tax-free?
All of it for government employees. For others, up to INR 20 lakh over the employee's lifetime.
