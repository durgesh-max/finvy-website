/ Winners of lotteries, contests and game shows · Income tax · Updated 2026-11-25

Tax on Lottery Winnings in India 2026: Flat 30% (31.2% With Cess), TDS Above Rs 10,000, Game Show Prizes and Prizes in Kind, a flat 30%, whatever your slab.

Lottery, game show, crossword, card game and similar winnings in India are taxed at a flat 30%, plus 4% cess (31.2%) and surcharge where your income is high, regardless of your tax slab or regime. The payer deducts 30% TDS when a single winning exceeds ₹10,000. No expenses can be deducted, and the basic exemption limit and the section 87A rebate cannot be used to reduce this tax.

/ Key rules

What applies to winnings.

PointRule
Tax rate30% flat (section 115BB of the 1961 Act), plus surcharge if applicable and 4% cess
TDS30% under section 194B when a single winning exceeds ₹10,000 (horse races under 194BB)
DeductionsNone: no expenses, no Chapter VI-A deductions, no basic exemption limit or 87A rebate against it
Prizes in kind (car, flat, gold)Taxed on market value; the winner must pay the tax before the prize is released
Online game winningsTaxed separately on net winnings (section 115BBJ, TDS under 194BA)
RegimeSame 30% in the old and new regimes
ReturnReport under income from other sources at the special rate; claim the TDS shown in your AIS and Form 26AS

From tax year 2026-27 these provisions continue in the Income-tax Act, 2025 under new section numbers.

/ Practical points

Before you collect the prize.

  • Prize in kind: agree the market value and pay the 30% tax (or give the payer the TDS amount) before taking delivery; keep the payer’s certificate.
  • Advance tax: pay any balance (cess, surcharge) in the next advance tax instalment; see advance tax dates.
  • No set-off: lottery ticket costs or losses cannot be set off against winnings.
  • Gifts are different: money received from relatives is exempt; gifts from others above ₹50,000 are taxed at slab rates; see gift tax rules.
  • Crypto and other income: virtual digital assets have their own 30% regime; see crypto tax in India.
  • NRIs: TDS on winnings paid to NRIs is also deducted at 30% plus applicable surcharge and cess; check your residence country’s rules on foreign tax credit.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How much tax is charged on lottery winnings in India?
A flat 30% plus 4% cess, which is 31.2%, plus surcharge if your total income is high. The rate is the same whatever your tax slab or regime.
Is TDS deducted on lottery winnings?
Yes. The payer deducts 30% TDS under section 194B when a single winning exceeds Rs 10,000. Any balance, such as cess or surcharge, is paid with advance tax or your return.
Can I use the basic exemption limit against lottery winnings?
No. Lottery and game show winnings are taxed at 30% from the first rupee; the basic exemption limit, deductions and the section 87A rebate cannot reduce this tax.
How is a prize in kind like a car taxed?
On its market value at 30% plus cess. The winner must pay the tax (or give the payer the TDS amount) before the prize is released.
Are online game winnings taxed like lottery winnings?
They are taxed at 30% too, but under a separate provision on net winnings (section 115BBJ, with TDS under 194BA).
