EPFO VISHWAS 2026: Settle Old Provident Fund Damages at Reduced Rates - Last Date 28 December 2026, a one-time window for employers with late PF payments before 14 June 2024.

VISHWAS 2026 is a one-time EPFO scheme that lets employers settle damages for provident fund contributions paid late before 14 June 2024 at much lower rates. Applications close on 28 December 2026, and EPFO has said the date will not be extended. Regular damages can run up to 37% a year. Under the scheme they are 0.25% a month for delays of up to two months, 0.50% a month for two to four months and 1% a month beyond four months. It covers cases pending in a court or tribunal, damages orders not yet recovered, notices without a final order, and late payments on EPFO’s record where no notice has been issued. You apply on the EPFO Employer Portal after paying the interest due on the late contributions, pay the computed amount within 15 days and receive a settlement certificate. Pending proceedings are then closed. The separate enrolment campaign for left-out employees closed on 31 October 2026.

/ What to do before 28 December

Steps for employers.

  • List the delays: download the challan history and identify months paid after the due date where payment was made before 14 June 2024.
  • Check for notices: gather any damages notices, orders, recovery letters and case papers.
  • Clear interest: pay the outstanding interest on the delayed contributions; the scheme module opens only after that.
  • Apply online: pick the right category on the Employer Portal and upload the documents; do not leave it to the last week.
  • Pay and keep the certificate: pay within the 15-day window and file the settlement certificate with your statutory records.
  • Fix the cause: review payroll and due dates under the new scheme; see what the EPF Scheme 2026 changed.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the last date for EPFO VISHWAS 2026?
28 December 2026 (some EPFO communications mention 29 December). EPFO has said the deadline will not be extended.
What are the reduced damages under VISHWAS 2026?
0.25% a month for delays of up to two months, 0.50% a month for delays of two to four months and 1% a month for longer delays, against regular damages of up to 37% a year.
Which PF defaults are covered by VISHWAS 2026?
Delayed provident fund payments made before 14 June 2024, whether a case is pending in a court or tribunal, an order is unrecovered, a notice has been issued, or the delay is only on EPFO's record.
How does an employer apply for VISHWAS 2026?
On the EPFO Employer Portal, after paying the outstanding interest on the late contributions: choose the category, upload documents, authenticate, pay the computed amount within 15 days and download the settlement certificate.
Is the Employees' Enrolment Campaign 2026 still open?
No. The campaign for enrolling left-out employees ran from 1 July to 31 October 2026 and has closed.
