/ Data protection · DPDP Act and Rules · Updated 2026-11-21

DPDP Rules 2025: Notified, With Main Duties From May 2027 - Timeline and What Indian and Foreign Companies Should Do Now, India’s data protection rules, where they stand in November 2026.

The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 were notified on 13 November 2025 under the DPDP Act, 2023. They apply in phases. Provisions on the Data Protection Board applied from notification. Consent manager registration opens about a year later, around 13 November 2026. The main duties on businesses apply around 13 May 2027: notices, consent, security safeguards, breach reporting, children’s data and the rights of individuals. The Act reaches foreign companies that process personal data in connection with offering goods or services to people in India. Penalties run up to INR 250 crore. A proposal to bring the main date forward by six months was floated in January 2026 but had not been notified as of September 2026. This page was corrected on 21 November 2026; an earlier version said the final rules were still awaited.

/ What to do before May 2027

A practical order of work.

  • Map the data: what personal data of people in India you collect, why, where it sits and who processes it for you.
  • Notices and consent: rewrite notices in clear language and build a way to record and withdraw consent.
  • Security and breach response: put reasonable safeguards in place and a process to notify the Board and affected people of a breach.
  • Vendors: update contracts with processors so they follow your instructions and safeguards.
  • Children’s data: check whether you process data of under-18s; verifiable parental consent is required.
  • Foreign companies: if you offer goods or services to people in India, the Act applies even without an Indian entity; see India services for foreign companies.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Have the DPDP Rules been notified?
Yes. The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 were notified on 13 November 2025 under the DPDP Act, 2023.
When do the DPDP duties apply to businesses?
The main duties, including notice, consent, security safeguards and breach reporting, apply about 18 months after notification, around 13 May 2027. Consent manager registration starts around 13 November 2026.
Does the DPDP Act apply to foreign companies?
Yes, where a company outside India processes digital personal data in connection with offering goods or services to people in India.
What is the penalty under the DPDP Act?
Penalties depend on the breach and run up to INR 250 crore.
Has the May 2027 date been brought forward?
A proposal to shorten the 18-month period to 12 months was floated in January 2026, but no notification had been reported as of September 2026.
