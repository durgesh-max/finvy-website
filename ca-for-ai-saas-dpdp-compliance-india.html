/ The integrated compliance stack

Three parallel rails.

  • DPDP Act 2023: AI training on Indian user data = Data Fiduciary role; consent + notice + purpose limitation + Significant Data Fiduciary tier if volume/sensitivity thresholds.
  • MeitY advisories: deepfake labelling, large model registration, content moderation for AI-generated output.
  • OIDAR GST (Section 2(17) IGST Act): 18% IGST on B2C Indian consumer AI product subscriptions - from first rupee.
  • Section 115BAA: 22% corporate tax for Indian AI SaaS (services, not manufacturing - so 115BAB not applicable).
  • ESOP 409A + Rule 11UA: high-valuation AI startup ESOP compliance for both US entity + Indian subsidiary.
  • DPIIT Startup India + Section 80-IAC: 3-year 100% tax holiday if eligible.

How does OIDAR GST interact with Section 115BAA?
No direct interaction. OIDAR GST is indirect tax on supply to Indian B2C customer; Section 115BAA is corporate income tax rate (22% + surcharge + cess). Separate layers. Both apply simultaneously if conditions met. OIDAR 18% is passed to customer; 115BAA 22% is on net profit.
