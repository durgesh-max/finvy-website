/ Why 15 December matters

The Section 234C math.

  • Q3 cumulative requirement: 75% of estimated full-year tax by 15 December.
  • Shortfall: Section 234C 1% per month simple interest for 3 months.
  • Example: INR 20 lakh shortfall → INR 60,000 interest cost (3 months × 1%).
  • Larger shortfalls (INR 1 crore+): INR 3 lakh+ interest - materially significant.
  • Also: Section 234B 1% per month from April 1 to date of regular assessment if full-year less than 90%.
  • Combined 234B + 234C: can be 3-5% effective annual interest on shortfall.

/ Last-minute Q3 computation

Fast workflow.

  1. Estimate FY 2026-27 total income: salary + business + capital gain + other sources + specific adjustments.
  2. Compute estimated tax: at applicable slab (new regime default) + surcharge + cess + specific-section tax (115BBH VDA 30%, 112A LTCG 12.5%, 111A STCG 20%, 115BAA 22% for companies).
  3. Reduce TDS / TCS already deducted (Form 26AS + AIS verification).
  4. Net advance tax liability = estimated tax - TDS/TCS - any Section 90/91 FTC.
  5. Q3 cumulative requirement = Net advance tax × 75%.
  6. Q3 payment due = cumulative requirement - advance tax paid in Q1 (15%) + Q2 (45%).
  7. Pay via Challan ITNS 280 Code 100 (Advance Tax) by 15 December.
  8. Keep Challan Identification Number (CIN) for Form 26AS reconciliation.

/ Common last-minute inclusions

What people miss.

  • Year-end bonus + Diwali bonus (new perquisite + Section 192 TDS impact).
  • Capital gain realised in October / November (ESOP exercise + sale, property sale, share sale).
  • Specific Section 115BBH VDA tax at 30% if crypto transactions.
  • Rental income with pending TDS from tenant.
  • Specific Section 56(2)(x) gift received that's taxable.
  • Specific business income spike.
  • Foreign-source income (if resident status): dividend + interest + rental + capital gain.
  • Specific LTCG under Section 112A (listed equity) at 12.5% post Finance Act 2024.

/ Section 234C relief for specific scenarios

The exceptions.

  • Unanticipated capital gains: Section 234C has specific relief if gain arose AFTER Q3 due date (15 December) + paid in subsequent instalment (Q4 15 March).
  • Dividend income: specific relief where dividend declared after advance tax due date.
  • Section 44AD / 44ADA presumptive scheme: single advance tax instalment by 15 March of FY (no Q1/Q2/Q3 obligation).
  • Senior citizens without business income: exempt from advance tax if income only from pension / interest / rent.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's the Q3 advance tax deadline for FY 2026-27?
15 December 2026. Cumulative requirement: 75% of estimated full-year tax by this date. Q1 (15 June): 15%. Q2 (15 September): 45% cumulative. Q3 (15 December): 75% cumulative. Q4 (15 March 2027): 100%.
What's Section 234C interest for Q3 shortfall?
1% per month simple interest for 3 months on the shortfall between 75% cumulative requirement and amount actually paid by 15 December. For INR 10 lakh shortfall: INR 30,000 interest. For INR 1 crore shortfall: INR 3 lakh interest.
Can I claim Section 234C relief for unanticipated capital gain?
Yes - if capital gain arose on or after 15 December (Q3 due date) + tax on it paid in Q4 (15 March): Section 234C relief applies. If gain was realised BEFORE 15 December + not included in Q3 payment: no relief + 234C interest applies.
Does Section 44AD/44ADA presumptive scheme have different advance tax?
Yes - single advance tax instalment by 15 March of FY (not 4 quarterly instalments). Simpler compliance + no 234C risk during Q1/Q2/Q3. Opt-in via ITR specific declaration + 5-year lock-in once opted.
