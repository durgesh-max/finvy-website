/ Spanish companies and NRIs in Spain · DTAA · Updated 2026-11-24

India-Spain DTAA (2026): Royalties and Technical Fees at 10% Under the MFN Notification, Dividend and Interest Rates, and How to Claim, royalties and technical fees fell to 10%, and past excess withholding can be refunded.

Under Notification No. 33/2024 of 19 March 2024, India gave effect to the most-favoured-nation clause in its treaty with Spain and cut the rate on royalties and fees for technical services paid to a Spanish resident to 10%, importing the rate from the India-Germany treaty. Before that, the treaty allowed 10% only on equipment royalties and 20% on other royalties and technical fees. The lower rate applies from tax year 2023-24, so Spanish recipients who suffered more than 10% can claim the excess by filing an Indian return. In this treaty royalties and technical fees sit in Article 13, and their definitions were not changed by the notification. Dividends and interest stay at the treaty rates, commonly reported as 15%.

/ Quick answers

What Spanish companies and NRIs ask.

  • What is the rate on royalties and technical fees? 10% of the gross amount for a Spanish resident beneficial owner, from tax year 2023-24 under Notification No. 33/2024.
  • Why did it change? The treaty's MFN clause, which India now applies only after a separate notification following the Supreme Court's 2023 ruling, was given effect for Spain in March 2024.
  • Did the definitions change? No. Only the rate was reduced; what counts as royalty or technical fees under the Spain treaty stays the same.
  • Can I get a refund for earlier withholding? Yes, for years from 2023-24 where tax above 10% was withheld, by filing an Indian return and claiming the treaty rate.
  • What about dividends and interest? Treaty rates, commonly reported as 15%; compare with the domestic rate and check the treaty text.

/ Claiming and refunds

Documents and next steps.

  • Documents: a Spanish tax residency certificate, Form 41 (formerly Form 10F) and a PAN or the documents allowed instead; see tax residency certificates.
  • Indian payers can deduct TDS at 10% on royalties and technical fees once the documents are in place.
  • Refunds: file an Indian return for each year from 2023-24 where tax above 10% was withheld, with the TRC and Form 41.
  • Scope check: because definitions did not change, confirm the payment is royalty or technical fees under the Spain treaty, not business profits taxable only with a PE.
  • Credit in Spain: Indian tax at the treaty rate is generally creditable against Spanish tax on the same income.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the India-Spain DTAA rate on royalties and fees for technical services?
10% of the gross amount for a Spanish resident, under Notification No. 33/2024 of 19 March 2024, which gave effect to the treaty's MFN clause using the India-Germany rate. It applies from tax year 2023-24.
What was the rate before the 2024 notification?
10% for royalties for industrial, commercial or scientific equipment and 20% for other royalties and fees for technical services.
Can Spanish companies get a refund of excess Indian tax?
Yes. For years from 2023-24 where tax above 10% was withheld on royalties or technical fees, file an Indian income tax return claiming the treaty rate, with a Spanish TRC and Form 41.
Did the notification change the definitions of royalty and technical fees?
No. It only reduced the rate. The definitions in the India-Spain treaty, where royalties and technical fees are covered in Article 13, stay as they were.
