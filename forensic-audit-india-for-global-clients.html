/ When clients call

Common triggers.

  • Whistleblower complaint about an Indian subsidiary, distributor or vendor.
  • Unexplained margin drops, inventory losses or cash leakage.
  • Suspected related-party diversion or round-tripping of funds.
  • Anti-bribery reviews for parents subject to the US FCPA or the UK Bribery Act.
  • Shareholder, joint-venture or commercial disputes that need an independent accounting view or expert testimony.

/ What we do

Method.

  • Ledger and transaction analytics on Tally or ERP data: duplicate payments, vendor-employee matches, unusual journal entries, round-sum and weekend postings.
  • Vendor and counterparty background checks, including MCA director and shareholding links.
  • Fund-flow tracing across Indian bank accounts and group entities.
  • Interviews and document review, coordinated through the client's counsel where privilege matters.
  • Report written to evidential standard, and expert-witness support in arbitration or court.

/ Indian legal context

Points foreign clients should know.

  • Statutory auditors must report suspected fraud above the prescribed threshold to the government under Section 143(12) of the Companies Act, 2013.
  • Fraud is punishable under Section 447 of the Companies Act, 2013.
  • Data handled in an investigation is subject to India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
