/ When Form AOC-5 is needed

Core trigger.

  • Section 128 Companies Act requires books of accounts to be kept at REGISTERED OFFICE of the company by default.
  • If company chooses to keep books at a DIFFERENT location (branch office, head office different from registered office, back-office location): must file Form AOC-5.
  • Rule 2A: file Form AOC-5 within 7 days of board resolution authorising books location change.
  • Section 450 default penalty if not filed.

/ Common AOC-5 scenarios

When companies trigger.

  • Registered office in Ahmedabad but books at corporate office in Mumbai: file AOC-5.
  • Registered office at CA / professional office + actual books at company's own office elsewhere: file AOC-5.
  • Multi-location business with centralised back-office / accounting hub.
  • Outsourced accounting to third-party service provider's location (BQP's office may be the books location for client).
  • Virtual registered office with physical books at different location.

/ Form AOC-5 structure + filing

The compliance.

  • Board resolution: company's board must pass resolution authorising keeping books at specific different location.
  • Form AOC-5 content: company CIN + PAN + registered office + different books location address + period from when books kept there.
  • Attach: certified board resolution.
  • DSC + file via MCA v3 portal within 7 days.
  • Fee: minimal MCA fee + late fee if filed beyond 7 days.
  • Section 450 default penalty: INR 10,000 + INR 1,000 per day continuing default + officer penalty.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

When is Form AOC-5 due?
Within 7 days of board resolution authorising keeping books at location different from registered office. Section 128 + Rule 2A Companies (Accounts) Rules 2014. Late filing attracts Section 450 default penalty.
What if books are at registered office?
No AOC-5 required - default rule. Company's books kept at registered office. AOC-5 ONLY needed when books location is different from registered office.
Does outsourcing accounting to CA firm trigger AOC-5?
Depends on physical location of books. If physical books / data kept at CA firm's office (common for outsourced accounting): yes - file AOC-5. If CA firm has only soft access to company's cloud accounting (QBO / Zoho / Tally cloud) with physical books at company's own office: not strictly triggered. Specific analysis needed.
