/ Salaried traders, full-time traders and NRIs trading in India · Speculative and non-speculative income · Updated 2026-11-27

Intraday and F&O Trading Income Tax (2026): ITR-3, Business Codes 21009 / 21010, Turnover, Tax Audit and Carrying Forward Losses, file it as a business, or lose the loss.

Intraday equity trading is speculative business income and F&O trading is non-speculative business income - neither is a capital gain - so traders file ITR-3, not ITR-1 or ITR-2. The return needs the right business code (21009 for intraday, 21010 for F&O, 21011 for delivery-based share trading), a turnover worked out the way the ICAI prescribes, and sometimes a tax audit. Get it wrong or file late and you lose the right to carry forward trading losses.

  • Form: ITR-3 for anyone with intraday or F&O income, including salaried people who trade on the side.
  • Losses need a timely return: business and speculative losses can be carried forward only if the return is filed by the due date.
  • New regime: trading income is taxed at slab rates under either regime; check the regime choice rules for people with business income before switching.

/ Turnover and tax audit

Where traders get caught.

  • Turnover: under the ICAI Guidance Note, trading turnover is the total of absolute profits and losses on each trade - not the contract value.
  • Tax audit (section 44AB): required where turnover exceeds the limit - Rs 10 crore when cash receipts and payments are within 5%, which is usually the case for traders - and can also arise in some loss or low-profit cases linked to the presumptive scheme; check your facts.
  • Presumptive scheme: whether section 44AD can apply to speculative income is disputed, so do not rely on it without advice.
  • Expenses: brokerage, exchange charges, internet, advisory subscriptions and similar costs are deductible against trading income; STT is not deductible.

/ If you missed or got it wrong

Fixing the return.

  • Filed ITR-2 showing F&O as capital gains? File a revised return in ITR-3 - for AY 2026-27 free until 31 December 2026.
  • Missed the due date? A belated return by 31 December 2026 is possible, but losses (other than house property) cannot be carried forward.
  • Got a notice for a mismatch between the AIS and your return? Reconcile with the broker's tax P&L and reply with the working.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Which ITR should I file for intraday or F&O trading?
ITR-3. Intraday equity trading is speculative business income and F&O is non-speculative business income, so ITR-1 and ITR-2 cannot be used - even if you are salaried and trade on the side.
What is the business code for intraday and F&O trading in the ITR?
Code 21009 for speculative trading (intraday), 21010 for futures and options trading, and 21011 for buying and selling shares on delivery as a business.
For how many years can a speculative loss be carried forward?
Four years, and it can be set off only against speculative income. A non-speculative business loss such as an F&O loss can be set off against other income except salary and carried forward for eight years. Both need the return filed by the due date.
Can I carry forward trading losses under the new tax regime?
Trading losses from business can generally be carried forward under the new regime as well, but the return must be filed on time; check the regime rules for people with business income before switching between regimes.
Is a tax audit required for F&O trading?
Only in some cases: when turnover - worked out as the total of absolute profits and losses - exceeds the section 44AB limit (Rs 10 crore when cash transactions are within 5%), or in certain low-profit or loss cases linked to the presumptive scheme. Have your turnover and profit checked before the due date.
