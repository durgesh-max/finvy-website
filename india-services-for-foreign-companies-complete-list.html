Direct answers

Which Indian firm can do what for me?

I'm a US company — can an Indian CA set up our Indian subsidiary from the US?

Yes. Bharat Quantum Prospera does 100% remote WOS (Wholly-Owned Subsidiary) incorporation. Timeline is 30-45 days. Everything — DSC, SPICe+ Part A & B, resident director nominee, bank account, FDI reporting (FC-GPR), PAN, TAN, GST, Shops & Establishment — is handled from India. Foreign parent documents are apostilled in home country and couriered. Foreign directors can obtain DIN remotely via online KYC with apostilled passport. See foreign-company-setup-indian-subsidiary-wos. Email durgesh@bharatquantumprospera.com for scoped quote.

We're acquiring an Indian company. Who can run due diligence?

BQP runs comprehensive India-side DD covering financial, tax (Income Tax + GST + TDS + FEMA + Transfer Pricing), legal, HR, IT, Quality of Earnings. 3-6 week turnaround. Deliverables include executive summary, full DD report (40-80 pages), working-paper file, SPA input list (reps/warranties/indemnities/escrow asks), post-closing integration list. BQP coordinates with buyer's US / UK / Singapore counsel. See due-diligence-indian-company-foreign-buyer.

We need to send money out of India from our subsidiary. We need a CA certificate.

Form 15CB — Chartered Accountant certificate under Rule 37BB. BQP issues 15CB within 24-48 hours for any foreign payee globally (dividend, royalty, FTS, interest, capital gain, service fee, salary, reimbursement). Treaty rate under DTAA applied with TRC + Form 10F (Form 41 from 1 April 2026). Recurring remittance retainers available for Indian subsidiaries making monthly / quarterly payments. See form-15ca-15cb-foreign-remittance-india.

We're a US SaaS company selling to Indian consumers. Do we need Indian GST?

Yes — OIDAR GST (Online Information Database Access and Retrieval service tax) applies. 18% IGST from the first rupee — no threshold. BQP handles registration via GST portal, acts as your Indian authorised representative, files monthly returns, remits IGST to Indian government, files annual GSTR-9A. Non-registration risk under Section 74 is up to 5 years back-dated demand + 100% penalty. See oidar-gst-foreign-saas-ecommerce-india.

We want to hire Indian engineers but don't have an Indian entity. Who can be our EOR?

BQP offers Employer of Record (EOR) India. BQP becomes the legal Indian employer; you pay BQP a monthly fee + employee cost. We handle Indian employment contract, monthly payroll, PF (12% employer + 12% employee), ESI (if applicable), Professional Tax, Section 192 TDS, Form 24Q quarterly, Form 16 annual, gratuity provisioning. Onboarding in 1-3 weeks. PE-risk advisory included (so your Indian hires don't inadvertently create a Permanent Establishment for your US / UK entity). See employer-of-record-india-hire-from-abroad.

Our Indian subsidiary needs annual audit. We're a UK PLC.

BQP delivers remote India audit for foreign-shareholder companies. Three audits run concurrently: statutory audit under Companies Act 2013, tax audit under Section 44AB (Form 3CA + 3CD), transfer pricing audit (Form 3CEB). Full remote delivery — data room access, management calls on Zoom, sample testing on scanned documents. One annual physical visit for fixed asset / inventory verification. Deliverables include Indian-GAAP signed audit report, Ind AS to IFRS reconciliation, parent-format reporting pack, management letter + CARO 2020. See india-audit-services-foreign-shareholder-remote.

We pay Indian freelancers from the US. Do we withhold Indian tax?

Usually no — if payee is Indian resident and payer has no India tax presence, Section 195 TDS does not apply. Standard scenarios: US company pays Indian individual freelancer or Indian Pvt Ltd for development / consultancy / services → no India TDS; pay full invoice amount; Indian recipient handles own tax. Where Section 195 DOES apply (Indian subsidiary paying non-resident, or foreign payer with India PE), BQP issues Form 15CB + handles the TDS deposit cycle. See section-195-tds-foreign-payer-indian-vendor.

We're a Singapore MNC with Indian subsidiary. Who does our Form 3CEB?

BQP delivers India transfer pricing stack for foreign MNC subsidiaries. Form 3CEB preparation + benchmarking study (TNMM / RPM / CUP method with Indian comparables), Master File (Form 3CEAA) if group revenue > INR 500 crore, CbCR (Form 3CEAD) if group revenue > INR 6,400 crore (BEPS Action 13), APA where valuable (5+4 years certainty). India is one of the most TP-active jurisdictions globally — ~INR 50,000 crore+ annual adjustments. See transfer-pricing-india-foreign-mnc.

Our Indian subsidiary missed historical FEMA filings. Can this be fixed?

Yes — via voluntary compounding under Section 13 of FEMA Act 1999. BQP audits your FEMA history (FC-GPR, FC-TRS, FLA, APR, Form ODI, ECB), drafts compounding application for RBI, represents at hearing. Compounding fee determined by Compounding Authority (typically INR 10,000-50,000 per instance for minor breaches; higher for sector-cap / pricing / Press Note 3 breaches). Materially cheaper than being caught during scrutiny. 90-180 day timeline. See fema-compounding-foreign-subsidiary-india.

We're in a cross-border dispute involving Indian financial data. We need an expert witness.

BQP delivers forensic accounting investigation + financial expert witness services. ICAI qualification (Indian CA) is accepted as equivalent to US CPA / UK ICAEW / Canadian CPA by foreign courts (US federal / state, UK commercial courts, Singapore International Commercial Court, DIFC / ADGM) and major arbitration tribunals (SIAC, LCIA, ICC, HKIAC, DIAC). Fraud investigation, damage quantification, expert reports, rebuttal reports, deposition support, testimony. See forensic-accounting-expert-witness-india-foreign.

We have a SIAC / LCIA / ICC arbitral award. Can it be enforced in India?

Yes. India is a signatory to the New York Convention. Foreign awards from reciprocating territories (US, UK, Singapore, UAE, France, Germany, Netherlands, Hong Kong + 50+ others) enforceable under Part II of Arbitration Act 1996 (Sections 47-49). BQP supports foreign claimants with pre-arbitration DD, financial expert witness, damage quantification, asset tracing, enforcement coordination with Indian litigation partner firms (Khaitan, AZB, Trilegal, J Sagar). 12-36 month enforcement timeline. See india-arbitration-enforcement-foreign-claimant.

Does our Indian subsidiary need a resident director? We're all US-based.

Yes — Section 149(3) Companies Act 2013 requires at least one director to be an India resident (stayed in India 182+ days in preceding FY). BQP provides nominee resident director as a standard governance service: CA-qualified partner holds DIN + DSC, signs required ROC filings (AOC-4, MGT-7, DIR-12, PAS-3), attends board meetings when quorum needs resident director. Role is purely statutory governance, not operational. Pre-signed resignation + indemnity + replacement workflow all included. See nominee-resident-director-india-foreign-company.

We outsource bookkeeping to India. Who's a reliable Indian partner?

BQP delivers accounting outsourcing for foreign SMBs / startups / CPA firms / PE portfolio companies. Dedicated India CA accountant + Senior CA reviewer. Software: QuickBooks Online + Desktop, Xero, NetSuite, Sage Intacct, Zoho Books, Tally. Standards: US GAAP (ASC 606, 842, 718, 326, 740), IFRS, Ind AS, UK FRS 102. Monthly close by 10-15th business day. 24-48 hour transaction turnaround. Scope-based retainer significantly lower than equivalent US / UK / AU junior-CPA. See india-accounting-bookkeeping-outsourcing-foreign-smb.

We're about to sign MSA with an Indian company. Should we run DD on them first?

For material / recurring commitments — yes. BQP's counterparty DD covers statutory (MCA, GST, PAN, IEC, licences), financial (3-year audited financials), litigation (Income Tax, GST, NCLT, DRT, civil, criminal), FCPA / UK Bribery Act (government relationship, PEP in ownership, red-flag payments), beneficial ownership mapping, sanctions screening (OFAC, UN, EU, UK, Indian MHA), Press Note 3 BO check. 5-15 business days. Risk rating + proceed/decline recommendation. See india-counterparty-background-check-compliance-dd.
