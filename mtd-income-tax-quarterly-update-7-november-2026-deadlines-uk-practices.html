/ UK · Making Tax Digital · Updated 2026-11-02

MTD for Income Tax: quarterly update due 7 November 2026, and every 2026-27 deadline.

Sole traders and landlords in the first Making Tax Digital for Income Tax cohort must send their second quarterly update, for 6 July to 5 October 2026, by 7 November 2026. HMRC will not issue penalty points for late quarterly updates in 2026-27, but the easement does not cover the tax return or payments, and it does not extend to the larger cohort joining in April 2027.

/ Penalties in the first year

What is relaxed and what is not.

  • For 2026-27, HMRC will not issue penalty points for late quarterly updates from the first MTD for Income Tax cohort.
  • The easement does not cover the 2026-27 tax return or late payment of tax.
  • Taxpayers who join from 6 April 2027 (qualifying income above GBP 30,000) get no such easement; their first quarterly update is due 7 August 2027.
  • Under the points regime, four points trigger a GBP 200 penalty, and each further missed deadline while at the threshold adds another GBP 200.

/ Who is in scope

And who joins next.

  • From 6 April 2026: sole traders and landlords with qualifying income (self-employment plus property) above GBP 50,000 in 2024-25.
  • From 6 April 2027: above GBP 30,000 (tested on 2025-26). From 6 April 2028: above GBP 20,000.
  • Partnerships are not yet in scope.

/ For UK practices

Four deadlines a year per client.

  • Quarterly updates turn one annual job into five touchpoints per client: four updates and the tax return.
  • Routine work (bank feeds, categorising transactions, preparing the quarterly summary in MTD-compatible software) can be handled offshore, leaving your team to review, advise and submit as agent.
  • Plan now for the April 2027 cohort, which is larger and has no penalty easement.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

When is the next MTD for Income Tax quarterly update due?
7 November 2026, for the period 6 July to 5 October 2026. The following updates are due on 7 February and 7 May 2027.
Are there penalties for a late MTD quarterly update in 2026-27?
No penalty points are issued for late quarterly updates in 2026-27 for the first cohort. Late tax returns and late payments are still penalised.
Who has to use MTD for Income Tax from April 2027?
Sole traders and landlords with qualifying income above GBP 30,000 in 2025-26.
How many points trigger an MTD penalty?
Four points for quarterly filers, leading to a GBP 200 penalty, with GBP 200 more for each further missed deadline at the threshold.
