  • Advance tax applies if tax after TDS is INR 10,000 or more; resident senior citizens without business income are exempt.
  • Interest for deferment: 1% a month on the shortfall, for three months for each of the first three instalments and one month for the last.
  • Interest for shortfall: 1% a month from 1 April 2027 if less than 90% was paid by 31 March 2027.
  • Interest is computed on amounts rounded down to the nearest INR 100.

/ What counts

Getting the estimate right.

  • Include capital gains, interest, rent and freelance or business income, not just salary.
  • Foreign income is taxable if you are resident and ordinarily resident; claim credit for foreign tax in the return.
  • Gains arising after an instalment date can be paid in the next instalment without deferment interest for that part.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

When is the third advance tax instalment for FY 2026-27?
15 December 2026, by which 75% of the year's tax (after TDS) should be paid.
What is the interest for missing the 15 December instalment?
1% a month for three months on the shortfall against 75% of the tax, under Section 425 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (earlier Section 234C).
Who has to pay advance tax?
Anyone whose tax after TDS is INR 10,000 or more, except resident senior citizens without business income.
When does Section 424 (earlier 234B) interest apply?
If less than 90% of the tax was paid as advance tax by 31 March, at 1% a month from 1 April until the balance is paid.
