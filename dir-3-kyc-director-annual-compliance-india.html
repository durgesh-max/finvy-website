DIR-3 KYC 2026: Now Once Every Three Years, Due 30 June, the annual filing is gone.

Director KYC is no longer annual. Under G.S.R. 943(E), dated 31 December 2025 and effective 31 March 2026, every DIN holder files DIR-3 KYC once every three consecutive financial years, by 30 June of the due year, instead of every year by 30 September. Changes to your mobile number, email or residential address must be reported within 30 days. There is no 30 September 2026 deadline.

/ What changed

Old rule vs new rule.

Until FY 2025-26From 31 March 2026
FrequencyEvery financial yearOnce every three consecutive financial years
Due date30 September30 June of the due year
Change in mobile, email or addressReported in the next annual KYCReported within 30 days; does not reset the three-year cycle
FormDIR-3 KYC eForm or WebPrimarily DIR-3 KYC Web on the MCA portal
Missed filingDIN deactivated; INR 5,000 to reactivateSame

Forms left in draft or pending payment on the MCA portal before 31 March 2026 were cancelled and must be filed again.

/ What you need to do

By situation.

  • Filed KYC for FY 2025-26: nothing more is needed for FY 2026-27 or FY 2027-28 unless your contact details change. Check your next due year on the MCA portal.
  • Changed mobile number, email or address: update within 30 days of the change.
  • New DIN: your first KYC falls due in the year your three-year cycle ends; diarise it when the DIN is allotted.
  • DIN already deactivated for an earlier missed filing: file the pending KYC with the INR 5,000 fee to reactivate before signing any MCA form.
  • Foreign directors: the same rule applies; keep the registered mobile and email reachable from abroad, because the Web filing uses OTPs.

/ Why it matters

DIN status blocks company filings.

  • A deactivated DIN cannot sign AOC-4, MGT-7, DIR-12 or other MCA forms, and the director cannot be appointed elsewhere.
  • Investors and lenders check DIN status in due diligence.
  • Directors of companies that stop filing accounts for three years face disqualification under Section 164(2), which is a separate issue from KYC.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is DIR-3 KYC still annual?
No. From 31 March 2026, under G.S.R. 943(E), DIR-3 KYC is filed once every three consecutive financial years, by 30 June of the due year.
Is there a DIR-3 KYC deadline on 30 September 2026?
No. The 30 September annual deadline no longer applies. Directors who filed for FY 2025-26 need not file for FY 2026-27 or FY 2027-28 unless their details change.
What if my mobile number or email changes?
Update your KYC within 30 days of the change on the MCA portal. This does not reset your three-year cycle.
What happens if I miss my DIR-3 KYC?
Your DIN is deactivated and you cannot sign MCA filings. Reactivation needs the pending KYC and a fee of INR 5,000.
