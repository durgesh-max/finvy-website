## What makes a review legitimate under Google's policy (brief internally; don't share)

- Reviewer must be a real past client (not a friend, not a family member, not a vendor).
- Review must be unincentivised (no discount offered, no gift).
- One review per client per year.
- Client writes their own words — never draft the review for them.
- Avoid simultaneous mass requests (Google penalises review-velocity spikes). Rhythm: 2-3 per week, not 25 in week 1.

## Alternative routes (if GBP review flow stalls)

- **Google Business Profile direct link in email signature** — passive ask every time you send an email. Add: "Found us helpful? [1-min Google review]"
- **Post-engagement feedback email at close** — short automated email at mandate close asking for feedback + Google review link.
- **Case study participation** — some clients prefer to contribute to a BQP case study (anonymised on case-studies.html) over a public Google review. Offer this as an alternative when a public Google review feels too formal for their brand.
