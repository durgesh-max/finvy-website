NRI Renting Out Property in India (2026): Tenant TDS at 31.2%, TAN, Form 144, Lower Deduction Certificate and Repatriation, the tenant deducts, the landlord claims it back.

When an NRI lets out a flat or house in India, the tenant must deduct tax from every rent payment, with no minimum threshold, at 30% plus cess (about 31.2%) under section 393(2) of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (formerly section 195). The tenant needs a TAN and files the quarterly non-resident TDS statement, Form 144 (formerly 27Q); the PAN-based no-TAN route from 1 October 2026 applies to buying property from an NRI, not to rent. The landlord is taxed only on rent after a 30% standard deduction, municipal taxes and loan interest, so the excess TDS comes back through the return, or is avoided with a lower deduction certificate (Form 128, formerly Form 13). Net rent can be sent abroad from the NRO account.

If this is the landlord's only Indian income, tax at the slab rates for the year is far below the INR 1,87,200 deducted, so most of it is refundable through the return, or avoidable with a lower deduction certificate. Interest on a loan taken for the let-out property is also deductible. Check the slab rates for the relevant year; NRIs do not get the rebate available to residents.

Most NRI landlords are over-deducted.

TDS is taken on the gross rent at about 31.2%, while tax is due only on rent after the 30% standard deduction, municipal tax and loan interest, at slab rates. A lower deduction certificate before the year starts keeps the money with you instead of waiting for a refund. WhatsApp CA Durgesh Chavda.

/ Landlord checklist

Through the year.

  • Before the year: apply for a lower or nil deduction certificate (Form 128) with an estimate of the year's rent and income, and give it to the tenant.
  • Bank account: receive rent in your NRO account; it cannot be credited to an NRE account.
  • Return: file the Indian return (usually ITR-2) by the due date and claim the TDS credit and refund.
  • Repatriation: rent is current income, so after tax it can be remitted from the NRO account with the CA certificate and bank forms (Forms 145/146, formerly 15CA/15CB), outside the USD 1 million limit that applies to capital.
  • Abroad: under India's tax treaties, income from immovable property is taxable in India; your country of residence taxes it too and gives credit for the Indian tax. Report it there (for example, US Schedule E, UK self-assessment, Canada T776, Australia rental schedule).

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How much TDS does a tenant deduct on rent paid to an NRI?
30% plus 4% cess, about 31.2%, on the gross rent, with surcharge if the landlord's income exceeds INR 50 lakh. There is no minimum threshold, unlike rent paid to residents.
Does the tenant need a TAN to pay rent to an NRI?
Yes. The PAN-based route without a TAN that started on 1 October 2026 applies to buying property from a non-resident, not to rent.
Which form does the tenant file?
The quarterly non-resident TDS statement, Form 144 under the Income-tax Rules, 2026 (formerly Form 27Q), and a TDS certificate for the landlord.
Can an NRI landlord reduce the TDS?
Yes. Apply for a lower or nil deduction certificate (Form 128, formerly Form 13) based on the expected income; the tenant then deducts at the certified rate.
How is NRI rental income taxed?
As income from house property: net annual value (rent less municipal taxes) minus a 30% standard deduction and interest on a loan for the property, taxed at slab rates. Excess TDS is refunded through the return.
Can rent be sent abroad?
Yes. Rent is current income; after tax it can be remitted from the NRO account with the CA certificate and bank forms, outside the USD 1 million limit for capital.
