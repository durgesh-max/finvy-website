NRI Income Tax Calculator India 2026-27: New vs Old Regime, Capital Gains, Surcharge and Your Refund After TDS, no 87A rebate for NRIs, so check before you assume nil tax.

NRIs pay Indian tax at the same slab rates as residents but without the section 87A rebate, so the ₹12 lakh tax-free limit does not apply: under the new regime tax starts above ₹4 lakh, and under the old regime above ₹2.5 lakh at any age. Capital gains are taxed at their own rates (20% on short-term equity gains, 12.5% on long-term gains), and TDS on NRI income is often higher than the final tax.     / How it works

    The rules behind the numbers.

    • Slab rates: new regime nil to ₹4 lakh, then 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25% and 30% above ₹24 lakh; old regime nil to ₹2.5 lakh, 5%, 20% and 30% above ₹10 lakh.
    • No 87A rebate for non-residents, and no higher senior-citizen exemption in the old regime.
    • Capital gains: 20% on short-term gains on listed shares and equity funds; 12.5% on long-term equity gains above ₹1.25 lakh; 12.5% on other long-term gains such as property. NRIs cannot use unused basic exemption against these gains.
    • Surcharge: 10% above ₹50 lakh, 15% above ₹1 crore, 25% above ₹2 crore (old regime 37% above ₹5 crore), capped at 15% on capital gains; plus 4% cess.
    • TDS and refunds: TDS on payments to NRIs is often above the final tax; the excess comes back when you file ITR-2 or ITR-3.

    FAQ

    Common questions, answered.

    How is income tax calculated for an NRI in India?
    Indian income taxed at slab rates is charged at the new-regime rates (nil to Rs 4 lakh) or old-regime rates (nil to Rs 2.5 lakh), without the 87A rebate; capital gains are added at their own rates, then surcharge and 4% cess.
    Is there a tax calculator for NRIs?
    Yes, the calculator on this page estimates an NRI's Indian tax for 2026-27 under either regime, including equity and other capital gains, surcharge, cess and the refund or tax due after TDS.
    Do NRIs get the Rs 12 lakh tax-free limit?
    No. That limit comes from the section 87A rebate, which is for resident individuals only.
    How much tax does an NRI pay on Rs 10 lakh of Indian income?
    Under the new regime about Rs 41,600 including cess on Rs 10 lakh of slab income, because there is no 87A rebate; under the old regime more, unless deductions reduce it.
    How can an NRI get a refund of excess TDS?
    By filing ITR-2 or ITR-3 for the year and claiming credit for the TDS shown in Form 26AS / AIS; the excess over the actual tax is refunded.
