/ Section 115JC AMT core

The 18.5% rate.

  • AMT applies to: individuals + HUFs + LLPs + Partnership firms (NOT companies - MAT under 115JB).
  • Rate: 18.5% of adjusted total income + applicable surcharge + 4% cess.
  • Adjusted total income: regular total income + specific deductions added back (Section 10AA SEZ, Section 35AD investment-linked, Section 80 deductions except 80P / 80IAC / 80IAB specifically).
  • Applies when: regular tax on income is LOWER than AMT on adjusted total income.
  • If AMT applies: taxpayer pays AMT; difference becomes AMT credit (carried forward 15 years).

/ Who's exempt + specific carve-outs

The exceptions.

  • Individual + HUF: AMT exempt if adjusted total income < INR 20 lakh.
  • Companies: subject to MAT (Section 115JB) not AMT.
  • Specific entities + specific income (per Section 115JC(2)): certain exemptions.
  • Section 80-IAC Startup India + Section 80IAB SEZ developer: specific interaction with AMT.

/ Common Section 115JC scenarios

Where it matters.

  • LLP with Section 10AA SEZ deduction: regular tax low; AMT applies.
  • Partnership firm with substantial Section 80 deductions.
  • Individual with Section 35AD investment-linked deduction (specified business).
  • HUF with substantial agricultural income exemption + other specific income.
  • LLP opting out of Section 115BAD alternative (29% rate without specific deductions).

/ Form 29C + CA certification

The compliance.

  • Form 29C: Chartered Accountant certificate on adjusted total income + AMT computation.
  • Filed with ITR (ITR-3 for individuals / HUFs; ITR-5 for firms / LLPs).
  • CA certifies adjusted total income computation + AMT comparison.
  • ICAI UDIN mandatory.
  • If AMT applies: AMT paid; AMT credit carried forward 15 years.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's Section 115JC AMT?
Alternate Minimum Tax at 18.5% applicable to individuals + HUFs + LLPs + Partnership firms (NOT companies - companies have MAT at 15% under Section 115JB). AMT applies when regular tax is lower than 18.5% of 'adjusted total income' (regular income + specific deductions added back).
Does AMT apply to small individuals?
Individual + HUF: AMT exempt if adjusted total income < INR 20 lakh. Above INR 20 lakh with specific deductions (10AA / 35AD / Section 80), AMT may apply. LLPs + Partnership firms: AMT applies regardless of threshold.
What's Form 29C and when is it filed?
Form 29C: CA certificate on adjusted total income + AMT computation. Filed with ITR by taxpayers to whom Section 115JC applies. ICAI UDIN mandatory. For LLPs + Partnership firms with specific Section 80 / 10AA / 35AD claims.
Can AMT credit be carried forward?
Yes - 15 years. If AMT paid exceeds regular tax in a year, excess becomes AMT credit. In future years when regular tax exceeds AMT, AMT credit can be set off against regular tax (up to extent of regular tax). Prevents double-taxation.
Is Section 115BAD a way around AMT for LLPs?
Section 115BAD: 29% concessional corporate tax for LLPs that opt out of specific deductions. If opted in, AMT (115JC) does NOT apply. Trade-off: forfeit Section 10AA + specific Chapter VI-A deductions for lower rate + MAT exemption. Decision modelling similar to Section 115BAA for companies.
