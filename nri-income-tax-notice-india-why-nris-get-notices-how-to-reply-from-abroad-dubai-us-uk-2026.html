NRI Income Tax Notice (2026): Why NRIs Get Notices From the Indian Tax Department and How to Reply From Abroad (Dubai, US, UK), read it, check it, reply on the portal from wherever you are.

An NRI usually gets an Indian income tax notice because the department can see a transaction in India - a property sale or purchase, large deposits, NRO interest or TDS - that does not match a return, or because no return was filed at all. Living in Dubai, the US or the UK does not stop India taxing Indian income. Every notice can be answered online on the e-filing portal, by you or by a chartered accountant you authorise, without travelling to India. The first steps: check the DIN, note the deadline in the notice, and do not ignore it.

/ How to reply from abroad

Everything happens on the portal.

  • Check the DIN: every genuine notice carries a document identification number you can verify on the e-filing portal (CBDT Circular 19/2019). Ignore emails asking you to pay through a link.
  • Open the notice: log in to the e-filing portal with your PAN and open Pending Actions > e-Proceedings. The PDF password is your PAN in lower case followed by your date of birth (DDMMYYYY).
  • Note the deadline: reply within the time stated in the notice; if you need more time, ask for an adjournment on the portal with a reason.
  • Authorise a CA: you can add a chartered accountant as your authorised representative on the portal so they can file the reply and attend video hearings.
  • Verify online: replies and returns are verified with net banking, a digital signature or Aadhaar OTP if you have one (NRIs do not need to link Aadhaar with PAN).
  • Do not ignore it: with no reply the officer can complete a best-judgment assessment, add income and levy interest and penalty.

/ NRIs in Dubai and the UAE

No UAE income tax does not mean no Indian tax.

  • Indian income stays taxable in India: rent, NRO interest and capital gains on Indian property are taxed in India; under the India-UAE DTAA, gains on immovable property are taxed where the property is.
  • Deemed residence: an Indian citizen with Indian income above Rs 15 lakh who is not liable to tax in any other country can be treated as resident (but not ordinarily resident) in India - a common trigger for queries on UAE-based NRIs.
  • Property purchases: notices often ask how an Indian property was paid for; keep NRE / NRO statements and remittance records.
  • Treaty rates: to use India-UAE DTAA rates you need a UAE tax residency certificate and Form 41 (old 10F).

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Why did I get an income tax notice as an NRI?
Usually because a transaction in India - a property sale or purchase, deposits, NRO interest or TDS - appears in the department's records (Form 168 / AIS) but not in a return, or no return was filed. Residential status, refunds and treaty claims also lead to queries.
Why did I get an income tax notice about my NRE account?
Usually because the NRE interest or large deposits appear in your AIS while your return shows you as resident, or does not explain them. NRE interest is exempt only while you are non-resident; if you have moved back to India and kept the NRE account, the interest becomes taxable and the account should be redesignated as a resident or RFC account. Reply with your days in India for the year, passport or travel records, the date the account was redesignated and the bank's interest certificate. If the interest was wrongly shown as taxable, give feedback in the AIS and explain it in the reply.
Can an NRI reply to an Indian income tax notice from abroad?
Yes. Notices are answered on the e-filing portal under e-Proceedings. You can reply yourself or authorise a chartered accountant as your representative; video hearings are available where a hearing is needed. No visit to India is required.
How do I know an income tax notice is genuine?
Check its DIN (document identification number) on the e-filing portal. Genuine notices also appear under Pending Actions when you log in with your PAN. Do not pay through links in emails or messages.
I live in Dubai and pay no tax there. Can India still tax me?
Yes on Indian income - rent, NRO interest and gains on Indian property are taxed in India. An Indian citizen with Indian income above Rs 15 lakh who is not liable to tax anywhere else can also be treated as resident (not ordinarily resident) in India.
What happens if an NRI ignores an income tax notice?
The officer can complete a best-judgment assessment on the information available, add income, and levy interest and penalty; refunds can be adjusted against the demand. It is always better to reply or ask for an adjournment within the time stated.
