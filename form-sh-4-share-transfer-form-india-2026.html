/ Form SH-4 core

The prescribed form.

  • Form SH-4: statutory share transfer deed under Section 56 Companies Act 2013.
  • Mandatory for every share transfer between members (physical shares; demat transfer is depository-based without SH-4).
  • Signed by: transferor + transferee + witness.
  • Stamp duty payable per Indian Stamp Act (central / state-specific).
  • Standard stamp duty: 0.015% of consideration (or face value if higher) in most states.
  • Specific state variations (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, etc.).

/ Post Rule 9B demat regime

Reduced SH-4 use.

  • Post Rule 9B mandatory dematerialisation of unlisted shares (October 2024 for private companies), most share transfers are now in demat form - depository-based transfer without SH-4.
  • SH-4 still relevant for: companies not yet Rule 9B compliant (unresponsive shareholders in physical form), small companies exempt from Rule 9B, specific legacy / inheritance share transfers.
  • Buy-back / specific restructuring may still use SH-4 for specific share class.

/ SH-4 + FC-TRS integration (foreign shareholder)

FEMA angle.

  • Share transfer between resident + non-resident OR between two non-residents requires Form FC-TRS filing within 60 days.
  • SH-4 is the share transfer deed; FC-TRS is the FEMA reporting.
  • Both required for share transfer involving foreign shareholder.
  • Rule 21 pricing guidelines verification + CA / Merchant Banker certificate.
  • See our FC-TRS filing checklist for details.

/ Common SH-4 execution issues

Where companies trip.

  • Stamp duty under-paid (wrong state rate applied, wrong consideration basis).
  • Missing witness signature.
  • SH-4 lodged with company > 2 months after execution (Rule 11 time limit).
  • Company's refusal to register transfer (specific grounds under Section 58).
  • Lost share certificates - must issue duplicate under Rule 6 before transfer.
  • Non-compliance with Rule 9B demat requirement for Rule 9B companies - transfer in physical form invalid post Rule 9B date.
  • Section 56(2)(x) buyer-side gift tax if transfer at below fair value.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

When is Form SH-4 needed?
For every share transfer between members in physical form. Post Rule 9B mandatory demat (October 2024 for private companies), most transfers are depository-based without SH-4. SH-4 still relevant for Rule 9B-exempt entities + legacy physical shares + specific restructuring.
What's the stamp duty on SH-4?
Per Indian Stamp Act + state-specific adjustments. Standard: 0.015% of consideration or face value whichever higher. Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat have specific state-level rates. Payable via physical stamp paper or e-stamping (preferred).
Can share transfer be rejected by company?
Section 58 Companies Act: company can refuse to register transfer only on specific grounds (not frivolous). Refusal: within 30 days + reasoned order + specific appeal mechanism to NCLT. Rare in practice for standard transfers.
