How it works

1. Create account on first visit using your BQP-registered phone number. We cross-check against your engagement letter to confirm you are a BQP client.

2. Sign in any time with phone + OTP. Firebase Auth handles the OTP (phone-based 2FA).

3. Upload or download documents from any folder. Files are stored in BQP's dedicated Google Drive workspace with your folder access-controlled.

4. BQP uploads too — when your tax return is filed or your ROC form is lodged, we drop the acknowledgement into the right folder.

5. Access log — every view, upload, download is logged for audit.
