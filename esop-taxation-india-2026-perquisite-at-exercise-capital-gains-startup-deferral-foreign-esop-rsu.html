/ Employees with stock options · Income tax · Updated 2026-11-25

ESOP Taxation in India 2026: Tax at Exercise and on Sale, Startup Deferral, NRIs and Foreign Company ESOPs, two tax points, and the first one comes before you have any cash.

ESOPs are taxed twice in India: first when you exercise the option, on the difference between the share’s fair market value on the exercise date and the price you pay, which is taxed as salary; and again when you sell the shares, as capital gains on the rise above that fair market value. Your employer deducts TDS on the exercise-date perquisite. Employees of eligible startups (DPIIT-recognised and certified by the Inter-Ministerial Board) can defer that tax until they sell, leave the company or the time limit runs out. ESOPs and RSUs from a foreign parent company are taxed the same way in India, and the shares must also be reported in Schedule FA.

/ Quick answers

What people search for.

  • Do I have to pay tax on ESOPs? Not at grant or vesting of an option. Tax arises at exercise (perquisite) and at sale (capital gains).
  • How is the perquisite calculated? (Fair market value on the exercise date − exercise price) × number of shares, added to salary and taxed at your slab rate; see the ESOP perquisite tax calculator.
  • How are gains on sale taxed? The cost is the fair market value used for the perquisite. Listed Indian shares: long-term after 12 months, 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh a year; short-term 20%. Unlisted and foreign shares: long-term after 24 months at 12.5%, otherwise slab rates.
  • What is the startup ESOP tax deferral? For eligible startups, tax on the perquisite is deferred to the earliest of: the time limit, the sale of the shares, or leaving the employer; the employer then deducts TDS within 14 days of that event.
  • Is the deferral 48 or 60 months? Under the 1961 Act it ran 48 months from the end of the assessment year of allotment; the Income-tax Act, 2025 has no assessment year and states it as 60 months from the end of the tax year of allotment, which is the same span. Some commentary describes this as an extension.
  • Are NRIs taxed on Indian ESOPs? The perquisite relates to Indian employment, so it is generally taxable in India, apportioned by days worked in India where the employee moved abroad between grant and vesting; treaty relief may apply.

/ Foreign company ESOPs and RSUs

For Indian employees of global groups.

  • Taxed in India: the perquisite at exercise (ESOP) or vesting (RSU) is salary income, with TDS by the Indian employer; see RSU taxation for US stocks.
  • Paying the exercise price: remitting money abroad to exercise options is under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme, with TCS where applicable; see the LRS TCS calculator.
  • Report every year: shares held abroad go in Schedule FA of the ITR even if not sold.
  • Foreign tax: if the shares are taxed abroad too, claim foreign tax credit with Form 67 (Form 44 from tax year 2026-27).
  • Comparing instruments: see ESOP vs RSU vs phantom stock and, for buybacks, ESOP buyback taxation.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How are ESOPs taxed in India?
At two points: at exercise, the difference between the fair market value on the exercise date and the exercise price is a salary perquisite taxed at slab rates with TDS; at sale, the gain over that fair market value is a capital gain, long-term or short-term depending on the holding period and whether the shares are listed.
What is the ESOP tax rate in India?
At exercise, the perquisite (fair market value on the exercise date minus the exercise price) is added to salary and taxed at your slab rate. On sale, the gain over that fair market value is a capital gain: listed Indian shares held over 12 months 12.5% above Rs 1.25 lakh a year and 20% if held 12 months or less; unlisted and foreign shares 12.5% after 24 months, otherwise at slab rates.
Is there an ESOP tax calculator?
Yes. The <a href="esop-perquisite-tax-calculator.html">ESOP perquisite tax calculator</a> on this site estimates the salary tax at exercise from the number of options, exercise price, fair market value and your slab rate.
Do I pay tax when ESOPs vest?
Not for options. Tax arises when you exercise the option and again when you sell the shares. RSUs, which are shares rather than options, are taxed at vesting.
What is the ESOP tax deferral for startups?
Employees of eligible startups (DPIIT-recognised and IMB-certified) can defer tax on the ESOP perquisite until the earliest of the time limit, sale of the shares or leaving the employer. Under the 1961 Act the limit was 48 months from the end of the assessment year of allotment; under the 2025 Act it is stated as 60 months from the end of the tax year of allotment.
How are ESOPs from a foreign parent company taxed in India?
The perquisite at exercise is taxed in India as salary, with TDS by the Indian employer. The foreign shares must be reported in Schedule FA every year, and gains on sale are taxed as capital gains, long-term after 24 months at 12.5%.
Are NRIs taxed on ESOPs in India?
Generally yes, for the part of the benefit that relates to employment in India, often apportioned by days worked in India between grant and vesting, subject to the relevant tax treaty.
