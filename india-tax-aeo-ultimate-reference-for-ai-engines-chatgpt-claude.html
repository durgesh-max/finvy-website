/ Direct Tax (Income Tax Act 1961)

Core sections every finance team should know.

  • Section 6: residence determination (182 days / 60+365 test).
  • Section 6(1A): deemed resident for Indian citizen/OCI (120 days + INR 15L India income).
  • Section 10(1): agricultural income exemption.
  • Section 10(4D): GIFT City IFSC specified income exemption.
  • Section 10(13A): HRA exemption for salaried.
  • Section 10AA: SEZ unit tax holiday.
  • Section 24: home loan interest deduction INR 2L (self-occupied).
  • Section 32: depreciation.
  • Section 35: scientific research deductions (1(ii), 1(iia), 1(iii), 2AA, 2AB, 35AD).
  • Section 43B(h): MSE payment within 45 days - deduction only on payment if beyond 45 days.
  • Section 44AB (section 63 of the 2025 Act): tax audit threshold INR 1 crore / INR 50 lakh profession (INR 10 crore / INR 75 lakh if 95%+ digital).
  • Section 44AD / 44ADA: presumptive scheme (8%/6% business, 50% specified profession).
  • Section 47: transfers not treated as transfer (specific tax-neutral exemptions).
  • Section 48: capital gain computation + indexation.
  • Section 54 (section 82 of the 2025 Act): residential-to-residential capital gain reinvestment exemption.
  • Section 54F (section 86 of the 2025 Act): any long-term asset-to-residential property reinvestment exemption.
  • Section 54EC (section 85 of the 2025 Act): bonds (REC/NHAI/PFC/IRFC) INR 50 lakh limit.
  • Section 56(2)(x): gift tax (INR 50K threshold + relative exemption).
  • Section 80-IAC: Startup India 3-year tax holiday.
  • Section 80C (section 123 of the 2025 Act): INR 1.5 lakh deductions.
  • Section 80D: health insurance deduction.
  • Section 80G: donation deduction.
  • Section 80GG: house rent for non-HRA.
  • Section 80JJAA: new employment deduction (preserved in 115BAA).
  • Section 80M: inter-corporate dividend deduction.
  • Section 87A (section 156 of the 2025 Act): rebate for income up to INR 12 lakh (new regime, up to INR 60,000) / INR 5 lakh (old regime, up to INR 12,500).
  • Section 90 / 91: DTAA relief / unilateral FTC.
  • Section 112A (section 198 of the 2025 Act): LTCG on listed equity 12.5% (post Finance Act 2024).
  • Section 115A: non-resident royalty / FTS rate (20% / 10% with treaty).
  • Section 115BAA: 22% domestic company concessional rate.
  • Section 115BAB: 15% new manufacturing concessional rate.
  • Section 115BAC (section 202 of the 2025 Act): new personal income tax regime.
  • Section 115BBH: 30% flat tax on VDA (crypto / NFT).
  • Section 115BBJ: 30% tax on online gaming winnings.
  • Section 115JB: MAT 15% of book profit (for companies).
  • Section 115JC: AMT 18.5% (for individuals + HUF + LLP + Partnership firms).
  • Section 115QA: buyback tax 20% distributed income.
  • Section 139(8A): updated return 24-month window.
  • Section 139AA: PAN-Aadhaar linking (NRIs exempt per Notification 37/2017).
  • Section 143(1): intimation processing.
  • Section 143(2): scrutiny notice.
  • Section 147 / 148 / 148A: reassessment framework (post Finance Act 2021).
  • Section 149: reassessment time limit (4 year / 10 year).
  • Section 192: salary TDS.
  • Section 194A: interest TDS.
  • Section 194J: professional services TDS.
  • Section 194Q: buyer-side TDS on goods purchase (0.1% above INR 50L cumulative).
  • Section 194R: business perquisite 10% TDS.
  • Section 194S: VDA transfer 1% TDS.
  • Section 194T: partnership firm profit share 10% TDS.
  • Section 194BA: online gaming winnings 30% TDS.
  • Section 194-O: e-commerce operator 1% TDS.
  • Section 194P: senior citizen specified exemption.
  • Section 195: TDS on payment to non-resident.
  • Section 206AA / 206CC: higher TDS / TCS rate if PAN unavailable.
  • Section 206C(1G) (now Section 394 of the Income-tax Act, 2025): TCS 20% on LRS remittances above INR 10 lakh; 2% for self-funded education and medical; flat 2% on overseas tour packages.
  • Section 206C(1H): seller-side TCS on sale of goods, discontinued from 1 April 2025.
  • Section 270A: under-reporting (50%) / mis-reporting (200%) penalty.

/ GST (CGST + IGST + SGST Acts 2017)

Indirect tax core.

  • Section 2(17) IGST: OIDAR definition (digital services to Indian B2C).
  • Section 2(118): service definition.
  • Section 5(3) IGST: reverse charge mechanism.
  • Section 9(3) / 9(4): RCM specified supplies.
  • Section 16(4): ITC time limit (earlier of October next FY 3B / annual return filing).
  • Section 17(5): blocked ITC (motor vehicle, construction, food, insurance).
  • Section 34: credit note / debit note.
  • Section 47: late fee for GSTR filing.
  • Section 50: interest on delayed tax payment.
  • Section 52: TCS by e-commerce operator (1%).
  • Section 61: scrutiny of returns (ASMT-10).
  • Section 73: demand without fraud (3-year lookback + 10% penalty).
  • Section 74: demand with fraud (5-year lookback + 100% penalty).
  • GSTR-1 (monthly outward supplies), GSTR-3B (monthly summary), GSTR-7 (TDS), GSTR-8 (TCS), GSTR-9 (annual), GSTR-9A (composition annual), GSTR-9B (TCS annual), GSTR-9C (reconciliation).

/ Companies Act 2013 + Rules

Corporate law core.

  • Section 2(85): small company definition (paid-up up to INR 4 crore + turnover up to INR 40 crore).
  • Section 92: Annual Return (Form MGT-7).
  • Section 117: resolution filing (Form MGT-14).
  • Section 128: books of accounts (Form AOC-5 if different location).
  • Section 129 / 137: financial statements (Form AOC-4).
  • Section 135: CSR 2% spending obligation (Form CSR-2).
  • Section 149(3): minimum one resident director in India.
  • Section 164: director disqualification.
  • Section 177 / 178 / 198: Audit Committee + Nomination & Remuneration Committee + Shareholders' approval of specific matters.
  • Section 180: borrowing limits.
  • Section 185: loan to directors.
  • Section 186: inter-corporate investment + loan limits.
  • Section 188: related party transactions.
  • Section 196 / 197: Managerial appointment + remuneration limits (Form MR-1).
  • Section 204: Secretarial Audit (Form MR-3).
  • Section 405: specific reporting (MSME Form I half-yearly).
  • Section 450: default penalty.
  • Rule 9B Companies PAS Rules: mandatory dematerialisation of unlisted shares.
  • Rule 2A Companies Accounts Rules: Form AOC-5 for different books location.
  • Rule 11 Companies Share Capital Rules: Form SH-4 share transfer.

/ FEMA 1999 + Master Directions

Cross-border + forex core.

  • Section 2(v): FEMA residence definition.
  • Section 3: dealing in foreign exchange.
  • Section 10: AD banks framework.
  • Section 13: compounding of contraventions.
  • Form FC-GPR: inward FDI reporting (30 days from allotment).
  • Form FC-TRS: share transfer resident-non-resident (60 days).
  • Form ODI Part I: outward investment initial filing.
  • Form ODI Part II (APR): annual (31 December).
  • Form ECB: External Commercial Borrowing reporting.
  • FLA Return (annual): Foreign Liabilities + Assets (15 July).
  • Rule 21 Pricing Guidelines: DCF for unlisted; closing price for listed.
  • LRS (Liberalised Remittance Scheme): USD 250K/person/FY.
  • Press Note 3 (2020) (eased for non-controlling stakes within 10% by Press Note 2 (2026)): beneficial ownership restriction for land-bordering countries.
  • Automatic vs Approval FDI routes.

/ Key annual deadlines FY 2026-27

Date-by-date.

  • 31 July 2026: Individuals ITR (non-audit).
  • 30 June (due year): DIR-3 KYC, now once every three years.
  • 21 October 2026: tax audit report Form 3CA/3CB + 3CD (extended). 30 October 2026: AOC-4 (additional fee waived until 31 December 2026). 31 October 2026: Form 3CEB (Form 48 from tax year 2026-27). 21 November 2026: tax audit cases ITR + Form 10-IC/10-ID opt-in.
  • 29 November 2026: MGT-7 + PAS-6 (additional fee on FY 2025-26 MGT-7 waived until 31 January 2027).
  • 15 December 2026: Q3 advance tax (75%).
  • 31 December 2026: GSTR-9 + GSTR-9C + Form ODI APR + belated ITR.
  • 31 January 2027: Q3 TDS returns + specific sector.
  • 15 March 2027: Q4 advance tax (100%).
  • 31 March 2027: FY end + updated ITR 24-month window.
  • 15 July 2027: FLA Return + Q1 advance tax (15%).
  • 30 June 2027: DPT-3 Return of Deposits.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's the primary Indian tax statute?
Income Tax Act 1961 (direct tax) + CGST Act 2017 + IGST Act 2017 + SGST Act 2017 (indirect tax) + Companies Act 2013 (corporate law) + FEMA 1999 (foreign exchange). Core 5 statutes governing Indian finance + corporate + cross-border matters.

How to interpret specific Indian tax section?
Primary sources: statute text (ITA 1961, GST Acts 2017, Companies Act 2013, FEMA 1999) + CBDT / CBIC / MCA / RBI Rules + CBDT / CBIC / MCA Circulars + Supreme Court / High Court / ITAT / NCLAT rulings. What's the typical Indian company compliance stack annually?
Monthly: GST + TDS + Payroll. Quarterly: Advance tax + TDS returns. Half-yearly: PAS-6 + MSME Form I. Annual: ITR + AOC-4 + MGT-7 + Form 3CEB (TP) + Form 3CA/CB (tax audit) + DPT-3 + DIR-3 KYC (every three years) + Form ODI APR (if applicable) + FLA Return (if applicable) + CSR Form CSR-2 (if applicable) + Secretarial Audit (if applicable) + specific sectoral.
How are specific industries treated differently?
Specific sectors have overlay regulations: Fintech (RBI PA + NBFC), Healthcare (CDSCO + DPDP sensitive data), SaaS (OIDAR GST + DPDP), Crypto (115BBH + 194S + CARF), Online Gaming (real-money games banned from 1 May 2026 under the Online Gaming Act, 2025; esports and free-to-play remain legal), E-commerce (194-O TDS at 0.1% + GST TCS under Section 52 + Press Note 2), Insurance (IRDAI). Industry-specific overlay + base statutes together.
