/ Repatriation · Step by Step · Updated 2026-11-06

How to Repatriate Money From India to the UK, US, Canada or Australia in 2026: NRO USD 1 Million, Form 15CA/15CB, Step by Step, from NRO account to your foreign bank.

NRIs can send NRE and FCNR money abroad freely. NRO money (rent, dividends, pension, interest, old savings) and property sale proceeds can be repatriated up to USD 1 million per financial year, April to March. The bank needs Form 15CA filed online and, in most cases, a Chartered Accountant's Form 15CB confirming tax has been paid; from 1 April 2026 these are Form 145 and Form 146 under the Income-tax Act, 2025. With documents ready, a 15CB can be issued within a day and the remittance usually completes in a few working days.

/ Step by step

NRO to your foreign account.

  1. Check the source: NRE/FCNR (free) or NRO/property sale (USD 1 million a year).
  2. Make sure Indian tax is settled: TDS on interest or rent, buyer's TDS on a property sale, or advance tax; file the Indian return where needed.
  3. CA certificate (Form 15CB / Form 146): the CA checks the nature of the money, tax paid and treaty position, and certifies it.
  4. Form 15CA (Form 145): filed online on the income tax portal using the 15CB details.
  5. Bank request: submit 15CA, 15CB, the bank's remittance form and undertaking, and proof of source (sale deed, statements).
  6. Remittance: the bank sends the money to your account abroad, usually within a few working days.

Selling property? Get a lower deduction certificate before the sale if the actual gain is small, and use our property TDS calculator.

/ Common mistakes

What delays repatriation.

  • Trying to send NRO money directly without a 15CB when the bank needs one.
  • Unpaid tax on the income behind the money, or no Indian return filed for the sale year.
  • Crossing USD 1 million in one financial year: split the remittance across April.
  • Moving NRO money into NRE first: that transfer is itself a repatriation within the USD 1 million limit and needs the same paperwork.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How much money can an NRI repatriate from India in a year?
From NRO accounts, including property sale proceeds, up to USD 1 million per financial year (April to March). NRE and FCNR balances have no limit.
Is Form 15CB required for NRO repatriation?
In most cases, yes: the bank asks for Form 15CA filed online and a Chartered Accountant's Form 15CB confirming the tax position. From 1 April 2026 they are Form 145 and Form 146.
How long does NRO repatriation take?
With documents ready, the CA certificate can be issued within a day and the bank usually completes the remittance in a few working days.
Can I transfer NRO to NRE?
Yes, but it counts as repatriation within the USD 1 million limit and needs the same 15CA/15CB paperwork.
