/ Pre-transfer verification

What to confirm.

  1. Share class + ownership: resident/non-resident status of both parties.
  2. Rule 9B demat compliance: both parties' shares in demat form (post Oct 2024).
  3. Specific FDI sector check + Press Note 3 beneficial ownership.
  4. Rule 21 Pricing Guidelines: DCF for unlisted; closing price for listed. CA / Merchant Banker certificate.
  5. Resident transferor: whether sale consideration above fair value is acceptable to buyer (Section 56(2)(x) buyer risk).
  6. Non-resident transferor: capital gain under Section 48 + treaty relief under DTAA + TRC + Form 10F (Form 41 from 1 April 2026).
  7. Specific transaction tax: Section 112A LTCG 12.5% + Section 194-IA 1% TDS if property-adjacent + specific Section 112 / 115AD rates.

/ Document execution

The paperwork.

  • Share Transfer Agreement (STA): specific terms, consideration, representations, warranties.
  • Form SH-4: statutory share transfer deed + stamp duty + witness.
  • Rule 21 pricing certificate: from CA / Merchant Banker.
  • Non-resident party's KYC: passport + address proof + apostille.
  • Board resolution: company approves transfer (Section 56 Companies Act).
  • Form 15CA (Form 145 from 1 April 2026) + 15CB: if consideration flows out of India to non-resident.
  • FIRC: if consideration flows into India.

/ FEMA FC-TRS filing

The 60-day deadline.

  • Within 60 days of share transfer transaction.
  • Filed on FIRMS portal (RBI).
  • Filing responsibility:
  •   — Resident to Non-Resident: by resident.
  •   — Non-Resident to Resident: by resident buyer.
  •   — Non-Resident to Non-Resident: by Indian company.
  • Attach: Rule 21 pricing certificate + consent letters + board resolution.
  • Non-filing / late filing: FEMA Section 13 compounding.

/ Post-transfer compliance

Ongoing.

  1. Update share register + statutory records.
  2. Form MGT-14 if specific resolution required.
  3. Form PAS-3 if new allotment component.
  4. DP account updates for Rule 9B demat shares.
  5. Tax: capital gain disclosure in transferor's ITR + Section 194-IA 1% TDS if property; Section 112A/115AD for listed shares; Form 15CA/15CB for cross-border remittance.
  6. Non-resident transferor's refund of excess Section 195 TDS via Indian ITR.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's the key document in share transfer involving non-resident?
Share Transfer Agreement (STA) + Form SH-4 (statutory deed) + Rule 21 pricing certificate (CA/Merchant Banker) + Form FC-TRS (within 60 days). For cross-border remittance: Form 15CA + 15CB additionally.
What's Rule 21 pricing requirement?
FEMA Rule 21: share issue/transfer to non-resident must be at fair value. Unlisted: DCF method certificate. Listed: closing price. Resident cannot sell cheap to non-resident; cannot buy expensive from non-resident. CA / Merchant Banker certifies - must be in file before FC-TRS filing.
Does Section 194-IA apply to share transfer?
No - Section 194-IA is 1% TDS on sale of immovable property above INR 50 lakh. Share transfer is governed by Section 195 (if non-resident) or Section 194A/C/J (specific sections). Section 112A LTCG 12.5% on listed equity (post Finance Act 2024). Specific rate per asset type + residence.
What if FC-TRS was missed historically?
FEMA voluntary compounding under Section 13 - typical INR 10,000-50,000 per instance. Materially cheaper than RBI inspection. BQP integrated FEMA compounding service for historical FC-TRS misses.
