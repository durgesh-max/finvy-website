/ Resident property sellers · Capital gains · Updated 2026-11-28

Tax on Property Sale in India 2026: 12.5% LTCG or 20% With Indexation, How to Calculate the Profit, 1% TDS and How to Save Tax, hold more than two years and the rate drops to 12.5%.

Profit on selling a house, flat or land held for more than 24 months is taxed at 12.5% without indexation; if you bought it before 23 July 2024, a resident individual or HUF can instead pay 20% with indexation, whichever is lower. Property sold within 24 months is taxed at your slab rate. The buyer deducts 1% TDS when the price is ₹50 lakh or more, and the gain can be saved by buying another house or investing in capital gains bonds.

/ How to save tax on property sale

Exemptions and planning.

  • Buy another house (section 54, now section 82): reinvest the long-term gain on a residential house in a new house within 1 year before or 2 years after the sale (3 years if constructing), up to ₹10 crore; see section 54 rules.
  • Sold land or a commercial property (section 54F): invest the full net sale proceeds in a residential house to exempt the gain proportionately.
  • Capital gains bonds (54EC, now section 85): invest up to ₹50 lakh within 6 months in specified bonds with a 5-year lock-in; see 54EC bonds.
  • Not ready to buy before filing: deposit the amount in the Capital Gains Account Scheme before the return due date.
  • Which option fits: see the 54 vs 54F vs 54EC decision tree.
  • Keep records: purchase deed, stamp duty receipts, improvement bills and the sale deed support the cost and the gain.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How much tax do I pay on selling a property in India?
If held for more than 24 months, 12.5% of the gain without indexation; residents who bought before 23 July 2024 can choose 20% with indexation if that is lower. If held for 24 months or less, the gain is taxed at your slab rate.
How do I calculate profit on a property sale?
Sale price (or stamp duty value, if it exceeds the price by more than 10%) minus the cost of acquisition (purchase price plus stamp duty, registration and brokerage paid on purchase), improvement costs and selling expenses such as brokerage on the sale; for indexation, the cost is adjusted with the cost inflation index.
Is TDS deducted when I sell property?
Yes. A resident buyer deducts 1% TDS under section 194-IA when the price is Rs 50 lakh or more and deposits it against your PAN; you claim it in your return.
How can I avoid tax on property sale?
Reinvest the gain in a residential house under section 54 (section 82 of the 2025 Act), invest the net proceeds under 54F if you sold land or commercial property, or buy up to Rs 50 lakh of 54EC bonds within 6 months.
How is a joint development agreement taxed for the landowner?
Where an individual or HUF landowner signs a registered joint development agreement, section 45(5A) taxes the capital gain in the year the completion certificate for the project is issued, not when the agreement is signed or possession is handed over. The sale consideration is the stamp duty value, on that date, of the landowner's share in the project plus any cash received, and the developer deducts TDS on the cash part. If the landowner transfers their share before the completion certificate, this deferral is lost and the general rules apply; for a landowner that is a company or firm, the gain can arise earlier, typically when possession is handed over under the agreement, while courts have held that an unregistered agreement does not by itself trigger a transfer. The flats received then have a cost equal to the value taxed, which matters when they are sold later, and reinvestment reliefs under sections 54 / 54F may help. GST can also apply to the construction service on the landowner's share. Book a call before signing the agreement to structure it.
How is tax calculated when a jointly owned property is sold?
Each co-owner is taxed separately on their own share of the gain, in the ratio of ownership shown in the deed (or, where the deed is silent, the share each one paid for). Each works out their own cost, holding period and, for land or buildings bought before 23 July 2024, their own choice between 12.5% without indexation and 20% with indexation, and each can claim section 54, 54EC or 54F on their share. The buyer deducts TDS separately against each seller's PAN if the total sale price is INR 50 lakh or more, even when each share is below that. Book a call to work out each co-owner's tax before the deed is signed.
How is a flat owner taxed when the housing society is redeveloped?
It depends on what the agreement gives you. The new, usually larger flat received in place of the old one, the rent paid for temporary accommodation, and any corpus or hardship amount are each looked at separately, and tribunals have not treated them all the same way, so the wording of the development agreement matters. The issues to settle are whether anything is taxable in the year of the agreement or handover, how rent and corpus are treated, and what cost and holding period apply when the new flat is later sold. Book a call with the redevelopment agreement before you sign or file the return.
Is GST payable on an under-construction flat?
Yes. GST applies when you pay for a residential flat before the completion certificate is issued: 1% for affordable housing (carpet area up to 60 square metres in metro cities or 90 square metres elsewhere, and value up to INR 45 lakh) and 5% for other residential flats, without input tax credit for the builder. No GST is payable on a ready-to-move flat bought after the completion certificate, or on a resale flat. Stamp duty and registration are separate state levies. Book a call to check the GST and TDS on your purchase.
Is the 20% indexation option available to NRIs?
No. The option of 20% with indexation for property bought before 23 July 2024 is available only to resident individuals and HUFs; NRIs pay 12.5% without indexation.
