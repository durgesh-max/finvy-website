/ TDS · Updated 2026-10-30

Section 194Q TDS on Purchase of Goods: Complete Guide for 2026, thresholds, timing, rates and the end of the 206C(1H) overlap.

Section 194Q TDS applies when a buyer with turnover above INR 10 crore buys goods worth more than INR 50 lakh in a year from one resident seller: 0.1% on the excess. The old overlap with sellers' TCS under Section 206C(1H) ended when that TCS was discontinued from 1 April 2025.

/ The rule

Who, when, how much.

  • Who deducts: a buyer whose turnover exceeded INR 10 crore in the preceding financial year.
  • When: on purchases of goods from a resident seller exceeding INR 50 lakh in the financial year.
  • Rate: 0.1% on the amount above INR 50 lakh, at credit or payment, whichever is earlier.
  • Seller without PAN: 5%.
  • Not applicable: imports from non-resident sellers, and transactions where TDS under another provision or TCS (other than on sale of goods) applies.

/ Worked example

How the threshold works.

A buyer with last year's turnover of INR 40 crore buys goods worth INR 80 lakh from one supplier during the year. TDS applies on INR 30 lakh (the excess over INR 50 lakh) at 0.1%: INR 3,000. If the supplier has no PAN, the rate is 5%: INR 1.5 lakh.

/ Common mistakes

What to avoid.

  • Deducting on the full purchase value instead of only the excess over INR 50 lakh.
  • Counting the threshold across all suppliers instead of per supplier.
  • Still asking suppliers to confirm they will not collect 206C(1H) TCS; that provision no longer applies.
  • Applying the omitted Section 206AB higher rate.

Sources: VJM Global: amendments from 1 April 2025; CalcGuru: 194Q vs 206C(1H) after the change; Income Tax Department: Section 206AB omitted.

Last updated: 30 October 2026. From 1 April 2026, TDS and TCS provisions sit in Sections 393 and 394 of the Income-tax Act, 2025; the rules described here carried over.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

When does Section 194Q apply?
When the buyer's turnover in the preceding year exceeded INR 10 crore and purchases from one resident seller exceed INR 50 lakh in the year.
What is the rate under 194Q?
0.1% on the amount above INR 50 lakh; 5% if the seller has no PAN.
Is there still a 194Q and 206C(1H) overlap?
No. TCS under Section 206C(1H) was discontinued from 1 April 2025.
