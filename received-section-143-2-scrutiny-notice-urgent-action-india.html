/ What Section 143(2) means

The scrutiny trigger.

  • Section 143(2): Assessing Officer selects ITR for detailed scrutiny under Section 143(3).
  • Selection: CBDT risk-based framework (CASS - Computer Aided Scrutiny Selection), specific-criteria selection, manual selection by AO.
  • Time limit for AO to serve notice: 3 months from end of FY in which ITR filed. For FY 2025-26 ITR (filed by Oct 2026): notice by 30 June 2026 last date.
  • Once served: full scrutiny under Section 143(3) + 6-month assessment window + specific response + hearing + order.

/ Immediate action in first 24-72 hours

Preserve + plan.

  1. Acknowledge notice via Income Tax portal.
  2. Note response deadline (typically 15-30 days from notice).
  3. Preserve ALL records relevant to the AY: ITR + supporting docs + bank statements + Form 26AS (Form 168 from tax year 2026-27) + AIS/TIS + specific agreements + property / investment / share transaction evidence.
  4. Review ITR filed vs AO's likely 'reason for scrutiny' - what triggered selection.
  5. Engage CA immediately - 15-30 days is tight for substantive response.
  6. Identify specific risks: Section 148 reassessment if historical issue surfaces, Section 270A penalty exposure, specific sector-specific risks.

/ Response strategy

What to submit.

  • Reconciliation of income: ITR Schedule-by-Schedule reconciliation with Form 26AS + AIS/TIS. Explain any mismatches with supporting evidence.
  • Documentary refutation: specific receipts + bank statements + agreements + specific transaction evidence for each income head.
  • Legal position memos: for complex matters - DTAA treaty rate, Section 54 reinvestment, Section 115BAA opt-in, specific sector deduction, Section 56(2)(x) relative exemption, specific exemption claims.
  • Transfer pricing documentation: if cross-border transactions (Form 3CEB [Form 48 from tax year 2026-27] + benchmarking study).
  • Books of accounts: for business income - audited books + specific trial balance + specific GL extracts.
  • Specific exemption claims: DPIIT Startup India, Section 80-IAC, Section 10AA SEZ, Section 35 R&D - specific supporting documentation.

/ Hearing + order + penalty exposure

The subsequent path.

  • AO holds hearings (video / in-person) during 6-month window.
  • Specific document calls + specific issue drill-down.
  • Additions proposed - taxpayer response + specific objections.
  • Section 143(3) order within 6 months - specific additions / disallowances / adjustments.
  • Section 270A penalty: 50% under-reporting (good faith) or 200% misreporting (fraud / wilful concealment).
  • Section 234A/B/C interest on specific tax shortfall.
  • CIT(A) appeal within 30 days if adverse.
  • ITAT appeal if CIT(A) adverse.
  • High Court / Supreme Court for substantial questions of law.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's the response window for Section 143(2) notice?
Typically 15-30 days from notice service as specified. Can request extension on specific reasoning. Critical - first response establishes the record. No response leads to AO's version prevailing + specific adverse presumptions.
Can Section 143(2) scrutiny lead to Section 148 reassessment?
Yes - if during 143(3) scrutiny AO finds historical income escaping assessment in prior years, Section 148 reassessment can be initiated for those prior years (subject to 4-year or 10-year limits). Current-year scrutiny can expand to multi-year review.
What's the typical Section 143(3) assessment timeline?
6 months from notice date (or extended per Finance Act specific extensions). Multiple hearings during window. Order typically issued within 6 months. Specific documents + oral representations evaluated.
What's the penalty if additions sustained?
Section 270A: 50% under-reporting (good faith/arithmetic error) OR 200% misreporting (fraud/wilful concealment). Classification is key battle. Section 270AA voluntary disclosure can mitigate 200% to lower rate. Section 234A/B/C interest additional.
