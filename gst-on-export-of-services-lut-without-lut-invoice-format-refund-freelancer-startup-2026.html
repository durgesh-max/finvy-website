/ Startups and freelancers · GST · Export of services · Updated 2026-11-23

GST on Export of Services and LUT Filing on the GST Portal (2026): LUT vs Paying IGST, Exporting Without an LUT, Invoice Format and Refunds, zero GST, if you get five conditions and one form right.

No GST is payable on a genuine export of services from India: it is a zero-rated supply. If the client is outside India, the place of supply is outside India and you are paid in foreign currency, a GST-registered exporter files a Letter of Undertaking (LUT, Form RFD-11) once a year and invoices without IGST, then claims back GST paid on inputs. Without an LUT, you must charge 18% IGST on the export invoice and claim it back as a refund, which ties up cash. The invoice must carry the export endorsement, and under the LUT the money must arrive within one year or IGST becomes payable with interest. Freelancers below INR 20 lakh a year need not register at all. Since 30 March 2026, intermediary services for foreign clients can also be exports.

/ Quick answers

The questions exporters ask first.

  • Is there GST on export of services? No GST is collected on a valid export. It is zero-rated under section 16 of the IGST Act, and you can claim a refund of input tax credit.
  • Do I need an LUT? Yes, if you are registered and want to invoice without GST. Without it, you charge 18% IGST and claim it back.
  • Can I export services without an LUT? Yes, by paying IGST on each export invoice and claiming a refund of that IGST. It works, but your money is blocked until the refund comes.
  • Do I need GST registration to export services? Not if your aggregate turnover is up to INR 20 lakh in the year (INR 10 lakh in special category states). Exports count towards that turnover.
  • What must the invoice say? The export endorsement (below), the client’s name and address and the country, and the amount in foreign currency or with the exchange rate.

/ The five conditions

When a service counts as an export (section 2(6), IGST Act).

ConditionWhat it means in practice
1. Supplier in IndiaYou, your firm or your Indian company
2. Recipient outside IndiaThe contracting client is located abroad
3. Place of supply outside IndiaUsually the client’s location; not for services on goods or immovable property in India, or events in India. Intermediary services follow the general rule from 30 March 2026
4. Paid in convertible foreign exchangeOr in rupees where the RBI permits, such as Special Rupee Vostro accounts. PayPal, Wise or Payoneer receipts converted into India count, with the bank or platform advice as proof
5. Not two establishments of one entityAn Indian branch billing its foreign head office is not an export. An Indian subsidiary billing its foreign parent is a separate person and can export

If any condition fails, the supply is taxable in India at the applicable rate (usually 18%). Indian sourcing agents and back-offices: see the intermediary change from 30 March 2026.

  • File the LUT before the first export invoice of the financial year. It is online on the GST portal, takes minutes and is valid for that year only. If you have already exported this year without one, take advice before filing: the department can treat those invoices as supplies on payment of IGST.
  • Refund timing: since 1 October 2025, 90% of refund claims for zero-rated supplies are sanctioned provisionally where the system rates the claim low-risk (Rule 91(2); CBIC Instruction 06/2025-GST). The balance follows after verification.
  • Exports under LUT still go in GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B as zero-rated supplies; a nil tax amount does not mean nil reporting.

/ Invoice format

What an export invoice must show.

  • Your name, address and GSTIN; a serial number and date.
  • The client’s name and address, and the name of the country.
  • A description of the service and its SAC code.
  • The value, in foreign currency, with the rupee value at the exchange rate used.
  • IGST rate and amount (nil under LUT).
  • The endorsement under LUT: “SUPPLY MEANT FOR EXPORT/SUPPLY TO SEZ UNIT OR SEZ DEVELOPER FOR AUTHORISED OPERATIONS UNDER BOND OR LETTER OF UNDERTAKING WITHOUT PAYMENT OF INTEGRATED TAX”.
  • The endorsement when IGST is paid: “SUPPLY MEANT FOR EXPORT/SUPPLY TO SEZ UNIT OR SEZ DEVELOPER FOR AUTHORISED OPERATIONS ON PAYMENT OF INTEGRATED TAX”.
  • Signature or digital signature of the supplier.

The endorsement wording is from the proviso to Rule 46 of the CGST Rules. Many exporters also quote the LUT reference number (ARN) on the invoice.

/ Income tax and FEMA

The other two laws on the same receipt.

  • Income tax: export income is taxable in India like any other business income. Freelancers can often use the presumptive scheme; see tax for freelancers and consultants.
  • FEMA: the 2026 export regulations require service export proceeds within nine months; since 2 November 2026 the EDF filing is deemed done for individuals, and for companies up to INR 10 lakh per service. See the EDF relief.
  • Note the two clocks: GST allows one year for payment under the LUT; FEMA expects realisation within nine months.
  • Indian subsidiaries billing a foreign parent: exports under LUT, plus transfer pricing on the price; see safe harbour vs APA.
  • US clients: they usually ask for Form W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E; see US company paying an Indian contractor.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is GST applicable on export of services from India?
No GST is collected on a valid export of services. It is a zero-rated supply: you either export under a Letter of Undertaking without IGST, or pay IGST and claim it back. You can also claim a refund of GST paid on inputs.
Is GST applicable outside India?
GST is India's tax on supplies whose place of supply is in India; it is not charged on sales consumed abroad. Exports of goods and services are zero-rated, so they leave India without GST (under an LUT) or with a refund. Going the other way, Indian businesses pay GST on imported services under reverse charge, and foreign companies selling online services such as software, streaming or cloud subscriptions to Indian consumers must register and charge 18% IGST.
Does India have GST like VAT in other countries?
Yes. India's Goods and Services Tax, in force since 1 July 2017, is a destination-based VAT: CGST and SGST on supplies within a state, IGST on supplies between states and on imports. Since 22 September 2025 most goods and services are taxed at 5% or 18%, with 40% on luxury and sin goods.
Can I export services without an LUT?
Yes. You charge 18% IGST on the export invoice, pay it, and claim a refund of that IGST. The LUT route avoids blocking cash, so most exporters file it each year.
How is GST refund on export of services calculated?
Under an LUT, the refund is of unutilised input tax credit using the rule 89(4) formula: turnover of zero-rated supply of services multiplied by net input tax credit, divided by adjusted total turnover; for services, zero-rated turnover is based on payments received in the period, adjusted for advances. The claim is filed in Form RFD-01 with the invoice statement and bank or FIRA evidence, within two years from the relevant date, which for services is generally the date the foreign-exchange payment is received.
GST refund on export with payment of tax or without payment of tax: which is better?
Without payment (under an LUT) no IGST is charged on the export invoice and you later claim refund of accumulated input tax credit; this avoids paying tax first and is the usual route for service exporters. With payment, you pay IGST on the export, often from input tax credit, and claim refund of the IGST paid. Both routes go through Form RFD-01 for services, and provisional refund of up to 90% is available for zero-rated supplies on a risk-based assessment.
When should the LUT be filed for 2026-27?
Before the first export invoice of the financial year. It is filed online in Form GST RFD-11 and is valid for that financial year only.
How do I file an LUT on the GST portal?
Log in to the GST portal, go to Services, User Services, Furnish Letter of Undertaking (LUT), choose the financial year, tick the declarations, enter the names and addresses of two independent witnesses, and sign with a DSC or EVC (OTP). The portal generates an ARN and the LUT acknowledgement immediately, so you can start invoicing exports without IGST. Quote the LUT reference on export invoices, and file a fresh LUT each financial year. A person prosecuted for tax evasion above INR 2.5 crore cannot use an LUT and must give a bond instead.
What is the invoice endorsement for export of services under LUT?
SUPPLY MEANT FOR EXPORT/SUPPLY TO SEZ UNIT OR SEZ DEVELOPER FOR AUTHORISED OPERATIONS UNDER BOND OR LETTER OF UNDERTAKING WITHOUT PAYMENT OF INTEGRATED TAX, as required by the proviso to Rule 46 of the CGST Rules.
Does a freelancer need GST registration for foreign clients?
Not if aggregate turnover, including exports, is up to INR 20 lakh in the financial year (INR 10 lakh in special category states). Above that, registration is required, and an LUT lets you invoice without GST.
Is GST charged on foreign inward remittance?
Not on the money itself. Receiving a foreign remittance is not a supply; when it is payment for services you exported, the export is zero-rated, so under an LUT no GST is charged. Money from family abroad is not a supply either. The only GST is the 18% the bank charges on its own conversion or remittance fee, worked out on a small notional value of the conversion, not on the whole amount.
Is GST charged on outward remittance from India?
Not on the money sent. For an individual sending money abroad, the only GST is 18% on the bank's own charges - its remittance fee and the currency conversion, which is valued on the same notional Rule 32(2)(b) scale, not on the full amount. Remittances under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme also attract TCS once they cross Rs 10 lakh in a financial year, at a rate that depends on the purpose, and that TCS can be claimed against income tax. When a business pays a foreign vendor for services such as software, consulting or advertising, it must pay 18% IGST itself under reverse charge on the value of the import and can usually claim it back as input tax credit; the payment may also need withholding tax and Forms 145 / 146 (old 15CA / 15CB). How is GST on foreign currency conversion calculated?
GST at 18% is charged on a notional value of the conversion, not on the full amount. Under Rule 32(2)(b) of the CGST Rules, that value is 1% of the amount up to INR 1 lakh (minimum INR 250); INR 1,000 plus 0.5% of the amount between INR 1 lakh and INR 10 lakh; and INR 5,500 plus 0.1% of the amount above INR 10 lakh, capped at INR 60,000. So converting INR 5 lakh means GST on INR 3,000, about INR 540.
What is the GST rate on software services and does it apply to software exports?
Software development, SaaS and IT services are taxed at 18% when supplied in India. Software or IT services exported to a foreign client and paid for in foreign exchange are zero-rated, so no GST is charged under an LUT. OIDAR (online information database access and retrieval) services sold by foreign companies to Indian consumers are taxed at 18% in India.
Do I need a FIRC or FIRA for export of services?
Keep the bank's foreign inward remittance advice (FIRA, often called e-FIRC) or bank realisation record for each client payment. It proves the money arrived in convertible foreign exchange (or permitted rupees), which is one of the conditions for treating the service as an export and for any GST refund.
What happens if the foreign client pays late?
Under an LUT, if payment is not received within one year of the invoice (or an extended period allowed), IGST becomes payable with interest. FEMA separately expects realisation within nine months.
