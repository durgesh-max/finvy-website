Form FC-GPR Filing Checklist India 2026, 30-day deadline + checklist.

Form FC-GPR (Foreign Currency - Gross Provisional Return) is mandatory FEMA filing by Indian company receiving foreign inward FDI remittance: within 30 days of share allotment post remittance. Non-filing attracts FEMA Section 13 compounding. Here's the complete 2026 filing checklist.

/ Who must file + when

The core rule.

  • Indian company receives foreign inward remittance for share subscription (FDI).
  • Share allotment made to foreign subscriber.
  • Form FC-GPR filed within 30 days of allotment via FIRMS portal (RBI).
  • Non-filing or late filing: FEMA Section 13 compounding (voluntary) or RBI inspection + penalty up to 3x amount involved.

/ Required documents

The attachments.

  • FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate) from AD bank.
  • KYC of foreign subscriber (passport + address proof).
  • Board resolution authorising allotment.
  • Share allotment letter.
  • Pricing certification: CA / Merchant Banker certificate confirming price per Rule 21 Pricing Guidelines (DCF for unlisted; closing price for listed).
  • Non-resident declaration of eligibility.
  • CS certificate of compliance (where applicable).

/ Filing workflow

Step-by-step.

  1. Collect foreign shareholder KYC + apostilled documents pre-allotment.
  2. Verify FDI route: automatic vs approval; sector cap check.
  3. Press Note 3 beneficial ownership check for land-bordering country investments.
  4. Receive foreign remittance in AD-I bank account. Obtain FIRC.
  5. Allot shares via board resolution within 60 days of remittance (Rule 2A Companies Act).
  6. Issue share certificates + file Form PAS-3 within 30 days.
  7. Obtain pricing certificate from CA / Merchant Banker.
  8. File Form FC-GPR via FIRMS portal within 30 days of allotment.
  9. AD bank reviews + confirms successful filing.
  10. Preserve filing acknowledgement + all supporting documents 8+ years.

/ Common mistakes + fixes

What goes wrong.

  • Missed 30-day deadline: FEMA compounding under Section 13 - INR 10,000-50,000 per instance typically.
  • Pricing below Rule 21 guidelines: FEMA breach - compounding + possible unwinding.
  • Press Note 3 breach: beneficial ownership from land-bordering country without approval - serious compounding + unwinding risk.
  • Share allotment delay beyond 60 days: separate FEMA breach (Rule 2A pre-amendment was 180 days; now 60 days).
  • FIRC not obtained: cannot file FC-GPR without FIRC. Chase AD bank.
  • Wrong pricing method: unlisted uses DCF (Rule 21); listed uses closing price. Methodology must match Rule 21.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

When is Form FC-GPR due?
Within 30 days of share allotment to foreign subscriber. Allotment itself must be within 60 days of remittance receipt (Rule 2A Companies Act). So typical timeline: remittance → 60 days → allotment → 30 days → FC-GPR filing. Total 90 days from remittance to FC-GPR.
What's the penalty for late FC-GPR?
FEMA Section 13 compounding (voluntary): typically INR 10,000-50,000 per instance for minor delay. For prolonged non-filing or sector-cap breach: significantly higher, up to 3x amount involved. Voluntary compounding is cheaper than RBI inspection-driven proceedings.
What's Rule 21 pricing guidelines?
FEMA Rule 21 (Non-debt Instrument Rules 2019 + predecessors): share issue to non-resident must be at fair value. Unlisted: DCF method (discounted cash flow) by Merchant Banker or Chartered Accountant. Listed: closing price. Issue below Rule 21 pricing is FEMA breach.
