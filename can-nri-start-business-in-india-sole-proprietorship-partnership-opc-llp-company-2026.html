Can an NRI Start a Business in India? (2026) Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, OPC, LLP or Private Limited Company Compared, for NRIs in the US, UAE, UK, Singapore, Canada and Australia.

Yes. An NRI or OCI can own a business in India while living abroad, and the choice of structure decides how the money can move. A sole proprietorship or partnership firm can take an NRI's capital only on a non-repatriation basis (treated as domestic money, with no limit) unless the RBI approves otherwise. It cannot be in agriculture or plantations, real estate business or print media. Since 1 April 2021 an Indian citizen living abroad can form a One Person Company (OPC). An LLP needs at least one designated partner resident in India (120 days in the financial year). A private limited company needs one director resident in India (182 days in the financial year) and is the usual choice when profits or exit proceeds must go abroad: most sectors allow 100% foreign investment under the automatic route. Indian business income is taxed in India; NRIs living in the US also report the Indian entity to the IRS, and UAE-based NRIs should check India's deemed residence rule.

/ Working from abroad

What NRIs in the US and the Gulf ask.

  • While working in the USA: owning an Indian business is generally allowed, but actively working in it can conflict with some US visas (for example H-1B or F-1); take US immigration advice before you take an active role.
  • US reporting: a US tax resident who owns 10% or more of an Indian company usually files Form 5471 (Form 8865 for a partnership, Form 8858 for an entity treated as disregarded), plus FBAR and Form 8938 for Indian accounts, and the controlled foreign corporation rules can tax undistributed profits. Indian tax paid is creditable under the India-US treaty. See FBAR basics.
  • UAE and other no-tax countries: an Indian citizen with Indian income above INR 15 lakh who is not liable to tax in any other country can be treated as resident (not ordinarily resident) in India; see deemed residence for UAE NRIs.
  • Signing from abroad: directors and designated partners need a DIN and a Class 3 digital signature; documents signed abroad are usually notarised and apostilled. See DIN and digital signature from abroad.
  • Funding the business: equity, or a loan from you as director or shareholder under the 2026 ECB rules; see loans from NRI directors.

/ Tax and registrations

The first-year list.

  • Tax rates: a proprietorship is taxed at the NRI's individual slab rates; a domestic company can opt for 22% plus surcharge and cess (25.168%) under section 115BAA; an LLP pays 30% plus surcharge and cess. See LLP vs private limited.
  • Registrations: PAN, GST (if turnover crosses the threshold or for inter-state or export supplies), Udyam (MSME), shop and establishment licence, import-export code for trade, and a business bank account (banks' requirements for non-resident proprietors differ).
  • FEMA filings: none for non-repatriable capital in a firm or proprietorship; FC-GPR within 30 days of share allotment and the annual FLA return for a company with foreign investment. See FC-GPR checklist.
  • Annual compliance: income-tax return and tax audit where applicable; for companies and LLPs, ROC annual filings (AOC-4, MGT-7 or Form 8 and Form 11).

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can an NRI start a business in India?
Yes. An NRI can own a sole proprietorship or partnership on a non-repatriation basis, form a One Person Company (Indian citizens only), or set up an LLP or private limited company with repatriable foreign investment in most sectors.
Can an NRI open a sole proprietorship in India?
Yes, with capital on a non-repatriation basis under Schedule IV of the FEMA Non-Debt Instruments Rules, as long as the business is not agriculture or plantations, real estate business or print media. Repatriable investment in a proprietorship needs RBI approval.
Can an NRI become a partner in a partnership firm, and how is the firm registered?
Yes. An NRI or OCI can contribute capital to an Indian partnership firm on a non-repatriation basis under FEMA, except in barred activities such as agricultural or plantation work, real estate business and print media; repatriable foreign investment needs an LLP or company instead. The firm is formed by a partnership deed and gets its own PAN. Registration with the state Registrar of Firms under the Indian Partnership Act, 1932 is optional, but an unregistered firm cannot sue to enforce its contracts, so most firms register through the state portal with the stamped deed, partner IDs and address proof.
Can an NRI form a One Person Company?
Yes. Since 1 April 2021 an Indian citizen, whether resident in India or not, can incorporate an OPC. OCI cardholders and foreign citizens cannot; they can use a private limited company instead.
Can an NRI start a business in India while working in the USA?
Owning one is generally fine, but an active role can conflict with some US visas, so take US immigration advice. A US tax resident also reports the Indian entity to the IRS (Form 5471, 8865 or 8858) and files FBAR for Indian accounts.
Which structure is best for an NRI who wants profits abroad?
Usually a private limited company (or an LLP in sectors open to automatic FDI) funded with repatriable investment, so dividends and exit proceeds can be remitted. It needs one director resident in India for 182 days in the financial year.
