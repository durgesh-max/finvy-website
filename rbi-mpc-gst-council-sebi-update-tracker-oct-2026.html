/ RBI Monetary Policy update

Interest rate + liquidity framework.

  • RBI MPC continues data-dependent monetary policy stance.
  • Repo rate decisions impact: borrowing cost, bond yields, FX hedging cost, deposit rates.
  • Specific liquidity management framework changes impact banking sector + corporate treasury operations.
  • External Commercial Borrowings (ECB) framework: specific sector allocations + hedging mandates.
  • FEMA LRS + Form A2 updates track RBI master directions.
  • Scale-Based Regulation (SBR) framework for NBFCs continues implementation.

/ GST Council updates

Rate + clarification framework.

  • Periodic GST Council meetings issue rate clarifications + sector-specific guidance.
  • Recent focus areas: online gaming 28% framework fine-tuning, specific export of service clarifications, OIDAR classification, specific HSN rate rationalisation.
  • GSTR-9 + GSTR-9C FY 2024-25 deadline 31 December 2026 - any extension / amendment announcements tracked.
  • Section 43B(h) MSE payment year 2 impact clarifications.
  • GST Appellate Tribunal (GSTAT) operationalisation + specific procedure updates.

/ SEBI consultation papers

Capital markets regulation.

  • Periodic SEBI consultation papers on specific capital markets reforms.
  • Recent focus: SME IPO framework refinements, LODR Regulations updates, specific disclosure requirements, SBEB (ESOP) Regulations.
  • SEBI AIF Regulations: periodic updates on fund structuring, LP-GP agreements, specific disclosure.
  • SEBI research analyst / investment adviser regulation.
  • GIFT City IFSCA interface + specific cross-regulatory framework.

/ MeitY + DPDP + AI regulation

Technology sector.

  • DPDP Rules expected to finalise around Budget 2026 timeframe.
  • MeitY periodic AI advisories: deepfake labelling, large model deployment, intermediary rules refinement.
  • Digital India Act (DIA) draft under consultation - comprehensive digital governance framework.
  • IndiaAI Mission continues implementation + specific compute + model development support.
  • Section 69A IT Act specific content takedown framework updates.

/ CBDT notifications + clarifications

Direct tax specifics.

  • Periodic CBDT notifications on specific interpretations.
  • Recent focus: Form 10F electronic filing clarifications, Section 115BBH VDA (crypto) specific interpretations, Section 194R (business perquisite) industry-specific CBDT circulars, Section 194S (crypto TDS) clarifications.
  • Rule 11UA valuation framework updates.
  • Section 148 reassessment procedural clarifications.
  • DGIT Investigation specific framework.
  • Form 3CEB TP audit specific clarifications.
