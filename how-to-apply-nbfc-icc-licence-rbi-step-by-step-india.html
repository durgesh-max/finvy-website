NBFC-ICC (Investment and Credit Company) RBI licence for lending + specific financial services: 6-12 month application + approval process. / Pre-application preparation

What to assemble.

  • Indian company (Pvt Ltd) with specific NBFC-eligible object clause in MOA.
  • Minimum net worth INR 2 crore (NBFC-ICC base; higher for NBFC-ND-SI).
  • Promoter + director 'fit and proper' documentation.
  • Business plan: lending segment + risk framework + customer target + geography.
  • IT infrastructure: core lending platform + data localisation per RBI requirements.
  • CTO / CRO / Compliance Officer identified.
  • Capital adequacy structuring + Tier 1 + Tier 2 capital planning.
  • FDI structure if foreign shareholder (Pricing Rule 21 + FEMA FDI compliance).

/ Application via Form NBFC-RBI

The RBI filing.

  1. Online application via RBI's COSMOS portal (NBFC application module).
  2. Form NBFC-RBI: comprehensive application form.
  3. Attach: incorporation certificate, MOA/AOA, net worth certificate (CA-signed), promoter + director profiles, business plan, system architecture, CTO/CRO/Compliance Officer appointment letters.
  4. Application fee + specific state fees.
  5. CA/legal certification on specific aspects.

/ RBI review + examination

6-12 month cycle.

  • RBI pre-screening (1-2 months): completeness of application.
  • Promoter + director background check + fit-and-proper review.
  • Specific industry background verification.
  • Capital adequacy + risk framework review.
  • IT infrastructure inspection + security assessment.
  • RBI typically visits applicant's premises for specific validation.
  • Response to RBI queries (iterative).
  • In-principle approval if satisfactory - typically 6-12 months from application.

/ Post-in-principle approval + Certificate of Registration

The final step.

  1. In-principle approval valid 1 year - applicant must demonstrate operational readiness.
  2. CTO + CRO + Compliance Officer formally appointed.
  3. Lending platform operational.
  4. Specific risk management + ALM policies finalised.
  5. RBI inspection for Certificate of Registration (CoR) issuance.
  6. CoR issued - NBFC can commence operations.
  7. Ongoing compliance: monthly / quarterly RBI returns + annual Audit Report + Scale-Based Regulation (SBR) classification monitoring.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How long does NBFC licence take?
6-12 months from application to Certificate of Registration (CoR). Faster (4-6 months) if application well-documented + no RBI queries. Slower (12-18 months) if specific regulatory concerns + extended Q&A. In-principle approval precedes CoR - typically 6-9 months to in-principle.
What's the minimum net worth?
NBFC-ICC: INR 2 crore minimum. NBFC-ND-SI (Systemically Important Non-Deposit): INR 10 crore. NBFC-MFI (Microfinance): INR 5 crore urban / INR 2 crore rural. NBFC-AA (Account Aggregator): INR 2 crore. Specific category requires specific minimum. BQP evaluates optimal category per business.
Can foreign shareholder own NBFC?
Yes - FDI permitted up to specified sector cap (100% in most NBFC categories post 2016 reform). Pricing per Rule 21 Non-Debt Instrument Rules. FC-GPR filing within 30 days of allotment. Press Note 3 (eased for non-controlling stakes within 10% by Press Note 2 (2026)) beneficial ownership verification. BQP integrates FEMA FDI + NBFC licence.
What's RBI SBR (Scale-Based Regulation)?
RBI's Scale-Based Regulatory framework (2021): classifies NBFCs into Base Layer / Middle Layer / Upper Layer / Top Layer based on size + systemic importance. Additional compliance obligations for Middle + Upper + Top Layer. Current NBFCs placed in specific layers based on asset size + specific criteria.
