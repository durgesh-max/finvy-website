FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can a foreign company hire an Indian CA remotely without visiting India?
Yes. All Indian CA services for foreign clients — from WOS incorporation to annual audit to Form 15CB signing to profit repatriation — can be delivered 100% remotely. Data room access, management calls on Zoom, electronic document signing, FEMA filings, Section 195 compliance.
What services do foreign companies most commonly need from an Indian CA?
Setting up Indian subsidiary (WOS Pvt Ltd), Form 15CA/15CB for foreign remittance, due diligence on Indian target companies for M&A, OIDAR GST for foreign SaaS, transfer pricing (Form 3CEB), annual audit for Indian subsidiary, EOR for hiring Indian talent, profit repatriation, Section 195 TDS, India IP registration.
Does BQP work in time zones other than India?
Yes — BQP serves clients across US (PST, EST), UK, EU, Singapore, UAE, Australia. Calls scheduled to accommodate client time zones. Email/document response within 24 hours. Urgent matters (Form 15CB, FEMA deadline, assessment response) handled same-day.

Yes — standard. BQP's DD / tax / FEMA work feeds directly into US / UK / Singapore counsel's SPA / term sheet / closing documentation. Weekly status calls, co-ordinated workstreams, final deliverables formatted for buyer counsel integration.
