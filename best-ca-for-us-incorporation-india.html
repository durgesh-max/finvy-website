/ The full US incorporation service

What a BQP mandate includes.

A standard US incorporation mandate for an Indian founder covers:

  1. Scoping call — free 20-30 minute call where we understand your business, 12-month funding plan, and decide the right entity (Delaware C-Corp vs Wyoming LLC vs Delaware LLC).
  2. State formation — Certificate of Incorporation (C-Corp) or Certificate of Formation (LLC) filed with Delaware or Wyoming Secretary of State.
  3. Registered agent — appointed in-state (required for every US entity).
  4. EIN application — Form SS-4 via fax or international phone for non-SSN founders. Normally 4-8 weeks; faster via phone route.
  5. Governing documents — Operating Agreement (LLC) or Bylaws + Shareholder Agreement (C-Corp).
  6. US bank account introduction — Mercury or Brex, fully remote onboarding from India.
  7. FEMA ODI compliance — Form ODI filed with your Indian authorised dealer bank within 30 days of outward remittance. UIN obtained from RBI.
  8. 83(b) election (if C-Corp with vesting restricted stock) — filed by Certified Mail to the IRS within 30 days of grant.
  9. Year-1 compliance calendar — Delaware franchise tax, Form 5472 + pro forma 1120 (foreign-owned LLC), state registrations.

All of this handled under one engagement with written pricing before kickoff.
