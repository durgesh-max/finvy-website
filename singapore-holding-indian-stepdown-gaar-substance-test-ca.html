/ Why Singapore holdings get scrutinised

The treaty-shopping concern.

  • India-Singapore DTAA historically offered specific advantages (capital gain exemption + specific rates).
  • Capital gains exemption (Article 13) tapered via 2016 Protocol - full exemption till 2017 → specific transition → specific residual benefits.
  • Dividend rate 15% (reduced from 25% domestic).
  • Interest rate 15% + specific exemptions for banks + specific scenarios.
  • Royalty rate 10%.
  • India's GAAR (effective 1 April 2017) + MLI PPT (Covered Tax Agreement effective dates): deny benefits if main purpose = treaty benefit without substance.

/ Economic substance requirements

What CRA/Indian tax authorities look for.

  • Local employees: at least 1-3 Singapore-resident employees (depending on size/activity).
  • Local premises: physical office in Singapore (not virtual / mail-forwarding).
  • Local decision-making: board meetings in Singapore + specific commercial decisions taken locally; Singapore-resident directors majority.
  • Local expenses: specific operating costs in Singapore (salaries, rent, utilities, specific contracts).
  • Local banking: operational bank account(s) in Singapore + specific transaction history.
  • Local books: accounting records + financial statements prepared + audited in Singapore (Singapore FRS).
  • Local tax: Singapore corporate tax 17% paid on specific profits + specific exemptions utilised.
  • Commercial rationale: specific business reason for Singapore holding beyond tax (regional headquarters, specific commercial access, specific operational advantage).

/ GAAR + PPT application

The denial trigger.

  • India Income Tax Section 95-102 GAAR: deny tax benefit if arrangement = Impermissible Avoidance Arrangement.
  • Four tests: (a) main purpose = tax benefit, (b) arrangement lacks commercial substance, (c) created rights/obligations not at arm's length, (d) results in misuse/abuse of provisions.
  • MLI PPT (Multilateral Instrument Principal Purpose Test): applies to India-Singapore DTAA; deny benefit if 'reasonable to conclude' obtaining benefit was one of principal purposes.
  • Specific threshold INR 3 crore tax benefit before GAAR invoked (practical materiality).
  • Specific pre-2010 structures grandfathered.

/ Building defensible substance

The playbook.

  1. Singapore operating cost budget: SGD 300K-500K+ annually for genuine substance (3+ employees + office + specific operations).
  2. Singapore-resident directors (minimum 1 required by Singapore law; recommend 2+ for substance).
  3. Monthly board meetings in Singapore with specific commercial decisions (not just rubber-stamping).
  4. Local banking + specific transaction history (not just dividend pass-through).
  5. Specific regional functions in Singapore: regional sales, treasury, procurement, specific operational role.
  6. Audited financial statements under Singapore FRS + Singapore corporate tax 17% paid.
  7. TRC (Tax Residency Certificate) from IRAS + Form 10F electronic filing in India + Section 90(4) DTAA relief claim.
  8. Co-ordination with Indian subsidiary's compliance: FEMA FC-GPR + MGT-6 for beneficial ownership + annual review.
  9. Documentation: substance file maintained for GAAR + PPT defence (3-7 year retention).

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What makes a Singapore holding GAAR-proof?
Economic substance: 1-3+ local employees + physical office + local decision-making + local banking + Singapore tax paid + commercial rationale beyond tax (regional HQ / treasury / sales). Operational budget SGD 300K-500K+ annually. Specific documentation: substance file for GAAR + PPT defence.
Does India-Singapore DTAA capital gain exemption still work?
Post 2016 Protocol: full capital gain exemption tapered. Residual benefits exist for specific grandfathered acquisitions pre-2017. Current structures: capital gain on share sale by Singapore holding of Indian stepdown largely taxable in India at applicable rates. Specific DTAA relief for interest + dividend + royalty rates remains available.
What's MLI PPT and how does it affect Singapore structures?
Multilateral Instrument Principal Purpose Test: applies to India-Singapore DTAA. Denies treaty benefit if 'reasonable to conclude' obtaining benefit was one of principal purposes of transaction. Lower threshold than GAAR 'main purpose'. Both can be invoked parallel. Substance file + commercial rationale critical for defence.
Does BEPS Pillar 2 affect Singapore holdings?
Yes if MNE group consolidated revenue EUR 750M+. India implemented Pillar 2 from FY 2025-26: QDMTT (Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax) + IIR (Income Inclusion Rule). Singapore corporate tax effective rate 17% should satisfy 15% minimum. Specific QDMTT top-up if specific calculations. BQP + Singapore partner Pillar 2 assessment.
