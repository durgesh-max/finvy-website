GST Input Tax Credit Last Date for FY 2025-26: 30 November 2026 Under Section 16(4) - What to Claim, Reconcile and Fix Before the Cut-Off, credit not claimed by then is lost.

Input tax credit on purchases relating to FY 2025-26 must be claimed by 30 November 2026, or by the date you file the FY 2025-26 annual return (GSTR-9) if that is earlier. This is the rule in section 16(4) of the CGST Act, and credit not claimed by then is lost permanently. For monthly filers, the practical vehicle is the October 2026 GSTR-3B, due on 20 November and acceptable for this purpose if filed by 30 November. Before filing, reconcile your purchase register with GSTR-2B, chase suppliers who have not filed GSTR-1, and claim missed credits and credit notes. Reverse credit on invoices unpaid beyond 180 days. Filing GSTR-9 early closes the window early, so finish the credit review first.

/ Questions businesses ask

Common edge cases.

  • Credit notes and debit notes: the 30 November limit also applies to debit notes relating to FY 2025-26; check the date on the debit note under current rules.
  • Import IGST: claim credit on bills of entry for FY 2025-26 before the cut-off; reconcile with ICEGATE data.
  • Quarterly (QRMP) filers: the return that can carry FY 2025-26 credit must be filed by 30 November 2026; confirm the filing route before the date.
  • Suppliers who never filed: credit generally cannot be claimed; pursue the supplier for tax and keep evidence of payment.
  • After the cut-off: credit cannot be claimed in GSTR-9 itself; the annual return only reports what was claimed.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the last date to claim ITC for FY 2025-26?
30 November 2026, or the date of filing the FY 2025-26 annual return if earlier, under section 16(4) of the CGST Act.
In which return should FY 2025-26 ITC be claimed?
For monthly filers, usually the October 2026 GSTR-3B, due 20 November and accepted for this purpose if filed by 30 November 2026.
Can missed ITC be claimed in GSTR-9?
No. GSTR-9 only reports credit already claimed in returns; credit not claimed by the cut-off is lost.
Does filing GSTR-9 early affect the ITC deadline?
Yes. The window closes on the earlier of 30 November and the date the annual return is filed, so finish the credit review before filing GSTR-9.
What if my supplier has not filed GSTR-1?
The credit generally will not appear in GSTR-2B and cannot be claimed; follow up with the supplier before the cut-off.
