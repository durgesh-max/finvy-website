/ German VAT framework

What applies in Germany.

  • German VAT (Umsatzsteuer): 19% standard rate; 7% reduced rate for specific items.
  • Services from non-EU (e.g., Indian branch providing services to German GmbH): typically reverse charge mechanism (RCM) - German GmbH accounts for VAT on reverse charge.
  • Services from Germany to non-EU customer (B2B): typically zero-rated / out of scope for German VAT.
  • Specific place of supply rules under EU VAT Directive (Articles 44-45).
  • Intra-EU vs non-EU specific treatment.

/ Indian GST framework

What applies in India.

  • Indian GST (CGST + SGST + IGST): 18% standard rate on services; 5% / 12% / 28% specific rates.
  • Export of services (Section 2(6) IGST Act): zero-rated with LUT / bond or with refund of specific unutilised ITC.
  • Place of supply rules (Section 13 IGST Act): specific for cross-border service determination.
  • Services provided by Indian branch to German GmbH (immediate parent): specific analysis - is it export of service (zero-rated) or specific intra-establishment transaction?
  • Section 2(6) IGST export conditions: supplier in India, recipient outside India, place of supply outside India, consideration in convertible foreign exchange, supplier + recipient NOT merely establishments of distinct persons under same person.

/ The intra-establishment issue

Branch to HO treatment.

  • Indian branch + German GmbH head office = typically treated as same legal person.
  • Services between branch and HO: specific Section 2(17) IGST Schedule I 'supply even without consideration' trigger for related-party services.
  • CBIC Circular 161/17/2021 clarified: services provided by Indian branch to foreign HO are export of services if specific conditions met.
  • Zero-rated export status subject to specific documentation.
  • Alternative: services treated as not supply (intra-establishment) - no GST.
  • Specific case-law + CBIC clarifications continue to evolve.

/ Place of supply analysis

Section 13 IGST + Article 44 EU VAT.

  • Indian GST Section 13: services exported if place of supply outside India.
  • Default (Section 13(2)): place of supply = location of recipient.
  • Specific exceptions: real estate (location of property), personal services (location of performance), transportation (specific tests), specific OIDAR / telecommunication rules.
  • German GmbH recipient based in Germany + Indian branch supplier = default place of supply Germany + export of services from India + zero-rated.
  • German VAT reverse charge on recipient side (German GmbH): 19% VAT accounted + recoverable if specific input credit available.

/ Common scenarios

Service patterns.

  • Indian branch provides software development to German GmbH: default export of services (zero-rated) from India + German VAT reverse charge recoverable + no cascading tax.
  • Indian branch provides consultancy to German GmbH: similar export of services treatment subject to specific conditions.
  • Indian branch provides specific services to German GmbH's EU customers: specific analysis - who is contractual recipient + specific flow-through structure.
  • Indian branch procures from Indian vendors to supply to German HO: input credit chain + specific export documentation.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can Indian branch's services to German HO be zero-rated export?
Yes if specific conditions met: supplier in India (branch), recipient outside India (German GmbH), place of supply outside India, consideration in convertible foreign exchange, supplier + recipient NOT merely establishments of same person. CBIC Circular 161 clarified intra-establishment scenarios. Specific documentation + LUT / bond critical.
Does German GmbH pay VAT on Indian branch services?
Reverse charge mechanism: German GmbH accounts for 19% VAT on services received from Indian branch (non-EU supplier). If German GmbH is specific VAT-registered + specific input-credit-eligible: VAT recoverable - net zero tax. If specific partial exempt / non-input-credit: residual VAT cost.
What's the LUT (Letter of Undertaking)?
LUT: filed on GST portal by exporter annually. Allows export of services without payment of IGST + refund-later route. Alternative: pay IGST + claim refund on export (slower). LUT is strongly preferred for IT/services exporters including Indian branches of foreign entities.
Does BEPS Pillar 2 affect German GmbH + Indian branch?
Yes if MNE consolidated revenue EUR 750M+. Germany implemented Pillar 2 from FY 2024 (Mindestbesteuerungsgesetz). India from FY 2025-26. Specific QDMTT + IIR + UTPR. German + Indian effective rate calculations + specific top-up tax exposures. BQP + German partner Pillar 2 assessment.
