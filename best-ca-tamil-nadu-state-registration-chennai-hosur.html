/ Core Tamil Nadu state registrations

Mandatory.

  • Tamil Nadu Shops & Establishments Act: within 30 days of commencement.
  • Tamil Nadu Profession Tax (TNPT): employer + employee + monthly compliance. Max INR 2,500 per employee per year.
  • Tamil Nadu Labour Welfare Fund (TNLWF).
  • Tamil Nadu Factories Act: for establishments meeting specific worker + power thresholds.
  • TNPCB (Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board): CTE / CTO.
  • CMDA (Chennai Metropolitan Development Authority) regulations for Chennai metropolitan.
  • SIPCOT (State Industries Promotion Corporation of Tamil Nadu) if SIPCOT-based.

/ TN-specific industrial advantages

Where TN leads.

  • Auto cluster (Chennai + Hosur): Hyundai + Ford + BMW + Nissan + specific Indian OEMs. Supply chain depth.
  • Electronics (Oragadam + Sriperumbudur): Foxconn + Pegatron + Wistron (now Tata). Mobile phone manufacturing hub.
  • Tamil Nadu Semiconductor Policy 2024: state capex subsidy on top of central ISM. Semiconductor + display cluster near Chennai + Hosur. Specific MOUs with investors.
  • Tamil Nadu Electronics Policy 2024: comprehensive support for electronics manufacturing + ESDM (Electronics System Design and Manufacturing).
  • Chennai port + Ennore port: specific port + logistics ecosystem.
  • SIPCOT industrial parks: Oragadam + Sriperumbudur + Hosur + Krishnagiri + others.
  • Specific IT / ITES: Chennai IT corridor (OMR) + specific state support.

/ Chennai-specific attention

Metro variations.

  • Chennai Corporation Trade Licence: for specific business categories.
  • CMWSSB (Chennai Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board): water + sewerage connection.
  • TANGEDCO: electricity supply (HT connection for commercial / industrial).
  • CMDA building approval + specific zoning regulations.
  • Specific SEZ: MEPZ (Madras Export Processing Zone) + specific sector SEZ.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's Tamil Nadu's strength for manufacturing?
Auto cluster (Chennai + Hosur with Hyundai + Ford + BMW + Nissan ecosystem), Electronics cluster (Oragadam + Sriperumbudur with Foxconn + Pegatron + Wistron), SIPCOT industrial parks, Chennai port + Ennore port, state Semiconductor Policy 2024 + Electronics Policy 2024. Combined central + state incentive stack competitive with Gujarat + Karnataka.
What's SIPCOT and how to get allocation?
State Industries Promotion Corporation of Tamil Nadu - allocates industrial land in SIPCOT parks (Oragadam + Sriperumbudur + Hosur + Krishnagiri + others). Specific investment commitment + sector match required. Online application + specific priority for strategic sectors. BQP coordinates application.
What's Tamil Nadu Semiconductor Policy 2024 incentive?
State capex subsidy on top of central ISM Modified SPECS - specific semiconductor + display cluster near Chennai. Combined with Section 115BAB central (15% corporate tax) + ISM central (up to 50% capex subsidy) + state subsidy = among the most aggressive semiconductor incentive stacks.
Can I get Tamil Nadu Industrial Policy incentive even without SIPCOT?
Yes - Tamil Nadu Industrial Policy incentive applies state-wide (not SIPCOT-restricted). Capex subsidy + SGST reimbursement + electricity rebate + stamp duty exemption + specific sectoral additions. Online application + specific state facilitation.
What's MEPZ SEZ in Chennai?
Madras Export Processing Zone - dedicated SEZ in Chennai focused on manufacturing + specific export-oriented units. Section 10AA 100% tax holiday framework. Reduced customs + specific state incentives. Suitable for export-focused manufacturing + specific services.
