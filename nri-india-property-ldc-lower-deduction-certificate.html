NRI property sale, LDC saves you from the TDS-on-price trap.

NRI selling Indian property: Section 195 requires the buyer to deduct TDS on the sale consideration, not just the gain (12.5% plus surcharge and cess on long-term gains, up to 14.95%). On a INR 3 crore sale, that's about INR 44.85 lakh locked up as TDS even if actual capital gain tax is INR 5 lakh. A lower deduction certificate (LDC) fixes this: apply in Form 128 (formerly Form 13) under section 395 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (old section 197), get a certificate for your actual tax, and the buyer deducts only that. Apply as soon as the sale terms are agreed. Here's the complete LDC playbook.

/ The TDS-on-price problem

Why LDC exists.

Section 195 default: when buyer pays NRI seller for Indian property, buyer must deduct TDS at:

  • 12.5% + applicable surcharge (capped at 15%) + 4% cess on LTCG (transfers from 23 July 2024) (property held > 24 months).
  • 30% + applicable surcharge + 4% cess on STCG (property held <= 24 months).
  • TDS applies on gross sale consideration — not on gain. So even if your gain is modest or nil, up to 14.95% of the sale amount is withheld on a long-term sale.
  • TDS deposited by buyer with Indian government; NRI claims refund later in Indian ITR.

The problem: on INR 3 crore property with INR 50 lakh gain, actual tax under Section 112 (LTCG) at 12.5% (NRIs do not get the indexation option) = INR 6.25 lakh. But TDS without a certificate = about 14.95% of INR 3 crore = INR 44.85 lakh. About INR 38.6 lakh overpaid; locked in the TDS pool until you file an Indian return and the refund is processed. Huge cash flow trap.

Section 395 (old 197) certificate: NRI applies to Indian Tax Officer for certificate specifying lower TDS rate matching actual tax liability. Buyer then deducts the lower amount.

/ When LDC is worth the effort

Decision criteria.

  • Property sale value > INR 50 lakh: TDS of about INR 6.5 lakh (12.5% plus cess) or more. Usually worth the application effort.
  • Gain is less than sale value: standard where the purchase cost is substantial or Section 54 (section 82 of the 2025 Act) reinvestment planned. Material TDS saving.
  • Timing allows the application: the officer has to examine the application and may raise queries, so timing depends on the case and the documents. If the buyer wants to close very soon, the certificate may not be ready in time.
  • Section 54 / 54F reinvestment planned: capital gain will be fully / partially exempt → actual tax very low → LDC essentially zero rate → massive TDS saving.

For sale under INR 50 lakh or urgent closing (no LDC time), accept the default TDS and claim a refund in Indian ITR.

/ LDC application workflow

Step by step.

  1. Pre-requisite documentation:
  2.   — Property purchase deed (showing cost + date).
  3.   — Property sale agreement / draft (showing sale value + buyer details).
  4.   — NRI's PAN + proof of NRI status (passport + visa / OCI).
  5.   — Capital gain calculation (12.5% on long-term gains without indexation for NRIs) + Section 54 reinvestment plan if applicable.
  6.   — Last 2-3 years Indian ITR of NRI (shows capital gain treatment history).
  7. File Form 128 (old Form 13) on the TRACES portal (traces.tdscpc.gov.in):
  8.   — NRI logs in with PAN + password.
  9.   — Navigate to 'Statement/Payment → Request for Lower / No Deduction Certificate'.
  10.   — Fill Form 128 online: Section 195, nature of payment (immovable property sale), payer details (buyer PAN), amount, requested TDS rate.
  11.   — Upload supporting documents.
  12.   — Submit with DSC or Aadhaar OTP (if NRI has either).
  13. Assigned to Jurisdictional AO: application moves to Assessing Officer having jurisdiction over NRI's PAN.
  14. AO review: may ask for clarifications, additional documents. Response via portal.
  15. LDC issued: specifies the applicable TDS rate (e.g., 1% of sale consideration instead of the default rate). Valid for the specific transaction.
  16. Share LDC with buyer: buyer deducts the lower rate; deposits reduced TDS with government; files Form 27Q (Form 144 from 1 April 2026; all new form numbers) showing LDC reference.
  17. Final tax reconciliation: NRI files Indian ITR; any residual tax paid/refunded.

/ What LDC rate the AO typically approves

Realistic expectations.

  • LTCG on property held long term: the officer works out the actual tax on the capital gain (12.5% plus surcharge and cess, without indexation for NRIs) and expresses it as a rate on the sale consideration; the lower the gain relative to the price, the lower the rate.
  • LTCG with Section 54 reinvestment plan: if NRI commits to buy new residential property within 2 years (or construct within 3 years), capital gain exempt → the certificate can specify a much lower rate.
  • Section 54EC (section 85 of the 2025 Act) bonds: INR 50 lakh in REC / NHAI / PFC / IRFC bonds within 6 months of sale → INR 50 lakh of gain exempt → LDC reflects remaining tax.
  • Section 54F (section 86 of the 2025 Act): full LTCG exemption on sale of long-term asset (non-residential) if net consideration invested in one residential property → LDC near zero.
  • No reinvestment, substantial gain: the certificate rate reflects the actual tax (12.5% of the gain plus surcharge and cess).

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is Lower Deduction Certificate (LDC) for NRI property sale?
A certificate issued by the Indian tax officer under section 395 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (old section 197), specifying a TDS rate lower than Section 195 default (12.5% plus surcharge and cess on long-term gains, up to 14.95% of the price; 30% plus surcharge and cess on short-term gains), based on NRI's actual tax liability on the sale. Applied for in Form 128 (formerly Form 13) on the TRACES portal. Buyer deducts the LDC-specified rate instead of default - saves significant cash flow for NRI.
Can an NRI apply for a lower deduction certificate, and how do I apply online?
Yes. The NRI seller applies in Form 128 (old Form 13) on the TRACES portal, logging in with their PAN, before the buyer pays. The application gives the estimated income and tax for the year, the property and buyer details, the expected TDS, and the capital gains working, with supporting documents uploaded. The tax officer issues the certificate on TRACES, the seller downloads it and the buyer verifies it there.
What documents are required for a lower deduction certificate for an NRI property sale?
Usually: PAN, the sale agreement or MoU with the price and payment schedule, the purchase deed (or inheritance or gift documents and the previous owner's purchase deed), cost of improvement bills, a capital gains computation, proof of any planned section 54 / 54EC reinvestment, the buyer's details, and recent Indian income tax returns. The officer can ask for more, so a complete file avoids back-and-forth.

We compute the gain, prepare Form 128 and file it on TRACES with you.
How long does LDC take?
It depends on the case: a complete, well-documented Form 128 application moves faster, while queries from the officer or a change of jurisdiction slow it down. Apply as soon as the sale terms are agreed; the certificate should be in hand before the buyer pays.
What's the typical LDC rate approved by AO?
It depends on your actual tax. The officer works out the tax on the capital gain (12.5% plus surcharge and cess on long-term gains, without indexation for NRIs), allows for planned Section 54 / 54F / 54EC reinvestment, and expresses the result as a rate on the sale value. A small gain or a planned reinvestment means a low rate.
Can I apply for LDC after the sale is done?
No - LDC must be in place BEFORE buyer makes payment / deducts TDS. Once the default TDS is deducted and deposited, the only remedy is a refund by filing an Indian income tax return. LDC is prospective - apply well in advance.
Does the buyer need to do anything special with LDC?
The buyer can verify the certificate on TRACES, deducts only at the rate it specifies and quotes the certificate number when reporting the TDS - in Form 144 (old 27Q) with a TAN, or, for a resident individual or HUF buyer from 1 October 2026, in Form 141 with a PAN - and then gives the seller the TDS certificate showing the lower rate. Brief the buyer before the sale deed is signed.
