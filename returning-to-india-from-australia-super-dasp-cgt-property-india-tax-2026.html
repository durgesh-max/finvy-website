Returning to India From Australia: Super, DASP, CGT on Leaving and What India Taxes (2026), your visa decides the super.

What happens to your super depends on your visa. Former temporary visa holders can claim a Departing Australia Superannuation Payment (DASP) after leaving, with tax withheld at 35% on the taxed element; Australian citizens and permanent residents generally cannot, and their super stays until retirement age. Leaving Australia also triggers CGT event I1 on assets such as shares unless you elect to defer. In India, the first years back are usually non-resident or RNOR years; once you are resident and ordinarily resident, super and Australian accounts go in Schedule FA, and Form 40 can defer Indian tax on super until withdrawal.

/ Use the RNOR years well

Planning points.

  • Find your RNOR years with the residential status calculator; foreign income and gains received outside India are generally not taxed in India then.
  • DASP timing: a DASP paid into an account outside India in a non-resident or RNOR year is generally outside Indian tax; check the year before you claim.
  • Selling Australian assets: plan sales around both the CGT event on leaving and your Indian RNOR window.
  • NRE and FCNR accounts in India must be redesignated once you return for good; an RFC account can hold foreign currency. See NRE and NRO conversion on return.
  • Australian property you keep: rent is taxed in Australia as a foreign resident and, once you are resident and ordinarily resident, in India with credit for the Australian tax.

/ If you returned years ago

Fix it before 31 December 2026.

  • Super, accounts or shares built from salary earned while you were an NRI, not shown in Schedule FA after you became resident: FAST-DS Category 2, INR 1 lakh (asset up to INR 5 crore).
  • Australian rent, interest or dividends earned while resident and ordinarily resident and never offered to tax in India: Category 1, 60% within INR 1 crore.
  • Declare by 31 December 2026, pay after the Form 2 order, and report every year after.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can I withdraw my super when I leave Australia for India?
Only if you were on a temporary visa: once you have left and the visa has ended you can claim a Departing Australia Superannuation Payment, with tax withheld at 35% on the taxed element (65% for working holiday amounts). Citizens and permanent residents generally have to wait until retirement age.
Is DASP taxable in India?
Generally not if it is paid into an account outside India in a year you are non-resident or RNOR in India. Check your residential status for that year before claiming.
Is there an exit tax when leaving Australia?
Ceasing Australian residency triggers CGT event I1 on assets such as shares that are not taxable Australian property, unless you elect to defer. Australian real property stays in the Australian tax net.
Do I report super in my Indian return?
Yes, in Schedule FA once you are resident and ordinarily resident. Form 40 can defer Indian tax on income accruing in it until withdrawal, because Australia is a notified country.
I came back from Australia years ago and never reported my super. What now?
If it was built from salary earned while you were an NRI, FAST-DS Category 2 settles it for a flat INR 1 lakh if you declare by 31 December 2026.
