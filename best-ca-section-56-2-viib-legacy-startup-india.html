/ The legacy issue

What's still relevant.

  • Section 56(2)(viib) abolished by Finance Act 2024 - no new angel tax exposure for FY 2024-25 onwards.
  • But FY 2023-24 and earlier: Section 56(2)(viib) applied to shares issued at value exceeding fair market value (DCF).
  • Pre-abolition rounds attracted 'Income from Other Sources' at slab rate on excess value.
  • DPIIT recognised startups + specific residents exempt - but many non-DPIIT startups + specific angel investors trapped.
  • Reassessment notices via Section 148 + Section 148A can be issued up to 10 years back (for high-value cases).
  • Pending litigation + ITAT / High Court cases still active.

/ Common exposure scenarios

Where it comes up.

  • Pre-DPIIT recognition round: startup raised funds at high valuation before DPIIT application; valuation challenged post-fact.
  • Non-DPIIT startup: never applied for DPIIT; raised funds above DCF value.
  • Specific investor type: HUF / cooperative society / specific trust not covered by angel tax exemption.
  • Share issue to specific residents (not just angel investors) at premium.
  • Preference share issue + conversion with pricing issues.
  • Series B / C round where valuation jumped substantially + DCF didn't support.

/ Response strategies

If Section 148A show-cause received.

  1. Information review: what AO's basis for 56(2)(viib) application?
  2. DCF valuation re-verification: can original valuation be defended with contemporaneous docs?
  3. DPIIT eligibility retroactive check: was startup DPIIT-recognised at relevant time?
  4. Investor status check: was investor a 'resident' at relevant date?
  5. Section 148A(b) response + documentary refutation.
  6. If Section 148 proceeds: Section 147 reassessment representation.
  7. Section 270A under vs mis reporting defence.
  8. CIT(A) + ITAT appeal if Section 147 order adverse.
  9. Writ in High Court if Section 148A procedure fundamentally defective.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is Section 56(2)(viib) applicable to current funding rounds?
No - abolished by Finance Act 2024 for all funding rounds (not just DPIIT startups). Current + future rounds (FY 2024-25 onwards) are completely out of 56(2)(viib) scope regardless of valuation. Historical pre-abolition cases (FY 2023-24 and earlier) remain live.
Can AO still issue 56(2)(viib) reassessment notice?
Yes for pre-abolition years. Section 148A show-cause for AY 2019-20 (standard 4-year): closing March 2027. AY 2015-16 to 2018-19 (10-year extended if asset-form escaped income > INR 50 lakh): still open. Startups + angel investors with pre-abolition rounds may receive notices during November-December reassessment season.
Who was exempt from Section 56(2)(viib) pre-abolition?
DPIIT recognised startups meeting specific criteria + specific resident categories (certain funds, SEBI-registered VCFs). Non-DPIIT startups + non-exempt investors trapped. Specific CBDT notifications over 2019-2023 progressively expanded exemption scope.
What's the typical reassessment outcome?
Varies. Well-documented contemporaneous DCF valuation + DPIIT eligibility: usually sustainable. Weak documentation + aggressive pricing: material addition + Section 270A penalty (50% under-reporting or 200% misreporting). CIT(A) + ITAT appeal often reduces or eliminates addition on substantive grounds. Writ for procedural defects.
Can I apply for retroactive DPIIT recognition?
Yes - DPIIT application can be made at any time within eligibility window (incorporation + 10 years). If granted, Section 80-IAC claim prospectively. For 56(2)(viib) exemption retroactivity, specific CBDT clarifications apply - case-by-case assessment.
