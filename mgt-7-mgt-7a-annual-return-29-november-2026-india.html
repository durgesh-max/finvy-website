/ MCA Deadline · MGT-7 · Updated 2026-10-25

MGT-7 and MGT-7A Annual Return Due 29 November 2026, the second MCA deadline of the season.

The annual return is due within 60 days of the AGM. For an AGM on 30 September 2026, MGT-7 or MGT-7A is due on 29 November 2026. The late fee is INR 100 per day with no cap, and the same three-year non-filing rule that disqualifies directors applies here as well.

/ Which form

MGT-7 or MGT-7A.

  • MGT-7A: One Person Companies and small companies (paid-up capital up to INR 4 crore and turnover up to INR 40 crore, as revised in 2022).
  • MGT-7: all other companies, including most foreign-owned subsidiaries above the small-company limits.
  • Listed companies and companies with paid-up capital of INR 10 crore or more or turnover of INR 50 crore or more need MGT-8 certification by a practising company secretary.

/ What the return reports

Information that must match MCA records.

  • Shareholding pattern at year end, including foreign holders and their percentages.
  • Changes in shareholding and transfers during the year, which must match FC-GPR/FC-TRS filings for foreign holders.
  • Directors and KMP, board and general meeting dates and attendance.
  • Remuneration of directors and KMP, penalties and compounding during the year.
  • Indebtedness and the number of members, debenture holders and other security holders.

/ Cost of filing late

Fees and penalties.

  • MCA additional fee: INR 100 per day of delay, no cap.
  • Section 92(5) penalty: INR 10,000 on the company plus INR 100 per day, up to INR 2 lakh; INR 10,000 plus INR 100 per day on each officer in default, up to INR 50,000.
  • Section 164(2): three continuing years of non-filing disqualifies directors for five years.

/ Common rejections and how to avoid them

Where filings go wrong.

  • Shareholding in MGT-7 does not match the register of members or the demat position under Rule 9B.
  • Share allotments during the year without a matching PAS-3 on record.
  • Foreign shareholder changes without the corresponding FEMA filing, which then shows up in buyer due diligence.
  • Signing director's DIN deactivated after a missed DIR-3 KYC.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is the MGT-7 due date for FY 2025-26?
Within 60 days of the AGM. For an AGM on 30 September 2026, the due date is 29 November 2026.
What is the difference between MGT-7 and MGT-7A?
MGT-7A is a shorter return for One Person Companies and small companies. All other companies file MGT-7.
What is the late fee for MGT-7?
INR 100 per day as an additional MCA fee, with no maximum, plus possible penalties under Section 92(5).
Do foreign-owned Indian companies file MGT-7?
Yes. Most foreign subsidiaries exceed the small-company limits and file MGT-7. Foreign shareholdings must match the FEMA filings on record.
