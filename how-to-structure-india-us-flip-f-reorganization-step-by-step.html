/ What's F-Reorganization

The structuring.

  • F-reorganisation under US IRC Section 368(a)(1)(F): tax-neutral change in corporate form (same entity, different form).
  • Typical path: Delaware LLC (electing partnership tax under IRS Form 8832) → Delaware C-Corp.
  • Why needed: US VCs predominantly invest in C-Corp (not LLC). Pre-funding conversion required.
  • Benefits: US tax-neutral + preserves accumulated losses (NOLs) + continuity of corporate identity + preserves QSBS 1202 10-year holding period.
  • Specific documentation: Certificate of Conversion + bylaws + new C-Corp election + specific IRS forms.

/ Pre-F-Reorganization due diligence

What to verify.

  1. Current Delaware LLC status + member list + capital structure.
  2. Indian shareholder FEMA ODI history: Form ODI Part I + APR filings.
  3. LLC accumulated losses + specific tax attributes.
  4. Specific operational entities + their tax treatment.
  5. Section 7874 US inversion rules (if Indian shareholders own 80%+ of future C-Corp).
  6. Specific Form 5472 compliance history (foreign-owned LLC annual filing).

/ F-Reorganization execution steps

The workflow.

  1. Delaware filings: Certificate of Conversion + Certificate of Incorporation of new C-Corp + specific Delaware legal counsel coordination.
  2. US tax elections: IRS Form 8832 (classification election) + specific state tax forms + specific IRS Section 368 documentation.
  3. EIN continuity: typically same EIN carries over to new C-Corp.
  4. Bank account continuity: Mercury / Brex / Rho account updated to new C-Corp (specific KYC refresh).
  5. Indian FEMA ODI compliance: Form ODI update + specific MOA/AOA amendments for Indian shareholder's investment in C-Corp.
  6. Section 7874 US inversion check: ownership mix 60-80% specific limited US tax impact; 80%+ triggers inversion.
  7. Section 1202 QSBS continuity: 10-year holding period preserved via F-reorganisation.
  8. Section 83(b) election refresh: for founders with restricted stock.

/ Post-F-Reorganization compliance

Ongoing.

  • Form 1120 (C-Corp return) replaces Form 1065 (LLC/partnership return).
  • Form 5472 continues (foreign-owned C-Corp with 25%+ foreign ownership).
  • Delaware franchise tax (new C-Corp framework).
  • Indian FEMA ODI Form ODI Part II (APR) annual.
  • Section 115BAA opt-in review for Indian parent (if India-first structure).
  • Specific VC term sheet due diligence + closing conditions compliance.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's F-reorganisation and why is it needed?
US IRC Section 368(a)(1)(F): tax-neutral change in corporate form. For Indian startups: Delaware LLC → Delaware C-Corp conversion. VCs predominantly invest in C-Corp (not LLC). F-reorganisation enables pre-funding conversion while preserving tax attributes (NOLs, QSBS 1202 holding period, EIN continuity).
Does F-reorganisation trigger Indian tax?
Indian shareholders: holding of US LLC changes to holding of US C-Corp. Specific analysis: whether treated as 'transfer' under Section 2(47) + Section 112A capital gain. BQP integrates F-reorganisation with Section 47(viib) considerations where applicable. Specific structuring to minimize Indian tax impact.
What's Section 7874 US inversion risk?
If Indian shareholders own 80%+ of future US C-Corp: US treats C-Corp as 'surrogate foreign corporation' + specific US tax consequences (inversion). 60-80%: limited US tax impact. Below 60%: no inversion. Must plan ownership mix in F-reorganisation.
Does QSBS Section 1202 holding period continue?
Yes - F-reorganisation specifically preserves 10-year QSBS holding period. Original acquisition date carries over. Material for founders + early employees planning Section 1202 100% exclusion exit (up to USD 10M per shareholder OR 10x basis).
