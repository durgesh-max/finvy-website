Income tax refund delayed, and the interest you are owed.

Many taxpayers who filed on time are still waiting for refunds. The Department can process a return up to nine months after the end of the financial year in which it was filed, so a return filed in 2026 can lawfully be processed as late as December 2026. Refunds are often held up by mismatches the taxpayer can fix, and a delayed refund earns interest of 0.5% a month under Section 244A.

/ Common reasons

Check these first.

  • The return was never e-verified, or was verified late.
  • The bank account is not pre-validated on the portal, or the PAN-bank name does not match.
  • TDS claimed in the return does not match Form 26AS (Form 168 from tax year 2026-27) or AIS.
  • A defective-return notice or a proposed adjustment is waiting for your response.
  • The refund was adjusted against an earlier outstanding demand.
  • The return is selected for further verification or scrutiny.

/ What you can do

In order.

  1. Check the return status on the e-filing portal: filed, verified, processed, refund issued.
  2. Re-validate the bank account and make sure it is nominated for refund.
  3. Look under Pending Actions and e-Proceedings for notices and respond within the time given.
  4. Compare TDS in Form 26AS and AIS with the return; ask deductors to correct their TDS statements if needed.
  5. If processed but not paid, check the refund status and request a refund reissue on the portal.
  6. Raise a grievance through e-Nivaran on the portal if nothing moves.

/ Interest on delayed refunds

Section 244A.

  • 0.5% per month or part of a month.
  • For returns filed by the due date, interest generally runs from 1 April of the assessment year; for later returns, from the date of filing.
  • Interest can be reduced for delay caused by the taxpayer, and is not paid on very small refunds relative to the tax determined.
  • Interest received on a refund is taxable income in the year received.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Why is my ITR refund delayed in 2026?
Common reasons are a return that was not e-verified, a bank account that is not pre-validated, TDS mismatches with Form 26AS or AIS, pending notices, or adjustment against an old demand. The Department can also take up to nine months after the end of the year of filing to process the return.
How do I check my income tax or TDS refund status?
Log in to the income tax e-filing portal and open View Filed Returns: it shows whether the return is verified, processed and whether the refund has been issued, with any adjustment against old demands. You can also check by PAN and assessment year on the refund status page run by Protean (NSDL) for the refund banker. TDS deducted on your income is refunded only through your income tax return, so there is no separate TDS refund to track.
What does each ITR refund status update mean?
Typical stages are: return submitted and verified; processed with refund due (the refund is then sent to the bank); refund issued (credited or sent to the pre-validated account); refund failed (usually a bank account that is not pre-validated or a name mismatch, so re-validate it and raise a refund reissue request on the portal); and adjusted against an earlier demand (you can respond on the portal if you disagree). If the status stays at processing for long, check for a pending defective-return notice or adjustment.
How many days does a TDS refund take?
Once the return is e-verified and processed, the refund is usually credited directly to the pre-validated bank account within a few weeks. Processing itself can legally take up to nine months from the end of the financial year in which the return was filed, and delays usually come from a bank account that is not validated, a TDS mismatch or a pending notice.
How long can the Income Tax Department take to process my return?
Up to nine months from the end of the financial year in which the return was filed.
Do I get interest on a delayed refund?
Yes, generally 0.5% per month under Section 244A, from 1 April of the assessment year if you filed on time. It can be reduced for delays caused by the taxpayer.
How do I complain about a delayed refund?
Raise a grievance through e-Nivaran on the e-filing portal after checking that no notice or verification is pending.
How can an NRI claim a TDS refund, and into which account?
File ITR-2 (or ITR-3 with business income) claiming the TDS shown in Form 168 (old 26AS) and AIS, and e-verify it. The refund goes to the bank account pre-validated on the e-filing portal; for an NRI that is usually the NRO account, because Indian-source money belongs there. An income tax refund is not clearly listed among the credits allowed into an NRE account, so check with your bank before nominating one. A non-resident with no Indian account can give one foreign bank account in the return. For FY 2025-26 a belated or revised return can still claim the refund until 31 December 2026.
What should I send to check why my refund is stuck?
WhatsApp us your PAN, the acknowledgement of the return, the processing status or intimation under section 143(1) if received, any notice or communication on the portal, and Form 168 / AIS. We check for a bank-validation failure, a TDS mismatch, an adjustment against old demand or a pending notice, and tell you the fix: re-validation, a rectification request, a response to the notice or a grievance.
