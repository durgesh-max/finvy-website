/ Who must file PAS-6

Rule 9B scope.

  • All unlisted public + private companies (except small companies, government companies, Nidhi companies) that have securities in demat form under Rule 9B.
  • Small company threshold: paid-up capital up to INR 4 crore + turnover up to INR 40 crore (Section 2(85)). Most growth-stage startups cross this threshold.
  • Half-yearly filing: within 60 days of half-year end (30 September + 31 March).
  • September 2026 period: due 29 November 2026.
  • March 2027 period: due 30 May 2027.

/ What's in Form PAS-6

The reconciliation.

  • Authorised share capital + issued + subscribed + called-up + paid-up at half-year end.
  • Breakup by class of security (Equity + Preference + Convertible instruments).
  • Reconciliation: shares in demat form (NSDL + CDSL) vs physical form + issued but not demat'd + bought back + cancelled.
  • Changes during half-year: allotments + buyback + transmission + any share capital change.
  • Reasons for any difference between issued capital and demat + physical total.
  • CA certification confirming reconciliation accuracy.

/ Common filing issues

Where PAS-6 trips.

  • Shareholders unresponsive to demat request - still in physical form 2 years after Rule 9B became mandatory.
  • Reconciliation gaps between company's share register + NSDL / CDSL records.
  • ESOP exercise during half-year not reflected in demat form yet (processing timing).
  • Buyback / bonus / rights issue reconciliation.
  • Lost physical certificates - duplicate issuance under Rule 6 pending.
  • CA certification requires reconciliation workpapers - must prepare in advance.

/ Compliance checklist

The November 2026 action list.

  1. Export shareholder register as of 30 September 2026.
  2. Request NSDL + CDSL depository data for same date.
  3. Reconcile: company register vs NSDL + CDSL data + physical holders.
  4. Identify unresponsive demat shareholders (physical form 2+ years post Rule 9B).
  5. Document any reconciliation differences with narrative.
  6. Compile half-year changes: allotments / buyback / transmission / ESOP exercise.
  7. CA review + certification.
  8. File Form PAS-6 via MCA v3 portal by 29 November 2026.
  9. Attach: CA certificate + reconciliation workpapers + specific annexures as applicable.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

When is PAS-6 due for September 2026 half-year?
29 November 2026 - within 60 days of 30 September 2026 half-year end. For March 2027 period: 30 May 2027. Filed via MCA v3 portal with CA certification + reconciliation workpapers.
Which companies must file PAS-6?
All unlisted public and private companies (except small companies, government companies, Nidhi companies) that have securities in demat form under Rule 9B. Small company threshold: paid-up capital up to INR 4 crore + turnover up to INR 40 crore under Section 2(85). Most growth-stage startups cross threshold.
What CA certification does PAS-6 require?
Chartered Accountant certification confirming: share capital reconciliation accuracy, authorised + issued + paid-up matching, demat + physical reconciliation, any differences explained with reasons. Signed by practicing CA with ICAI membership + UDIN (Unique Document Identification Number).
What if I still have physical-share holders at September 2026?
File PAS-6 reflecting the physical holders in reconciliation. Narrative explanation that company has attempted demat (RPAD letters, emails, follow-ups) but specific shareholders remain unresponsive. Documentation of attempts is key - demonstrates company's reasonable efforts to comply with Rule 9B obligation to facilitate (not force) demat.
What's the late fee for missed PAS-6?
Section 47 CGST Act late fee - not applicable here. Companies Act Section 450 default penalty: up to INR 10,000 for first default + INR 1,000 per day of continuing default. More materially: Section 450 for every officer in default + ROC may initiate adjudication proceedings.
