How to Register an LLC or Company in the USA From India (2026): Steps, EIN, FEMA ODI Rules, Form 5472 and Indian Tax, the US filing takes days; the Indian side is where people slip.

You can form a US LLC or corporation from India online, without visiting the US: file with a state such as Delaware or Wyoming through a registered agent, get an EIN, and open a US bank account. The part most people miss is Indian law. A resident Indian putting money into a foreign company is making an overseas direct investment (ODI) under FEMA: it must be reported through your bank in Form ODI, fits within the USD 250,000 a year Liberalised Remittance Scheme limit, needs an Annual Performance Report by 31 December each year, and is allowed only in an operating business with no controlled subsidiaries below it. On the US side, a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person files Form 5472 every year (USD 25,000 penalty if missed). In India you stay taxable on worldwide income and must show the entity in Schedule FA.

/ Quick answers

What Indian founders ask first.

  • Can I register a US company from India? Yes, entirely online, through a registered agent in the state you choose. You do not need a US visa, address or SSN.
  • LLC or C-Corp? An LLC suits a services or trading business owned by one or a few people. A Delaware C-Corp suits a startup that will raise venture capital or issue stock options. See C-Corp vs LLC.
  • Do I need RBI approval? No prior approval for a resident individual investing in an operating business within the limits, but the investment must be reported through your bank as ODI, and the Annual Performance Report filed each year.
  • Do I pay tax in the US? It depends on whether the business has US-connected income. A single-member LLC is ignored for US income tax but still files Form 5472; a C-Corp pays US corporate tax.
  • Do I pay tax in India? If you live in India, yes, on your worldwide income, and the US entity must be reported in Schedule FA of your return.

/ The steps

From India to a working US entity.

StepWhat it involves
1. Choose entity and stateLLC or C-Corp; Delaware for investor-backed startups, Wyoming or Delaware for small LLCs
2. Registered agent and filingThe agent files the articles of organization (LLC) or certificate of incorporation (corporation) and gives you a US address for official mail
3. Operating agreement or bylawsOwnership, management and what happens if a member leaves
4. EINApply to the IRS on Form SS-4; no SSN needed. The EIN is required for the bank, payments platforms and filings
5. Indian ODI reportingRemit your capital through an authorised dealer bank, which reports it in Form ODI; keep share or membership certificates
6. US bank accountMany US banks and fintech accounts open remotely for non-resident owners with the EIN and formation documents
7. Yearly complianceState annual report or franchise tax, Form 5472 and pro forma 1120 for a foreign-owned single-member LLC, Indian APR by 31 December, Schedule FA in your Indian return

Beneficial ownership reporting: under FinCEN’s interim final rule of March 2025, companies formed in the US are currently exempt; see BOIR status.

/ The Indian side

FEMA, TCS and tax.

  • ODI rules for individuals: the US entity must run a real business (not financial services), and must not have a subsidiary or step-down subsidiary that you control. Invest within the USD 250,000 per financial year LRS limit. See FEMA ODI for a US entity.
  • TCS: the bank collects 20% TCS on LRS remittances for investment above INR 10 lakh in a year; it is credited against your Indian tax.
  • Annual Performance Report: due by 31 December each year for the previous financial year; see Form ODI APR.
  • Indian tax: as a resident you are taxed on worldwide income, including your share of the US business’s profit or the salary or dividends it pays you, with credit for US tax. Show the entity in Schedule FA.
  • Place of effective management: if the US company is in substance run from India, India can treat it as an Indian resident company and tax all its profits. Keep board decisions and records real.
  • Freelancers billing foreign clients often do not need a US entity at all; compare exporting services directly from India.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

How do I register an LLC in the USA from India?
Choose a state, appoint a registered agent who files the articles of organization online, sign an operating agreement, get an EIN from the IRS on Form SS-4, and open a US bank account. On the Indian side, remit capital through your bank as overseas direct investment in Form ODI and file the Annual Performance Report each year.
Do I need RBI approval to start a US company from India?
Not prior approval for a resident individual investing in an operating business within the LRS limit of USD 250,000 a year, but the investment must be reported through an authorised dealer bank in Form ODI, and the entity must not have a subsidiary that you control.
Can an Indian resident own a US LLC?
Yes. It is an overseas direct investment under FEMA. The LLC files Form 5472 in the US every year if it is a single-member LLC owned by a non-US person, and you report it in Schedule FA of your Indian return.
Do I need an SSN to get an EIN?
No. A non-resident owner applies on Form SS-4 without an SSN.
Is a US LLC owned by an Indian taxed in India?
If you live in India, your income from the LLC is taxable in India as part of your worldwide income, with credit for any US tax. If the LLC is managed from India, it can also be treated as an Indian resident.
