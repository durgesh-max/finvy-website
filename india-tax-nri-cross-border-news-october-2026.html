/ This month's changes

Newest first by effect.

  • 26-27 Oct 2026: GST: 8 CBIC circulars and changes from 1 November
    After the 57th GST Council (8 October): 90% provisional inverted duty refunds, no INR 1,000 minimum on courier export refunds, reverse charge and GST TDS on scrap and e-waste, registration and adjudication safeguards. Read more · Source: TaxO
  • 26 Oct 2026: Rule 96(10) omitted retrospectively from 2017
    Circular 264/10/2026 accepts the Supreme Court's Goodluck India ruling: IGST refund demands on exporters based on Rule 96(10) cannot be sustained. Read more · Source: TaxGuru
  • 30 Oct / 30 Nov 2026: UAE corporate tax and top-up tax deadlines
    Returns for 31 January 2026 year-ends due by Friday 30 October; multinational groups under the 15% top-up tax must register by 30 November. Read more · Source: Crowe UAE
  • Oct 2026: H-1B fee in limbo: Indian professionals weigh return
    The US$100,000 H-1B fee is not being collected after court rulings, but the position is unsettled. Returning NRIs should plan RNOR status and US retirement accounts. Read more · Source: Forbes
  • 2026: Any foreign individual can buy listed Indian shares
    FEMA Schedule III opened beyond NRIs and OCIs: under 10% per individual, 24% aggregate, reported on Form LEC (IFI). Read more · Source: India Briefing
  • 16 Aug - 31 Dec 2026: FAST-DS 2026 foreign asset disclosure window open
    Small taxpayers can declare undisclosed foreign income or assets: up to INR 1 crore at 60%, or up to INR 5 crore of taxed-but-unreported assets for a flat INR 1 lakh. Closes 31 December 2026. Read more · Source: Outlook Money
  • 1 Oct 2026: No TAN needed to buy property from an NRI
    Resident individual and HUF buyers can pay TDS under their own PAN through Form 141. Rates unchanged: 12.5% long-term, 30% short-term, plus surcharge and cess. Read more · Source: Outlook Money
  • 28 Sep 2026: Audit-case ITR due date moved to 21 November
    CBDT Circular 07/2026: tax audit report 21 October, ITR 21 November. Transfer pricing cases unchanged: Form 3CEB 31 October, ITR 30 November. Read more · Source: India Briefing
  • 8 Oct 2026: New penalty-waiver form under the Income-tax Act, 2025
    Notification 134/2026 introduced a revised Form 161 for penalty-waiver applications under Section 440(2) of the new Act. Read more · Source: India Briefing
  • From 1 Apr 2026: Section 195 is now Section 393(2)
    The Income-tax Act, 2025 is in force. TDS on payments to non-residents made from 1 April 2026 falls under Section 393(2); TCS, including on LRS remittances, under Section 394. Read more · Source: ClearTax
  • From 1 Apr 2026: TCS on education and medical remittances cut to 2%
    Self-funded education and medical remittances under LRS: 2% above INR 10 lakh (was 5%). Overseas tour packages: flat 2%. Other LRS remittances: 20% above INR 10 lakh. Read more · Source: Lulu Forex
  • From 1 Jan 2026: US 1% tax on cash-funded remittances
    Transfers abroad paid in cash, money orders or cashier's checks attract a 1% US excise tax. Bank-account and US card-funded transfers are exempt. Read more · Source: Cherry Bekaert

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What are the biggest Indian tax changes in October 2026?
GST changes from 1 November after the 57th GST Council, retrospective relief for exporters on Rule 96(10), the FAST-DS foreign asset disclosure window (closes 31 December 2026), PAN-based TDS for buying property from NRIs, and the audit-case ITR date moving to 21 November 2026.
What changed for NRIs in 2026?
Buyers of NRI property no longer need a TAN, TCS on education and medical remittances fell to 2%, the FAST-DS window opened for undisclosed foreign assets, and the US introduced a 1% tax on cash-funded remittances.
Is Section 195 still in force?
For payments made or credited from 1 April 2026, TDS on non-resident payments is under Section 393(2) of the Income-tax Act, 2025.
