/ What a startup-ready CA actually does

Beyond tax returns.

Most Indian CA firms serve startups the way they serve any SME — GST, Income Tax, ROC filings. That's necessary but not sufficient. A startup-ready CA also handles:

  • Incorporation structuring — Pvt Ltd vs LLP vs Delaware vs Singapore depending on your 24-month plan.
  • DPIIT Startup India recognition — eligibility, application, Section 80-IAC tax exemption positioning, Section 56 angel-tax position (post-Finance Act 2024 abolition).
  • Fundraising readiness — cap table structuring, SAFE vs CCPS vs CCD choice, pricing-guideline-compliant valuation certificate, Form FC-GPR within 30 days of foreign-investor closing.
  • ESOP plan design + 409A valuation — Section 17 Indian ESOP + 409A for Delaware C-Corp if applicable, grant mechanics, exercise-year perquisite tax planning.
  • Flip to Delaware — pre-US-VC flip execution: share swap, FEMA FC-TRS, Form ODI, Indian capital gains per shareholder, 83(b).
  • Cross-border tax — DTAA on US / UAE / UK / Singapore flows, transfer pricing, Section 195 TDS.
  • SME IPO advisory — BSE SME / NSE Emerge end-to-end: readiness audit, DRHP, SEBI inspection, merchant banker co-ordination.
  • Post-funding compliance — board governance, shareholder reporting cadence, FEMA APR, transfer-pricing documentation.

/ Startup lifecycle services

What BQP supports at each stage.

Idea / pre-seed: entity choice, SPICe+ incorporation, DPIIT recognition, PAN/GST/MCA setup, cap table initialisation, co-founder equity + vesting documentation.

Seed round: SAFE / CCPS structuring for Indian company or Delaware C-Corp flip for US VC round, valuation certificate, Form FC-GPR, 83(b) if Delaware.

Series A: term-sheet review, cap table modelling across scenarios, ESOP pool sizing, transfer pricing setup if cross-border, VC compliance pack.

Series B+ growth: international structuring (Delaware / Singapore / UAE), transfer pricing scaling, treasury planning, CFO support.

Pre-IPO: SEBI listing readiness audit, DRHP preparation, SEBI inspection prep, corporate governance restructuring, auditor co-ordination.

IPO + post-listing: listing day co-ordination, post-listing SEBI compliance, Clause 49, insider trading policies, quarterly reporting.

Exit / M&A: sell-side support, buyer diligence response, capital gains tax planning for founders.
