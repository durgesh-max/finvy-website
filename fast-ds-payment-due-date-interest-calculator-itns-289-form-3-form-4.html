FAST-DS Payment: Due Date and Interest Calculator, ITNS 289 Challan, Form 3 and Form 4 Certificate (Step by Step), what to do after you file Form 1.

Filing Form 1 by 31 December 2026 is only the first step. The Department issues Form 2 (the order of the amount payable) within one month from the end of the month you declare. You then pay through challan ITNS 289 within two months from the end of the month you receive Form 2, or within a further two months with simple interest of 1% for every month or part of a month. After paying, you file Form 3 with the proof, and the Department issues Form 4, the certificate that gives you immunity. Use the calculator for your own dates.

    / Step by step

    From Form 2 to the certificate.

    1. Watch for Form 2. It arrives in your e-Filing account (and by email) within one month from the end of the month you filed Form 1. Check the amount against your own working.
    2. Create the ITNS 289 challan. Before login: incometax.gov.in → e-Pay Tax → the “Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers Disclosure Scheme, 2026 [Challan No. ITNS 289]” tile. After login: e-File → e-Pay Tax → Income Tax Act, 1961 → New Payment → FAST-DS (ITNS 289).
    3. Pay the exact Form 2 amount by net banking, card or UPI through an authorised bank. One ITNS 289 challan can be used for the amount in Form 2.
    4. File Form 3 (intimation of payment) electronically with the challan details as proof, within the time allowed.
    5. Download Form 4, the certificate, once the Department verifies the payment. Keep it with your Form 1 and Form 2.
    6. Fix Schedule FA from 2026-27 onwards, so the same assets are reported every year.

    / Mistakes to avoid

    These cannot be undone.

    • Wrong challan or minor head. The portal warns that an incorrect payment under ITNS 289 cannot be corrected or refunded. Double-check the challan type before paying.
    • Paying before Form 2. ITNS 289 is for the amount determined in Form 2; wait for the order.
    • Forgetting Form 3. Paying is not the end: without Form 3 the Department cannot issue the certificate.
    • Missing the extended date. Interest stops being an option after the further two months.
    • Assuming the amount is refundable. Amounts paid under the scheme are not refunded.

    FAQ

    Common questions, answered.

    When do I pay under FAST-DS?
    After you receive Form 2, the order of the amount payable. Pay within two months from the end of the month in which you receive it, or within a further two months with simple interest of 1% for every month or part of a month.
    Which challan is used for FAST-DS?
    Challan ITNS 289, available on the e-Filing portal under e-Pay Tax (the Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers Disclosure Scheme, 2026 tile) or after login under e-File > e-Pay Tax > New Payment.
    What are Form 3 and Form 4 under FAST-DS?
    Form 3 is your intimation of payment, filed with proof after paying. Form 4 is the certificate the Department issues after verifying the payment.
    Is the 31 December deadline for payment?
    No. 31 December 2026 is the last date to file the declaration (Form 1). Payment follows Form 2, which comes later.
    Can a wrong ITNS 289 payment be refunded?
    The portal states that an incorrect payment made under ITNS 289 cannot be corrected or refunded, so check the challan and amount carefully.
