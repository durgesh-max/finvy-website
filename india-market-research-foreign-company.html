/ Research workstreams

What's covered.

  • Market sizing: TAM / SAM / SOM in India by segment, geography, customer type. Growth rate forecast + driver analysis.
  • Competitive landscape: top 10-20 players with revenue estimate, product/service, pricing, positioning, strengths, weaknesses. Market share analysis where data permits.
  • Regulatory mapping: licence requirements, sector FDI caps, GST / tax treatment, data protection (DPDP Act), labour, environmental.
  • Distribution channel analysis: direct / dealer / distributor / e-commerce / modern trade / general trade mix; margin stack across channel; channel partner identification.
  • Pricing benchmarks: competitor pricing by product / tier; INR vs comparable US / EU pricing; price elasticity hypothesis.
  • Customer insights: qualitative interviews with 20-30 potential customers (business or consumer segment); quantitative survey if sample sizing warrants (300-1000 respondents).
  • GTM recommendation: entry route (own-vs-partner), geography priority, segment priority, timeline, investment required, risk factors.

/ Research methodology

Primary + secondary.

Secondary research:

  • Industry reports (CRISIL, ICRA, Nomura, government reports, trade bodies — FICCI, CII, NASSCOM, PHDCCI).
  • Public financial filings (MCA data for Indian competitors, annual reports).
  • Trade publications + press.
  • Patent filings for innovation mapping.
  • News + regulatory announcements.

Primary research:

  • Expert interviews (industry analysts, former executives, trade association heads) — 10-20 interviews typical.
  • Customer interviews (B2B or B2C target customers) — 20-30 qualitative or 300+ quantitative.
  • Dealer / distributor interviews for channel insight.
  • Mystery shopping / audit (for consumer / retail research).
  • Site visits (plant / retail / warehouse) where operational research applies.

/ Deliverables

What foreign clients receive.

  • Executive summary (5-10 pages): key findings + go/no-go recommendation + top-3 risks + investment ask.
  • Full report (40-80 pages): all workstreams in detail with charts + tables + case examples.
  • Data appendix (Excel): competitor database, interview transcripts, survey results, market model, scenario analysis.
  • Board presentation (15-25 slides): investment-committee / board-ready narrative.
  • GTM roadmap: 12-24 month quarterly milestone plan with sequencing + resourcing.

Weekly status calls during the engagement. Mid-point readout to share preliminary findings. Final presentation to foreign-client management or board.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Can an Indian firm do market research for a US / UK company remotely?
Yes - this is standard. Primary research (expert interviews, customer surveys, site visits) is conducted by India-based team; secondary research (reports, filings) is accessed online. Final deliverables shared digitally; presentation via Zoom to foreign-client board / investment committee.
How long does India market research take?
4-8 weeks typical: scoping (1 week), secondary research + framework building (2 weeks), primary research (interviews + surveys, 2-3 weeks), analysis + report drafting (1-2 weeks). Rush engagements possible in 2-3 weeks for specific scope.
What's included in TAM / SAM / SOM sizing?
TAM (Total Addressable Market): total market revenue potential if 100% penetration. SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market): segment achievable given product-market fit + geography. SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market): realistic 3-5 year capture. All built bottom-up from Indian industry data + top-down cross-check against secondary sources.
Do you provide primary interviews with Indian customers?
Yes - typically 20-30 qualitative interviews with target B2B or B2C customers for research scope. For quantitative studies, we deploy 300-1000+ respondent surveys via panel partners. Interview transcripts shared with foreign client (with respondent anonymisation where applicable).
Can BQP's research feed into our investment committee / board decision?
Yes - deliverables are formatted specifically for investment-committee / board use: executive summary with go/no-go recommendation, 15-25 slide board deck, full report, data appendix. Final presentation delivered via Zoom to IC or board directly. Standard output for VC / PE / strategic investor use cases.
