/ Section 44AB tax audit threshold

Who's in scope.

  • Turnover above INR 1 crore (goods) / INR 50 lakh (profession) for AY 2026-27.
  • Business with 95%+ digital receipts: INR 10 crore turnover threshold.
  • Professional with 95%+ digital receipts: INR 75 lakh threshold.
  • Section 44AD / 44ADA presumptive scheme + turnover below threshold = exempt.
  • Due date: 31 October of AY (vs 31 July for non-audit). TP cases 30 November.

/ Form 3CA vs 3CB

Which applies.

  • Form 3CA: for taxpayers who are required to get accounts audited under other law (Companies Act, LLP Act, etc.). Standard audit report + tax audit cross-references under same financial year.
  • Form 3CB: for taxpayers audited only for tax audit (not under any other statute). Specific tax-audit-only report.
  • Form 3CD: 44-clause particulars common to both 3CA + 3CB.

/ Clause 22 Section 43B(h) focus

The year-2 critical clause.

  • Clause 22 expanded post Finance Act 2023 for Section 43B(h) MSE compliance.
  • Current year disallowance: MSE expense paid beyond 45 days.
  • Prior year reversal: MSE expense disallowed in prior year, paid during current year - allowed as deduction in current year.
  • Udyam certificate collection + aging + Section 15 MSMED interest documentation.
  • Cross-reference with MSME Form I half-yearly ROC filing.

/ Clause 44 GST reconciliation focus

The IT-GST interface.

  • Clause 44 reconciles Income Tax turnover with GST turnover (GSTR-1 + GSTR-9).
  • Common adjustments: credit notes / debit notes timing, exempt supplies, year-end sales returns, discounts, FX for exports.
  • Narrative explanation for each unreconciled item.
  • Feeds into GSTR-9 Reconciliation (31 December).
  • ICAI 2024 guidance on Clause 44 methodology.

/ ICAI UDIN

The audit identifier.

  • UDIN (Unique Document Identification Number) mandatory for every CA audit report + certificate.
  • Generated on ICAI portal before signing + attached to Form 3CA/3CB.
  • Verifies CA membership + prevents misuse of CA signature.
  • UDIN check against ICAI records part of post-filing verification.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's the tax audit turnover threshold for AY 2026-27?
INR 1 crore (goods) / INR 50 lakh (profession) standard. INR 10 crore (goods) / INR 75 lakh (profession) if 95%+ digital receipts. Section 44AD / 44ADA presumptive scheme + turnover below threshold = exempt.
What's Clause 22 of Form 3CD?
Post Finance Act 2023 expansion: specific disclosure of Section 43B(h) MSE expense disallowance in current year + prior year reversal when paid during current year. Udyam certificate collection + 45-day aging + Section 15 MSMED interest. Cross-references MSME Form I half-yearly ROC filing.
What's Clause 44 Form 3CD GST reconciliation?
Reconciliation of Income Tax turnover with GST turnover (GSTR-1 + GSTR-9). Common adjustments: credit notes timing, exempt supplies, year-end returns, discounts, FX exports. Narrative explanation for each unreconciled item. ICAI 2024 guidance clarifies methodology.
Is UDIN mandatory on Form 3CA/3CB?
Yes - ICAI UDIN mandatory for every CA audit report + certificate since 2019. Generated on ICAI portal before signing + attached to Form 3CA/3CB. UDIN verifiable by taxpayer + tax authority against ICAI records.
What's the late fee for missing 31 October tax audit?
Section 234F late ITR filing fee + Section 271B penalty for late tax audit: 0.5% of turnover or INR 1,50,000 whichever lower. ITR filing blocked until Form 3CA/3CB linked on portal. Section 148A show-cause risk from AO.
