/ BEPS Action 13 recap

The CbCR framework.

  • OECD BEPS Action 13 (2015 report): three-tier TP documentation - Master File + Local File + CbCR.
  • CbCR threshold: MNE group consolidated revenue EUR 750M+ (or local-currency equivalent) in prior year.
  • CbCR filed by ultimate parent entity (UPE) + exchanged bilaterally among tax authorities.
  • Information included: revenue + profit + tax + employees + assets by jurisdiction.
  • India implemented via Section 286 Income Tax Act + Rule 10DB.
  • Hong Kong implemented via Section 58A-E Inland Revenue Ordinance.

/ Indian subsidiary's CbCR obligations

Form 3CEAC/AD.

  • Form 3CEAC (Intimation): Indian subsidiary of foreign MNE must file by 2 months before due date of CbCR (typically by 30 November following FY) - informs CBDT about CbCR filing status.
  • Field: Indian subsidiary's name + MNE's name + UPE's name + jurisdiction + CbCR filing entity + specific agreement for Exchange of Information (EOI).
  • Form 3CEAD (CbCR filing): if UPE in jurisdiction without EOI agreement OR surrogate / local filing applicable - Indian subsidiary (or designated entity) files complete CbCR in India by 12 months from end of reporting FY.
  • Hong Kong-India CbCR Exchange of Information: Hong Kong's AEOI framework + specific CbCR agreement + specific jurisdictional analysis.
  • Specific penalty: INR 5-50 lakh for failure to furnish Form 3CEAC/AD on time.

/ Hong Kong parent's filing obligations

The HK side.

  • Hong Kong Inland Revenue Ordinance Section 58A-E: CbCR filing for ultimate parent entity (UPE) resident in Hong Kong with MNE consolidated revenue HKD 6.8B+ (approx EUR 750M).
  • Deadline: 12 months from end of reporting FY.
  • Filed to IRD (Inland Revenue Department) via specific portal.
  • Automatic exchange: IRD shares with Indian CBDT if specific EOI / CbCR MCAA agreement.
  • Hong Kong joined Multilateral Convention on Mutual Administrative Assistance in Tax Matters (MCMAATM) + specific CbCR MCAA.

/ Master File (MF) + Local File (LF)

The accompanying docs.

  • Master File (Form 3CEAA): group-wide overview including org structure + business description + intangibles + inter-company financial activities + financial position. Threshold: INR 500 crore consolidated revenue + specific international transaction thresholds.
  • Local File: country-specific detail of specific Indian subsidiary's international transactions.
  • Specific separate filing of Form 3CEB (statutory TP audit report).
  • Specific retention: 8 years + specific case-specific extensions.

/ Hong Kong-specific considerations

The HK context.

  • Hong Kong Profits Tax rate: 16.5% (two-tiered: 8.25% on first HKD 2M; 16.5% thereafter) - specific QDMTT applicable from FY 2025 for MNEs.
  • Hong Kong territorial source taxation: specific offshore claim possible for non-HK sourced income.
  • India-Hong Kong DTAA: operational since 2017 - full treaty + MLI covered.
  • Dividend from Indian subsidiary to HK parent: 5-10% treaty rate under India-HK DTAA.
  • Specific HK Economic Substance requirements (if offshore claim) + specific substance rules.
  • Specific BEPS Pillar 2 HK QDMTT applicable from 1 January 2025.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What's the CbCR threshold for Hong Kong + Indian subsidiary?
MNE group consolidated revenue EUR 750M+ (approx HKD 6.8B+ / INR 7,000 crore+) in prior year. CbCR filed by UPE (HK parent) with HK IRD + exchanged with Indian CBDT via EOI. Indian subsidiary files Form 3CEAC intimation + specific Form 3CEAD if specific scenarios.
What's the Form 3CEAC filing deadline?
2 months before CbCR due date = typically 30 November following FY. For FY 2025-26 (ending 31 March 2026): Form 3CEAC due by 30 November 2026. Form 3CEAD (if applicable): 12 months from end of FY = 31 March 2027.
Does India-Hong Kong DTAA offer specific benefits?
Yes - operational since 2017. Dividend 5% treaty rate for 10%+ holdings (10% for others). Interest 10%. Royalty 10%. Capital gain: taxable in state of residence subject to specific real-estate / substantial-holding carve-outs. MLI covered agreement + PPT applies - substance file for HK structure essential.
Does Hong Kong implement BEPS Pillar 2?
Yes - QDMTT (Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax) in Hong Kong from 1 January 2025. IIR (Income Inclusion Rule) + UTPR expected. 15% minimum effective rate. HK Profits Tax 16.5% should satisfy for standard structures but offshore-claimed profits may trigger top-up. BQP + HK partner Pillar 2 assessment.
