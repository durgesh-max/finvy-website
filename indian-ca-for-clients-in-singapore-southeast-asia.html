/ NRI taxation for residents of Singapore & SE Asia

Indian tax when you live abroad.

  • Singapore taxes residents on Singapore-source income and most foreign income remitted is exempt in practice, so Indian income is usually taxed only in India.
  • India-Singapore DTAA: capital gains on Indian shares acquired after 1 April 2017 are taxable in India; dividend and interest relief needs a TRC and Form 10F (Form 41 from 1 April 2026).
  • Indian property, ESOPs from Indian employers and returning-to-India planning.

Can a client in Singapore & SE Asia work with BQP without visiting India?
Yes. Engagements run by email, shared drive and video calls; returns are e-filed and documents are e-signed. Physical visits are only needed for rare items such as original-document notarisation, which can usually be done locally and apostilled.
Does BQP handle NRI tax returns for people living in Singapore & SE Asia?
Yes: Indian income tax returns, treaty claims with TRC and Form 10F, lower deduction certificates for property sales, Form 15CA/15CB (Forms 145/146 from 1 April 2026) for repatriation, and planning for a return to India.
Can BQP do due diligence on an Indian company for a buyer in Singapore & SE Asia?
Yes: financial, tax, GST, FEMA and Companies Act diligence, delivered as a red-flag report and a full report with input for the share purchase agreement.
